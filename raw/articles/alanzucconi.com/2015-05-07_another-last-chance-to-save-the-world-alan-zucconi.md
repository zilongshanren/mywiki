When it comes to save the world, there aren’t that many options available. Especially when you’re an anthropomorphic purple rabbit from the future. This is the main reason why, on the 6th of May, I joined [BunnyLord’s Snazzy Fantastic Global Megaparty](https://www.facebook.com/events/1439648393001345/) at Loading Bar. That, and the free pizzas.

If you have missed it – spoiler alert! – it was a success. Even despite the fact it could have been mistaken for a UKIP pre-election party. I blame the purple rosettes for that. And the purple ties. And the purple balloons. And the purple rabbit in the middle of the room.

Incidentally, the [Not A Hero](http://store.steampowered.com/app/274270/) launch party has also been an occasion to celebrate [John Ribbins](https://twitter.com/flappyfingers)‘ birthday. (And I want to stress the fact that he had chosen *FlappyFingers* as his Twitter handle waaay before the appearance of FlappyBird)

There’s only one thing I’m not convinced of: wouldn’t have been easier to have *free biscuits zucchini for everyone*?
