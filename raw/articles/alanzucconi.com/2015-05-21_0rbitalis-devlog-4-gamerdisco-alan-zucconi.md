One of my favourite recurring events in London surely is [Gamerdisco](https://www.facebook.com/Gamerdisco). After having showcased ACCELERUNNER few months ago, I came back with the final release of 0RBITALIS.

Being a game developer for quite a few years, it’s not uncommon to meet YouTube-rs and Let’s-Play-ers. But I surely wasn’t expecting to find [TomSka](https://www.youtube.com/user/TomSka), [Bertie Gilbert](https://www.youtube.com/user/BertiebertG), [Sammy Paul](https://www.youtube.com/user/ICOEPRproductions), [Dean Dobbs](https://www.youtube.com/user/DeanDobbs), [Elliot Gough](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UClGxMg3LjbPZ-HDL-77SMiQ) and few others attending. Shame on me, I run out both of business cards and biscuits.
