### Mini Metro | [Dinosaur Polo Club](https://twitter.com/dinopoloclub) | [steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/287980/) | [website](http://dinopoloclub.com/minimetro/)

One of my most important design principles is elegance. And I cannot think about any other game which beautifully achieves it such as Mini Metro. Initially developed during [Ludum Dare](http://ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-26/comment-page-1/?action=preview&uid=21881), the game has gone a long way. Yet, it has always remained faithful both to its mechanic and its style. Mini Metro really has, in a nutshell, everything a 2D game can ask for. Including one of the highest score on Steam and and audio experience designed by [Disasterpeace](https://twitter.com/disasterpeace). Now the real question: why didn’t I thought about something so simple and ingenious myself? :p

There are thousands of games which are basically better clones or Super Mario; yet, 2D can still surprise you. Mushroom 11 is the perfect example of how deeply unexplored the genre is. The player takes control of a mouldy creature which can change shape to solve several different puzzles. The game has been one of my highlights from EGX London, especially since I had a lovely chat with [Itay Keren](https://twitter.com/itayke) and [Julia Detar](https://twitter.com/quiltingcrow) about its game mechanic. Itay has also give [one of the most insightful talk](http://www.gdcvault.com/play/1022243/Scroll-Back-The-Theory-and) at GDC San Francisco, which is an absolute must for all the people who are working on a 2D game.

### Interloper | [Monogon Games](http://www.monogongames.com/) | [steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/356420/) | [website](http://www.huethegame.com/)

I do not deny that I got curious about this game as soon as I knew that it won the Student Showcase in the IGF Award. Interloper is a 2D game about strategy – and that’s not new – but unlikely any other game you may have played before, it adopts a very unique mechanic. I had the chance to beta test the game and yes, it is as addictive as it looks. One of its developers is [Maxim Schoemaker](http://Maxim Schoemaker), possibly best known for appearing in [GDC’s most viral picture](http://www.gdconf.com/news/gdc_in_pictures_highlights_fro.html) (bottom of the page).
