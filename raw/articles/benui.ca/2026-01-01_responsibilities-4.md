I worked at **Epic Games** as a **UI Programmer** from May 2023 to January 2025. I mostly worked on LEGO Fortnite [Odyssey](https://kids.lego.com/campaigns/fortnite) (survival and crafting) and [Brick Life](https://www.fortnite.com/news/lego-fortnite-brick-life-build-your-life-in-the-big-city) (social hangout).

From: 2023 to 2025 (~1.8 years).

Location: Vancouver, Canada

## Responsibilities

- Implemented full-screen widgets like the Village Management screen. Wrote code that gathered the required data from various C++ systems and provided them to technical designers.
- Adding
**data replication**to server-only systems to ensure data is available in client-side UI features. - Ensured that code worked with the gigantic existing Fortnite codebase.
- Improved workflow for team members (and the community at large) by adding improvements to Unreal Editor.
- Worked extensively with
**Game Feature Plugins**, separating functionality for the live release schedule. - Used Unreal's
**MVVM system**to implement "apps" on the player's phone.
