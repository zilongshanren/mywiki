Hi, I'm **ben**, online I go by **benui**.

I grew up in the UK, lived and worked in the video games industry in Tokyo, Paris and Vancouver. I have something like **10 years' experience** as an **Unreal Engine UI programmer**, as well as experience with Unity and custom C++ engines.

I'm passionate about creating amazing user experiences not only for players but also for fellow devs; effortless workflows that let designers and artists create freely and make them love their jobs.

I previously worked on [LEGO Fortnite](https://benui.ca/about/lego-fortnite) at **Epic Games**. Before that, I was the UI Programmer on city-builder [Industries of Titan](https://benui.ca/about/industries-of-titan) and action-RPG [Vampyr](https://benui.ca/about/vampyr). For more see my [Portfolio](https://benui.ca#games).

I love sharing what I learn about Unreal Engine, so I created the ben ui website. After running it from 2017 to 2025, I transitioned the tutorials, documentation, tips and links to a new community site, ** Unreal**.

I've been lucky to be able to open-source code that I wrote for Brace Yourself Games as plugins on GitHub. For the full list see [Code](https://benui.ca#code) below.

I also try to give back to the community that helped me, by mentoring people and giving talks for university gamedev courses. I've given talks for the [University of British Columbia](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~sheffa/games_course/) and Birmingham City University. In 2023 I helped organize the relaunch of the [#notGDC Non-conference](https://notgdc.io/).

My CV/resume is available as a [PDF](https://benui.ca/cv/) and a [printable webpage](https://benui.ca/cv-print/).

## About the Site

I started benui.ca in 2017 as a place to write down things I got to grips with Unreal Engine. Information about building user interfaces in Unreal Engine was scattered around the web in forum posts and Unreal Wiki entries, so I wanted to collate it into a single place, for the selfish reason that it would make it easier for me to find the information again in the future.

Between 2017 and 2025 it grew into a popular resource for developers making UIs in Unreal Engine, with tens of thousands of unique visitors and page views per month. I received messages from people in industry and university educators that mention it as a useful source of information.

In January 2022 I created the [benui Discord server](http://discord.benui.ca/) as a place to discuss UI-related issues. It has attracted many professional developers who contribute to discussions on implementing in UMG and Slate, UI materials, localization and more.

In April 2025 I moved Unreal Engine-related content from benui to a new community-run site called ** Unreal**.
