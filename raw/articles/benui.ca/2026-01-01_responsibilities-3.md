I worked on **city-builder** [Industries of Titan](http://www.industriesoftitan.com) as the **UI programmer and designer**, in a core team of 5~6.

From: 2018 to 2021 (~3 years).

Location: Vancouver, Canada

Create a sprawling industrial city. Compete with the other Corporations for resources, territory, and power. Do battle with ships, technology, influence, or the sheer productive power of your factories. Stake your claim to the Industries of Titan in this innovative sim/strategy game!

## Responsibilities

- Implemented game UI in
**Unreal Engine 4**using C++ (UMG and Slate) and Blueprints. - Implemented
**centralized asset styling**system and enhanced standard Unreal widgets. - Created a custom
[rich text markup system](https://github.com/BraceYourselfGames/UE-BYGRichText)that accepted Markdown-like formatting. The system supported inline formatting, inline icons and tooltips. - Created a custom
[fan-translation-friendly localization system](https://github.com/BraceYourselfGames/UE-BYGLocalization). The system allowed fans to drop in CSV file translations, and gracefully fell back to English for untranslated strings. - Created
**tools to improve the workflow**of designers, artists and writers, allowing them to seamlessly define data in Google Sheets and see their changes in-game. - Created
**in-game bug reporter**and to accept player feedback, screenshot and saves. - Created
**UI art and designed the user experience**in collaboration with the art director, with the aim to let players understand the inner workings of their city without being overwhelmed.
