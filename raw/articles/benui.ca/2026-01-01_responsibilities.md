While at [Brace Yourself Games](http://www.braceyourselfgames.com/), I had the opportunity to contribute to [Cadence of Hyrule: Crypt of the Necrodancer Featuring The Legend of Zelda](https://cadenceofhyrule.nintendo.com/), performing additional programming.

From: 2019 to 2019 (~0.6 years).

Location: Vancouver, Canada

Save the legendary land of Hyrule in a game from the makers of Crypt of the NecroDancer, set to the world and music of the Legend of Zelda series in the Cadence of Hyrule: Crypt of the NecroDancer Featuring The Legend of Zelda game exclusively for the Nintendo Switch system.

## Responsibilities

- Implemented game UI in C++, using an in-house custom engine.
- Used Japanese language skills to aid collaboration with Nintendo.
