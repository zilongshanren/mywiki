Late in 2025 I quit Spotify after using it for 15+ years.

Since then I have been mostly buying albums on Bandcamp.

I was curious how much money I have been spending, and in particular, how much of it is going to the artists. Especially in comparison to a very long time subscribing.

Here is my back-of-an-envelop math:

### Spotify

| Subscription duration | 15 years | |
| Monthly subscription cost | $13 | |
| Total cost to me, over 15 years | 15 years x 12 months x $13 | $2,340 |
| Time listening to music a day | 3 hours | |
| Average song length | 3.5 minutes | |
| Average song plays per day | 51 plays | |
| Total song plays over lifetime | 51 x 15 years x 365 days | 281,571 plays |
| Song payout to artists | $0.003 per play | |
Total to artists over 15 years | 281,571 x $0.003 | $844.71 |
Amount to artists per year | $844.71 / 15 | $56.31 |

This seems kind of insane to me. Over 15 years I gave Spotify over $2,300. And artists saw maybe $800 of that.

Remember that per-year value of **$56**, let's compare it to buying albums on Bandcamp.

### Bandcamp

I like buying stuff on Bandcamp. It's easy, there's no required app, you get FLAC files, the artists get 70%, the prices are reasonable, it's not Amazon or Apple.

| Average price per album | $8 | |
| Albums purchased in last 6 months | 85 | |
| Total spent | $8 x 85 | $680 |
| Revenue share to artists | 70% | |
Total to artists over 6 months | $680 x 70% | $476 |

This is a conservative estimate of how much is going to the artists, as on [Bandcamp Friday](https://daily.bandcamp.com/features/bandcamp-fridays) the artist revenue share goes up to 100%.

I've definitely been buying more albums in the last 6 months than I would in the future. On average I think I'm buying around 10 albums a month as I slow down. So how does that compare to Spotify?

| Albums per month | 10 | |
| Average price per album | $8 | |
| Total spend per month | 10 x $8 | $80 |
| Revenue share to artists | 70% | |
Total to artists per month | $80 x 70% | $56 |

It's a fun coincidence that the numbers work out like that.

So artists are getting the same amount of money from me **per month** that they would have been getting **per year** when I streamed stuff on Spotify.
