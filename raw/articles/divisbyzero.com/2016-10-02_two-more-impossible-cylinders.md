Earlier this year, I wrote a [couple](https://divisbyzero.com/2016/07/05/sugiharas-circlesquare-optical-illusion/) of blog [posts](https://divisbyzero.com/2016/07/06/make-a-sugihara-circlesquare-optical-illusion-out-of-paper/) about reverse engineering [Sugihara’s impossible cylinder illusion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWfFco7K9v8). I then wrote it up more formally, and it has appeared in *Math Horizons* ([pdf](https://divisbyzero.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/richesonimpossiblecylinder-1.pdf)).

The example I gave on my blog and in the article was a cylinder that looked like a circular cylinder, but like a square cylinder in the mirror. You can download a [printable pdf template](https://divisbyzero.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/papercirclesquare.pdf) to make your own, and you can watch this video that I made.

Today I designed two more impossible cylinders. The first looks like a square cylinder and like a triangular cylinder in the mirror. Download a [printable pdf template](https://divisbyzero.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/papersquaretriangle.pdf) to make your own.
