I’m continuing my exploration of TikZ (here is [my first post about TikZ](https://divisbyzero.com/2013/04/09/bubble-diagrams-for-functions-in-latex-using-tikz/)). I will be showing my Discrete Math class how to “count” the positive rational numbers. (See [this old blog post](https://divisbyzero.com/2009/09/11/cardinality1-html/) for more information about countable sets.) I used TikZ to create the picture below.

Here is the [source code for this figure](https://www.writelatex.com/docs?snip_uri=http://users.dickinson.edu/~richesod/latex/rationals.tex&splash=none). If you click on the link you can get an editable copy of the document in WriteLatex.

The paper “Recounting the rationals twice” is really good along these lines, developing a single tree expansion that underlies the stern-brocot and calkin-wilf trees.
