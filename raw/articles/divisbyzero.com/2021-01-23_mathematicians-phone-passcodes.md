A character [in a novel I was reading](https://www.google.com/books/edition/The_Fear_Index/7Q-QHjEJ08gC?hl=en&gbpv=1) used the passcode 1729 for his house’s security system. He did so because of the famous [Hardy-Ramanujan anecdote about the number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1729_(number)). That got me to thinking. What would mathematicians of the past have used for their passcodes? I tweeted some ideas and got some great responses with additional suggestions.
