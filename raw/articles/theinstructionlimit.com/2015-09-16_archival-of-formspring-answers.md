From June 2012 to August 2013, I had a *ask me anything* kinda page on [Formspring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring.me), a social network/website that hosted such pages (similar to [ask.fm](http://ask.fm/), which I believe came in later). I didn’t realize that it was regarded as a pretty crappy site, so I stuck with it for more than a year… and later in 2013, it was sold and re-branded as *Spring.me*, started getting flooded with spam, and finally died last week, in September 2015.

Thankfully, I did export the answers I had written in March 2013, since Formspring added a tool to do so when they announced that they would close or rebrand. ~~It’s a shame that I lost everything between March and August~~, but here’s what I saved anyway :
