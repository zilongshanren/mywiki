[Alexandre Paes](http://blog.ponto-dot.com/) submitted a patch for my [CL-Growl library](http://nklein.com/software/cl-growl/) so it now works with CCL (aka. Clozure, formerly OpenMCL), too.

Here is the source tarball: [cl-growl_1.1.2010.04.29.tar.gz](http://nklein.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/04/cl-growl_1.1.2010.04.29.tar.gz) and the corresponding GPG signature: [cl-growl_1.1.2010.04.29.tar.gz.asc](http://nklein.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/04/cl-growl_1.1.2010.04.29.tar.gz.asc). For other ways to access this code, see the [CL-Growl page](http://nklein.com/software/cl-growl/).

Thank you, Alex!
