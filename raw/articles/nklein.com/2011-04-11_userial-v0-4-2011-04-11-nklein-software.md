Made an addition to the [USerial](http://nklein.com/software/unet/userial/) library to support logging of binary messages (so far only [cl-log](http://www.nicklevine.org/cl-log/) supported).

(serialize-log :log-category :uint32 uint-to-log :string "string to log" ...)
