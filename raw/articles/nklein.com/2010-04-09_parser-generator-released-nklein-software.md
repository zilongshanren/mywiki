A few weeks back, I described an [XML Parser Generator](http://nklein.com/2010/03/xml-parser-generator/) that I was working on. At the time, it could generate the parser it used itself. Now, it’s got Objective-C support and Lisp support. (The Lisp support is slightly better than the Objective-C support right now. With the Objective-C backend, you can create arrays of structs, but not arrays of strings or integers.)

Here is the [Parser Generator home page](http://nklein.com/software/parser-generator/).
