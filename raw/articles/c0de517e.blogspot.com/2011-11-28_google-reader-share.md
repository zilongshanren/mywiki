[google reader share](http://www.google.com/reader/shared/c0de517e)is dead (not updated anymore). That's not me being lazy, but google being a bit evil and trying to shove google+ down our throats by killing the old facilities instead of integrating plus into the existing stuff. So, there is no reader share anymore, and my iPad newsreader (reeder), which was responsible of most of the share posts, does not support google+ yet. Stay tuned.
