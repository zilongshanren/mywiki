To get your Steam key, just select the game you have bought from us, enter your registered e-mail address and click 'submit'.

If you lose your key just enter your e-mail again and we'll resend it.

If you need to update your e-mail address in our database, please [contact us](https://www.puppygames.net/contact)
and we'll sort it out as soon as we can.
