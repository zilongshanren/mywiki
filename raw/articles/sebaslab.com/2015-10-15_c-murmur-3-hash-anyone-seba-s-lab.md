If you need a fast and reliable (read very good to avoid collisions) hashing algorithm (not cryptographic), look no further, Murmur 3 is here to the rescue.

Take it from [here](https://github.com/sebas77/Murmur3.net/blob/master/Murmur3hash.cs), it’s compatible with Unity3D.

Directly translated from the [c++ version](https://code.google.com/p/smhasher/source/browse/trunk/MurmurHash3.cpp).

More info on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MurmurHash) and on this definitive answer on [stack overflow](http://programmers.stackexchange.com/questions/49550/which-hashing-algorithm-is-best-for-uniqueness-and-speed)
