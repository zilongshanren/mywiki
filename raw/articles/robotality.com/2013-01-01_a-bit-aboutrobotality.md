Robotality is an independent game development studio, founded in 2013 by the brothers Simon and Stefan Bachmann in co-operation with Nia Schmidheiny.


Our headquarters are in Hamelin, Germany, and we are incredibly proud to be a team of people from all over the world! We believe that a small tight-knit and passionate group of devs can produce truly unique game experiences.


Our first games Halfway and Pathway are now joined by our newest title and successor to Chucklefish’s Wargroove: Wargroove 2! Since the start we have dedicated ourselves to crafting beautiful story-worlds and action-driven tactical combat. We love pixel art and will put a butt on any sprite.
