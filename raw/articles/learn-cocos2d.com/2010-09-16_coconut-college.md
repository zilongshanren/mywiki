I’m the Dean of Coconut College, and I wanted to say thanks for mentioning us. A note to you and all your readers, Coconut College is still under heavy development. In fact, I’m working on a complete rewrite at the moment, and my goal is to complete the site by the time Cocos2d 1.0 ships.

Do you really think the layout is too wide? I’ll admit that I have little or no web design skills, but I kind of like the way it looks, and it displays nicely on the iPad. I’m not quite sure I’m going to add and ‘About’ page, this is more of a stealth operation for me.

Anway, you have a great site here, and I’m impressed with how much you’ve contributed to Cocos2d learning. Keep up the good work.

Other than that, I think the site feels very professional and polished, especially compared to what’s out there. You got the looks and you seem to be on the right way, content-wise.
