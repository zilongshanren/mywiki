`#include <KKRootViewController.h>`


Kobold2D root view controller implementation. If you need more than the default functionality, you should prefer to subclass [KKRootViewController](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/api-ref/1.0/Kobold2D/html/interface_k_k_root_view_controller/) rather than writing your own implementation. Remember to always call [super (overridden method)] in your implementation to allow [KKRootViewController](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/api-ref/1.0/Kobold2D/html/interface_k_k_root_view_controller/) to do its job.
