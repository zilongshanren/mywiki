Here are the classes, structs, unions and interfaces with brief descriptions:

[AppDelegate](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_app_delegate/) | The Application Delegate which receives all the OS callbacks |
[AssetHelper](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_asset_helper/) | Makes loading device-dependant resoures (assets) easier |
[GameBackground](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_game_background/) | Provides the background image/animations of the scene |
[GameHUD](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_game_h_u_d/) | Displays Score Labels and could also be used to show the graphic frames enclosing score labels (optional) |
[GameObjects](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_game_objects/) | Contains all moving game objects which can be given a [Path](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_path/) |
[GamePaths](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_game_paths/) | Contains, manages and creates all Paths |
[GameScene](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_game_scene/) | The scene (CCLayer) where the game takes place |
[GameUI](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_game_u_i/) | Displays the User Interface the player can interact with during the game |
[IncomingMarker](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_incoming_marker/) | They are the small warning signs that appear at the screen borders when a new object is moving in from outside the screen |
[MainMenu](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_main_menu/) | [MainMenu](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_main_menu/) is a placeholder for your pregame menu |
[MathHelper](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_math_helper/) | Helpful Math routines and commonly used calculations |
[Motivationals](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_motivationals/) | Manages motivational labels when landing, praising the player so he wants to see more of them |
[MovingObject](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_moving_object/) | Moving objects are the objects the player can touch to draw a [Path](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_path/) for them |
[ObjectDef](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/struct_object_def/) | Contains all configurable gameplay parameters of any [MovingObject](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_moving_object/) |
[ObjectDefs](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_object_defs/) | Singleton that gives you access to all Object Definitions and is the central class for creating [MovingObject](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_moving_object/) types |
[Path](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_path/) | A path is a list of CGPoint with special attributes that are drawn on screen |
[PathPoint](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/struct_path_point/) | All information to define a single [PathPoint](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/struct_path_point/) |
[<PauseDelegateProtocol>](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/protocol_pause_delegate_protocol-p/) | Callback for bringing up the game pause menu |
[TargetObject](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_target_object/) | Defines the position, radius and type of places where your objects can land, dock, etc |
[TargetObjects](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_target_objects/) | [TargetObjects](../../../line-drawing-game-starterkit-documentation/html/interface_target_objects/) manages the landing pads, docks or whatever you choose |
