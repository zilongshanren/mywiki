And here I thought [Zwoptex](http://zwoptexapp.com/), and that’s going to be it in terms of Texture Atlas creation. Much to my surprise I found the TexturePacker tool. But in comparison to Zwoptex, it doesn’t have a GUI, it’s a command line utility. On the other hand, it does everything automatically and is probably going to be a great choice for any automated procession. It can output in both Cocos2D and Corona formats.

The [TexturePacker](http://code-and-web.de/texturepacker) is available from the [code’n’web](http://code-and-web.de/) website and costs around $15 (€10).

It has a few very interesting features, for example removal of duplicate images and color reduction. Check out the [feature comparison chart on the Cocos2D wiki](http://www.cocos2d-iphone.org/wiki/doku.php/tips:zvt).
