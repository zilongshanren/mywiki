This action simulates a page turn from the bottom right hand corner of the screen It's not much use by itself but is used by the PageTurnTransition.

Based on an original paper by L Hong et al. [http://www.parc.com/publication/1638/turning-pages-of-3d-electronic-books.html](http://www.parc.com/publication/1638/turning-pages-of-3d-electronic-books.html)
