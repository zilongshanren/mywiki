This list is a collection of exemplary games and apps made with the Cocos2D for iPhone game engine. They should be seen as reference products in terms of what you can do with Cocos2D and simply the variety of games and apps developers have created with Cocos2D. This post is linked to from the Cocos2D book.

**If you wish to add a game or app to this list**, and you can provide prove or guarantee that it was made with Cocos2D, **please leave a comment**. From time to time I’ll move the notable mentions up to the actual blog post and delete it from the comments (to avoid duplication) but you can still grab some attention and a little traffic to your game by simply adding a comment to it here. And don’t forget that the official Cocos2D website has a [games page](http://www.cocos2d-iphone.org/games/), where you can also add yours.

**Note:** the order of the apps in this list is arbitrary, although I try to group similar apps together.

### The “Great Apps Made With Cocos2D” List

It’s priced highly but worth every cent, and if you need an App to brag about your new iPad: this is it!](http://itunes.apple.com/app/the-elements-a-visual-exploration/id364147847)

The flowers need your constant attention and the gameplay is addictive just like any Tamagotchi-style game. Oh, and it happens to be made by two former colleagues of mine. It’s just a beautiful game, and so is their follow-up game](http://itunes.apple.com/app/bloomies/id369149331)

**StickWars****is a sort of Tower Defense game where you defend your castle from incoming stick figures by flicking them in the air or literally shaking them to the ground. The developer, John Hartzog,**

[had never before worked with Objective-C or on mobile devices](http://www.cocos2d-iphone.org/forum/topic/192)but he pulled it off. StickWars remains to this date within the Top 100 games and continues to be updated even a years after the initial release.

[is also a Castle Defense game, except that this time hordes of zombies are attacking and you get explosives, 16 ton weights, shotguns and other cool items that make a bloody mess to fend them off. Your castle is your barn and if you can defend it, you’ll be rewarded with a slow motion animation of the final zombie losings its, err, unlife. The outstanding feature of this game is certainly the ragdoll animation system that allows zombies to walk, crawl or otherwise try to move even if they lost some of their limbs.](http://itunes.apple.com/app/zombiesmash/id349095099)

**ZombieSmash**[revives a simple gameplay concept of a scrolling level where your character always falls down except when you touch the screen to boost his jetpack. The extraordinary part here is that the game’s graphics are extremely well made and the overall](http://itunes.apple.com/app/super-turbo-action-pig/id360371122)

**Super Turbo Action Pig**[presentation of the game](http://www.cocos2d-iphone.org/forum/topic/4837), the trailer, the

[website](http://joystickgenerals.com/)and the humor are setting a great example.

**Trainyard**[saves and loads the game just as the user left it](http://www.cocos2d-iphone.org/forum/topic/7019)and even allows users to

[share puzzle solutions on the web](http://www.trainyard.ca/solutions/), using a duplicate of the game engine written in Flash. This all besides being a really innovative puzzle game where you lay tracks and combine trains to match them with colored trainyards.

It’s an intense space shooter with plenty of game modes. You can even play it in multiplayer via a Bluetooth connection and it allows you to use your own iPod music.

**Fuji Leaves**[It’s intensely fascinating to play this game](http://www.cocos2d-iphone.org/forum/topic/5559), trying to come up with interesting scores and just the right placement of leaves. Before you know it, an hour has passed.
