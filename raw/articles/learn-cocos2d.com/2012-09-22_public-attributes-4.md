Contact impulses for reporting. Impulses are used instead of forces because sub-step forces may approach infinity for rigid body collisions. These match up one-to-one with the contact points in [b2Manifold](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/api-ref/2.0/Box2D/html/structb2_manifold/).
