[CCParticleSystemQuad](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/api-ref/latest_2.x/cocos2d-iphone/html/interface_c_c_particle_system_quad/) is a subclass of [CCParticleSystem](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/api-ref/latest_2.x/cocos2d-iphone/html/interface_c_c_particle_system/)

It includes all the features of ParticleSystem.

Special features and Limitations:

initilizes the texture with a rectangle measured Points

Sets a new texture with a rect. The rect is in Points.
