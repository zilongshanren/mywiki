Now, this is just absurd.

Lima Sky, developers of App Store hit Doodle Jump, are [trying to remove all Apps with “Doodle” in the title](http://www.148apps.com/news/lima-sky-claiming-copyright-term-doodle/) from the App Store. On the basis of a trademark claim. With the help of Apple.

They do own the trademark for “Doodle Jump”. They currently do **not** own the trademark for “Doodle”. That would be like the developers of “Angry Birds” (trademarked) trying to remove all Apps with “Angry” in the title. Or “Birds”. Poor [ornithology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ornithology) apps.

Yup, it’s nothing but absurd. Or shall I say: [EDGEy](http://www.148apps.com/news/mobigames-edge-ios/)?
