Nate Weiss, author of the commercial [iPhone Game Kit](http://www.iphonegamekit.com/), would like you to help build the first community driven RPG game for the iPhone. [Read his announcement.](http://www.iphonegamekit.com/blog/code/develop-the-game-kits-community-rpg-and-be-a-part-of-gaming-history/)

He wrote a [game design document](http://www.iphonegamekit.com/pics/Quexlor-Community.pdf) for you to wet your appetite and learn what needs to be done. It’s an ambitious project that I believe would be awesome to take part in, especially if you don’t have the time and energy to build a complete game project by yourself, but you still like to take part in fruitful game development activities. What the game needs most is to design levels using the popular [Tiled Map Editor](http://www.mapeditor.org/), and new artworks for tiles, characters and cutscenes.

You do need to have a copy of the iPhone Game Kit to participate, but currently it’s on sale with 30% off and costs only $69. For that you get the complete source code and assets for the Quexlor action RPG and a 150 page game development eBook. I think his product and ebook are excellent and well worth the money, and I’m currently running two ads for Nate on a voluntary basis.
