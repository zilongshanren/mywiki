Continuing with yesterday’s [Box2D Car demo](../../../2010/12/linkvent-calendar-day-14-box2d-topdown-car-demo/) I’d like to stay on the topic of Box2D and present to you the [Tilemap based Box2D world](http://www.thetconcept.com/?p=489) put together by [theTconcept](http://www.thetconcept.com/?page_id=24), a website hosted by a group of italian and mexican designers, writers and coders.

In their tutorial they explain how to create Box2D collisions from a [Tiled Map Editor](http://www.mapeditor.org/) world, by using the object group layer. The one you can use to place arbitrary rectangles on in Tiled. The resulting Xcode project is [available for download](http://www.thetconcept.com/blog/download/cocos2d/cocos2d-box2d_demo.zip).
