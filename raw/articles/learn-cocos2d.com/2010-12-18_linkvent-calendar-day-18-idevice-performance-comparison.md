This one is for the “techies” among you who would like to know about low-level performance specs of the iOS devices. Maybe you’ve heard of [Mike Ash](http://mikeash.com/) before? He’s a Mac programmer who writes about his profession on a weekly basis and [Mike’s development blog](http://www.mikeash.com/pyblog/) is well worth browsing for highly interesting material. Back in March 2008 he published one of the first [iPhone performance comparison for common operations](http://mikeash.com/pyblog/performance-comparisons-of-common-operations-iphone-edition.html).

Stuart Carnie then took this performance comparison and updated it with results for iPhone 4 and iPad, and published them in his [Micro-Benchmarking blog post](https://aussiebloke.blogspot.com/2010/01/micro-benchmarking-2nd-3rd-gen-iphones.html) along with the source code. If you’re interested to learn how long it takes to perform a floating point division or a 1 MB memcopy across several iOS devices, these are the tests you were looking for.
