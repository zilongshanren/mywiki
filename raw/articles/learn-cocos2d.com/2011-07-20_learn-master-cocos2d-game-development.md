### Attraxxion Postmortem

And the first link comes from John Talarico, co-founder of [Runaway Creations, Inc](http://www.runawaycreations.com/). He posted a two-part Postmortem about their first Cocos2D based game Attraxxion (App Store: [full version](http://itunes.apple.com/app/attraxxion/id391232531?mt=8) / [free lite version](http://itunes.apple.com/app/attraxxion-lite/id398075756?mt=8)) following the regular What Went Right and What Went Wrong convention of postmortems.

Attraxxion is a physics puzzle game that involves gravity, shooting masses at the proper angle towards the sun so that eventually a solar system forms and is able to harbor alien life. The game has been in development for 7 months by a team who didn’t have any previous Mac development experience but they have extensive experience developing software in general.
