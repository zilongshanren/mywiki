In this [episode of the Cocos2D Podcast](http://cocos2dpodcast.wordpress.com/2011/09/05/kobold2d-with-steffen-itterheim/) it’s me who is being interviewed. I’m talking about [Kobold2D](http://www.kobold2d.com/), which is my take on keeping the Cocos2D wheel spinning. Over the past 2.5 years I learned a lot about Cocos2D, and I came across plenty of things that I feel should really be corrected. Kobold2D is that course correction towards a better overall user experience.

In this podcast you’ll learn more about the project’s goals, its history and future. I’m also giving away the download link for the Kobold2D Preview builds.
