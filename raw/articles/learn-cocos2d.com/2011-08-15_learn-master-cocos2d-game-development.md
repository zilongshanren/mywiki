This question comes up a lot since PR ([Public Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_relations)) companies don't publicly show their price list to everyone. I can at least give you some rough ballpark numbers although your experience may vary since every PR company charges differently.

For a single [Press Release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Press_release), expect to pay at least $1,000 to $2,000.

For an EPK ([Electronic Press Kit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_press_kit)), expect to pay about $2,000.

You can also send your Press Releases via services that are more suitable to Indies in pricing:[The Indie Press Release Service](http://www.gamerelease.net/)[Game Press Release Submission Service at Mitorah Games](http://www.mitorahgames.com/Submit-Game-Press-Release.html)

If you know more Press Release Services please post a comment!
