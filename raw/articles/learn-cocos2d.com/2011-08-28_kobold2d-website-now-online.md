The [Kobold2D Website](http://www.kobold2d.com/) is now online. Go, have a look and let me know what you think.

Here are some pointers to get you started about Kobold2D (in case you haven’t heard) and the Kobold2D website:

Not sure what that means? In essence Kobold2D wants to make development with Cocos2D easier and more powerful for you. And in case you’re wondering: yes, Kobold2D is

**free and open source**.Some of the things you should know about Kobold2D:

- Kobold2D has an
[installer that gets you started in no time](http://www.kobold2d.com/display/KKDOC/Installing+Kobold2D) - Kobold2D has 8 (soon: 12+) exciting
[template projects to get started with](http://www.kobold2d.com/display/KKDOC/Creating+a+Kobold2D+Project) - Kobold2D provides all the libraries you’ll need: Cocos2D, Cocos3D, Wax, Lua, iSimulate, ObjectAL, Chipmunk SpaceManager, SneakyInput and more
- Kobold2D has
[all the documentation](http://www.kobold2d.com/display/KKDOC/Kobold2D+Reference+Documentation): online & offline HTML, Xcode Help - Kobold2D
[answers your frequently asked questions](http://www.kobold2d.com/display/KKDOC/Kobold2D+FAQ)… - Kobold2D
[helps you move your Cocos2D Projects over](http://www.kobold2d.com/display/KKDOC/Migrating+a+Cocos2D+Project)…
