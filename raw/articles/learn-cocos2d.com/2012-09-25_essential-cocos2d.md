##### What’s the difference to the *Learn Cocos2D 2* book?

Both the [ Learn cocos2d 2](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/store/book-learn-cocos2d/) book (published by Apress) and

**Essential Cocos2D**are designed to complement each other.

**Learn Cocos2D 2** is for starters, who have little or no experience with either one of: Cocos2D, iOS, or game development in general. It’s an **all-encompassing, beginner level tutorial book**.

**Essential Cocos2D** is for those who want to learn specific aspects of cocos2d in detail, how to use it and best practices. It’s an **invaluable reference and a problem/solution handbook**. It’s also the place to get help when you get stuck. And through your feedback the documentation is continuously improving and expanding.
