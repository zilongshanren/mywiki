Mohammad Azam will be hosting a FREE webinar: [Introduction to iPhone Game Development Using the Cocos2D framework](http://iosdevblog.wordpress.com/2010/12/05/introduction-to-iphone-game-development-using-cocos2d-free-webinar/).

This webinar takes place **TOMORROW** (Dec 11th)! I replicate his blog post here with the crucial info:

The webinar will help getting the new comers get started with iPhone development. It will be all code based with minimal or no slides. The webinar will be hosted on [www.freebinar.com](http://www.freebinar.com/). Additional details are listed below:

**Webinar URL:** [http://www.freebinar.com/highoncoding1](http://www.freebinar.com/highoncoding1)

**Date:** 12/11/2010 (December 11 2010)

**Time:** 11:00 AM ([US Central Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone_(North_America)))

**Limit:** 150 people

Mohammad is originally a .NET programmer and frequent speaker at tech events. Recently he started diving into iOS development. He has written several tutorials about Cocos2D and he’ll get another Linkvent Calendar post soon that lists all of the Cocos2D screencasts he made.
