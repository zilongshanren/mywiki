The [latest episode of the Cocos2D Podcast](http://cocos2dpodcast.wordpress.com/2011/06/20/earning-additional-revenue-using-iads-iap-selling-source-code-etc/) is out!

This time Azam and I talk about the options you have to earn additional revenue from or with your apps, using iAd ads, In-App Purchases, selling your source code and other ideas.

You might also want to read my blog post [How to make over $18,000 in six months selling source code](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/) about the financial success of my [Line-Drawing Starterkit](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/store/line-drawing-game-starterkit/). By the way, I’ll be releasing a new game kit in autumn. More details in the coming weeks.
