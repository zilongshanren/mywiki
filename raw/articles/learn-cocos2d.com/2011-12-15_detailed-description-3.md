[CCTransitionZoomFlipX](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/api-ref/1.0/cocos2d-iphone-mac/html/interface_c_c_transition_zoom_flip_x/): Flips the screen horizontally doing a zoom out/in The front face is the outgoing scene and the back face is the incoming scene.
