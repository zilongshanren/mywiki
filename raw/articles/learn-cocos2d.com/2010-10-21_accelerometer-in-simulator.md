AccSim written by Otto Chrons is an App ([download on iTunes](http://itunes.apple.com/de/app/accelerometer-simulator/id336476721?mt=8)) that allows you to control your game running in the Simulator with your iOS device. Meaning, the game plays in the Simulator but accelerometer input is actually coming from your device! You can learn more about it on the [Built.By.Me website](http://builtby.me/2010/10/use-the-accelerometer-in-the-iphone-simulator/), which includes a sample video:

Hmmm … somehow [this game seems strangely familiar](http://www.learn-cocos2d.com/2010/07/cocos2d-book-chapter-5-bigger/). 😀

AccSim doesn’t pack the same punch as [iSimulate](http://www.vimov.com/isimulate/features/) does, which can also be used for sending multi-touch input, GPS and Compass events to the Simulator, plus a lot more. But the good thing about AccSim is that it’s not just free but the [AccSim source code](https://code.google.com/p/accelerometer-simulator/wiki/Home) is open source!
