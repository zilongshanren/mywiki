The Silhouette effect colors each scene element based on its distance from the camera.

![Silhouette](../../assets/1c039293cbb349ac.jpg)


# Parameters

**Enabled**- Is the effect active?**Near Color**- The color of elements resting on the near clip plane of the camera.**Far Color**- The color of elements resting on the far clip plane of the camera.
