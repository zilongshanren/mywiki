Imitates the effect seen when wrapping a 2D image across a barrel with a bulge in the centre. The corners of the screen are pulled in towards the centre more than the edges.

![Barrel Distortion](../../assets/51ac45cbd85758f3.jpg)


# Parameters

**Strength**- How strongly to distort the screen. Higher values pull pixels towards the center more.**Background Color**- Color to use for the outer edge of the screen.
