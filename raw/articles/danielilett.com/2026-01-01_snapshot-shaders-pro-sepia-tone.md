Turns the screen sepia-toned based on pixel luminance. Sepia-tone looks like an old yellowed photograph.

![Sepia Tone](../../assets/0dd5cc3ac2584c79.jpg)


# Parameters

**Blend**- The strength of the effect. A value of one results in a fully sepia-toned effect; zero makes no change to the original pixel values.
