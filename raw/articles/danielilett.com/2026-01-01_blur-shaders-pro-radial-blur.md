A Gaussian Blur which gets stronger towards the edges of the image.

# Parameters

**Strength**- The size of the blurring kernel (and the strength of the effect). Larger smoothing kernels require more pixel operations per frame.**Luminance Threshold**- The proportion of the screen which stays unblurred in the middle.
