The SNES effect quantises each colour channel to a set number of levels. 6 levels gives the approximate colour palette of the SNES, and 4 gives the approximate colour palette of the NES.

![SNES](../../assets/3dec4f1255e8683c.jpg)


# Parameter

**Enabled**- Is the effect active?**Banding Levels**- The number of quantisation levels to use.
