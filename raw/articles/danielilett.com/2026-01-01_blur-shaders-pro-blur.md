The **Blur** effect blurs each pixel based on the colors of nearby pixels.

![Blur](../../assets/615ef21b7bbc07cb.png)


# Parameters

**Strength**- The size of the blurring kernel (and the strength of the effect). Larger smoothing kernels require more pixel operations per frame.**Blur Type**- Toggle between**Gaussian Blur**(the blurring kernel uses a Gaussian curve to soften further from the middle) and**Box Blur**(all pixels in the blurring kernel are weighted evenly).
