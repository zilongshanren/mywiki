Modifies the color of the scene slightly to simulate the physical imperfections you would find on analogue film formats.

![Noise Grain](../../assets/0513b9caeaada60a.jpg)


# Parameters

**Strength**- How strongly the noise changes the scene colors.**Speed**- How quickly the noise values change.**Noise Size**- The size of the noise ‘particles’ on the screen.**Noise Interpolation**- Either Hermite or Quintic. Hermite is faster, but Quintic produces very slightly nicer results.
