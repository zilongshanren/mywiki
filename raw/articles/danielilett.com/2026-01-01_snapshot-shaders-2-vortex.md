Swirls the image around a center point in a vortex pattern.

# Parameters

**Strength**- How strongly to whirl pixels around the vortex. Higher values mean pixels make more rotations around the screen.**Center**- The center point of all vortex rotations, as a normalized screen coordinate.**Offset**- How far to offset the image prior to performing the vortex effect.**Rotation**- How much to rotate the entire screen uniformly post-vortex, as a proportion between no rotation and one full rotation.
