The Synthwave effect overlays lines onto the scene in world space in the X, Y, and Z axes (or a subset of those axes).

![Synthwave](../../assets/b38110ffa21fb85e.jpg)


# Parameters

**Background Color**- The color of the background if Use Scene Color is off.**Line Colors 1 & 2**- HDR-enabled colors which span the screen from the bottom (1) to the top (2).**Line Color Mix**- Controls the proportion of Line Colors 1 & 2 across the screen.**Line Width**- Thickness of the lines in world space units.**Line Falloff**- Falloff distance between lines and background in world space units.**Gap Width**- Distance between lines in each of the three axes.**Offset**- Distance of the grid system from (0, 0, 0).**Axis Mask**- Controls which axes the lines appear on.**Use Scene Color**- Replaces the background color with the original scene color if turned on.
