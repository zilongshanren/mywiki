*Hologram Shaders Pro* adds a range of new hologram effects to Godot Engine. From basic holograms with **Fresnel highlights** to advanced holograms with two kinds of **glitch** effect, **scanlines**, to stylistic **dot matrix** and **gridline** holograms, there’s plenty here to make your games look futuristic!

Use the **Effects List** menu button on the left to navigate through each effect in the pack.

# ✨ Features

The hologram shaders in this pack were designed to be flexible. Some of the shaders support the same features as other shaders, but if you’re looking for just a simple Fresnel highlight, use the **Basic** hologram. If you want gridlines, the **Grid** hologram will give you that. For the most choice, choose the **Uber** hologram.

## 🎚️ Tons of settings

Each effect comes with plenty of settings, which makes each hologram very versatile. You can use them to make basic transparent objects with a bit of rim lighting, or you can create a complicated hologram which glitches out sporadically and scans from top to bottom over time with a fuzzy, noisy body.

## ✅ Supported Versions

This asset pack was created with Godot 4.5, and compatibility is only guaranteed on version 4.5 and higher. It is compatible with all renderers (Forward Plus, Compatibility, Mobile).
