*Hologram Shaders Pro* adds a range of new hologram effects to Unity. From basic holograms with **Fresnel highlights** to advanced holograms with two kinds of **glitch** effect, **scanlines**, to stylistic **dot matrix** and **gridline** holograms, there’s plenty here to make your games look futuristic!

Use the menu button on the left to navigate through each effect in the pack.

# ✨ Features

The hologram shaders in this pack were designed to be flexible, with variants of the core effect with different feature sets and one Uber hologram with all core effects, which can also be turned off with keyword toggles. As the effects were created with Shader Graph, they will work in all of Unity’s render pipleines: URP, HDRP, and Built-in.

## 🎚️ Tons of settings

Each effect comes with plenty of settings, which makes each hologram very versatile. You can use them to make basic transparent objects with a bit of rim lighting, or you can create a complicated hologram which glitches out sporadically and scans from top to bottom over time with a fuzzy, noisy body.
