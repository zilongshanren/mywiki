# 30 standout reasons to attend the 30th edition of GDC next week

The <a href=www.gdconf.com/?_mc=BP_LE_CON>30th edition of GDC</a> kicks off next week, and organizers are eager to let you know about all the great sessions, bootcamps, roundtables, interactive spaces and cool events at the show!

March 8, 2016

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

Get ready: The [Game Developers Conference](http://www.gdconf.com/?_mc=BP_LE_CON) kicks off next week in San Francisco, and organizers are eager to let you know about all the great sessions, bootcamps, roundtables, interactive spaces and cool events that will be available for you to check out during the week-long show!

There's still time to [buy a ticket](http://www.gdconf.com/attend/passes.html?_mc=BP_LE_CON), too -- online registration for GDC 2016 will remain open through this Wednesday, March 9th at 11:59 PM Pacific, and you can even buy a pass at the door if you like.

This will be the 30th edition of the Game Developers Conference, and organizers are celebrating the momentous occasion in a variety of ways -- including putting together a beautiful infographic (teaser below!) that will give you a quick overview of [30 great things to see and do at the 30th GDC](http://www.gdconf.com/attend/30reasons.html?_mc=BP_LE_CON).

So take a peek at the [big list](http://www.gdconf.com/attend/30reasons.html?_mc=BP_LE_CON), and as you're putting together your GDC 2016 session schedule in the [Schedule Builder](http://schedule.gdconf.com/list?_mc=BP_LE_CON) don't forget to set some time aside to check out the variety of other lounges, exhibits, and [events](http://www.gdconf.com/events/index.html?_mc=BP_LE_CON) that will be set up across the Moscone Center throughout the week.

![](../../assets/e001f593e8aa4f3d.img)


GDC 2016 itself will take place March 14-18th at the Moscone Center in San Francisco. For more information on GDC 2016, visit the show's [official website](http://gdconf.com/?_mc=BP_LE_CON), or subscribe to regular updates via [Facebook](http://www.facebook.com/GameDevelopersConference?_mc=BP_LE_CON), [Twitter](http://twitter.com/Official_GDC?_mc=BP_LE_CON), or [RSS](http://feeds.feedburner.com/GameDevelopersConference?_mc=BP_LE_CON).
