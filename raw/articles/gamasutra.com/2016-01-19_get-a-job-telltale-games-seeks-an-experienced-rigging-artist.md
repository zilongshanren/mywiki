Location: San Rafael, California

Telltale Games is looking for a Senior Rigging Artist to join our team! Telltale is an award-winning independent developer and publisher of video games and was named 2014’s ‘Most Innovative Company in Gaming’, by Fast Company.

Telltale was also recognized by Metacritic as the No. 1 overall publisher for quality content in 2014. Do you want to help us make great games?

Responsibilities:

Create Character/Prop rigging and skinning

Create facial shapes for expressive characters

Implement art assets in game

Identify and develop art pipeline solutions

Work with artists and engineers to ensure game assets are up-to-date and working

Mentor Junior Technical Artists

Allocate and manage proper rigging staffing for all projects

Review rigs for all projects

Cleanup character and environment models

Organize art assets, create file naming

Provide Maya and engine support for the Art team


Essential Skills:

Strong organizational skills and communication

Minimum 4 years Character Rigging experience for video games

Knowledge of current rigging techniques

Modeling and topology experience

Ability to multi-task and prioritize

Mel/Python experience required

Interest in game production

Experience with cloth dynamics and motion capture a plus


Candidates must be legally authorized to work and accept new employment in the U.S.

Telltale Games is an Equal Opportunity Employer and ADA compliant.

Interested? [Apply now](http://jobs.gamasutra.com/job/senior-rigging-artist-san-rafael-california-29890).
