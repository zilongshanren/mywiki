Location: Raleigh, NC

Founded in 2014 by Cliff Bleszinski and Arjan Brussee, Boss Key Productions is a new video game development studio based in the heart of Raleigh, NC. BKP believes in an agile, no-nonsense approach to product and studio building with an emphasis on high quality product in a friendly environment while remaining as transparent as possible to the gaming community.

Boss Key is currently partnered with Nexon, co, on a project that is codenamed “BlueStreak,” a free to play science fiction first person shooter.

Boss Key Productions is looking for a talented Visual Effects (FX) Artist to create stunning visual effects for our titles. The ideal candidate will show strong attention to detail backed up with excellent technical understanding and ability to achieve optimum results. Industry experience or equivalent preferred and all applications should be accompanied with examples of work.

The primary job responsibilities of the Visual Effects Artist are:

Be experienced at creating a wide range of high quality particle effects using proprietary particle tools and various 3rd party software

Interact closely with rendering programmers and technical art to maintain and improve our effects systems and tools

Have the ability to collaborate effectively with other team disciplines (code, design, animation)

Demonstrate creativity and technical ability within the constraints of PC development

Develop assets optimized for current PC systems

Troubleshoot and problem solve visual effects related issues


Requirements

Industry experience, with shipped title demonstrating real-time particle effects

Exceptional understanding of Photoshop and/or After Effects for creating textures and effects sequences

Experience with 3dsMax modeling and UV mapping

Self-motivated, with a strong ability to prioritize multiple tasks effectively

Strong communication and organizational skills

Effective time management skills to meet deadlines without sacrificing quality

The ability to view visual reference of an effect, deconstruct it and subsequently re-create it in a real-time game engine is essential

Strong knowledge of environmental effects, lighting, and dynamics

Only US citizens or individuals with a valid green card should apply


Pluses

Deep proficiency and skill playing first-person shooters

Expert understanding of and experience playing highly challenging, cooperative experiences


Required Application Materials

Resume

Cover letter

Portfolio of relevant work

Only online artwork submissions will be accepted (e.g. website, blog or online album). Willingness to complete art test if required



**Only submittals with all three will be considered.

Interested? [Apply now](http://jobs.gamasutra.com/job/fx-artist-raleigh-north-carolina-28713).
