If the phrase “Cutter, you’ve got 5 minutes left in your shift” makes your pulse quicken, then you’re intimately familiar with the beautiful in-universe user interface of Hardspace: Shipbreaker.

And we’ve got good news for all the Cutters out there—in one of many talks about the game at the all-digital 2021 [Game Developers Conference](https://gdconf.com/?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-29) (running July 19th-23rd), Blackbird Interactive’s Vidhi Shah will be presenting a session dedicated to breaking down the game’s excellent UI.

In [“Cutting Apart The Diegetic Interface of ](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/cutting-apart-the-diegetic-interface-of-hardspace-shipbreaker/880725?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-29)[Hardspace: Shipbreaker](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/cutting-apart-the-diegetic-interface-of-hardspace-shipbreaker/880725?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-29)[”](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/cutting-apart-the-diegetic-interface-of-hardspace-shipbreaker/880725?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-29) Shah will be showcasing various UI design styles and the process of creating an amalgamation of approaches best suited for Hardspace: Shipbreaker's interfaces in order to maintain immersion while addressing usability and accessibility needs.

Get more info on GDC 2021 and gear up for Shah’s talk when you [register for your pass today.](https://gdconf.com/passes-prices?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-29)
