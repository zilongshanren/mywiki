![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

As 2017 comes to a close, Gamasutra editors are rounding up their [top games of 2017](https://www.gamasutra.com/search/index.php?search_text=%22best+of+2017%22&submit=Search). And as a site for game devs, we’d like all of you to share your picks too in our [blogs section](https://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/).

[Blog now](https://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/edit/blog/item/) and share which games left an impression on you this year—the ones that excelled in terms of art, design, and technical achievement. Your list doesn’t have to include a certain number of games. Just tell us what you liked and explain why as a game dev you liked it.

We look forward to seeing your choices!
