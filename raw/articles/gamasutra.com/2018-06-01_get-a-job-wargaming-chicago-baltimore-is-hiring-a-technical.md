Location: Chicago, Illinois

Don’t know what Wargaming does? Well, Wargaming is a global team of gamers established in 1998 with one important mission – let gamers have fun. Across the globe, we have experienced teams of passionate and driven gaming professionals striving to push our games further every day. In 2008, Wargaming released its flagship title, World of Tanks, which led to opportunities for our teams to develop and support games like World of Warships, World of Planes, Total War Arena, and much more.

If you love everything video games, then we’re definitely looking for you. Our team is growing, and we have projects you’ll be spearheading with your name on it.

GREAT, BUT WHAT WILL YOU DO?

Wargaming is looking for someone that can use content authoring and game engine tools to integrate character motions and procedural effects to create responsive, compelling, and attractive player- and AI-controlled character movement in a new title. We’re also looking for someone that can develop and maintain animation processes, rigs, pipelines, and animation systems.

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR:

Excellent communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills, interdepartmental fluency

Experience using in-game animation systems, with a thorough understanding of blend trees, IK, state machines, tagging, and scripting

Experience with planning and implementing real-time animation systems, walk cycles, strafing, aiming, environmental interactions, etc.

Experience with 3ds Max, Maya, MotionBuilder


THESE WOULD BE SOLID BONUS:

Experience with motion capture pipelines

Proficiency with dynamic IK, physics (Havok), and rigging/animation for use with those systems

Familiar with version control software

Mel, Python, Lua experience

Familiar with modern console animation systems

Basic modeling and realistic animation skills

Passion for video games and game development
