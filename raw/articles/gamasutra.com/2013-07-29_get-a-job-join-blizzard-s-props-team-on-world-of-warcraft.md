Location: Irvine, CA A Sr. Prop Artist can work on everything from an Orc battle wagon to a melted candle. This gives the game designers a huge library of objects to use in dungeons, towns, quests and more. There's no telling what task will be needed next, so a great prop artist needs to be a swift and efficient modeler with strong painting skills and have a skull full of great ideas. Do you have a solid background in the foundations of art such as structure, composition and color? How about a portfolio crammed full of concepts, models and texture maps? If so, the World of Warcraft Prop Team wants to meet you. Interested? [Apply now](http://jobs.gamasutra.com/jobseekerx/viewjobrss.asp?cjid=33580&accountno=48212).
