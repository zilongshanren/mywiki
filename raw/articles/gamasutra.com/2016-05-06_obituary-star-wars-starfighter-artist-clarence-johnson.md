Veteran artist and technical director, Clarence Johnson, has passed away.

Early in his career Johnson set up shop at LucasArts, where he served as a 3D modeler on Star Wars: Starfighter, and created art for its follow-up, Star Wars: Jedi Starfighter.

More recently, Johnson helped animate The Walking Dead: Survival Instinct, and prior to that worked on the well-received Ghostbusters: The Video Game as environment technical director.

Johnson's other credits include Golden Axe: Beast Rider, Iron Man, Magic: The Gathering - Battlegrounds, and There.

News of his passing was broken on [We Are Game Devs](http://www.wearegamedevs.com/2016/05/04/in-memoriam-clarence-johnson/#.VyuBFz1vtKM.facebook), which has since been flooded with messages celebrating his life.
