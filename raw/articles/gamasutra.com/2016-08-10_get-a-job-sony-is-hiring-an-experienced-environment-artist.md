Location: San Mateo, CA

Are you a Senior Environment Artist ready to be part of something completely different? Our team currently led by some of our industry’s best artistic and creative veterans is looking for their next Sr. level artist to help build something magical. Come join our San Mateo studios. Greatness Awaits!

Our Senior Environment Artist must:

Possess the ability to effectively collaborate with a wide-range of development team members outside of their direct discipline to optimally achieve artistic goals and implementation

Act as a high level contributor to the development of the artistic techniques and processes utilized to define and achieve a product’s visual style or encourage artistic evolution within the studio

Develop a comprehensive knowledge of the art production pipeline and all engine, tools and systems associated with the creation of art assets

Ensure environment/level assets are of the highest artistic quality and uphold the integrity of the artistic vision of the game as a whole

Be capable of resolving an assortment of moderately complex artistic development issues for both artists and other team members

Serve as an artistic resource for overall product or studio needs, by collaborating and discussing general research, developing cutting-edge techniques and testing new systems or tools, techniques, providing training, transmitting industry related information and conveying concerns


Qualifications

Strong traditional fine art skills, training and production experience (illustration, painting, organic forms, color theory, perspective, 2D & 3D design.)

Proficient in the use of Unreal 4 Maya (or 3D modeling package) and Photoshop.

Unreal 4 experience desirable

Previous or next-gen video game platform experience is desirable

Good communication, organizational, time management and interpersonal skills

Works under minimal direction to determine and develop approach to solutions

B.A. in fine arts or relevant curriculum or equivalent skillset

Minimum 5 years' industry experience or related experience

2 or more published titles considered a strength
