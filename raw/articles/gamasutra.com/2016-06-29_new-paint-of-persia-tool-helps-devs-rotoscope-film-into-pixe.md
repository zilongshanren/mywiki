Inspired by the sort of rotoscoped animations seen in Jordan Mechner's Prince of Persia, one game developer has built a custom tool, [Paint of Persia](https://dunin.itch.io/ptop), that developers can use to rotoscope their own pixel art animations.

It's a neat new tool designed explicitly for game developers, though Paint of Persia's simple premise (lay a customizable window with adjustable transparency, basic pixel art tools and an animation frame manager over your desktop) allows for a broad variety of uses.

Paint of Persia's developer, who lives in France and operates under the name [Dunin](https://dunin.itch.io/), submitted it as part of a [Toolbox Jam](https://itch.io/jam/toolbox-jam) that calls on participants to make tools that people can use to make games. While the tool is still in alpha, Dunin has already used it to make games like [Anthropomorphic Subject](https://dunin.itch.io/suspect).

The jam wraps up today, and the [list of submissions](https://itch.io/jam/toolbox-jam/feed) is well worth browsing if you're at all interested in new tools for making games.
