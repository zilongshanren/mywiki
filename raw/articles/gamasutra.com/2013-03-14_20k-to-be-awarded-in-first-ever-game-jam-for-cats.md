There's a game jam happening in Los Angeles the weekend before GDC which we're pretty sure is the first of its kind. Friskies maker Purina is sponsoring what it is calling the "Games for Cats Hackathon," a weekend-long jam where aspiring developers will compete to create games (or apps) for cats to play with. The grand prize-winning team will take home $15,000, with $3,000 and $2,000 awards for the runners-up. Thinking of entering, but don't know where to start? Gamasutra's got you covered: check out [5 tips for making video games your cat wants to play](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/174230/5_tips_for_making_video_games_your_cat_wants_to_play.php). Event registration is open [here](http://gamesforcatshackathon.eventbrite.com/).
