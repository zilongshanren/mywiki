Location: Sparks, MD Firaxis Games is one of the world's premier game development studios, and home of legendary designer Sid Meier. In 2005, Firaxis Games was acquired by Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc., joining its 2K publishing label. Job Description:

Assists Art Director and team in establishing visual style for production art

Work with the Art Director to maintain consistent artistic style

Create 2D callouts and other production designs for assets

Actively participate in artistic and technical problem solving

Be able to identify inefficiencies in the concept-to-production process and help formulate resolutions

Produce a wide range of highly creative character, creature, hard surfaced and environment concept art

Communicate clearly with designers, artists, and programmers

Produce assets on schedule, meeting or exceeding team quality metrics
