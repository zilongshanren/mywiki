Location: Redwood City, California

Outpost Games is looking for an Animator to help breathe life into our believable characters. This person brings raw animation skills, technical know-how, and can work well with all disciplines.

Responsibilities:

Create multiplayer characters that let players create believable and memorable performances

Collaborate with engineers to perfect gameplay and ensure characters feel just right


Requirements:

Maya experience

In-game animation experience

A successful track record

Being friendly and collaborative

Self-directed


Plusses:

Unreal Engine experience

Loves playing PC multiplayer games

Strong design sense and ability to provide design feedback
