The [2024 Independent Games Festival Awards](https://igf.com/finalists-and-winners?_mc=edit_gdcsf_gdcsf_le_x_15_x_2024) finalists have been revealed—with a story of love, loss, family, and food at the forefront.

Organizers of the [2024 Game Developers Conference](https://gdconf.com/?_mc=edit_gdcsf_gdcsf_le_x_15_x_2024) are happy to unveil this year's finalists and honorable mentions for the 26th annual IGF Awards, part of the longest-running festival, summit, and showcase celebrating independent games and their creators.

Visai Games' Venba leads with nominations in four categories: Excellence in Audio, Excellence in Narrative, Excellence in Visual Art and for the Seumas McNally Grand Prize. Venba is a narrative cooking game in which players experience life as an Indian mom who has immigrated to Canada with her family in the 1980s and weaves together conversations and food to tell her family’s stories of love, loss and more.

For those interested in learning more about the development of Venba, creative director Abhi Swami will be giving a [postmortem](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/independent-games-summit-good-enough-is-better-than-good-a-postmortem-of-venba/901533?_mc=edit_gdcsf_gdcsf_le_x_15_x_2024) on Venba at GDC 2024, and composer/sound designer Neha Patel will be speaking about the game's award-nominated [sound design](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/independent-games-summit-venba-an-intimate-journey-through-sound/901387?_mc=edit_gdcsf_gdcsf_le_x_15_x_2024).

Venba is followed by five other finalist titles who share the distinction of having three nominations apiece: 1000xRESIST by Fellow Traveller, A Highland Song by Inkle, Anthology of the Killer by Thecatamites, COCOON by Annapurna Interactive (which has a [talk featured](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/pondering-orbs-the-rendering-and-art-tools-of-cocoon/902878) at GDC 2024), and Mediterranea Inferno by Santa Ragione. All of these games are nominated for the Seumas McNally Grand Prize alongside Venba.

The Independent Games Festival is part of GDC 2024, which will be held in San Francisco March 18-22.

This year's nominees were selected from a list of over [500 entries](https://gdconf.com/news/igf-accepts-over-500-entries-gdc-2024?_mc=edit_gdcsf_gdcsf_le_x_15_x_2024), thanks to the hard work of our expert juries in each individual category (more on them here and here)—who chose the finalists after playing, discussing, and meticulously evaluating each of the games in consideration. Be sure to submit your vote for the [IGF Audience Award here](https://informa-tech.formstack.com/forms/26th_annual_independent_games_festival_audience_award?_mc=edit_gdcsf_gdcsf_le_x_15_x_2024). Voting will close on January 31 at 11:59pm PT.
