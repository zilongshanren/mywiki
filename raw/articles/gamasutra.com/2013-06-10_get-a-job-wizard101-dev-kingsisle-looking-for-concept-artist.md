Location: Austin, TX KingsIsle Entertainment is currently looking for Concept Artists at mid and senior levels of experience to work on an exciting new family-focused AAA MMO video game. We are the creators of the popular and critically acclaimed Wizard101 and Pirate101, which together have garnered over 35 Million registered players to date, and are two of the fastest growing websites in the U.S. This is an opportunity to be a part of a rapidly growing and highly successful entertainment company. Responsibilities

Candidate will work with art and design to craft and maintain the project's art style and manage concept art creation

Candidate will be expected to translate the vision of the designers to concept art and ensure that all work conforms to the technical and stylistic specifications set forth

Effectively relate vision to developers and artists

Approach systematically all areas of concept art creation

Produce a wide variety of drawings, including character, item and environment, that define the look and function of non-photorealistic in-game assets

Work closely with project leads to define the style and look of the game

Create storyboards, sketches, in-game 2D screens, layouts and color comps
