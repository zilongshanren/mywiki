Art dealership Cook & Becker has launched [a new online gallery](http://www.candb.com/en/) and physical exhibition space that features limited edition art works from various video games. The Utrecht, Netherlands-based company is looking to curate and sell these exclusive collections, and has the support of several video game artists. Featured artists include the likes of Jesse van Dijk, concept artist at Bungie Studios, Maciej Kuciara, concept artist at Naughty Dog, Julien Renoult, artist on Team Fortress 2, and Killzone developer Guerrilla Games. Maarten Brands, co-founder of Cook & Becker, explained, "It is undeniable that some of the best and most influential artists are working within the entertainment and design industries." "Also this new group of autonomous and post-autonomous artistic talent often works completely digital. We wanted to do something with this. These types of art have not yet been represented well enough... something we aim to rectify with truly some of the best international artists working in these spaces." The exhibition also aims to highlight artists in other entertainment sectors, including film and comic books.
