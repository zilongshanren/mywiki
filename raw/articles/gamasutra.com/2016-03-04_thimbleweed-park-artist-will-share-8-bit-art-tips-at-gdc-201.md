![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

[GDC 2016](http://gdconf.com/?_mc=BP_LE_CON) is happening this month, and organizers would like to quickly let you know about a cool talk from longtime game artist Mark Ferrari about neat ways you can create 8-bit and "8-bitish" graphics for your game.

Ferrari has created art for everything from Lucasfilm Games' Zak McKracken and the Alien Mindbenders to veteran adventure game designer Ron Gilbert's Thimbleweed Park, and he'll share what he's learned from all that work in "[8-Bit & '8-Bitish' Graphics -- Outside the Box](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/8-bit-8-bitish-graphics-outside-the-box)."

Check out this talk to see Ferrari demonstrate some of his techniques for drawing 8-bit game graphics, including his celebrated methods for use of color cycling and pallet shifting to create complex and realistic background animation effects without frame-animation.

He will also discuss his ongoing work on Ron Gilbert's retro adventure game in deeper detail and demonstrate techniques for using Photoshop to create what he calls '8-bitish' graphics for retro games today.

Sound good? There are lots of other great talks taking place on the [Visual Arts](http://schedule.gdconf.com/track/visual-arts?_mc=BP_LE_CON) track at GDC 2016, including Campo Santo's Jane Ng talking about "[Making the World of ](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/making-the-world-of-firewatch?_mc=BP_LE_CON)[Firewatch](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/making-the-world-of-firewatch?_mc=BP_LE_CON)," "[Animating Quadruped Characters in ](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/animating-quadruped-characters-in-the-flame-in-the-flood?_mc=BP_LE_CON)[The Flame in The Flood](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/animating-quadruped-characters-in-the-flame-in-the-flood?_mc=BP_LE_CON)" from The Molasses Flood's Gwen Frey and Naughty Dog's Andrew Maximov's session on the "[Technical Art Culture of ](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/technical-art-culture-of-uncharted-4?_mc=BP_LE_CON)[Uncharted 4](http://schedule.gdconf.com/session/technical-art-culture-of-uncharted-4?_mc=BP_LE_CON)."
