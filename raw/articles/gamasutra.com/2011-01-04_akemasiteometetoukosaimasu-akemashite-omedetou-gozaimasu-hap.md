# あけましておめでとうございます！！(akemashite omedetou gozaimasu/Happy New Years)

Ringin' in the New Years with artwork.

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](https://eu-images.contentstack.com/v3/assets/blt740a130ae3c5d529/bltba62518415cda0e2/652fe6ddbc479f8697ef691f/default-cubic.png?width=1280&auto=webp&quality=80&format=jpg&disable=upscale)

My son is on winter break and so now it's 3 of us home all day. It really is a challenge to give them the attention they need and get work done. It's been at a snails pace. Soon I will start teaching again at the Eikiawa (English Conversation School), and I REALLY don't want to go back. Not when I feel this good. So I announced Burn and it's been going at a steady pace (in between contract gigs). So today I want to show what I've been up to. Below is a video of a early run through of the level for iHardware 3G+. It's really fun to work on hardware with these specs. Forces you to give up some things. Whenever I hear people complain about how weak it is, I just tell them to look at God of War for the PSP.
