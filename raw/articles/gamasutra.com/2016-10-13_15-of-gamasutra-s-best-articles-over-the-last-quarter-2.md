# Developer blogs and articles

[Pokemon Go](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/ChrisFurniss/20160722/277631/Pokemon_GO_and_the_good_things_that_can_come_from_a_bad_UI.php)[ and the good things that can come from a bad UI](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/ChrisFurniss/20160722/277631/Pokemon_GO_and_the_good_things_that_can_come_from_a_bad_UI.php) – Chris Furniss ([@chrisfurniss](https://twitter.com/chrisfurniss)), senior UX/UI designer, PopCap Seattle.

PopCap Seattle UI/UX expert Chris Furniss argues how Pokemon Go’s confusing UI is applying meaningful friction and is feeding into the dense social experience of the game.

[Postmortem: ](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/AdriaanJansen/20160905/266289/Post_Mortem_Renowned_Explorers.php)[Renowned Explorers](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/AdriaanJansen/20160905/266289/Post_Mortem_Renowned_Explorers.php) – Adriaan Jansen ([@abbeyadriaan](https://twitter.com/abbeyadriaan)), co-founder and designer at Abbey Games.

The development of Renowned Explorers: International Society was a crazy ride. You can read about every twist and turn right here.

[AR is an MMO](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/RaphKoster/20160711/276834/AR_is_an_MMO.php) – Raph Koster ([@raphkoster](https://twitter.com/raphkoster)), veteran game designer (Ultima Online, Star Wars Galaxy, Crowfall, more) specializing in MMOs.

In the wake of Pokemon Go, it's a wise idea to look at the history of MMOs and virtual worlds for lessons learned.

[Why successful games in China rarely thrive in the West](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/MantinLu/20160916/281528/Why_Successful_Games_in_China_Rarely_Obtain_the_Equivalent_Success_in_the_West.php) – Mantin Lu ([@MantinLu](https://twitter.com/mantinlu)), director of product development at Seasun Inc.

Mantin Lu looks at some of the differences in taste, culture, monetization mindset, and development mentality between East and West.

[Design tips: In-game proportion and scale](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/YuriySivers/20160726/277626/Design_Tips_InGame_Proportions_and_Scale.php) – Yuriy Sivers ([@yuriysivers](https://twitter.com/yuriysivers)), senior visual designer at Kongregate.

Many developers often face issues designing proportions of individual characters, and the relationship between these characters and the surrounding environment. Here's an approach you can take

[Designing a combat HUD: Key user experience insights](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/OliverJanoschek/20160714/277048/Key_user_experience_insights_gained_during_the_creation_of_Dreadnoughts_combat_HUD.php) – Oliver Janoschek ([@theonlyrapante](http://www.twitter.com/theonlyrapante)), senior UI artist for Dreadnought at Yager.

Oliver Janoschek, senior UI artist at Yager explains how to use UX patterns like Fitts' Law to successfully improve upon your in-game UI.

[Designing lag-compensating weapons in ](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/NeemaTeymory/20160906/280377/Why_Making_Multiplayer_Games_is_Hard_Lag_Compensating_Weapons_in_MechWarrior_Online.php)[Mechwarrior Online](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/NeemaTeymory/20160906/280377/Why_Making_Multiplayer_Games_is_Hard_Lag_Compensating_Weapons_in_MechWarrior_Online.php) – Neema Teymory, senior network engineer at Piranha Games.

Making multiplayer games is hard and there are many problems developers need to solve. In this article, I will discuss what MechWarrior Online's weapons system does to deal with lag.

[Patch the process](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/RamiIsmail/20160807/278721/Patch_the_Process.php) – Rami Ismail ([@tha_rami](https://twitter.com/tha_rami)), one-half of Nuclear Throne developer Vlambeer.

Vlambeer's Rami Ismail wants to talk about day one patches -- but no, this isn't (really) about No Man's Sky.

[Wonder Boy: The Dragon's Trap](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/OmarCornut/20160912/281112/Wonder_Boy_The_Dragons_Trap_Reverse_engineering_an_1989_original_the_quest_for_accuracy.php)[: Reverse-engineering a 1989 original](http://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/OmarCornut/20160912/281112/Wonder_Boy_The_Dragons_Trap_Reverse_engineering_an_1989_original_the_quest_for_accuracy.php) – Omar Cornut ([@ocornut](https://twitter.com/ocornut)), technical director and programmer at Lizardcube.

Remaking the 1989 action-adventure game Wonder Boy III: The Dragon's Trap wasn't just about nostalgia, but an exercise in developing a game that's faithful and respectful to the original's gameplay.
