Newsbrief: NaturalMotion Games has reportedly hired Dan Borthwick, formerly common technology lead at Electronic Arts' social game subsidiary Playfish, to lead its mobile-focused studio in London. Borthwick is the latest in a string of social game veterans who have recently departed from EA, including Playfish's [former London headquarters GM and VP John Earner](http://gamasutra.com/view/news/39884/Report_EA_loses_another_social_gaming_head.php), Playfish CTO Sami Lababidi, [EA Interactive head Barry Cottle](http://gamasutra.com/view/news/39626/Zynga_poaches_a_key_EA_exec_to_head_bizdev.php), and EA Interactive VP Sebastien de Halleux. One of the founding developers for Playfish, Borthwick will oversee the London studio's efforts producing free-to-play iPhone and Android titles, [according to a report](http://www.develop-online.net/news/39851/NaturalMotion-Games-appoints-new-London-studio-boss) from UK trade site Develop. Its previous releases include My Horse, Jenga, and the Backbreaker series.
