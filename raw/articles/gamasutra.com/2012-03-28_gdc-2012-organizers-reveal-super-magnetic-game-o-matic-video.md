March 28, 2012

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

At this month's [Game Developers Conference 2012](http://gdconf.com/) in San Francisco, video game artist group iam8bit teamed up with the show's organizers to host the irreverent and exciting "[Super Magnetic Game-O-Matic](http://gdconf.com/iam8bit/)," a creative event featuring top artists from throughout the industry. Throughout the conference, show attendees re-arranged giant word magnets to create some far-fetched game ideas, while professional artists rendered their own interpretations of the concepts they found interesting -- or just plain strange. Now that the event has come to a close, show organizers have showcased the results via [an exclusively filmed YouTube highlights reel](http://youtu.be/o6lKhsRuOl0), including timelapse shots of the sketchers in action, as well as [a hi-res Flickr gallery](http://www.flickr.com/photos/officialgdc/sets/72157629157644740/) of the art created. The featured artists for the event specialized in all kinds of artistic disciplines, from UI design to concept art, and represented a wide range of companies from all over the game industry. The notable list of artists at the event included Blizzard Entertainment's Ben Thompson, Weta Workshop's Greg Broadmore, Double Fine artists Levi Ryken and Derek Brand, and much more. The subjects of their "Game-O-Matic" contributions might have been a bit more silly than their typical professional work, but their talent shone through all the same. From this exciting mix of artists came fantastic and quite often hilarious renderings of game pitches like "Extreme Nanny Adventure Karting," "Tyler Perry's 3D Dino Ninja Revolution," and "Meat Gravity Tale - 4D Space Edition," to name a few.
