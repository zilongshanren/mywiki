Next week, Gamasutra will launch a special week of content focused on mobile games -- and we want to hear about your experiences. During this special mobile games week, we'll cover a number of the key challenges surrounding development and sales of mobile games, including finding audiences on noisy app stores, optimizing design for touch interfaces, and best practices for app monetization. Are you a developer of mobile games who's found out a useful development technique, or have tips for getting your game discovered? Have you successfully (or unsuccessfully) converted a mobile game to another platform, or vice versa? We want to know about it, so we can share it with our readers. You can submit your stories [directly to Gamasutra's blog section](http://www.gamasutra.com/static2/blogsubmissions.html). Not sure what to write? Need to brainstorm? Just [email blog director Christian Nutt](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#22414a504b51564b434c0c4c5756566257404f0c414d4f) and he'll help you with any questions. Gentle reminder: Posts which exclusively promote products and services [are against Gamasutra's guidelines](http://www.gamasutra.com/static2/blogsubmissions.html).
