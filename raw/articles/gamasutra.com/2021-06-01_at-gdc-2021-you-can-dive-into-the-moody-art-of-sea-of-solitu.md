![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

Sea of Solitude won critical acclaim for its incredibly resonant storytelling backed by a particularly gorgeous art style.

And at the all-digital [2021 Game Developers Conference](https://gdconf.com/?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-28) (running July 19th – 23rd), you can dive into the creation of that art style in an upcoming talk from creative director Cornelia Geppert.

In [“Paint It Black: The Art of ](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/paint-it-black-the-art-of-sea-of-solitude/880582?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-28)[Sea of Solitude,](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/paint-it-black-the-art-of-sea-of-solitude/880582?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-28)[”](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/paint-it-black-the-art-of-sea-of-solitude/880582?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-28) Geppert will lay open how - despite a small team size - Jo-Mei translated a 2D art style into a 3D environment and, how the team built special tools to help the team create this unique look in the Unity engine.

Whether you’re a game artist or just work with them, you’ll want to check out Geppert’s talk at GDC 2021. [Be sure to ](https://gdconf.com/passes-prices?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-28)[register today!](https://gdconf.com/passes-prices?_mc=blog_x_gama_un_x_gdcsf_x_x-28)
