Location: Redmond, WA ArenaNet is looking for a proactive, detail oriented and enthusiastic Art Production Manager who is comfortable taking the initiative in supporting our world class art team. Responsibilities The Art Production Manager reports into Production and is primarily responsible for art asset creation. The APM will work with Leads and project management to manage and drive asset scheduling, milestone deliverables, identifying dependencies and possible workflow blockages, facilitating communication between internal departments and managing high risk areas of production. This role also works with outsource partners on asset creation, so budgeting and outsource management experience is desired. Interested?
