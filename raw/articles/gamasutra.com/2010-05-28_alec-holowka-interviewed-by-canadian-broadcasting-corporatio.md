Holowka's Aquaria, for which he partnered with [Derek Yu](http://www.derekyu.com/), was recently part of the massively successful [Humble Indie Bundle](http://www.wolfire.com/humble), which raised well over a million dollars of funding and donations for Child's Play and the Electronic Frontier Foundation.

[Aquaria's original soundtrack](http://infiniteammo.bandcamp.com/) can be listened to in its entirety on Bandcamp. Compositions are by Holowka, while mastering was done by Super Meat Boy composer Danny B. The current project on the horizon for Infinite Ammo is a platformer about a marionette, called Marian.
