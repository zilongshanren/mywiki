Every year a ton of smart people speak at the various Game Developers Conference events around the world, and most of those presentations are recorded and uploaded to the [GDC Vault](http://www.gdcvault.com/) for safekeeping.

Here at Gamasutra we've been working since 2013 to spotlight notable talks that are made freely available to watch on the Vault, and we've built up a pretty significant archive of [Vault highlights](http://www.gamasutra.com/news/vault/).

As part of our ongoing effort to curate this stuff, we've taken the liberty of collecting below some of the most memorable postmortems ever delivered at GDC.

It's a feast of free viewing, as the developers behind great games -- big and small, old and new -- deliver candid and informative looks back at their work.

[Shenmue ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/219314/Video_Shenmue_postmortem_from_Yu_Suzuki_and_Mark_Cerny.php)[postmortem from Yu Suzuki and Mark Cerny](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/219314/Video_Shenmue_postmortem_from_Yu_Suzuki_and_Mark_Cerny.php)

In this video Yu Suzuki (pictured), the influential Japanese game designer famously known for his work at Sega, delivers a Classic Game Postmortem talk about his Dreamcast cult classic Shenmue at GDC 2014.

This GDC 2014 postmortem featuring six of Lucasfilm Games' earliest employees asks the questions: "What mix of creative and business forces shaped this studio?" and "Could it be duplicated today?"

[There and Dad Again, an ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/234409/Video_There_and_Dad_Again_an_Octodad_Dadliest_Catch_postmortem.php)[Octodad: Dadliest Catch ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/234409/Video_There_and_Dad_Again_an_Octodad_Dadliest_Catch_postmortem.php)[postmortem](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/234409/Video_There_and_Dad_Again_an_Octodad_Dadliest_Catch_postmortem.php)

At GDC Next 2014, Young Horses cofounders Phil Tibitoski and John Murphy discussed the methods they used to create a successful [joke] game, work better as a team, and learn to love again.

[The successes and failures of ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/227337/Video_Postmortem_The_successes_and_failures_of_SteamWorld_Dig.php)[Steamworld Dig](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/227337/Video_Postmortem_The_successes_and_failures_of_SteamWorld_Dig.php)

Brjann Sigurgeirsson reveals how he and the team at Image & Form stumbled through the process of making SteamWorld Dig a multi-platform hit in this GDC Europe 2014 talk.

[Broken Sword ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/224981/Video_A_Broken_Sword_postmortem_from_creator_Charles_Cecil.php)[postmortem from creator Charles Cecil](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/224981/Video_A_Broken_Sword_postmortem_from_creator_Charles_Cecil.php)

Game industry veteran Charles Cecil speaks about his work writing and directing Broken Sword: Shadow of the Templars, the award-winning 1996 adventure that raised the bar for storytelling in games.

[Eugene Jarvis delivers a postmortem of his classic game ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/233111/Video_Eugene_Jarvis_delivers_a_postmortem_of_Robotron_2084.php)[Robotron: 2084](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/233111/Video_Eugene_Jarvis_delivers_a_postmortem_of_Robotron_2084.php)

Industry veteran and Raw Thrills cofounder Eugene Jarvis delves into the design of his seminal arcade game during this upbeat classic game postmortem of Robotron: 2084 at GDC 2014.

[Zach Barth's postmortem of his work making ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/238883/Video_Zach_Barths_SpaceChem_postmortem.php)[SpaceChem](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/238883/Video_Zach_Barths_SpaceChem_postmortem.php)

Speaking at GDC 2013, developer Zach Barth digs into the differences between SpaceChem and other puzzle games, sharing sales data and advice on how to make your own design-based puzzle games.

[Deconstructing the success of ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/220879/Video_postmortem_Deconstructing_the_success_of_Puzzle__Dragons.php)[Puzzle & Dragons](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/220879/Video_postmortem_Deconstructing_the_success_of_Puzzle__Dragons.php)

GungHo Online CEO Kazuki Morishita took the stage at GDC 2014 to give a polite, measured look back at how the game was designed to entice players while generating sustainable profit.

[Making a truly sci-fi game with ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/226095/Video_Postmortem_Making_a_truly_scifi_game_in_MirrorMoon_EP.php)[MirrorMoon EP](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/226095/Video_Postmortem_Making_a_truly_scifi_game_in_MirrorMoon_EP.php)

What does it really mean to make a "sci-fi" game? Santa Ragione's Pietro Righi Riva explores the question while giving a postmortem talk about his game MirrorMoon EP during GDC Europe 2014.

[Dave Lebling's postmortem of his classic game ](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/217428/Video_Zork_Classic_Game_Postmortem_from_GDC_2014.php)[Zork](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/217428/Video_Zork_Classic_Game_Postmortem_from_GDC_2014.php)

Infocom co-founder Dave Lebling explains how he created Zork and blazed a trail for interactive fiction game development in a postmortem talk at GDC 2014.
