Location: San Francisco, CA

The game studio that made the critically-acclaimed game The Witness is looking for experienced contract artists to help build cool stuff in 3D.

The skills we are looking for are environment art, character art, and character animation.

We need people who are very comfortable making 3D games from white box to polished product, in Maya/Max and in-engine. If you apply, you should be someone who can work in interesting, creative styles, and have the imagination and self motivation to take an abstract mockup and turn it into something stunning and meaningful, while also paying close attention to gameplay limitations.

Good communication skills and the ability to take and incorporate critique is a must, a generally pleasant and chill nature is a plus. If you excel in more than one of the outlined skills, we especially want to hear from you!

We would prefer someone who is based around San Francisco and can work in the office, but are willing to work with someone remotely if they are very good.
