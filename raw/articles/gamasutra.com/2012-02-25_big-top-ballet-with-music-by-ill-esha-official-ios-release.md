# Big Top Ballet with Music by ill-Esha Official iOS Release!

Big Top Ballet will be available for your iPhone, iPad and iPod touch on the App Store this Tuesday February 28, 2012. If you want your chance at winning a free copy of the iOS game, be sure to Like ill-esha on Facebook!

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

![Big Top Ballet Official Release to iOS! Big Top Ballet Official Release to iOS!](http://vectorbloom.com/images/GamePoster_iOSReleaseDate_450w.png?width=1280&auto=webp&quality=80&disable=upscale)


Big Top Ballet (single player mode) will be available [on the App Store](http://itunes.apple.com/app/big-top-ballet/id446671519?mt=8) for your iPhone, iPad and iPod touch in just 3 days, Tuesday, February 28, 2012. Big Top Ballet is a trippy game with awesome visuals and crazy electronica beats by ill-esha featuring her incredible custom composition 'Kaleidoscope'.

If you want a chance at winning a free copy of [Big Top Ballet](http://bigtopballet.com), be sure to Like ill-esha on Facebook and watch for the promo code contest that will be hosted from [ill-esha's Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/ill.esha.music) on Monday, February 27, 2012. Get ready to spin round!
