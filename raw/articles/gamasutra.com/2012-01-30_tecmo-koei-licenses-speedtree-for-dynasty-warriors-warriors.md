Newsbrief: Today, Japanese publisher Tecmo Koei announced that it has licensed IDV's foliage middleware SpeedTree for use in two upcoming action titles. Tecmo Koei said it plans to use the tech for the multiplatform Dynasty Warriors 7, the latest in the company's long line of historical fiction brawlers, and Warriors Orochi 3, a similar title with a more fantasy-inspired premise. Late last year, IDV [released a new version of SpeedTree](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/38427/New_Optimized_SpeedTree_Version_Adds_Support_For_Vita_Mac_DirectX_11.php), adding support for Mac, DirectX 11, and PlayStation Vita.
