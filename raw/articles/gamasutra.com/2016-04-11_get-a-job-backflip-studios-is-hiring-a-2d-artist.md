Location: Boulder, CO

Backflip Studios is looking for a highly talented 2D illustrator with 3D modeling experience.

We're a mobile game app developer and publisher located in Boulder CO. Majority owned by Hasbro, this partnership leverages our expertise and Hasbro's deep library of intellectual property.

A 2D Artist creates a variety of game assets, from concepts to background, icons, characters, and more. Collaborating with developers and other artists, you will balance creativity with a practical flexibility in creating compelling content that reflects the game style.

The right candidate will be a detail oriented, creative self starter with a solution oriented outlook. You should have the ability to apply feedback and demonstrate artistic adaptability.

This role requires the following skills and experience:

3 years experience as a professional illustrator

3D modeling experience (Maya or 3DS Max)

Master proficiency in Adobe CC

Experience in Unity or Unreal

We prefer a game developer background with the familiarity of processes and practices

If you have mobile experience, that's the icing on the cake!


This position is based at our office in Boulder, CO. Relocation assistance is available.

Applicants must be able to legally work in the USA

Interested? [Apply now](http://jobs.gamasutra.com/job/2d-artist-boulder-colorado-30338).
