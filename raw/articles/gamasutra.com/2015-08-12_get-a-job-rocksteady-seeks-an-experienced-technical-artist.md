Location: London, England

Rocksteady Studios is looking to add to its family of permanent staff! We are looking for a talented Senior Technical Artist who is ready for the challenge of producing AAA titles on the latest platforms.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Facilitate the development of the art vision by bringing technical solutions on-board

Create strong technical guidelines for the art creation

Contribute to the evolution of the art pipeline

Assist and review the asset creation pipeline

Mentor the artists to follow the technical art bible

Share knowledge and develop other members of the team


SKILL REQUIREMENTS

Strong portfolio of examples showing creative and efficient use of technical art

Experience with the Unreal engine workflow

Proactive in researching new technologies and available new tools

Strong knowledge of shader and rendering technique

3D math knowledge (linear algebra and vector math)

Ability to design, author and maintain tools used in the production pipeline

Knowledge of 3DS Max, Photoshop and all common creation software

Problem solving mentality

Knowledge of scripting languages

Good knowledge of optimisation and performance processes

Good art background and art skills

Good communication and written skills

Strong passion for gaming is a must

Be a positive, active, and contributing team player

Ability to work productively and effectively with the Art team, the Art Producer and Art Director and to communicate effectively with the other disciplines on the team in a technical and creative level


BENEFITS

Private healthcare scheme

Contributory pension scheme

Discretionary performance bonus scheme

Discretionary royalty package scheme

Long-service holiday reward

Brand new custom designed office

Regular team meals/outings

Table football

Free fruit and soft drinks


Please send us your cover letter, CV and a link to your portfolio / demo reel!
