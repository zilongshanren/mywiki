Here is a list of the cooking supplies that you will surely need in your kitchen. Take this as a guide so that you will not miss a thing and soon enough, you will have the cooking supplies for your culinary adventure.


Pots and Pans


You will surely need this to cook your dishes or boil water. It would be best if you have different sizes of each so that you can use the right one for a certain amount of dish. It is suggested that you go for the best brand, for pots and pans are the ones being exposed constantly to heat in cooking.


Chef’s Knife


This tool is important for chopping your meat, fruits and vegetables for your salad. A kitchen would not exist without a knife. Knives are one of the basic tools a kitchen should have.


Cutting Board


This tool is where the raw products are to be chopped. This is important so that your dining table would be saved from being chopped upon, since tables are the natural chopping board for many people.


Wooden Ladles


Such tool is needed to mix the raw ingredients and for tasting as well. Wooden ladles are the safest kind, for it does not react whenever it is exposed to heat.


Mixing Bowls


Mixing bowls are important, for this is where raw ingredients are being mixed. This is especially used for those ingredients that are in liquid form.


Can Opener


As the name itself says, this tool is used to open cans. There are times wherein you will use raw ingredients that are canned; this tool will help you open it up effortlessly.
