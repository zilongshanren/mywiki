The British Academy of Film and Television Awards named Bethesda's Dishonored the best game of 2012 in a ceremony Tuesday night. The first-person action game managed to beat out awards ceremony favorite Journey, though that game walked away with five awards of its own, including Game Design, Online Multiplayer, Original Music, Audio Achievement and Artistic Achievement. Other games honored include Far Cry 3, The Unfinished Swan, The Walking Dead and The Room, among others. Valve's Gabe Newell was honored with a fellowship award. The full list of nominees and winners is available at the [BAFTA website](http://awards.bafta.org/award/2013/games).
