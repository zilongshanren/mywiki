The Game Audio Network Guild has announced the nominees for the 14th annual G.A.N.G. Awards, which will take place on March 17.

The awards aim to recognize the achievements of the games industry's best and brightest soundsmiths, with the winners being chosen by members of the G.A.N.G. advisory board, board of directors, and sub committees.

Leading this year's nominations are Everybody's Gone to the Rapture, Star Wars: Battlefront, and Ori and the Blind Forest, which are up for a variety of awards including Music of the Year, Best Mix, and Best Original Soundtrack Album.

We've put together a select list of nominees, but you can check out [the full list of finalists by clicking here](http://www.audiogang.org/finalists-for-the-14th-annual-game-audio-network-guild-awards-announced/).
