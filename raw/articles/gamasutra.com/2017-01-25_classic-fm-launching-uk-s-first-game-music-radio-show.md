Newsbrief: UK radio station Classic FM [plans to launch](http://www.classicfm.com/music-news/25th-birthday/) the country's first radio series dedicated to symphonic video game music.

The six-week series will kick off on April 22, and will run every Saturday from 9pm to 10pm GMT.

The Chinese Room co-founder and composer Jessica Curry, who masterminded the scores for Dear Esther and Everybody's Gone to the Rapture, [will host the new show](https://twitter.com/jessicacurry2/status/824187696990064641).
