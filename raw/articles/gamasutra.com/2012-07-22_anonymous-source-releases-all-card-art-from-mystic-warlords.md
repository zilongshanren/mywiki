# Anonymous source releases all card art from Mystic Warlords Of Ka’a to the public.

We were contacted by one of the ex-players of the now defunct Mystic Warlords Of Ka'a game on Facebook and he was able to somehow salvage all that great card art from the game and put it into a Rapidshare account for anyone to download.

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

This is the latest blog entry from my game company website, [LUTOPiA.Co](http://www.LUTOPiA.Co).

We were contacted by one of the ex-players of the now [defunct Mystic Warlords Of Ka'a game on Facebook](http://www.lutopia.co/clash-of-the-titans-ea-vs-zynga-2/) and he was able to somehow salvage all that great card art from the game and put it into a Rapidshare account for anyone to download. He said that he just wanted to see if the hardcore fans of this game, which first appeared being played by the characters in the sitcom [The Big Bang Theory](http://youtu.be/eCufNLhDDlw), could somehow curate it or even resurrect it in some form. We don't know who this person is or what his relationship is with [Dire Wolf Digital](http://www.direwolfdigital.com/) and we didn't ask, we're just glad that someone cared enough about the game to keep this great game from being vanished to oblivion and never to be seen again.
