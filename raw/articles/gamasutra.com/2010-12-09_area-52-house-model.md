This is the Area 52 house I am modeling. Still has a ways to go, but this is the progress.I can only work on this in my spare time when I'm not working on the Cortex engine and BCK (first DCA title) so progress is slower than if I devoted my time entirely to this house. Anyhow, here is a rendering of the house with some roof texture on the Belfry and other parts of the roof finally.

And here is one with the porch, stairs, and the foundation / basement added to the house. At this point, the house is structurally done.
