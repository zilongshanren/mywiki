## The Game Developer Podcast

The official podcast for GameDeveloper.com brings you illuminating conversation and commentary from brilliant people in the video game industry.

Hosted by senior editor Bryant Francis, the Game Developer podcast is a bi-weekly interview show chronicling the triumphs, catastrophes, and everything in-between of game development, sharing lessons and strategies fellow developers can use to hone their craft. The Game Developer Podcast is edited by Pierre Landriau, and features music by [Mike Meehan](https://mikemeehanmusic.com/).

[Recent episodes](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/podcasts) include conversations with Liz England to follow up on her famous 2014 blog post detailing "The Door Problem of Game Design", a dive behind the scenes of SAG-AFTRA's recent strike win with interactive media negotiating committee chair Sarah Elmaleh, and more, with new episodes published every other Friday.

The [full archive of 50+ Game Developer Podcast episodes](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/game-developer-podcast/id1476405424) is packed with timeless and evergreen game development insights and takeaways for your listening pleasure as well. Previous podcast hosts Alissa McAloon, Danielle Riendeau, and Kris Graft explore everything from game preservation with legendary adventure game designer Charles Cecil and live game production tips from then-The Sims 4 VP of franchise creative Lyndsay Pearson to multiple advice-rich interviews with game marketing expert Chris Zukowski and many more episodes covering a variety of games, studios, and topics from over the years.
