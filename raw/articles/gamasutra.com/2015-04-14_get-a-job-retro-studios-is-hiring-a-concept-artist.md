Location: Austin, Texas

Founded in 1998, Retro Studios is a wholly owned subsidiary of Nintendo Company, Ltd. Retro is a state-of-the-art game development studio, working in conjunction with Nintendo to bring award-winning games to Nintendo's cutting-edge next-generation platforms.

Retro Studios is located in beautiful Austin, Texas. With rolling hills, scenic waterways, abundant wildlife, vibrant music and film scenes, and a laid-back cosmopolitan culture, Austin is a dynamic city with an excellent quality of life.

Austin's sunny weather also supports a great range of outdoor activities, providing plenty of venues for top-notch game developers to recharge and unwind.

Purpose/Summary

Individual contributor that collaborates with Creative Director, Artists, and Designers to establish the visual aesthetic for game software through the production of Concept Art, Storyboards, and Promotional Materials

Essential Duties

Collaborate with the Creative Director, Artists, and Designers to realize and standardize assigned game software art factors such as look, feel, composition, color, and mood

Able to understand goals and direction for a given project and adapt a broad range of styles from realistic to artistic in order to produce character concept art with appropriate sense of color, drama, personality, and design

Provide conceptual Character, Environmental, and Object art in the form of sketches, paintings, and digital imagery

Able to design and create concept art to support gameplay clarity and immersion

Able to take ownership of concept work for various characters and environments while enjoying collaboration with other disciplines such as Design, Art, and Engineering in order to achieve high-quality, innovative concept art

Perform research as necessary to participate in initial conceptual design

Deliver artistic presentations as required

Work with Creative Director to provide aesthetic direction and guidance to production artists

Transform incomplete ideas or verbal guidance into coherent visual concepts

Create storyboards and background illustrations

Design and create front-end/UI graphics and promotional artwork

Evaluate, recommend, and implement new tools

Design and create artwork for in-game Characters and Environments

Collaborate with the Creative Director, Artists, and Designers to create digital 3D assets consistent with the overall art direction


* This job description outlines primary duties and requirements and is not intended to identify all tasks that may be performed; individuals occupying the position may be required to perform other duties. The company may modify job duties from time to time, either in practice or in writing.

Requirements/Knowledge/Experience

Strong 2D drawing skills

Mastery of fundamental artistic principles

Online portfolio with high-quality examples of character and environment concept art

Ability to execute a wide range of artistic styles

Capable of quickly becoming proficient with Retro’s 2D art production tools

Fluid and eager problem solver

3D art and modeling skills a plus

Character-intensive concept background a plus

Have three or more years' industry experience, including at least one complete product cycle

Undergraduate degree in art-related subject, completion of an intensive art training program, or equivalent


Work Environment

Fast-paced environment with fluctuating production requirements throughout the cycle

May be necessary to change specific duties, projects, schedule or direction on short notice

Overtime, weekend, and holiday work may be required
