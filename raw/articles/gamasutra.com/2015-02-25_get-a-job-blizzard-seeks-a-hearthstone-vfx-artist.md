Location: Irvine, CA

The Hearthstone team is looking for a visual effects (VFX) production artist. The focus for this position will be working closely with the art director and designers to bring the team’s artistic vision to life.

Responsibilities include creating evocative visual effects in the fun / whimsical style that is synonymous with Hearthstone, developing these same effects from concept to implementation, and working to maintain a consistent aesthetic across all of the effects in the game.

The ideal candidate has a firm grasp of color, animation, storyboard, visual design, and able to convey all of these in their visual effects.

REQUIREMENTS

Experience with creating consistently charming, eye-catching, real-time effects (FX) using Photoshop, Maya, and in-game FX systems

Skilled in developing effects that work across a wide range of system configurations (looks great on the newest graphics chips but still holds up on a variety of mobile devices)

Superior eye for lighting, value, color, details, and the ability to use them in creating beautiful visual effects that support gameplay

Experience creating real-time, in-game VFX for a variety of platforms from desktop to mobile

Self-motivation, excellent communications skills, and able to work within a team

Positive attitude and able to receive and provide objective feedback

A passion for games


PLUSES

Familiar with the Unity game engine

Passion for creating charming and lighthearted imagery synonymous with Hearthstone

Previous game production experience


SUBMISSION / PORTFOLIO REQUIREMENTS

Cover Letter is required

Portfolio, and / or reel demonstrating relevant artistic skills required

Only online artwork submissions will be accepted (e.g. website, blog or online album).

Must be willing to complete an art test if requested to do so
