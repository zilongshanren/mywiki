Location: Framingham, MA

Disruptor Beam is building a new generation of games for every screen, based on popular universes like Game of Thrones and Star Trek. This is an opportunity to join a rapidly growing company, alongside a veteran team, working with some of the most iconic brands in entertainment.

We are seeking an experienced Cinematic Artist to create compelling, high-impact trailers and other animated videos that will help grow the audience for our successful mobile and web games.

As a member of the Marketing team, the Cinematic Artist will be responsible for all aspects of cinematic production, including concept development, storyboarding, casting and VO performance, animation, lighting, cinematography, sound design, editing, and post-production, ensuring that their work reaches a consistently high level of quality.

The Cinematic Artist will also assist in the creation of studio-based productions, including video blogs, live streams, and event recordings; and collaborate with Product Development from time to time on in-game assets such as cinematic intros and cut-scenes, as well as pre-visualizations to aid in prototyping.

Applicants must submit a reel of representative work in cinematics to be considered.

What You'll Do:

Drive the development of visual storytelling at Disruptor Beam

Setup and capture full-motion animation in Unity

Conceptualize, shoot, edit, and post-produce game trailers and in-game cinematic

Work across disciplines to strike a balance between gameplay, promotion, and visual storytelling

Create compelling cutscenes and interactive dialogs with excellent pacing, acting, and timing using animation libraries.

Communicate with designers and leads to strike a balance between gameplay and visual storytelling.

Balance quality with efficiency when working under tight deadlines

Follow and maintain pipelines and technical requirements.

Help produce 3D elements and / or any other related tasks


What We Seek:

Passion for making movies

B.A. in film or cinema studies, or work-equivalent experience

At least 3 years of experience as a cinematic artist or cinematic lead

Demonstrated ability to setup, animate and capture compelling, original 3D animations

Technical understanding of run-time engine assets; must be able to maximize existing assets for cinematic use

Outstanding filmmaking skills, including the effective use of color, form, light and composition

Ability to communicate visually, and to identify and define visual design issues and solutions

Willingness to search for technical solutions to realize your creative vision

Strong affinity for Star Trek, Game of Thrones and other fantasy/SF/horror IP

A passion for games, particularly mobile games and strategy games

Good verbal, written, and visual communication skills

Dedication, adaptability and accountability in your work

Have created cinematics for at least one shipped game

Unity game engine

2D animation

3D modeling software (Maya, 3DMax, etc.)


What You’ll Get

We offer world class health insurance. You'll have access to a national network of doctors that provides comprehensive coverage when you need it most.

We believe that rest unlocks creativity, so we encourage our employees to take advantage of our open vacation policy. Our employees take as much vacation as they want, when they want it, as long as they’re getting their work done.

Start work earlier or later according to your inner clock as the core office hours don’t start until 10 am.
