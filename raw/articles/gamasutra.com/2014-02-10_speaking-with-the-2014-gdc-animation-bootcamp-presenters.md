# Speaking with the 2014 GDC Animation Bootcamp Presenters

Listen to the 2014 Animation Bootcamp Presenters discuss their upcoming presentations

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

Animators are making a push to get more involved with GDC this year, and the Animation Bootcamp is a big part of that. [I gathered each of the presenters on the ReAnimators podcast ](http://reanimators.net/?p=520) to discuss a little bit about who they are and what their talks will be about.

Give it a listen and then be sure to[ join us](http://www.gdconf.com/attend/) on Monday, March 17th to get into all the different facets and challenges that define game animation.
