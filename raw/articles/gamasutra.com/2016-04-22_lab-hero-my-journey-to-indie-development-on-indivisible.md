# Lab Hero my journey to INDIE development on INDIvisible!

Kai Kennedy voice actor extraordinaire interviewed me about my journey from college student to AAA development to Indie development with Lab Zero games.

![Game Developer Game Developer logo in a gray background | Game Developer](../../assets/de0d06fe69cb2dbe.png)

2016 has turned out to be a huge transistion for me. I went from working on Mass Effect Andromeda with a team in the hundreds to working on Indivisible at Lab Zero Games with at team of 15.

Kai Kennedy who voiced Beowulf in Skullgirls Second Encore asked me about my journey to Labe Zero Games and what transpired is a 90 minute conversation about everything from playing games as a kid to working on Uncharted 3 to finally the Indie dream. It's a long listen but I hope you enjoy it!
