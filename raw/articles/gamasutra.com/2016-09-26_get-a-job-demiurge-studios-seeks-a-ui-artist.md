Cambridge, MA

You're a special kind of artist. When you hear designers talk about hovering, you think button states. It's hard talking to 3d artists - they keep misusing the term "wireframe." Recurring nightmare: a string gets infected by German and grows to TEN TIMES its normal size.

Special.

You create beautiful, functional UI in a range of styles. Kitten petting game? Easy. Underwater merman village? Check!

You know how to give designers the beautiful button they want, not the functional button they're asking for.

You know it's important to land that back button in just... the right... spot. There.

You love UI challenges, and Demiurge needs you.

If you care about the user’s experience from the very first splash screen all the way through the ending credits, we’d love to talk with you!

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

Design & implement static and animated user interfaces for video games to be shipped on iOS, Android and PC

Execute UI design with a keen eye for detail at all levels - attend to the overarching screen-to-screen UI placement as well as the border treatment in an achievement icon

Work independently and collaboratively with team members to solve UI challenges

Quickly mockup & communicate design intents in a fast-paced, highly iterative development environment

Accurately estimate your work and expected development times while making smart tradeoffs between quality and pace


QUALIFICATIONS

Expert user of industry standard tools including Photoshop, Illustrator and Flash

Experience designing screen flows for console, PC and/or iOS titles

A thorough understanding of visual and textual communication principles

Comfortable giving and receiving work critiques in open sessions

Excellent verbal and written communication skills

A passion for creating high quality user experiences in a team setting


PREFERENCE TO APPLICANTS WITH

Experience developing Flash interfaces

Experience developing interfaces in Unity

Experience making interfaces for mobile games

Credit on titles localized for French, Italian, German, Spanish and Japanese

A degree in UX, fine art or graphic design

Experience creating marketing materials or developing brands

Outside interests!


Please send a resume and a portfolio website for consideration. Please include the URL for your website on your resume.

Interested? [Apply now.](http://jobs.gamasutra.com/job/ui-artist-cambridge-massachusetts-30956)
