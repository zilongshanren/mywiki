Welcome to the exhibition Game Artistory.

In an alternative Universe where human kind created her first videogame at the same time she started to make cave paintings. In a world where videogames are considered Fine Art and videogame makers the Master of Arts. In a time when you can find iconology, symbols and themes from videogame in every higher art form. Welcome to a new world where the art museums shows exhibitions with Game Artisory!
