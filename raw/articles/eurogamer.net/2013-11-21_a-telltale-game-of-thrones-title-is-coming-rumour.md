The Walking Dead and [The Wolf Among Us](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/the-wolf-among-us) developer Telltale is supposedly working on a game based on George R.R. Martin's Game of Thrones series.

Chalk it up as a rumour for now, but [IGN](http://www.ign.com/articles/2013/11/21/telltale-is-developing-a-game-of-thrones-game?abthid=528e88d37372424d2c00003d) is claiming "multiple sources" have confirmed this info, even if Telltale itself is staying mum on the issue.

"We get these sorts of rumours about what we could be working on next cropping up all the time," the developer told us. "Officially we have no comment."

Last month Telltale CEO Dan Connors told [IGN](http://www.ign.com/articles/2013/10/30/telltale-games-talks-star-wars-dream-ip-and-its-future) that he "can't wait" to announce what new properties the studio would be handling next and that it has a "few licenses that definitely fall into our dream IP scenario."

If this happens, it probably won't be for awhile as Telltale is busy working on The Walking Dead: Season 2 and the Fables-based fantasy noir The Wolf Among Us.
