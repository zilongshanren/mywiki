## Abra 100% perfect IV stats in Pokémon Go

This week’s Spotlight Hour is a great opportunity to catch a Abra with perfect IV stats.

'Perfect', of course, means two things in Pokémon Go. First, there’s the maxed out, 100% IV version, which is the 15/15/15 you’re looking for your 4* Pokédex, [raids](https://www.eurogamer.net/pokemon-go-raid-hour-raid-battles-raid-passes-raid-rewards-4819) and [Master League](https://www.eurogamer.net/pokemon-go-master-league-recommendations-7002). Yet, because of how CP is calculated using these three stats, a perfect IV Pokémon is usually only ever the best version of itself in the Master League, raids and Go Rocket battles.

Of course, you can’t see the IV of a Pokémon without catching it first, but with a little research beforehand, you can tell if these are the droids you’re looking for, as the late Great Ben Kenobi once said, based on the CP alone.

## Is Alakazam good in PVP?

No, it's awful. It's really, really bad. It used to be decent in PVE, but then Mewtwo came out blew it out of the water. As for PVP, it is so extremely glassy that it really can't stand up to anything it's not Super Effective against.

So, let's look at its moves. Alakazam runs **Psycho Cut, Fire Punch and Shadow Ball**. In fairness, this moveset would be ok if Alakazam lived long enough to use it.

## Is there a shiny Abra in Pokémon Go?

Yes! As with all Kantonian Pokémon, **shiny Abra, shiny Kadabra and shiny Alakazam are available in Pokémon Go**.

Shiny Abra was released in April 2020, at the start of its Community Day event.

## What does shiny Abra look like?

Sun-bleached.

Simply put, shiny Abra goes from looking yellow and brown to slightly paler yellow and brown. Shiny Kadabra does the same.
