The Xbox 360 sold 960,000 units in the US last week, Microsoft has announced.

As detailed by [Major Nelson](http://majornelson.com/2011/11/29/xbox-360-records-the-biggest-week-in-xbox-history/), that makes it the most successful seven days in the console's history.

800,000 of those were sold in a single 24 hour period - presumably annual US shopping blowout Black Friday.

In addition, Microsoft also sold 750,000 Kinect units over the same period.

Nintendo also announced [an impressive set of Black Friday figures](https://www.eurogamer.net/super-mario-3d-land-is-fastest-selling-portable-mario-ever) earlier this week. The Wii sold 500,000 on Friday alone, the DS moved 900,000 over the entire week and the 3DS saw a 325 per cent sales bump over a two week period.

Sony is yet to reveal how the PlayStation 3 fared.
