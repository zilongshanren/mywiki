Harmonix has announced 15 new songs that will be in its upcoming downloadable offering [Rock Band Blitz](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/rock-band-blitz).

The new playlist includes Tear's for Fear's Shout, Elton John's I'm Still Standing and Iron Maiden's Wicker Man.

The full game contains 25 new songs. Every piece of existing [Rock Band](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/rock-band) DLC will be compatible with Rock Band Blitz, and all the songs in Blitz will function in [Rock Band 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/rock-band-3) as well.

The full 15 songs revealed are listed below. For more info, see [Eurogamer's Rock Band Blitz preview](https://www.eurogamer.net/rock-band-blitz-preview-harmonix-returns-to-its-roots) from GDC.

- All-American Rejects - Kids in the Street.
- Barenaked Ladies - One Week.
- Blink-182 - Always.
- Collective Soul - Shine.
- Elton John - I'm Still Standing.
- Fall Out Boy - A Little Less Sixteen Candles....
- Foster the People - Pumped Up Kicks.
- Great White - Once Bitten Twice Shy.
- Iron Maiden - Wicker Man.
- Living Coulour - Cult of Personality.
- P!nk - Raise Your Glass.
- Quiet Riot - Metal Health.
- Rick Springfield - Jessie's Girl.
- Shinedown - Diamond Eyes.
- Tears for Fears - Shout.
