[007: First Light](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/007-first-light) is a May release [we've been playing close attention to](https://www.eurogamer.net/007-first-light-james-bond-preview-hitman-uncharted-comparisons) for a long time, yet we now won't get every version at once: the [Nintendo Switch 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/platforms/switch-2) version has been delayed.

[Following insider Graczdari breaking the news](https://www.ppe.pl/news/406745/007-first-light-opoznione-w-jednej-wersji-informator-podaje-szczegol.html) earlier today, the game's social media accounts [have confirmed](https://x.com/007GameIOI/status/2041863487291294093?s=20) the delay.

"007 First Light comes to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC on May 27th, 2026, and Nintendo Switch 2 later this summer," the [007 First Light X account](https://x.com/007GameIOI/status/2041863487291294093?s=20) announced. "We're excited to see players discovering James Bond's reimagined origin story, and we are looking forward to bringing you the best game experience possible across all platforms."

[IO Interactive](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/io-interactive)'s action-adventure game, based on the famous fictional British spy, seemed otherwise in good shape earlier this week in a new [10-minute story-focused developer diary.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti0Bofd70o)

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pzkNzkSqqic)

Though most people are confident in the developer's pedigree and experience, having made a name for itself with the Hitman series, [007: First Light faced a major delay in late 2025](https://www.eurogamer.net/merry-christmas-james-bond-game-007-first-light-has-been-delayed). Earlier this year, the studio also caused confusion with [inconsistent PC system requirements](https://www.eurogamer.net/io-interactive-revises-and-reduces-007-first-light-pc-system-specs-after-gamers-highlight-inconsistencies-in-previous-versions).

IO has had to build on its immersive sim and stealth expertise with cinematic and explosive action sequences for this game. In February, [gameplay director Andreas Krogh explained](https://www.eurogamer.net/007-first-light-gameplay-director-talks-batman-arkham-and-uncharted-influences) how they'd looked at modern classics like [Uncharted](https://www.eurogamer.net/video-game-franchises/uncharted) and the [Batman: Arkham](https://www.eurogamer.net/batman-arkham-games-order) games for inspiration, so you could say 007: First Light has been properly shaken and not just stirred.
