2K Drive has launched worldwide on the [App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/app/2k-drive/id568869205?mt=8&ls=1).

The £4.99 565MB app was made by Lucid Games, the Liverpool-based studio made up of some of the people who worked at Project Gotham and Blur developer Bizarre Creations.

There's single-player, multiplayer, over 100 unique events and over 25 tracks in five environments. There are 25 licensed cars from the likes of Fiat, Ford and Nissan.

"Lucid has been thrilled to work with 2K in creating a rich and authentic mobile gaming experience that transcends traditional racing games on the platform," said studio head Pete Wallace.

"2K Drive immerses players in a vast expanse of dynamic content that allows each user to tailor their individual experience to better suit their own interest and level of engagement with the auto genre."
