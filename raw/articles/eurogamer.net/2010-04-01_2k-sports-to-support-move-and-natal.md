2K Sports is to support the new motion controllers for the Xbox 360 and PS3 in its games, according to an interview with marketing boss Jason Argent.

Argent didn't offer any details - but when asked by [the[a]listdaily](http://www.thealistdaily.com/news/feature-2k-sports-steps-up-to-the-plate/) if there were plans to include functionality for "new motion controllers" in future 2K Sports games, he simply said, "Yes... stay tuned!"

Sports games are a natural fit for motion control, especially baseball; Argent was discussing the latest in 2K's MLB (Major League Baseball) series.

Expect to hear more at E3 this year, when Microsoft is due to lift the lid on its Project Natal 3D camera device, and Sony will show more of [PlayStation Move](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/playstation-move).
