Once upon a time Sega had an Aliens RPG and an Aliens FPS. The Aliens FPS became [Aliens: Colonial Marines](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/aliens-colonial-marines); the Aliens RPG was canned.

The RPG was in development at Obsidian Entertainment (Knights of the Old Republic 2, [Fallout: New Vegas](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/fallout-new-vegas)) and [was apparently "ready to ship"](https://www.eurogamer.net/obsidian-aliens-rpg-was-ready-to-ship).

A video of that Aliens RPG has emerged that shows various animations from the game. The video, by character artist Danny Garnett, named the game Aliens: Crucible.

The video popped up on [the Guru3D forum](http://forums.guru3d.com/showthread.php?t=375045) and was picked up by [DSOgaming](http://www.dsogaming.com/news/obsidians-canceled-aliens-crucible-rpg-new-video-shows-off-some-of-the-games-animations/) (via [OXM](http://www.oxm.co.uk/50304/sinister-footage-of-obsidians-cancelled-aliens-rpg-leaks/?cid=OTC-RSS&attr=OXM-General-RSS)).

The Aliens RPG was to be for PC, PS3 and Xbox 360. It was announced bloody ages ago, in 2006.
