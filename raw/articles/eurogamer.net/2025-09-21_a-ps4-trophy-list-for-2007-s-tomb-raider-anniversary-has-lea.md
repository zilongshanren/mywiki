A trophy list for a PS4 version of 2007's [Tomb Raider: Anniversary](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/tomb-raider-anniversary) has popped up online.

While we've heard nothing official about a Tomb Raider: Anniversary re-release – let alone confirmation of a third remastered collection – the achievements are available on the PlayStation trophy tracking site, [True Trophies](https://www.truetrophies.com/game/Tomb-Raider-Anniversary/trophies).

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hPfdFytYKQQ)

Odds are, however, this is probably just a port rather than the next instalment of [Aspyr's remaster efforts](https://www.eurogamer.net/tomb-raider-4-6-remastered-review), not least because there are substantially fewer trophies than we would expect from a modern remaster, and as the redditor who spotted the leak [posits](https://www.reddit.com/r/TombRaider/comments/1nmlwuc/anniversary_leaked_for_ps4/), we'd probably see trophies for Legend and Underworld, too, if Anniversary was part of wider collection.

As the OP [explains](https://www.reddit.com/r/TombRaider/comments/1nmlwuc/comment/nfe1z1i/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web3x&utm_name=web3xcss&utm_term=1&utm_content=share_button) it, "the style of this leaked list tracks with other games released for Sony’s classics catalogue, e.g. the small number of trophies and the design of the images. If it were Saber’s remaster, it would likely have a large, expansive list of things to do, and the images would be in the same style as TR1-6 were".

That doesn't mean Aspyr *isn't* working on a new remastered collection, of course, only that this probably isn't it. We'll probably find out for sure one way or the other soon, though.
