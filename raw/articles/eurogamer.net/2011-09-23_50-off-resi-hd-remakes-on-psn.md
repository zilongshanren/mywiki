PlayStation Plus members can pick up Resident Evil: Code Veronica X HD at half price (£7.99) when it launches on PSN next Wednesday, Sony has announced.

Good news for non PS Plus members too. Buy [Resident Evil 4](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/resident-evil-4) HD and you'll also get 50 per cent off Code Veronica for the next two weeks, as detailed on the [PlayStation Blog](http://blog.eu.playstation.com/2011/09/23/resident-evil-code-veronica-x-hd-hits-psn-wednesday-50-off-for-plus-members/).

Eurogamer's Rich Stanton wasn't terribly impressed with the effort that Capcom has put into the ports. Read his [Resident Evil 4 HD](https://www.eurogamer.net/resident-evil-4-hd-review) review for more details.
