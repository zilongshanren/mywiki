Hitman developer [IO Interactive](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/io-interactive) has detailed the PC requirements for its upcoming James Bond origin story, 007 First Light.

You can check out the minimum and recommended PC requirements for 007 First Light below. The team has not yet listed the 4k specs.

**Minimum:**

- Processor: Intel Core i5 9500K / AMD Ryzen 5 3500
- Graphic Card: NVIDIA GTX 1660 / AMD RX 5700 / Intel discrete GPU equivalent
- RAM: 16 GB
- Video RAM: 8 GB
- Storage: 80 GB minimum
- OS: Windows 10/11, 64-bit
- Resolution: 1080p
- 30 FPS

**Recommended:**

- Processor: Intel Core i5 13500 / AMD Ryzen 5 7600
- Graphic Card: NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti / AMD RX 6700 XT / Intel discrete GPU equivalent
- RAM: 32 GB
- Video RAM: 12 GB
- Storage: 80 GB minimum
- OS: Windows 10/11, 64-bit
- Resolution: 1080p
- 60 FPS

Along with the requirements, the studio has announced a partnership with Nvidia for 007 First Light, saying the power of GeForce's latest RTX technologies will allow the team to "deliver a PC experience that matches the level of quality we believe the Bond franchise deserves".

[007 First Light is set to arrive on 27th May](https://www.eurogamer.net/merry-christmas-james-bond-game-007-first-light-has-been-delayed). As well as PC, the game will also be available across PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox ROG Ally X, Xbox ROG Ally and [Nintendo Switch 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/platforms/switch-2).

At The Game Awards last month, it was [announced that rock musician and Hunger Games actor Lenny Kravitz will star in 007 First Light as black market dealer Bawma](https://www.eurogamer.net/007-first-light-trailer-unveils-lenny-kravitzs-villainous-side).

For more on IOI and Bond, our Alex recently got a closer look at 007 First Light, stating it's "so much more than Hitman", and thanks to "its 'breathing' structure", [it looks like "the ultimate composite video game"](https://www.eurogamer.net/007-first-light-james-bond-preview-hitman-uncharted-comparisons).
