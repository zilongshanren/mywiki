A-Men 2, the sequel to the Vita launch title, has been announced.

[Bloober Team](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/bloober-team)'s rock hard puzzle game is due out on PlayStation 3 and Vita via PlayStation Network on 5th November 2013 in North America and 6th November in Europe.

Bloober reckons A-Men 2 is "arguably the most punishing game on PSN and Vita to date, where only precision and speed of thought will be rewarded with progress". You have been warned.

As in the previous game, you control the A-Men, a Special Forces unit. There are 44 stages across four new worlds. Its debut trailer is below.
