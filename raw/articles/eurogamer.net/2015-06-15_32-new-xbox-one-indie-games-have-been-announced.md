Microsoft has announced 32 new indie games coming to Xbox One via its ID@Xbox program.

The console manufacturer simply added a teaser trailer for each title to its [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/xbox/videos) page.

Here's a full list of what's been announced:

- The Toilet Chronicles
- Soccertron
- Fermi's Path
- Quantum Rush: Champions
- The Bridge
- Deathtrap
- Blues and Bullets - Episode 1
- Blast 'Em Bunnies
- Bailar Latino
- The Great Emoticon
- Dark Raid Bot Wars
- Bacon Man
- Spaera
- Space Dust Racers
- Munch Time 2
- Raging Justice
- Sword Coast Legends
- Prismatica
- Gemini
- ENKI
- Tachyon Project
- Zenith
- Phantasmal: City of Darkness
- Super Rude Bear Resurrection
- Glitchrunners
- The Incredible Adventures of Van Helsing
- Planet of the Eyes
- Leo's Fortune
- Shiny
- Ostrich Island: Escape from Paradise
- Hush
- Neonchrome Alpha
