**Go to the 12,000 Star Wars: The Old Republic Beta Keys page to claim yours!**

Eurogamer and EA are delighted to offer 12,000 readers a chance to participate in beta testing for [Star Wars: The Old Republic](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/star-wars-the-old-republic).

BioWare's monster online world, tipped as the first serious challenger to World of Warcraft, is ramping up for a global launch on 20th December. The online RPG lets you join the Star Wars universe as either a Republic or Imperial character in the early-years time-frame of BioWare's much-loved Knights of the Old Republic games.

Go to the 12,000 Star Wars: The Old Republic Beta Keys page to receive a code which, when [redeemed at SWTOR.com](http://www.swtor.com/redeembetacode), will guarantee you an invite to a forthcoming Old Republic beta weekend. You may need to register a Eurogamer account first, too.

We can't confirm the dates of the beta weekend at present, but should be able to announce them soon. An invite will be sent at a later date to the email address you register when redeeming your code at SWTOR.com.

Please also note that you will need to redeem your code by 11.59pm on Friday 18th November. Codes are not redeemable after this time.

Keys are handed out on a first-come first-served basis, so don't delay. It's as if twelve thousand left mouse buttons clicked at once... and were suddenly silenced.
