Relic has released a 64-bit update for [Company of Heroes 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/company-of-heroes-2).

The update should result in greater stability and overall performance for the vast majority of players, Relic said in a [blog post](http://www.companyofheroes.com/blog/2021/01/18/64-bit-update-now-live).

It also enables crossplay between Steam and Microsoft Game Pass / Windows Store users.

If your PC hardware does not let you run the new 64-bit update, there's a 32-bit legacy beta on Steam.

Meanwhile, an extensive community map patch, which includes 10 new maps and five reworked maps, is also now live. A winter balance patch is due out soon.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Opuv8m_8-zI)

Company of Heroes 2 is over seven years old, so it's great to see Relic continue to update the superb World War 2 real-time strategy game.

There have been plenty of rumours about a third game in the series, although Relic currently has its hands full developing [Age of Empires 4](https://www.eurogamer.net/microsoft-reminds-us-age-of-empires-4-is-still-in-the-works) for Microsoft.
