If you've been looking for an ultralight gaming mouse that won't break the bank and has been a [long-term favourite of ours](https://web.archive.org/web/20220718215721/https://www.eurogamer.net/digitalfoundry-best-ultra-light-mouse-for-fps-csgo-valorant#m42), this Cherry Xtrfy M42 RGB Wireless from Currys that I've spotted might just be a bang-on choice for you.

Xtrfy know a thing or two about some of the [best ultralight mice](https://www.eurogamer.net/digitalfoundry-best-ultra-light-mouse-for-fps-csgo-valorant), being a group that formed out of the legendary Ninjas in Pyjamas eSports organisation of Counter Strike fame, and the M42 RGB in wired and wireless flavours has been one of their longest serving models, coming with an ergonomic shell, a 19,000 DPI sensor and light 67g weight. For just [£14](https://zdcs.link/QPVpqD) from Currys, that almost feels too good to be true.

The M42 was originally designed as a smaller, ambidextrous take on the older M4, therefore making it a lovely choice for those of us without larger hands. Its plastic shell, in spite of being ambidextrous, has some pleasant contouring to make it comfortable for extended use, plus the divisive honeycombed pattern for saving weight. 67g remains pretty lightweight for a gaming mouse, even at times when we've seen over 20g come off it for the lightest customers, and makes wrist-flicks and high-intensity movements less cumbersome than with a heavier rodent. As with its wired counterpart, this wireless version also comes with a choice of two back panels to choose between a raised and less prominent bump.

Inside, it comes with a solid PixArt 3370 sensor that provides up to 19,000 DPI of sensitivity for fast and responsive tracking, although that's easily dialled back if you want more control in games. Connectivity is handled either by a flexible wired cable or a 2.4GHz wireless receiver for a low latency, cable-free connection that I found to work well in my time with the mouse as a secondary gaming option. The battery life here is rated for up to 75 hours, giving you a couple of working weeks between charges, and there is some RGB lighting across the mouse if you want it.

The Cherry Xtrfy M42 RGB Wireless for just £14 from Currys feels too good to be true, and if you want a potent lightweight gaming mouse for less, this is for you.
