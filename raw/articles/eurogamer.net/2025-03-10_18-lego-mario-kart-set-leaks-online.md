**UPDATE 1.33pm:** And, there it is. The Lego Super Mario: Mario Kart - Mario & Standard Kart set has been officially revealed by Nintendo.

You can check out a short teaser for the upcoming build, which will release on 15th May, below.

The upcoming set will retail for £149.99 here in the UK. If interested, [you can pre-order via Lego now](https://www.lego.com/en-gb/product/mario-kart-mario-standard-kart-72037).

**ORIGINAL 11.57am: **Today, 10th March (or Mar10) is Mario Day, and ahead of an official announcement an 18+ Lego Mario Kart set has leaked online.

The set - which will presumably be revealed officially later - popped up on the officially certified [Australian Lego Store website](https://lego.bricksmegastore.com/products/lego-super-mario-mario-kart-mario-standard-kart), and is reported to be launching on 15th May for $249.99 AUD.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WBzuCVIuOtM)

It's known as the Lego Super Mario: Mario Kart - Mario & Standard Kart set. As you can see in the header image above and further images below, this set features a buildable Mario complete with a posable head and arms. The Nintendo mascot is sitting behind the wheel of Mario Kart's Standard Kart, described as "the most iconic Mario Kart vehicle of all".

According to the listing, it will comprise 1972 pieces, with the complete build measuring 8.5 in. (22 cm) high, 12.5 in. (32 cm) long and 7.5 in. (19 cm) wide.

"Place the Standard Kart on the special stand, which allows you to show it at a dynamic angle as if it is being driven at speed or drifting in a Mario Kart race," the listing reads. "This authentically detailed buildable model makes eye-catching Lego Super Mario decor in any fan’s bedroom, living room, game room or office."

[Nintendo and Lego teased its Mario Kart collaboration](https://www.eurogamer.net/lego-super-mario-kart-sets-will-be-ready-to-race-in-2025) during last year's Mario Day. It kept any real details very short, though.
