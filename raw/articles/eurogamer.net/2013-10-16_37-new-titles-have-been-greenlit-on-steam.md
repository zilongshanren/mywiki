The latest batch of accepted Steam Greenlight titles [has been announced](http://steamcommunity.com/games/765/announcements/detail/1514625615931012214) and it's a doozy with 35 games - and a couple of non-games - coming to Valve's portal.

Highlights include Infinitap Games' successfully Kickstarted psychological horror curio [Neverending Nightmares](https://www.eurogamer.net/semi-autobiographical-psychological-horror-game-neverending-nightmares-awakens-on-kickstarter), Treasure's polarity-switching pioneer shmup [Ikaruga](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/ikaruga), and Cyril Megem's one-man RTS/MOBA/shooter [Universum: War Front](https://www.eurogamer.net/universum-war-front-is-an-rts-moba-shooter-hybrid-developed-by-one-guy).
