The 3DS has finally unseated the PSP at the top of Japan's weekly hardware sales chart, after months of Sony domination.

According to the latest Media Create numbers, reported by [Andriasang](http://www.andriasang.com/e/blog/2011/06/23/media_create_sales/), Nintendo's new handheld sold 40,649 units last week, well up on the 24,649 it managed [the week before](https://www.eurogamer.net/yakuza-of-the-end-tops-japan-charts).

The arrival on shelves of [Ocarina of Time 3D](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/legend-zelda-ocarina-time-3d) is no doubt behind the spike. The revamped Zelda classic sold a very healthy 164,110 copies in its first four days on sale.

The PSP came in second with 31,741 sales, but also saw an upswing off the back of new Level-5 IP Little Battle eXperience.

Here's the full hardware chart:

- 3DS: 40,649 (Last week: 24,649)
- PSP: 31,741 (26,681)
- PS3: 16,081 (17,104)
- Wii: 6,678 (6,357)
- DSi LL: 5,464 (5,283)
- DSi: 5,322 (5,232)
- X360: 2,875 (2,370)
- PS2: 1,346 (1,291)
- DS Lite: 109 (146)
- PSP go: 55 (69)

Little Battle eXperience just pipped [Ocarina of Time](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time) to the software crown, though reports suggest that the Zelda game sold out after Nintendo decided on [a modest 150k week-one shipment](http://www.andriasang.com/e/blog/2011/05/03/zelda_shipment/).
