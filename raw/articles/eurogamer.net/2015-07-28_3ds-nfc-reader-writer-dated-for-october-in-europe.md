Nintendo's NFC Reader/Writer accessory, that allows folks to use their Amiibo on older 3DS and 2DS handhelds, will arrive in Europe on 2nd October, Nintendo has announced via [Twitter](https://twitter.com/NintendoUK/status/626014554007465984).

According to North American retailers such as [Target](http://www.target.com/p/nintendo-3ds-nfc-reader-writer-accessory/-/A-23937309) and [Best Buy](http://www.bestbuy.com/site/nintendo-nfc-reader-writer-for-nintendo-3ds-multi/4288800.p?id=bb4288800&skuId=4288800), the accessory will arrive in that territory on 25th September where it goes for $19.99 (about £13).

The peripheral will require two double A batteries.

New Nintendo 3DS includes the NFC function, so this accessory is only for older models of Nintendo's handheld devices - i.e. 3DS, 3DS XL and 2DS.
