3DS games such as Zelda: [A Link Between Worlds](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/legend-zelda-link-between-worlds) and [Star Fox 64 3D](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/star-fox-64-3d) will be re-released next month via the budget-price Nintendo Selects range.

Six 3DS titles will relaunch under the Nintendo Selects banner on 16th October - the first games for the handheld to join the scheme.

They are:

[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds](https://www.eurogamer.net/the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds-review-2)[Star Fox 64 3D](https://www.eurogamer.net/star-fox-64-ds-review)[nintendogs + cats](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendogs-cats-review)[Mario Tennis Open](https://www.eurogamer.net/mario-tennis-open-review)[Yoshi's New Island](https://www.eurogamer.net/yoshis-new-island-review)[Mario Party: Island Tour](http://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/3ds/mario_party_island_tour)

All are worthy of your 3DS game collection except for the latter two - Yoshi's New Island was critically panned, while Mario Party: Island Tour offers little new to the series and nothing improved from moving the multiplayer gameplay onto the small screen.

The inclusion of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds is a nice surprise - it is one of the newer titles on the list, and well worth a play for any Zelda fans.

Notable exclusions from the range include any of the Mario platformers - presumably they will come later.

Nintendo previously compiled a Selects range, formerly named Player's Choice, for the Game Boy and Game Boy Advance, and every home console since the SNES.
