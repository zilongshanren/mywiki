## Fortnite Ice Sculptures, Three Dinosaurs and Four Hot Springs locations at a glance

For the Week 9 [Challenges](https://www.eurogamer.net/fortnite-challenges-weekly-daily-reset-times-6003), you are tasked with finding the Ice Sculptures, Three Dinosaurs and Four Hot Springs locations in sequence.

Here are the Ice Sculptures, Three Dinosaurs and Four Hot Springs locations at a glance, numbered in order of completion:

First, you don't have to worry about whether or not this needs to be completed in one match - they will unlock one match at a time, once the previous one is completed. This means dropping direct to each location from the Battle Bus is the best way to go.

Second, you need to make sure you are in the middle of the three specified locations - whether that's Ice Sculptures, Dinosaurs or Hot Springs - and then dance. Any will do - even a quick dab, if you have it unlocked.

Here are the Ice Sculptures, Three Dinosaurs and Four Hot Springs locations in further detail...

## Fortnite Ice Sculptures location

As you might expect, the three Ice Sculptures are found in the snowy, south-west corner of the map.

Specifically, you'll find them just south-west of Polar Palace. Just at the foot of the series of ice shelves is a group of trees.

Land in the middle of these to find the Ice Sculptures location, and dance. Make sure it's actually in the middle of the group of Ice Sculptures and not on the platform - otherwise it won't work.

Once that's done, load up another match and find the Three Dinosaurs location.

## Fortnite Three Dinosaurs location

Fortnite's Three Dinosaurs Location is found in the desert area on the map - to the south-east.

Specifically, you need to follow the main road running horizontally through the desert, then as it begins to turn north, you'll find the Three Dinosaurs surrounding the road.

While on the road, and in the middle of these Three Dinosaurs, dance to complete this step of the challenge.

With that complete, time for the third and final location - the Four Hotsprings.

## Fortnite Four Hotsprings location

The Four Hotsprings takes you to the other biome on the map - the jungle to the north-east.

You'll find the Hotsprings to the north-west of the volcano. Since this is the only location on the list visible on the map, you shouldn't miss it.

Again, make sure you are in the middle of the specified objective - so in-between the Four Hotsprings - then dance. This will then complete the challenge.
