Nintendo has added three more classic N64 games to its Switch Online expansion pack: 1080° Snowboarding, [Harvest Moon](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/harvest-moon) 64 and [Jet Force Gemini](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/jet-force-gemini).

With these latest additions, we will all be able to shred it on the slopes with some sick flips and spins (or, in my case, go barrelling down into a snowy heap) before nipping over to do a spot farming. Then, we can head to space, where our craft is decked out with a pair of hanging dice.

You can watch a little teaser for them below.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bX6JgcpOI6I)

Jet Force Gemini made its Nintendo Switch debut in Japan earlier this month. However, it seemed to cause some issues for the hardware when played in widescreen.

As [Ed previously reported](https://www.eurogamer.net/jet-force-gemini-on-switch-has-broken-widescreen-mode), the game supports both 4:3 and 16:9 modes. However, the widescreen mode does not properly scale and instead stretches the image with letterboxing.

Meanwhile, as part of [Eurogamer's round-up of some of the best Nintendo 64 games](https://www.eurogamer.net/virtual-console-n64-most-wanted-article) (from all the way back in 2007), we called Jet Force Gemini an "extremely slick and well-made third-person shooter".

"Heavily anime-influenced visuals and character designs set the perfect tone for a game in which you were on a mission to stop the plans of an evil space insect overlord."

Do any of these games take your fancy? Personally, I am looking forward to introducing my kids to Harvest Moon 64 this weekend.
