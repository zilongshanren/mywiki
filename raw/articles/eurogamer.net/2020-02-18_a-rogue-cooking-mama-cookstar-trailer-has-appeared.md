What's on the menu? A Cooking Mama game for Switch, apparently, and if a newly-discovered trailer is to be believed it could be arriving as soon as March. Emphasis on *could*.

The unlisted video was first posted on YouTube in November, but was only unearthed today thanks to a listing on Dutch store [Nedgame](https://www.nedgame.nl/nintendo-switch/cooking-mama-cookstar/8355829085/). Publisher Planet Entertainment has now [made the original video private](https://youtu.be/FashVB5zAWY), but you can watch a re-upload below. Judging by the fact half the video original video was just a black screen, it was clearly unveiled a little early.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPH_9xNeHTk)

The trailer gives a release date of March, and lists a retail price of $39.99 (£30.71), but as the video is a few months old and the linked website leads nowhere, it's worth taking this with a pinch of salt.

News of a Cooking Mama game for Switch first appeared last August, when the game [appeared on both Australian and German ratings sites](https://www.eurogamer.net/new-cooking-mama-game-appears-on-australian-and-german-ratings-sites). That was shortly followed by a press release leak which revealed the game would [come with a vegetarian mode](https://www.eurogamer.net/vegetarian-cooking-mama-fans-rejoice-the-new-game-has-a-feature-just-for-you), and the appearance of several screenshots (via [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2019/08/cooking-mama-cookstar-first-information-screenshots)). Seems like nearly everyone's managed to spill the beans on this one.
