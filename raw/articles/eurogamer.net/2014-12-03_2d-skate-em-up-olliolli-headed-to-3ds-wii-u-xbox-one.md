3DS, Wii U and Xbox One versions of 2D skating side-scroller [OlliOlli](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/olliolli) will launch in early 2015, developer [Roll7](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/roll7) has announced.

The game's new ports are being worked on by fellow British studio Curve, which also handled the Wii U and Xbox One editions of [Thomas Was Alone](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/thomas-was-alone).

Meanwhile, Roll7 is currently busy building OlliOlli 2: Welcome to Olliwood, due for PlayStation 4 and PS Vita next year, and Not a Hero - a 2D cover-based shooter for PS4, Vita and Steam.
