43.8 million player votes later, the winners of the 2025 Steam Awards have been announced, with [Hollow Knight: Silksong](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/hollow-knight-silksong) securing top prize, Game of the Year.

Team Cherry's eagerly anticipated sequel beat off competition from [Arc Raiders](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/arc-raiders), Dispatch, Kingdom Come Deliverance 2, and [Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/clair-obscur-expedition-33) to secure the top prize, while [Silent Hill f](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/silent-hill-f) won Outstanding Visual Style Award and Arc Raider's won Most Innovative Gameplay.

[Hades 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/hades-2) won Best Game on Steam Deck, Dispatch Outstanding Story-Rich Game, while Clair Obscur won Best Soundtrack.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MFfuZzmiPNw)

Split Fiction, Battlefield 6, Where Winds Meet, Elden Ring: Nightreign, and Eurogamer's 2025 Game of the Year, Blue Prince, went away empty-handed.

Here's the full list of winners:

**Game of the Year**: Hollow Knight: Silksong**VR Game of the Year**: The Midnight Walk**Labo(u)r of Love Award**: Baldur's Gate 3**Best Game on Steam Deck Award**: Hades 2**Better with Friends Award**: Peak**Outstanding Visual Style Award**: Silent Hill f**Most Innovative Gameplay Award**: Arc Raiders**Best Game You Suck At Award**: Hollow Knight: Silksong**Best Soundtrack Award**: Clair Obscur: Expedition 33**Outstanding Story-Rich Game Award**: Dispatch**Sit Back and Relax Award**: RV There Yet?

"Congratulations to all of the nominees and winners, and thank you to all who voted," Valve said. "Huge shout out to everyone who made Steam such a great community this year, from the players to the developers. Here's to a 2026 filled with making and finding your NEXT new favourite games (and rediscovering old faves)!"

ICYMI, [Arc Raiders was Steam's biggest-selling game over the holiday period](https://www.eurogamer.net/arc-raiders-was-steams-best-selling-game-over-the-holiday-period). SteamDB recently revealed that the biggest-selling games by revenue on the digital platform from 23rd-30th December were Arc Raiders, Battlefield 6, and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, respectively.
