Sega has embraced its inner meme and released a Sanic T-shirt DLC for [Sonic Forces](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/sonic-forces).

In case you had any doubts, the release was confirmed on Twitter via the official Sonic the Hedgehog account.

The free DLC includes an Sanic T-shirt for your custom character inspired by the Sanic the Hegehog meme - a poorly-drawn Microsoft Paint version of Sonic the Hedgehog.

.This isn't the first time a developer has included a meme in its game. Not all fans saw the funny side when [Nintendo referenced the doge meme in Legend of Zelda: Triforce Heroes](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendo-fans-arguing-over-a-zelda-doge-meme).

And, on a very different note, Bungie came under fire just two months ago after it [included imagery which fans linked to the kek meme in Destiny 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/bungie-removes-hate-symbol-from-destiny-2) - which has recently become better known for its alt-right connotations.

The Sanic t-shirt DLC is now available for free on PlayStation Store, Nintendo eShop and the Microsoft Store for your platform of choice.
