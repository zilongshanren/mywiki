EA plans to release a 10-hour trial of [Mass Effect: Andromeda](http://geni.us/zOvEm) for Access subscribers.

The hefty trial of BioWare's sci-fi action RPG comes out on 16th March - five days before the full game comes out on 21st March in the US (it's 23rd in the UK). It'll surely let you make a decent dent in the game before it launches proper.

Access is EA's subscription. You get early access trials such as this one as well as a number of games to download from the Vault ([EA recently added Battlefield: Bad Company 2 and Battlefield 3 to the Vault](https://www.eurogamer.net/battlefield-bad-company-2-and-battlefield-3-have-been-added-to-ea-access)). Origin Access is available on PC via Origin, and EA Access is available on Xbox One only.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQfT5UYoK6A)

Like with other Access trials, your Andromeda progress automatically carries over to the full game if you buy, so you can pick up right where you left off.
