Amazon UK has cut the price of [Zelda: Breath of the Wild](https://www.eurogamer.net/zelda-breath-of-the-wild-walkthrough-guide-tips-4857) for Nintendo Switch.

The game's eye-watering £60 price tag has been reduced by a tenner - or by £12 if you have Amazon Prime.

Amazon's move to [£49.99 for Zelda](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Legend-Zelda-Breath-Nintendo-Switch/dp/B01N1083WZ/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&linkCode=ll1&tag=eurgam-m-21&linkId=7c8a6f93d607b929450ce3512b483dc2) matches UK retailer Argos, which introduced [its own offer of £49.99](http://www.argos.co.uk/product/6696229) yesterday.

[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/mario-kart-8-deluxe) is listed for £50, while mini-game compilation 1-2 Switch is still £40.

Argos has knocked a fiver off these, however - making Mario Kart 8 Deluxe £44.99 and 1-2 Switch the slightly more palatable £34.99.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xogBQnr99hA)

Breath of the Wild will launch on 3rd March alongside Nintendo Switch - and on the same date for Wii U (where it costs £40). Differences between the two editions are minimal, although the [Switch version runs at a higher resolution](https://www.eurogamer.net/zelda-breath-of-the-wild-switch-and-wii-u-differences-outlined).

Nintendo was criticised for its Switch pricing following last week's Direct reveal, with most saying the new products are too expensive. The Switch itself is £279.99. [Expect to fork out around £75 for a second set of Joy-Cons](https://www.eurogamer.net/a-pair-of-nintendo-switch-joy-con-controllers-costs-75). The Pro controller isn't cheap, either.
