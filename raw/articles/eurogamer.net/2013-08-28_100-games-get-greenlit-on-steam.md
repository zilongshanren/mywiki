Lately Steam Greenlight has been letting games through in smaller, more frequent batches, but that all came to a head earlier today when Valve [greenlit a whopping 100 titles](http://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=171260210).

This bring the total number of Greenlight-accepted titles on the service to 260. There's loads of good finds in today's mammoth batch: many of which we've written about before.

Some highlights include:

Paranautical Activity: A first-person shooter roguelike that [passed up an Adult Swim publishing deal](https://www.eurogamer.net/first-person-roguelike-paranautical-activity-not-allowed-past-greenlight-even-with-a-publisher) when it turned out the publisher didn't pull enough weight to strong-arm Valve into letting the game forego Greenlight. It then [went to Kickstarter](https://www.eurogamer.net/fps-roguelike-paranautical-activity-sets-its-sails-for-kickstarter) where it just [surpassed its goal](http://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1125357480/paranautical-activity-old-school-fps-meets-rogueli) of $10K.

[Mutant Mudds](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/mutant-mudds) was famously [rejected by Steam](https://www.eurogamer.net/valve-rejects-mutant-mudds-for-release-on-steam) and developer Renegade Kid has been pushing it on Greenlight for the longest time. "Mutant Mudds has been Greenlit for release on Steam. I am in shock," [tweeted](https://twitter.com/JoolsWatsham/status/372739398271397890) Renegade Kid founder Jools Watsham earlier today.

Road Rash's unofficial spiritual successor [Road Redemption](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/road-redemption) made the cut as well, following [its successful Kickstater campaign](https://www.eurogamer.net/road-redemption-meets-its-kickstarter-goal-with-two-days-to-go).

Following suit are other Kickstarter successes like the action-roguelike [Delver's Drop](https://www.eurogamer.net/physics-based-dungeon-crawler-delvers-drop-funded-on-kickstarter) and Neverhood spiritual successor [Armikrog](https://www.eurogamer.net/the-neverhood-spiritual-successor-armikrogs-kickstarter-ends-a-success).

Curiously, [Shadow of the Eternals](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/shadow-eternals) also made the cut, despite its [second unsuccessful crowdfunding campaign](https://www.eurogamer.net/shadow-of-the-eternals-dev-vows-to-continue-despite-second-kickstarter-failure).

I'm also happy to see Teslagrad on there, after [having played its excellent demo last week](https://www.eurogamer.net/stylish-puzzle-platformer-teslagrads-demo-is-out-now-awesome).
