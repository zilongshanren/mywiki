[Sea of Thieves](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/sea-of-thieves) has welcomed a further five million people aboard since this January, making for an all-time player count of 15 million.

2020 has been a big year for the game - bigger than the whole of 2019, which in turn was bigger than its launch year in 2018.

Writing on the Xbox Wire [blog](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/07/20/sea-of-thieves-passes-15-million-players-since-launch/), Sea of Thieves boss Joe Neate revealed that last month, June 2020, was the game's biggest month ever.

3.3 million players logged on over its 30 days, a number boosted by the game's launch on Steam. More than a million copies of the game have been sold on Steam since its launch there on 3rd June.

"On behalf of all of us at Rare, I'd like to say a big thank you to everyone who's ever played Sea of Thieves for helping to get us this far," Neate concluded. "It's a game that we love making, and there's plenty more to come."

Rare will continue its work building on Sea of Thieves for the foreseeable future, while a second team at the studio puts together [the intriguing-looking Everwild](https://www.eurogamer.net/rare-announces-everwild-its-next-big-new-ip). It'd be nice to see some more of that at Xbox's next-gen event on Thursday!
