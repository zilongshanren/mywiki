[A Hat in Time](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/a-hat-in-time) is heading to consoles next week.

According to the game's [official Twitter](https://twitter.com/HatInTime/status/935193163857383424), the cute 3D platformer will hit Xbox One and PlayStation 4 on 6th December. You can watch the announcement trailer below.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=um_3_cmnSmg)

The development team also revealed splitscreen will be released next year as part of A Hat in Time's first free DLC, and that the console version will be digital only.

A Hat in Time was [due to be released on Xbox One and PS4 in autumn](https://www.eurogamer.net/after-four-years-a-hat-in-time-is-finally-coming-out-on-pc-ps4-and-xbox-one), alongside the PC and Mac versions, however the console release date was pushed back - despite the team [insisting 6th December is still autumn](http://lmgtfy.com/?q=when+is+fall).

The platformer sees you play as a space-travelling little girl who stitches magical hats. Her journey is abruptly ended when she runs out of fuel - time pieces - which are lost and scattered across a nearby planet.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDNz8JDIPck)

Sitting proudly on Eurogamer's [recommended games list](https://www.eurogamer.net/reviews), Christian Donlan described the game in his [A Hat in Time review](https://www.eurogamer.net/a-hat-in-time-review) as "marvellous" and a "warm curio".

A Hat in Time will cost £22.99 on Xbox One and PS4, the same price as the current Steam version.
