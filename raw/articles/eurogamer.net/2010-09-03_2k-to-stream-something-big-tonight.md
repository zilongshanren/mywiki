2K Games will unveil "something big" on the first day of the Penny Arcade Expo this week and will stream the announcement on the internet.

You'll be able to tune in at [the 2K Games Live site](http://www.ustream.tv/2kgameslive) from 10am PDT (6pm BST / 7pm CEST) today.

There's no word on what to expect, but there have been [rumblings for weeks](https://www.eurogamer.net/gearbox-to-talk-duke-forever-at-pax) that Gearbox Software is to take up the reins on [Duke Nukem Forever](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/duke-nukem-forever), so it could be that.

We'll be peering into the screen right alongside you later today to find out.
