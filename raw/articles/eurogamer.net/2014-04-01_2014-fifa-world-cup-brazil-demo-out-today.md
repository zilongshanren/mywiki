**UPDATE:** The FIFA 2014 World Cup demo is now available on PlayStation 3. You can download it now from [PlayStation Network](https://store.sonyentertainmentnetwork.com/#!/en-gb/games/ea-sports-2014-fifa-world-cup-brazil-demo/cid=EP0006-NPEB90528_00-FIFAWCUP2014DEMO). There it's 1.7GB.

**ORIGINAL STORY:** EA today releases a demo of upcoming football game 2014 FIFA World Cup Brazil.

It's live now for Xbox 360 (Xbox Live Gold subscribers only). You can download it from the [Xbox Marketplace](http://marketplace.xbox.com/en-GB/Product/EA-SPORTS-2014-FIFA-World-Cup-Brazil-Demo/66acd000-77fe-1000-9115-d802454189d6?noSplash=1), where it weighs in at 1.66GB.

It'll be out later today on PlayStation 3, according to the EA Sports FIFA Twitter account.

In the demo you can play as Mexico, Brazil, USA, England, Australia, Japan, New Zealand or Ivory Coast in Kick Off mode.

The game proper comes out on 17th April.
