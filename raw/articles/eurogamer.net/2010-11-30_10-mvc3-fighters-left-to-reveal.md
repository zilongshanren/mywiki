Marvel vs. Capcom 3's already bulging roster is set to swell even further – according to Japanese magazine Famitsu there are still 10 characters left for Capcom to reveal.

The game will have "30 + alpha" characters, producer Ryota Niitsuma told [Famitsu]( http://www.famitsu.com/news/201011/30036323.html) (translated by [Andriasang]( http://www.andriasang.com/e/blog/2010/11/30/niitsuma_on_mvc3/)).

This, Famitsu noted, means that as of 30th November there are still about 10 characters waiting to be announced.

It is unclear how many of these characters will be released as downloadable add-ons. Earlier this month Capcom announced Jill Valentine and Shuma-Gorath as DLC.

What we do know, however, is that Monster Hunter characters will NOT feature. While Capcom considered it, the "atmospheres for the two games are too different".
