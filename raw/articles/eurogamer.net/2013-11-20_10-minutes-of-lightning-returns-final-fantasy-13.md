Can action role-playing game Lightning Returns: Final Fantasy 13 get the series back on track?

[Square Enix](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/square-enix) takes its last swing at the current generation - at PS3 and Xbox 360 - starting in Japan tomorrow. The game launches here on Valentine's Day (14th February).

This 10-minute recording spotted by [All Games Beta](http://www.allgamesbeta.com/2013/11/lightning-returns-final-fantasy-xiii-11.html) shows Lightning Returns from a variety of angles: Lightning exploring the wilderness, Lightning in town and Lightning fighting a dragon. It's a good-looking game.
