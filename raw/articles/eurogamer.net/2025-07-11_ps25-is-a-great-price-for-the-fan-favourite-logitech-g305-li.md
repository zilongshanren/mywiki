Great gaming mice don't have to be expensive - and the Logitech G305 Wireless is the case in point. It's currently going for [£24.99 on Amazon](https://zdcs.link/a5wmVk) in the final day of the Prime Day sales after a 58 percent (!) discount.

That's a great price for a comfy egg-shaped mouse with Logitech's Lightspeed wireless, 250 hours of battery life from a single AA and a Hero 12K optical sensor. I received the G305 to review some time ago, but even after getting one for free I bought two more in different colours because I liked it so much.

There are even some nice aftermarket mods you can do, like replacing the AA battery with a AAA Lithium one to make the mouse significantly lighter. You'll need a [AA to AAA adapter](https://zdcs.link/akKqrW) - or a small ball of tinfoil - but it's well worth doing. More enterprising fans of the mouse have even used its guts to [build their own 3D printed mice](https://www.reddit.com/search?q=g305+mod+subreddit%3Amousereview&restrict_sr=&sort=relevance&t=all) in a range of wild shapes.
