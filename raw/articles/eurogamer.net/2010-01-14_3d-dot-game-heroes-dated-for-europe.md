SouthPeak's bringing PS3 exclusive [3D Dot Game Heroes](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/3d-dot-game-heroes) to Europe in May.

The date was announced on [the European PlayStation blog moments ago](http://blog.eu.playstation.com/2010/01/13/3d-dot-game-heroes-coming-to-europe/).

3D Dot Game Heroes is a LEGO-like retro take on iconic action adventure games like [The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past](https://www.eurogamer.net/r-zelda3-gba).

In a fortunate twist of events, Eurogamer has just reviewed the game on Japanese import. We'll let [our 3D Dot Game Heroes review do the talking](https://www.eurogamer.net/3d-dot-game-heroes-review).
