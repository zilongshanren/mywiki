Age of Mythology is getting a new expansion 13 years after the game came out.

Ensemble Studios' strategy game launched on PC in 2002, and, a year later, saw the release of expansion pack The Titans.

Now, a new expansion pack called Tale of the Dragon has been announced. It's in development at Forgotten Empires and SkyBox Labs, the two studios who have worked on Age of Empires 2 HD Edition and last year's Age of Mythology: The Extended Edition, which includes the base game and the Titans expansion, as well as Steamworks integration, [Twitch](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/twitch) support, an enhanced observer mode, native HD widescreen and improved water and lighting.

Forgotten Empires will hold a livestream on [Twitch](http://www.twitch.tv/ageofempires) on 2nd October to reveal more on Tale of the Dragon.
