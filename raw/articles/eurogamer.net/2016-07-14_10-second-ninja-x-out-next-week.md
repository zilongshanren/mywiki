10 Second Ninja X, the latest version of the fun PC platformer, comes out on Tuesday, 19th July. That's for PC, PlayStation 4, Vita and Xbox One.

10 Second Ninja X asks you to complete its levels in just a matter of seconds. 10 seconds, to be exact. The quicker you complete the level, the more stars you earn.

Publisher Curve launched a promotion tied into a discount for the game. It released a five level demo and set a target of 50,000 stars that would spark a 40 per cent discount. The target was met.

And so, 10 Second Ninja X's SRP of £7.99 / $9.99 / €9.99 will have 40 per cent off for the first two weeks.
