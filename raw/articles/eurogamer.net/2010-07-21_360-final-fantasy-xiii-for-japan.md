An Achievement list on the official Xbox website suggests [Final Fantasy XIII](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/final-fantasy-xiii) will be released for the Xbox 360 in Japan.

Sprawling role-playing game Final Fantasy XIII was released on both the PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in the West.

In Japan, however, it is a PS3 exclusive. Is that about to change?

[The Achievement list](https://login.live.com/login.srf?wa=wsignin1.0&rpsnv=11&ct=1279702132&rver=5.5.4177.0&wp=SAPI&wreply=https:%2F%2Flive.xbox.com%2Fxweb%2Flive%2Fpassport%2FsetCookies.ashx%3Frru%3DhttpZ3AZ2FZ2FliveZ2ExboxZ2EcomZ2FjaZ2DJPZ2FprofileZ2FAchievementsZ2FViewAchievementDetailsZ2EaspxZ3FtidZ3DZ09Z5DZ3BcoZ28j7Z05Z07&lc=1041&cb=B001041httpZ3AZ2FZ2FliveZ2ExboxZ2EcomZ2FjaZ2DJPZ2FprofileZ2FAchievementsZ2FViewAchievementDetailsZ2EaspxZ3FtidZ3DZ09Z5DZ3BcoZ28j7Z05Z07&id=66262) (Xbox Live login required – thanks [Andriasang](http://www.andriasang.com/e/blog/2010/07/21/ffxiii_x360_release/)) spills the beans.

Apparently nearly all games that have had their Japanese language Achievements discovered in advance of official release announcement were later released.

The big gaming news of E3 2008 was, of course, that Final Fantasy XIII would be released on the Xbox 360. The announcement drove some absolutely mental.
