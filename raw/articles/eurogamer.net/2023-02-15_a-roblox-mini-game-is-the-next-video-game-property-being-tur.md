Creatures of Sonaria, a popular mini-game featured in [Roblox](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/roblox), is being made into a TV series.

As reported by [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2023/02/roblox-video-game-creatures-sonaria-tv-adaptation-lucasfilm-1235259168/), the deal has been done "inspired by the recent success of The Last of Us".

The project is being spearheaded by former Lucasfilm executive Catherine Winder, executive producer on the long-running [Star Wars: The Clone Wars](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/star-wars-clone-wars) series, now at her new company Wind Sun Sky.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DrEZt_HCTrc)

More recently, Winder has worked on The [Angry Birds](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/angry-birds) Movie and its sequel.

Creatures of Sonaria is a fantasy survival mini-game where you play as a magical creature in a world full of dragons and other fantastical beasts. Here's a little taste:

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3UuXZ7nTEU)

Wind Sun Sky will now adapt the mini-game's world into a "rich universe" for your TV screens. The project is the first Roblox mini-game to be adapted in this way.

"We will continue to look for rich universes with cross platform, immersive potential, like Creatures of Sonaria, to partner up with and extend into the kind of gamified storyworlds that global audiences and fans are looking for in the metaverse," Winder said.

There's no word yet on when the series might debut.
