Team Ninja announced a new Dead or Alive game at a private Tokyo Game Show dinner earlier today.

It's a 3DS fighting game called [Dead or Alive: Dimensions](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/dead-or-alive-dimensions), and includes all the story points between the first game and [Dead or Alive 4](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/dead-or-alive-4), according to [GamePro](http://www.gamepro.com/article/news/216539/team-ninja-reinvents-itself-with-ninja-gaiden-dead-or-alive).

Team Ninja hopes to recapture the hardcore fighting game audience with Dimensions as well as please veteran fans of the franchise. No release date was mentioned.

Also revealed was Ni-oh, a new collaboration with developer Kou Shibusawa. It's a samurai game. No release date or platforms were revealed.
