It looks like Nintendo has settled on a name for its [unwieldy analogue stick 3DS cradle](https://www.eurogamer.net/3ds-circle-pad-extension-preview).

According to a Nintendo trademark filing spotted by [Siliconera](http://www.siliconera.com/2011/10/25/nintendo-3ds-circle-pad-attachment-dubbed-circle-pad-pro-for-the-west), it'll be called the Circle Pad Pro when it launches in the West early next year.

Still no word on exactly when that might be, but it'll likely be before Resident Evil: Revelations arrives on 27th January, which Nintendo has [confirmed](https://www.eurogamer.net/resident-evil-revelations-release-date) is compatible with the new peripheral.

Over in Japan, the add-on launches in December alongside [Monster Hunter Tri G](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/monster-hunter-tri-g).
