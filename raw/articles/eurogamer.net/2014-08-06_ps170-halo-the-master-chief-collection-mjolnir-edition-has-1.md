The £169.99 [Halo: The Master Chief Collection](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/halo-the-master-chief-collection) Mjolnir Edition includes a 12" Master Chief statue.

That's in addition to the stuff included in the £52.99 Limited Edition: a steel book case, a physical map book featuring all the maps in the game, and the DLC Grunt Funeral skull.

The 12" Artfx statue depicts Master Chief wearing his Mjolnir armour and wielding a Battle Rifle while walking on a ramp in a Forerunner facility.

It's got two sets of interchangeable arms so you can pose the Chief with the Battle Rifle at the ready or held over his shoulder with a grenade in his other hand. There are limited articulation at the neck and waist so you can create your own poses.

The Mjolnir Edition is exclusive to [GAME](http://www.game.co.uk/en/halo-the-master-chief-collection-mjolnir-edition-only-at-game-co-uk-304812/#moreDetails) in the UK.

Halo: The Master Chief Collection is an Xbox One exclusive that packages in [Halo: Combat Evolved](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/halo-combat-evolved), [Halo 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/halo-2): Anniversary, [Halo 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/halo-3) and [Halo 4](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/halo-4). It includes all the multiplayer maps ever released, as well as beta access to Halo 5: Guardians.
