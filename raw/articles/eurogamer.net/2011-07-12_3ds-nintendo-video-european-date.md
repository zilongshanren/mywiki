The 3DS Nintendo Video service will launch tomorrow, 13th July, in Europe.

Nintendo Video offers 3D video content such as comedy clips, cartoons and music. And they're all automatically broadcast to your 3DS via SpotPass.

Initial content will include Oscar's Oasis, a cartoon series, and Magic Tricks for your Nintendo 3DS - a collection card tricks and optical illusions.

Promised future content spans movie trailers, music concerts, animations and more. Eastenders 3D?
