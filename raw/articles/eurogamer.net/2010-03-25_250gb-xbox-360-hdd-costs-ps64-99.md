The new 250GB Xbox 360 hard drive will cost £64.99 here and not £79.99 [as once thought](https://www.eurogamer.net/ms-unveils-standalone-250gb-hdd).

That's according to Xbox Community Manager Graeme Boyd, who also revealed that a data transfer kit will be bundled with the new drive, allowing content to migrate from old to new.

"You probably saw the announce of the 250GB Xbox HDD, but just to confirm: It is coming to UK/Europe, RRP is £64.99/99.99 Euro," [Tweeted Boyd](http://twitter.com/AceyBongos/status/11026934063), aka AceyBongos.

"Lower the pitchforks and douse the torches - the 250GB *does* come with a data transfer kit. Sorry for the confusion," [he chirped](http://twitter.com/AceyBongos/status/11027942374).

The 250GB Xbox 360 hard drive will sell from 16th April in the UK.

Before then, Microsoft will lower the price of existing 120GB HDD drive to £80. Yes, that's more expensive than 250GB drive that arrives in under a month. We're checking 'why'.
