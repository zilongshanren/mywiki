Activision has made the Call of Duty: Modern Warfare and Warzone Season 6 update available to pre-download on PlayStation 4, but not PC or Xbox One.

The update, which weighs in at just over 20GB, was released unannounced by developer [Infinity Ward](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/infinity-ward), letting PS4 users get the download in before Season Six kicks off tomorrow morning. I needed to free up 62.55GB of space in order to download the 20.413GB update.

[Infinity Ward let PS4 owners pre-download the Season 5 update](https://www.eurogamer.net/you-can-pre-download-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-and-warzones-massive-36gb-season-5-update-on-ps4-only) (which was 36GB) back in August, so it's following suit here.

Pre-downloading is welcome, of course, but it's unfortunate you can't do it on PC and Xbox One. According to Activision: "Xbox does not support the ability to pre-download. Similarly, Battle.net does not currently support pre-downloads for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare or Warzone."

Season 6, which launches at 7am UK time tomorrow, 29th September, [adds fast-travel to Warzone via a new Metro system](https://www.eurogamer.net/call-of-duty-warzone-is-getting-fast-travel-via-a-subway-system), as well as [new operators](https://www.eurogamer.net/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-adding-farah-and-nikolai-as-operators), weapons and a fresh battle pass.
