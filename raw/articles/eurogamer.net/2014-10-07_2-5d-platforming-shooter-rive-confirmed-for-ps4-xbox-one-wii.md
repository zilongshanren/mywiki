**ORIGINAL STORY 25/09/2014 7pm:** Back in July Toki Tori developer Two Tribes [revealed its next title](https://www.eurogamer.net/toki-tori-dev-reveals-new-2d-shooter-rive), a 2.5D run-'n'-gun shooter about a mech called Rive. Now, the Dutch developer has clarified on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/TwoTribesGames/status/515151703029850112) that it's coming to PS4, Xbox One, Wii U and Steam.

"We'll be handling PC and Wii U in-house and working closely with a porting partner for PS4 and Xbox One," the developer [added](https://twitter.com/TwoTribesGames/status/515152080932450304). "All versions will be awesome!"

Rive is playable right now at [EGX London](http://www.egxlondon.net/) (that's Eurogamer Expo for the uninitiated, and yes, we are related). Come stop by and give it a go.
