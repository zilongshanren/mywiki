A year ago developer Bohemia launched a €500,000 competition called Make Arma Not War to uncover the best modifications for army game [Arma 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/arma-3). After all, mod culture runs deep at Bohemia, and it's where [DayZ](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/dayz) came from.

Today the finalists are revealed, 50 entries whittled down from 250. They span four categories but the grand prize - €200,000 - focuses on the Total Modification category.

A Total Modification is a mod that "delivers new context of gameplay and setting represented by an array of add-ons and playable content", the [competition website](http://makearmanotwar.com/prizes) states. "Total modification can focus on any type of gameplay ... genre or setting."

In that category there's already a mod popular with the community, called Epoch - an eerie open-world survival game with a heavy bent on building. It's set in the year 2035, after the mass extinction of billions of people, the remnants of an advanced society strewn all around.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FSkVNSHnkkg)

Another Total Modification mod that caught my eye was [Reign of Jurassic](http://makearmanotwar.com/entry/qpHbtld3Ct#.VMpBW2isWUM), probably because it features an abandoned island with dinosaurs on it.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wl75KD6YAXM)

In the Single-Player category there are a couple of notable inspired-by mods: [Grand Theft Altis](http://makearmanotwar.com/entry/F0BQ0uHTFX#.VMpBkmisWUM), in which you play Nikos, leader of a gang trying to maintain peace and normality for citizens in his district; and [Hitman Mission](http://makearmanotwar.com/entry/EKw0fmyN88#.VMpBmGisWUM), a "tribute" to the Hitman series of games.

In the Multiplayer category there's a mod called [Get Wrecked](http://makearmanotwar.com/entry/8dc5ygSwQV#.VMpQvWisWUM) that sounds an awful lot like Scrapheap Challenge, that old telly show hosted by Kryton off Red Dwarf. You scour junk yards to make your beast of a machine that will survive battle with all others.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JiDJwpT4_Q4)

There's also a creepy-sounding survival mod called [Hunting Trip](http://makearmanotwar.com/entry/MTZBcHURWG#.VMpSB2isWUM), that has an actual swamp monster, and there's a one-versus-everyone spy game called [The Hunt](http://makearmanotwar.com/entry/7IZ207C8c7#.VMpQm2isWUM).

[Bohemia's website](http://makearmanotwar.com/finalists) has links to all the mod finalists. The winners will be announced in March.
