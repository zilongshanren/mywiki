While many [Apex Legends](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/apex-legends) players have been engrossed in the shooter's latest time-limited event, Shadow Royale, others have noticed something unusual in the skies above both King's Canyon and World's Edge - a UFO.

As spotted by Apex dataminer Shrugtal - who also revealed last week that [an Arenas Mode might be on the way to Respawn's battle royale](https://www.eurogamer.net/dataminer-discovers-clues-pointing-to-apex-legends-arena-mode) - it looks as though the unusual ship might have something to do with the imminent arrival of Season 7 and all-new Legend, Horizon.

[Respawn Entertainment](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/respawn-entertainment)'s free-to-play battle royale shooter Apex Legends will be making its way to Steam on [4th November](https://www.eurogamer.net/apex-legends-arrives-on-steam-this-november-alongside-season-7), which coincides with the arrival of Season 7. Switch owners eagerly awaiting the game's arrival, however, will unfortunately need to wait a little longer, with the developer now confirming a delay on Nintendo's platform.

In related news, in the patch notes for Aftermarket - the latest Apex Legends update - explained that the dev team had been struggling to nerf Wraith for some time, and [previous changes to her abilities](https://www.eurogamer.net/apex-legends-weakens-wraiths-get-out-of-jail-free-card) had not been enough to balance the character. As a result, [Wraith now has a new set of sprint animations](https://www.eurogamer.net/apex-legends-removed-wraiths-naruto-run-because-it-was-too-powerful), which are designed to make her stand more upright and "expose a larger area of her body to gunfire".
