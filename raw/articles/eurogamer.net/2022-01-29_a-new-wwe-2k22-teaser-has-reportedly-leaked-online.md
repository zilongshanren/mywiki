An "alternate" trailer for the upcoming wrestling game, [WWE 2K22](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/wwe-2k22), has reportedly leaked online.

Shared earlier today by a YouTube channel called WWE Attire and Shows, the new teaser - entitled "It Hits Different" - gives us almost two minutes of WWE action. However, it stopped short of revealing where the trailer had come from.

You can watch it below but I'd be quick if I were you; there's no way of knowing how long this will stay online if it's an authentic leak (thanks, [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/wwe-2k22s-new-trailer-has-leaked-early/)):

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9v6_2yTm3rk)

This latest leak comes just a couple of weeks after other correct details about the game, including its cover star and pre-order bonuses, [popped up online prematurely, too](https://www.eurogamer.net/wwe-2k22-details-including-the-cover-allegedly-leak-online).

WWE 2K22 is set to release on 11th March, 2022, for PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X, and Xbox One.

The annual franchise took a year off in 2021 after Visual Concepts' [WWE 2K20](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/wwe-2k20) was widely panned, prompting Sony to [issue refunds to unhappy customers](https://www.eurogamer.net/sony-issues-refunds-to-unhappy-wwe-2k20-playstation-4-players) as countless players posted clips of [bugs and other issues](https://www.eurogamer.net/wwe-2k20-suffered-an-embarrassing-y2020k-bug-yesterday) and Visual Concepts attempted to [patch up the game](https://www.eurogamer.net/2ks-hilariously-shoddy-wwe-2k20-gets-first-round-of-fixes-in-new-patch).

Ultimately, reception to the broken WWE 2K20 was so poor that a [planned WWE 2K21 follow-up was scrapped](https://www.eurogamer.net/wwe-2k21-canned-after-disastrous-wwe-2k20). Instead, we saw the [launch of the cartoonish WWE 2K Battlegrounds instead](https://www.eurogamer.net/wwe-2k-battlegrounds-enters-the-ring-september-2020), while the main series was given more time to rest up before returning. We'll find out in March if it was worth the wait.
