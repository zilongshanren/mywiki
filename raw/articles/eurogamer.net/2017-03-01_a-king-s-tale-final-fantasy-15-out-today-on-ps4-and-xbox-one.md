A King's Tale, the old-school brawler set 30 years before [Final Fantasy 15](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/final-fantasy-xv), comes out today for everyone - and it's free.

You can download it now from the [Xbox Games Store](https://www.microsoft.com/en-GB/store/p/A-KINGS-TALE:-FINAL-FANTASY-XV/BW0D9LH9GG6D#) and the [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/#!/en-gb/games/a-king's-tale-final-fantasy-xv/cid=EP0082-CUSA05946_00-AKINGSTALEFFXV00).

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KULE_Rcxxo)

A King's Tale features retro graphics and a fast-paced combat system, as well as Final Fantasy staples such as big swords and bigger summons.

It's about King Regis, father to Final Fantasy 15 star Noctis, who tells his son a fairy tale to send him to bed. You play this story, assuming control of young Regis, who is joined by Weskham, Cid and Clarus.
