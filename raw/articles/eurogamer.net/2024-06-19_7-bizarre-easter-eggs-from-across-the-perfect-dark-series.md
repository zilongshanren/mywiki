Perfect Dark has been in the news quite a lot recently. First there was the impressive [gameplay reveal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofUi9DR9sc4) for the upcoming reboot at this month's Xbox Showcase and, literally yesterday, Nintendo [announced](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendo-switch-online-adds-mature-games-selection-starting-with-perfect-dark-and-turok) that the original Perfect Dark is now available to play on Switch.

All of this Perfect Dark news got me perfectly nostalgic, so I jumped back into the original three games and put together a little list video based on some of the most bizarre (and naughty) Easter Eggs and features that Rare's series has to offer.

Give it a watch on the player above or there's the YouTube version embedded below if you'd prefer to push that one into your peepers. Enjoy!
