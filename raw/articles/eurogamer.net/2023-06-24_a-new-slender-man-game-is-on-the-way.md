One of horror's most iconic figures is making a comeback: Slender Man.

Whilst, as yet, developer Blue Isle Studios is keeping tight-lipped on what's next for the supernatural being, the team recently dropped a tweet teasing a return for the creepypasta mascot a full decade after Slender: The Arrival.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9mgp8U_-y1s)

Born by way of an internet urban legend in 2009, Slender Man has popped up in many fan-made and indie horror games, but his most notable in-game appearances include Slender: The Eight Pages in 2012 and 2013's Slender: The Arrival before a highly-publicised stabbing incident and moral panic saw the fictional entity go dark for several years.

Now, however, it seems that it's back:

A countdown has also popped up at [slenderarrival.com](https://www.slenderarrival.com/), leaving some to speculate that a remake or remaster of The Arrival may be on the way. Whatever it is, we don't have long to wait – the countdown is set to expire in 33 days' time, which is 28th July, 2023. Watch this space, eh? (Thanks, [VG24/7](https://www.vg247.com/slenderman-is-coming-back-new-countdown-and-teaser-suggests)).

One of the last times we saw Slender Man in a game was in the Halloween update for [Phasmophobia](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/phasmophobia), which included a [surprise cameo from one of horror's most iconic figures](https://www.eurogamer.net/as-a-halloween-treat-slender-man-is-infiltrating-phasmophobia-games).
