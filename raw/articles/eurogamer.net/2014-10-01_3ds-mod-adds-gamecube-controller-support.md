A modder has got Nintendo's classic GameCube controller working with the 3DS - just in time for the release of Super Smash Bros.

The pad, a favourite of hardcore Smash fans, is shown in the video below working perfectly with Smash Bros. on 3DS (thanks, [Nintendo Life](http://www.nintendolife.com/news/2014/10/modder_shows_how_to_play_the_3ds_with_a_gamecube_controller)).

Some technical work is needed to get the solution working, but the parts you'll need will only cost around a tenner.

Detailed instructions are on [github](https://github.com/dekuNukem/gc3ds) for those who want to try it themselves - although most Smash Bros. fans will probably find it easier to just wait for the game's upcoming Wii U edition.

Nintendo is [planning to release an official Wii U GameCube controller adaptor](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendo-unveils-wii-u-gamecube-controller-adapter) for fans who wish to play Smash Bros. using the veteran pad.

The USB device will have room for four controllers (including the wireless Wavebird variety). A limited number of fresh 'Cube pads will also go on sale.
