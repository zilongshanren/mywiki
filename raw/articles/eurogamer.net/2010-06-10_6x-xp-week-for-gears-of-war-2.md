Epic Games is running a 6x XP week for [Gears of War 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/gears-war-2) during E3.

That's according to producer Rod Fergusson, who [tweeted](http://twitter.com/GearsViking) the news last night.

"It's official, we're going with 6x XP for the week of E3. So that's 3 times the current double XP," he explained using advanced maths.

"It will run from June 14th @ 9AM EDT till June 21st @ 9 AM EDT." 9am EDT is 2pm BST as far as we can remember.

Epic is showing off [Gears of War 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/gears-war-3) at E3 next week, so do remember to take a few minutes out from kerb-stomping your friends to check in on that.
