A demo has been released for Frozen Synapse.

What?

You know, that confusing-looking but actually bloody brilliant PC game.

What?

The one Eurogamer smacked a 9/10 kiss on.

Oh!

You can download the demo from either [Steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/98200/) or [GameFront](http://www.gamefront.com/files/20661958/FrozenSynapseDemo.exe).

The demo contains a randomly generated single-player Skirmish as well as three hand picked missions from the campaign.

To celebrate, the Steam price of [Frozen Synapse](http://store.steampowered.com/app/98200/) will be halved from £19 to £9.50.

Gushed Alec Meer in Eurogamer's [Frozen Synapse review](https://www.eurogamer.net/frozen-synapse-review): "Frozen Synapse takes the old, the stuffy and the traditionally glacial and it makes it brand new, instant and brutal. It's such an achievement."
