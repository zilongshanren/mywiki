After being made playable in cars, on fridges, [via Twitter](https://www.eurogamer.net/doom-is-now-playable-on-twitter) and even in a tiny LCD screen shoved inside a pregnancy test, we may have finally hit our limit for the things that can run Doom.

On the tiniest LCD screen I've ever seen, here's Doom running inside a single Lego brick.

Brilliantly, this brick is a Lego element typically used as a computer screen, with the insides hollowed out to insert a miniature circuit and LCD display.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76_0aUOr1B8)

The creation was put together by Doom enthusiast James Brown, who shared his work on Twitter (thanks, [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/someone-just-made-the-original-doom-playable-on-a-lego-brick)).

The LCD screen offers a 72x40 resolution - just enough to make out what's going on. Sort of.
