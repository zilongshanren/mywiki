1997 Cult classic puzzle platformer Abe's Oddysee is free on [Steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/15700/) until 6pm tonight.

You can add it to your account now, and if you do, it's yours forever. The offer ends 6pm tonight.

Meanwhile, there's 75 per cent off all the other Oddworld games. The Oddboxx, for example, which includes [Oddworld: Abe's Exoddus](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/oddworld-abes-exoddus), [Oddworld: Abe's Oddysee](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/oddworld-abes-oddysee), [Oddworld: Munch's Oddysee](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/oddworld-munchs-oddysee), and [Oddworld: Stranger's Wrath HD](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/oddworld-strangers-wrath-hd), is down to £2.49 from £9.99.

And there's 50 per cent off the Abe's Oddysee remake, [Oddworld: New 'n' Tasty](http://store.steampowered.com/app/314660/). It's £7.49 down from £14.99.
