Sega has announced a limited free trial for real-time strategy game [Company of Heroes 2: The British Forces](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/company-heroes-2-british-forces) that runs for two days before launch.

It begins at 6pm UK time on 31st August and ends at 10pm UK time on 2nd September.

The trial is exclusive to Steam and limited to 55,000 players. Developer Relic will soon announce how it will hand out keys.

[Company of Heroes 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/company-of-heroes-2) is a World War 2 real-time strategy game, and The British Forces is a standalone expansion that is sold for £9.99 on its own or as an addition to the main game with a 20 per cent discount. It's out 3rd September.
