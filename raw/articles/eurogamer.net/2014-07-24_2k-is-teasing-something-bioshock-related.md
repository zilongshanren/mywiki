[BioShock](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/bioshock) publisher 2K Games appears to be teasing a new BioShock project according to an image [tweeted by the publisher today](https://twitter.com/2KInternational/status/492258386290171904).

"Oooo, what COULD this mean?!" the publisher coyly stated of the following image of a woman showcasing an apple in an ad for "Eve's Garden at Poseidon Plaza," a familiar locale to those who've explored Rapture.

Now Poseidon Plaza was part of the first BioShock's Fort Frolic level. Could this mean this is just teasing a port of the first game on a new platform? Or maybe we'll see series creator Ken Levine's proposed [Bioshock Vita tactics spin-off](https://www.eurogamer.net/bioshock-vita-would-have-been-a-final-fantasy-tactics-style-game) finally come to fruition.

Irrational Games, the studio that created the first BioShock as well as [BioShock Infinite](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/bioshock-infinite), massively shrunk in size as [all but 15 of its staffers were let go in February](https://www.eurogamer.net/ken-levine-steps-away-from-bioshock-to-create-something-new). [BioShock 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/bioshock-2) and its Minerva's Den DLC, however, were developed by 2K Marin, the studio that went to work on [The Bureau: XCOM Declassified](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/the-bureau-xcom-declassified). Unfortunately this studio was [also shuttered](https://www.eurogamer.net/the-bureau-bioshock-2-dev-2k-marin-esstentially-shut-after-staff-layoffs-report) in October. So who could be taking the reins on Rapture now? God only knows.
