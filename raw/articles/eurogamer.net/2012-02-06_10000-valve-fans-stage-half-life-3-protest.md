10,000 Valve fans logged on to play [Half-Life 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/half-life-2) en-masse in an attempt to make their campaign for more Half-Life information heard.

But that number is far less than the 50,000 users expected to make the gesture.

The [A Call for Communication (Half-Life)](https://www.eurogamer.net/a-call-for-communication-half-life-steam-group-passes-10-000-members) Steam Group is lobbying Valve to release more information on the future of the Half-Life series - be that Half-Life 3 or [Half-Life 2: Episode 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/half-life-2-episode-3).

Still, the group managed to raise Half-Life 2's player figures during the event to 11th on Steam's listing, with in-game numbers up from the average 3000 active players to more than 13,000, [Kotaku](http://kotaku.com/5882437/so-howd-that-half+life-2-protest-go) reports.

Group members have more than doubled since reaching 20,000 [last week](https://www.eurogamer.net/half-life-3-fan-campaign-staging-mass-gameplay-session).

"The lack of communication between Valve and the Half-Life community has been a frustrating experience. While continued support for current and future products is greatly appreciated, fans of the Half-Life series have waited years for a word on when the franchise will return," the group's description reads.
