Not pre-ordered a PlayStation 4 and still want one at midnight? 150 Tesco Extra stores will stock Sony's next-gen console across the UK tonight.

You can use Tesco's handy [PS4 midnight launch store checker](http://www.tesco.com/store-locator/uk/?target=launch&utm_source=social&utm_medium=redirect&utm_campaign=PS4_Launch) to find your nearest branches which will stock the console.

No pre-order is necessary, which means that you might want to get there in good time - you probably won't be the only one.

The retailer is going head-to-head with Asda, which will also sell the PlayStation 4 from tonight in 155 of its stores (although not all are open at midnight).

We listed all the relevant [Asda branches](https://www.eurogamer.net/asda-secures-extra-ps4-stock-for-those-without-pre-orders) earlier this week. Be aware that consoles will be sold on a first come, first served basis and be limited to one per customer.

Best of luck!
