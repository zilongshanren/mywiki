**A Universal Time** is a [Roblox](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/roblox) action RPG that’s full of characters from popular anime and manga series like Jujutsu Kaisen, Jojo’s Bizarre Adventure, and Dragon Ball Z. In it, you’ll battle difficult bosses, explore the world, and collect new abilities as you level up, all as part of your mission to become the strongest fighter around.

There are quite a few resources you’ll need (or just want) to collect throughout your AUT journey, and if you’re short on any of them, you can use **A Universal Time codes** to score freebies like Skin Crates, different currencies, and even exclusive mounts and other cosmetics.

Developer Universe Time Studio usually shares new codes in the game’s [official Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/aut) or straight onto the game’s Roblox page, but we’ve done all the hard work for you by rounding up all the current and expired codes as well as instructions on **how to redeem codes in A Universal Time** so you can get right back to fighting.

Not sure how to redeem codes in A Universal Time? Here’s what you’ll need to do:
