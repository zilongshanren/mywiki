Wii U promises PS3 and Xbox 360 levels of third-party support - many of the same types of games, in fact.

But there have been tech demos and there have been showreels ([flaunting Xbox 360 and PS3 code](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendo-conference-videos-were-ps3-360)). So which games have been confirmed for Wii U so far?

**Confirmed: **

[Pikmin 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/pikmin-3-confirmed-for-wii-u)[Smash Bros.](https://www.eurogamer.net/smash-bros-comes-to-wii-u-and-3ds)[Lego City Stories](https://www.eurogamer.net/lego-city-stories-coming-to-wii-u-and-3ds)[Darksiders II](https://www.eurogamer.net/darksiders-ii-confirmed-for-wii-u)[Ninja Gaiden: Razor's Edge](https://www.eurogamer.net/exclusive-ninja-gaiden-for-wii-u)- Batman: Arkham City
[New Ubisoft FPS IP](https://www.eurogamer.net/ubisoft-reveals-wii-u-games)[New Ubisoft multi-sports family IP](https://www.eurogamer.net/ubisoft-reveals-wii-u-games)[Ghost Recon Online](https://www.eurogamer.net/ubisoft-reveals-wii-u-games)[Assassin's Creed](https://www.eurogamer.net/ubisoft-reveals-wii-u-games)[Rabbids](https://www.eurogamer.net/ubisoft-reveals-wii-u-games)[Killer Freaks From Outer Space](https://www.eurogamer.net/killer-freaks-from-outer-space-for-wii-u)
