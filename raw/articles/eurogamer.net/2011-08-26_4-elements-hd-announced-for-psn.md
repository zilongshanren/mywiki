PC puzzler 4 Elements is on its way to the PlayStation Network, Sony has announced.

Due out on 31st August for £6.29/€7.99, Boolat Games' 4 Elements HD mixes Bejeweled-style gem matching with some light RPG trimmings.

As detailed on the [PlayStation Blog](http://blog.eu.playstation.com/2011/08/26/move-compatible-4-elements-hd-coming-to-psn-31-august/), there are 64 levels to beat, optional Move controls, leaderboards and trophies.
