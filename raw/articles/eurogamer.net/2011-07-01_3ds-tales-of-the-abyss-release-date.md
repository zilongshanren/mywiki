3DS role-playing game Tales of the Abyss will be released on 10th November in Europe, Namco Bandai has announced.

It's a recreation of the PS2 game of the same name. Abyss 3DS gains dual-screen and 3D support.

Tales of the Abyss follows snooty hero Luke fon Fabre, who soon finds himself on an epic adventure to save the world - as if he'd be doing anything else in an RPG.
