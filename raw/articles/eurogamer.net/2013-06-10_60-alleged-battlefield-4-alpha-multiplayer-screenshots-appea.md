A large pile of [screenshots allegedly from the Battlefield 4 alpha multiplayer test](http://imgur.com/a/oBF5P) have appeared online.

They're on on imgur (via [Reddit](http://www.reddit.com/r/Games/comments/1fyy1j/leaked_battlefield_4_xbox360_aplha_screenshots/)) and show a basic texture-less build of the game being played.

There are some picture taken from the perspective of being inside a tank, but most are on-foot albeit high up in high-rise buildings in a metropolis. Some menu loadout screens can be seen and they confirm the classes Assault, Engineer, Support and Recon.

EA declined to comment on the screenshots at this time.
