A [Dead by Daylight](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/dead-by-daylight) dating sim may be on the way.

As shared on Twitter by [DBDLeaks](https://twitter.com/LeaksDbd/status/1496016213100662784), developer Behaviour appears to have trademarked the title Hooked On You: A Dead By Daylight Dating Sim.

Filed on 16th February 2022, it's currently unknown whether this is a brand new title, extra content for the main game, or just a joke.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bgICOqJ_5Ys)

A dating sim would be fitting for the community though. Dead by Daylight may be a horror game, but fans just can't stop thirsting after its suite of killers.

That goes for the game's winking K-Pop killer The Trickster, to Hellraiser's Pinhead declaring "I came", and the bunny mask-wearing Huntress.

And with the game being so [popular with the LGBT+ community](https://www.eurogamer.net/dead-by-daylight-the-smash-bros-of-horror-committed-to-diversity), you bet that a Dead by Daylight dating sim would aim to be as inclusive as possible.

Eurogamer has contacted Behaviour for comment.

In the meantime, streamers have been [jump-scared by the game's latest killer](https://www.eurogamer.net/streamers-unaware-at-new-dead-by-daylight-killer-scare), taken from cult Japanese horror Ringu.
