[Square Enix](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/square-enix)'s 3DS game Final Fantasy Theatrhythm is now [freely available](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/app/theatrhythm-final-fantasy/id572828338?mt=8) for iOS devices, the developer has announced.

But, unlike the 3DS version, the iPod, iPhone and iPad edition only comes with two tracks pre-installed. You have to buy the rest through in-app purchases. Extra tracks are priced at 69p each.

The rhythm-based Final Fantasy spin-off launched on 3DS earlier this year with 76 songs included. A further 56 tracks have been released as DLC (each sold to the tune of 90p).

On iOS, the game currently has 111 tracks to download, with exclusive tunes from [Final Fantasy Tactics](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/final-fantasy-tactics), [Final Fantasy 7](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/final-fantasy-vii): Advent Children and GameCube spin-off Crystal Chronicles.

iOS users can also pay to download extra exclusive characters such as Tifa from Final Fantasy 7 and Serah from 13-2.

How much will it cost to download the lot? [Siliconera](http://www.siliconera.com/2012/12/13/how-much-theatrhythm-final-fantasy-costs-if-you-buy-everything/) has done the sums and calculated you'll pay $143 (roughly £88) for every piece of content.
