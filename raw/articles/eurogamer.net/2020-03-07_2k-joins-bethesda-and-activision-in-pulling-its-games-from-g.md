2K is the latest publisher to pull its games from GeForce Now, Nvidia's streaming service.

Already we've seen [Activision](https://www.eurogamer.net/geforce-now-has-pulled-activision-blizzard-games-because-they-werent-supposed-to-be-there-in-the-first-place) and [Bethesda](https://www.eurogamer.net/bethesda-pulls-its-games-from-nvidias-streaming-service-geforce-now) remove (most of) their portfolios, and now players have lost access to 2K's games too, including franchises like Borderlands, Bioshock, Mafia, X-Com, and Civilization, amongst others.

As Wes summarised [a few weeks back](https://www.eurogamer.net/bethesda-pulls-its-games-from-nvidias-streaming-service-geforce-now), GeForce Now ties into your existing PC library across a range of online storefronts, allowing you to play your games on computers, smartphones and tablets. It offers two access tiers to the system: the free offering lets users access the cloud system for a session of up to one hour - good enough for a game of [Fortnite](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/fortnite). After that, there's nothing stopping the user from starting another session, though if the servers are fully occupied, a wait may be required.

Then there's the Founder's Edition tier, priced at £4.99 / €5.49 / $4.99 per month for the first 12 months - with the first three months free via a trial. Founders get to jump the queue for server availability and can also access hardware-accelerated ray tracing features in supported games. ([Digital Foundry has more here](https://www.eurogamer.net/digitalfoundry-2020-has-geforce-now-quietly-killed-google-stadia).)

"Per publisher request, please be advised 2K Games titles will be removed from GeForce Now today," Nvidia announced in a [forum post](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/forums/gfn-announcements/22/346564/2k-games-on-geforce-now/?cjevent=cfe27a12606611ea8389004d0a180513) overnight. "We are working with 2K Games to re-enable their games in the future."

"This is really garbage," [complained one user](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/forums/). "I bought extra borderlands 3 because I can play it with the highest resolution and graphics settings on GFN. another game that I can no longer use. Tsss, there are fewer and fewer. Meanwhile it would be better to buy a better pc than to rent a high end pc."

"At this point GeForce Now is cancelled, just accept the fact that you are not capable to provide a good cloud gaming service and move on, I cancelled my subscription,"[ added another](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/forums/gfn-announcements/22/346564/2k-games-on-geforce-now/2410015/).

"Sorry guys but this is getting laughable," [said someone else](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/forums/gfn-announcements/22/346564/2k-games-on-geforce-now/2409884/). "By the time my free 3 months end, there will be nothing to play, so what reason would I have to start paying?"
