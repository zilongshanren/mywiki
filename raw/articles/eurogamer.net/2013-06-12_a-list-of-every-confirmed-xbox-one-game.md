Below lies a list of every Xbox One game confirmed to date, split into titles exclusive to Microsoft's new console and multiplatform releases.

Some games on the list we've known of for a while - Crytek's Roman action game Ryse, for example, or [Square Enix](https://www.eurogamer.net/companies/square-enix)'s Thief. Others were revealed this week during Microsoft's game-stuffed E3 press conference, such as Capcom's [Dead Rising 3](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/dead-rising-3) or the free-to-play [Killer Instinct](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/killer-instinct).

Of the multiplatform games, a few are console exclusives but will also appear on PC - Respawn's [Titanfall](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/titanfall), for example, which is currently not in development for any Sony platforms, and [Project Spark](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/project-spark).

The 51 games (and counting) will begin launching from November onwards, when Xbox One is set to arrive in the UK and another 20 countries around the world.
