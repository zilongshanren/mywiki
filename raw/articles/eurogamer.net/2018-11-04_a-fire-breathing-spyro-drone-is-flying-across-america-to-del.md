A Spyro the Dragon drone is flying from New York City to Los Angeles to "personally" deliver a copy of Spyro Reignited Trilogy to Snoop Dogg.

It seems the pair become BFFs after [Snoop retweeted Spyro's "I'm back" tweet](https://twitter.com/SnoopDogg/status/1056942171620728832), adding: "Yooo my lil neffew @SpyroTheDragon lookin straight !! Bring me that new Spyro Reignited Trilogy!"

Since then, Spyro's been on the move to make Snoop's dreams a reality. So far, the drone - which can also breathe fire - has stopped [to set alight a Toasty-lookalike](https://twitter.com/SpyroTheDragon/status/1058040979456806912), [watch someone get a tattoo of him](http://v), and [do a little drag racing over Spooky Swamp](https://twitter.com/SpyroTheDragon/status/1058418462656249862).

To keep abreast of the latest, keep an eye on the [Spyro Twitter page](https://twitter.com/SpyroTheDragon). Thanks, [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/life-size-spyro-the-dragon-drone-that-can-breath-fire-is-flying-across-america-towards-snoop-dogg.78928/).

Just in case you missed it, [a new Half-Life mod replaces Gordon Freeman with Spyro the Dragon](https://www.eurogamer.net/play-half-life-the-way-it-was-always-meant-to-be-played-as-spyro-the-dragon). Year of the Dragon is now out in early access, complete with a playable demo to give you a taster.

Spyro Reignited Trilogy is out on 13th November, 2018, on PlayStation 4 and Xbox One.
