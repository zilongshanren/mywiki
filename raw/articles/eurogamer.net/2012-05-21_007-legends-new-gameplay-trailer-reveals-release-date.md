[007 Legends](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/007-legends) launches on 16th October, a new trailer has revealed.

That's a Tuesday, the day games launch in the US. We're checking with Activision now to see whether it's a global date.

The trailer reveals mission one of the PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 shooter: Moonraker, which is based on the 1979 film of the same name starring Roger Moore. Five more missions will be revealed ahead of launch.

Legends will knit together story elements from a total of six 007 films, including the new movie. It's being developed by Eurocom, the studio behind The World Is Not Enough for N64 and the more recent [GoldenEye 007](https://www.eurogamer.net/goldeneye-007-review) for Wii.

The 007 Legends trailer is stuck on the end of the first trailer for the new James Bond movie, Skyfall.
