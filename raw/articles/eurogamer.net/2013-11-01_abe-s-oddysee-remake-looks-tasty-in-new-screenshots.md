Just Add Water has released nine new screenshots of Oddworld: New 'n' Tasty. There they are down there.

New 'n' Tasty is a current and next-gen remake of the original Abe's Oddysee, which came out in 1997 for the PSone.

The screenshots show in-game sections alongside some of the updated cutscenes, all, according to JAW, rendered in-engine.

The game is due out on PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, Vita, Wii U, PC, Mac and Linux. JAW has applied to Microsoft's new ID@Xbox plan, but an Xbox 360 or Xbox One version remains unconfirmed.
