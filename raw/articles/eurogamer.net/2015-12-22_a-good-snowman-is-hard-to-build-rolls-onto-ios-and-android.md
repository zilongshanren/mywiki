A Good Snowman Is Hard To Build, the festive holiday puzzle game by designer Alan Hazelden (Sokobond, These Robotic Hearts of Mine) and artist Benjamin Davis (Sushi Snake) is out now on [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/app/a-good-snowman-is-hard-to-build/id1040930654?ls=1&mt=8) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.agoodsnowman).

The cutesy grid-based puzzler is priced at £3.99 / $4.99 on mobile devices with no in-app purchases. It's also permanently seen a price drop on [Steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/316610/) where it's generally £6.99 / $10, but 50 per cent off at £3.49 / $4.99 until 4th January.

Last year for the holidays our YouTube editor Ian Higton [plowed his way through A Good Snowman Is Hard To Build's Early Access build](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jvb1vpT2ElY&feature=youtu.be), which you can watch below.
