PlayStation VR launch title Battlezone has co-operative multiplayer for up to four players, developer Rebellion has announced.

The '101' trailer, below, details the drop-in, drop-out nature of the co-op campaign. The video provides a pretty detailed look at Battlezone's campaign, which also features a nifty roguelike structure for added replayability.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sVRKnzERPFE)

I had a [hands-on](https://www.eurogamer.net/watch-battlezone-has-won-me-over-to-playstation-vr) with Battlezone's E3 demo earlier this year and I liked it so much that it convinced me to preorder a PSVR, so this multiplayer announcement comes as a nice surprise.

Don't forget, if you fancy a blast on Battlezone before its release in October, it will be just one of many playable PlayStation VR titles available at next week's [EGX.](https://www.egx.net/egx)
