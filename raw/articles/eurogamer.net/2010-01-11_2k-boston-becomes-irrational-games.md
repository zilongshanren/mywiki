2K Boston has changed its name back to Irrational Games.

According to [GameInformer](http://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2010/01/08/the-return-of-irrational-games.aspx), the developers became fed up of people referring to the studio as "2K Boston (formerly Irrational Games)" and decided that it would be better to embrace its history.

What's more, the change will apparently result in a more open and communicative Irrational Games, with a [new website](http://www.irrationalgames.com/) (currently a countdown timer) set to launch with community features, game development information and unseen details of [System Shock 2](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/system-shock-2), Freedom Force and the original [BioShock](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/bioshock).
