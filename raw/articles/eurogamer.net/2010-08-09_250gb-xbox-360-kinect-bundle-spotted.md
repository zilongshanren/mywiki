Microsoft will release motion sensor Kinect bundled with the 250GB Xbox 360 slim model, if its own packaging is to be believed.

[Joystiq](http://www.joystiq.com/2010/08/06/250gb-xbox-kinect-bundle-spotted-on-4gb-bundle-packaging/) noticed the bundle on Microsoft's Australian packaging for the 4GB Xbox 360 Kinect bundle, announced [last month](https://www.eurogamer.net/microsofts-aaron-greenberg-interview-1) with a £250 price point.

The Xbox 360 S inside the unannounced bundle looks like the new 250GB model revealed during Microsoft's E3 2010 press conference.

The 250GB model also has the following note: "Hard Drive includes game and demos."

Could Kinect launch title [Kinect Adventures](https://www.eurogamer.net/games/kinect-adventures), which comes with Kinect, be on the 250GB model's hard drive?
