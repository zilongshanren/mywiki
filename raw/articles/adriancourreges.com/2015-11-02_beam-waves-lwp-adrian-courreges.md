[Light Beams LWP](http://www.breakingbyte.com/beam-waves/) is a procedural wallpaper for Android I released in 2014.

It features waves of light dancing on the screen, it is also very lightweight and can easily sustain 60 FPS. Several options are available like color presets, FPS cap, FBO render size to further tune performance.
All the animation is produced by a single fragment shader.
