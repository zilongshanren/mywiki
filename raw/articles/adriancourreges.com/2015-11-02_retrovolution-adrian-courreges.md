Retrovolution is a 3D clone of the classic Arkanoid/Breakout games.


Like most block breakers the goal is to destroy all the bricks by making the ball bounce all around. The player loses a life when the ball hits the floor. The concept has been taken into 3D by creating a 3-dimensional play ground with a paddle controlled in a FPS-style. Weapons are also available.

This demo has been built upon the [C4 engine](http://www.terathon.com/c4engine/)
and uses the [nVidia PhysX API](http://www.nvidia.com/object/nvidia_physx.html).
