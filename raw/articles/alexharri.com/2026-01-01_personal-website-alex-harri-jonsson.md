# About me

Hey, I'm Alex. I'm super interested in software and design. I've been writing about software-related topics since 2019, [see blog](https://alexharri.com/blog).

I've been working full-time as a software developer since May 2018, mostly at early-stage startups. I'm currently working at [NeckCare](https://neckcare.com/).

Aside from work and writing, I enjoy physical activities of all kinds — primarily [padel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel) and weightlifting. I'm also a father to a wonderful daughter.

## Career

As a very early employee at [Taktikal](https://www.taktikal.com/), and subsequently as a Tech Lead, I played a big part in the development of Taktikal's first products. I worked there for 4 years. Those were incredibly formative years — I gained invaluable experience in building applications from scratch and maintaining them as they evolve.

I joined [GRID](https://grid.is/) in 2022. I worked on their JavaScript-based spreadsheet engine running in the browser, their formula parser written in Rust, and I wrote some Python for the back-end. [See "Making GRID's spreadsheet engine 10% faster"](https://alexharri.com/blog/grid-engine-performance).

After GRID, I spent a year and a half developing [Arkio's](https://www.arkio.is/) modeling tools (Arkio is an architectural modeler and model reviewer written in C#). I learned a ton of 3D geometry and mathematics there. A few months into the job I wrote [ "Planes in 3D space"](https://alexharri.com/blog/planes) to share what I learned about the math behind planes.

I'm now working at [NeckCare](https://neckcare.com/) with two of my coworkers from GRID — they, and the rest of the team at NeckCare, are awesome people to work with. I'm enjoying life a lot right now.

## Writing

My most popular post so far has been [ "The web’s clipboard, and how it stores data of different types"](https://alexharri.com/blog/clipboard), which I wrote in 2024. It looks at the evolution of the web's clipboard APIs and their limitations.

My first post of note was [ "The Engineering behind Figma's Vector Networks"](https://alexharri.com/blog/vector-networks). It's a

*lengthy*post covering many topics, containing over 200 diagrams and illustrations.

The first post I wrote containing interactive elements is [ "Multi-cursor code editing: An animated introduction"](https://alexharri.com/blog/multi-cursor-code-editing-animated-introduction). Written in 2022, it uses animated instances of the VS code editor to demonstrate use cases and techniques for multi-cursor code editing. This was quite a popular post!

I'm incredibly proud of my work on [ "Planes in 3D space"](https://alexharri.com/blog/planes). That post contains over 50 interactive 3D illustrations, intended to provide a visual and intuitive understanding of planes.
