# Licenses

All original code in Chickensoft's open source projects is licensed under the permissive [MIT License](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/). Code that is not original is provided under the license of the original author. Non-original code will have its license provided in the directory of the repository it resides in.

Chickensoft projects will only include code licensed under a permissive license that allows closed-source commercial projects to use the code without disclosing their own source code. Most of these licenses simply require attribution to the original author. For more information, see [Choose a License](https://choosealicense.com/).

The Chickensoft logo is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 (CC BY-ND 4.0) license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/). In short, you may copy and redistribute the logo in any form (on your game's splash screen, for instance), even in commercial projects. However, you must provide attribution to Chickensoft somewhere in your project or credits, and you may not redistribute modified versions of the logo.

The 3D Chickensoft Mascot Model and animations, used in the Game Demo, are under different licenses.

The Chickensoft 3D Mascot Model is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). In short, this allows you to use, make derivatives, and share your derivatives, even for commercial purposes, so long as you attribute and give credit to Chickensoft somewhere in your project or credits.

Since the Chickensoft mascot represents Chickensoft, we require attribution if you use it for anything other than personal use — that's it!

The Chickensoft 3D Mascot Model animations are licensed under the [CC0 1.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This allows you to use, make derivatives, and share your derivatives, even for commercial purposes, without any attribution or credit required.

Why? So that you can re-target the animations to your own similar models and rigs without needing to worry about where you got them from. There need to be more totally free game animations in the world!
