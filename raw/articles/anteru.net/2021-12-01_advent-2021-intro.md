If you like to read news on the web, or follow people on Twitter, you often hear about problems, things someone doesn’t like, or the odd random issue that crops up. I’m not sure if it’s a sign of the times or not, but we often tend to see the bad parts and forget that there is a lot out there which works. As the year is coming to and end, I figured it’s high time to try something different for once. Until December 24th, I will write one blog post every day to showcase a project, programming language, tool or frameworks that I like and use regularly. No nuanced review focusing on the pros and cons, with a full breakdown of all problems I encountered, but instead a quick whirlwind tour through projects that I’m grateful for. Join me for an advent of praise like there never before!
