Just new, the [notes](https://anteru.net/notes) section. Often enough, I find some small tidbit of knowledge which is useful but does not warrant a full blog post. In order to keep those around, I’ve added a notes section. Over time, some blog posts will be moved there for easier finding and to have all updates in a central place. After my [latest round of blog updates](https://anteru.net/blog/2010/11/15/1541/), it’s easy for me now to add a custom field which [indicates that a blog post has been moved to the notes](https://anteru.net/blog/2009/09/07/582/).
