I created this example project for a Tech Art Class I gave for SAE School Australia (but gave the class later also at [Enjmin](https://simont.de//projects/simont_content/assets/logo_enjmin.png), [Nord University](https://simont.de//projects/simont_content/assets/logo_nord.png), [Howest](https://simont.de//projects/simont_content/assets/logo_howest.png) & [Vertex School](https://simont.de//projects/simont_content/assets/logo_vertexschool.png)).

It is an extension of my previous project called [“One Shader to Rule them All”](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/4XLk04). The basic idea of both projects: (Almost) all materials are based on only one (super duper) shader to speed up the creation of vfx materials. In addition I wanted to learn more about stencil buffers and challenged me by making a magical card with a little interior inside and a dragon being able to peek out of the card.
