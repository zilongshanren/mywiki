I'll try to finally organize all the things I do and put them all in a single place, in a single website. This is like the third billiontenth time that I created a personal website and I always end up abandoning and deleting the website.

It's been some time since I've been inspired by [BEEPLE's Everydays](https://www.beeple-crap.com/everydays) and in the past I started a daily routine of something in many different periods and always abandoned after a few days.

Considering that today Beeple sold his [5000 Days Everydays NFT](https://www.forbes.com/sites/abrambrown/2021/03/11/beeple-art-sells-for-693-million-becoming-most-expensive-nft-ever/) on Christie's for US$ 69 million – a historic day for art – it's a good day to finally kick off my own Everydays project and routine, which I prefer to call Dailies.

So, Hello World again!
