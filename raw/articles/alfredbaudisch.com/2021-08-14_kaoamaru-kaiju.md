- Game made in 72h for the
[Game Jam Ludum Dare 46](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/46/kaoamaru-kaiju)(the theme was*Keep it Alone*). - I worked alone and made, from start to finish, all the graphics, models, sounds, music and code during the Jam.
- Available for free on
[itch.io](https://splitpainter.itch.io/kaoamaru-kaiju). - A dark cinematic narrative experience – it has no gameplay.
- It has dozens of secrets and meanings, that still haven't been unconvered by anyone.
