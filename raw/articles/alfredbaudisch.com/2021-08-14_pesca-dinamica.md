My father, Reinold Baudisch, was one of the most famous and important anglers in Brazil. For more than 40 years he founded different projects, all related to fishing: stores, newspapers, magazines, radio talk show, courses, books and a TV fishing show (his memorial/museum website is coming soon).

From 2001 to 2019 he was the owner, host and producer of Pesca Dinâmica, a TV fishing show that aired in the Brazilian open TV. By 2019 the show had close to 2000 episodes.
