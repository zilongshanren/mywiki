- Trello inspired kanban board made with the
[Godot Engine](http://godotengine.org/)and GDScript, powered by an online real-time collaborative backend made with[Elixir](https://elixir-lang.org/)and[Phoenix Channels](https://phoenixframework.org/)(with the possibility of additional backend languages and frameworks)

Godello is Godot Engine proof of concept for:

- Business applications
- Advanced GUI
- Complex data architecture and data modeling
- Real-time online data flow
- Connected multi-user collaborative interfaces and interactions

In the end, the idea is to showcase Godot as a viable option for native Desktop applications and tools, no matter how simple or complex/advanced the application and the interface are.
