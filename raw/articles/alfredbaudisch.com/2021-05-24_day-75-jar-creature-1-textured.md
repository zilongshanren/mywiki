I'm very satisfied with how this turned out. It's the kind of weird creature that came out of a PS1 game, just like the way I like it. Very Silent Hill / [Baroque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroque_(video_game)) like.

Also, it's the first time I [textured with texture projection](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r9LC2p1Cqiw), and probably the first time I textured without leaving seams or texture stretch marks!

I'll call this "Jar Creature 1", because I plan to do more of those.

PS1 creature / monster modeled and textured with Blender.
