My wife found in Skagen – Denmark, a rectangular and squared yellow house and a white car, just like children's drawings – she even said: "oh this is how I used to draw houses and cars"! She got so excited about her finding that she took many photos and got very happy with it.

![](../../assets/534c69c247b478b7.jpg)

To make her a surprise, I picked up one of her photos and reproduced it into Blender.
