Bridport, UK. August 15th, 2023.

The latest title in the bestselling Clutter
franchise launched today on Steam. UK-based Grey Alien Games is publishing **Clutter
RefleXIVe: The Diceman Cometh – Collector’s Edition **on behalf of Puzzles by Joe, an Atlanta,
GA-based game developer behind the hit casual game series.

**Clutter
RefleXIVe: The Diceman Cometh – Collector’s Edition **is not only the 14th installment
of this ever-popular hidden object/puzzle hybrid game
series, but it also features over 2800 levels, and a very challenging 100 level
Main Quest.

An extra hard Dice Quest and a No-Motion Old-School Quest are included, along with hundreds of picture puzzles and 300 new Box Quotes puzzles. Box Songs variation, Crazy Singletons & Circular Abstratica are among other super-popular puzzles to enjoy. Randomly generated levels mean endless replayability.

Clutter games are a firm favourite in the casual gaming world, and have generated over $3.5 million in sales to date.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “The Clutter series just goes from strength to strength – fans cannot get enough. We are delighted to bring it to Steam.”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy.
