Bridport, UK. November 9th, 2023

![](../../assets/c02cbbecabba4333.png)

Grey
Alien Games is delighted to announce that it launched **Jewel Match Aquascapes
2 Collector’s Edition **on Steam for PC today.

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European casual game developer behind numerous classic titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match games to Steam.

This new Jewel Match Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version.

Players will discover a plethora of novel gameplay elements as they explore over 200 levels in this feature-packed Match-3 adventure. These include submarines, dynamic conveyor belts and cunning octopuses.

‘Match-3 fans will love the plentiful levels and the generous extras included in this undersea-themed Collector’s Edition,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett. ‘Jewel Match Aquascapes 2 Collector’s Edition is a must-have for puzzle enthusiasts – why not dive in?’

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
