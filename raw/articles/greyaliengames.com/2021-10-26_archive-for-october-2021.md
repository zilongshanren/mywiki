Bridport,
UK. October 26th 2021

**Jewel Match
Twilight 3 Collector’s** Edition
launches today on Steam. UK-based Grey Alien Games is publishing the game on
behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic
casual titles.

**Jewel Match Twilight 3 Collector’s Edition** is a match-3 quest that includes 180 huge levels and three different play modes. With two difficulty settings to choose from, players will restore 9 eerie scenes and have the opportunity to buy dozens of upgrades and power-ups from the shop. Replayable mini-games games include jigsaw and hidden object scenes.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “Players on Steam can now enjoy Jewel Match Twilight 3 Collector’s Edition. This creepy-crawly match-3 is perfect entertainment in the run up to Halloween.”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy. Grey Alien has also brought a number of other titles by Suricate Software in the Jewel Match franchise to Steam.

**Jewel Match
Twilight 3 Collector’s Edition** launches today, 26th
October, 2021, at 4PM UK time.
