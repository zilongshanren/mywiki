*From the creators of Regency Solitaire comes a thrilling
new take on Shadowhand, blending elegant solitaire gameplay with a gripping
historical narrative.*

**November 21, 2024** –

![](../../assets/f88f60bb617fd816.png)

** Shadowhand Solitaire**,
a fresh and streamlined twist on the original

*Shadowhand: RPG Card Game*, is now available on Steam and Itch.io! Designed for casual players,

**offers an immersive solitaire-only experience, where fans of**

*Shadowhand Solitaire**Regency Solitaire*can dive into a captivating story without the complexity of turn-based combat.

The game features simplified
inventory and character stats, redesigned combat encounters as solitaire
puzzles, and exclusive achievements. With sumptuous art, a dramatic storyline,
and atmospheric settings, *Shadowhand Solitaire* is a prequel to the *Regency
Solitaire* series, set in the shadowy world of 1770s England.

Players follow Lady Cornelia
Darkmoor, a bold young aristocrat who leads a double life as the daring
highwaywoman, Shadowhand. This historical adventure unfolds across 22 richly-detailed
chapters with over 150 solitaire levels, allowing players to confront foes by
clearing cards to progress. With *Relaxed*, *Normal*, and *Hard*
difficulty modes, players can tailor the challenge to their liking.

Existing *Shadowhand: RPG Card
Game* players can look forward to a specially-discounted Steam bundle, while
casual solitaire enthusiasts are invited to experience the mystery, danger, and
elegance of 18th-century England.
