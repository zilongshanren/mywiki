Bridport, UK. May 31st, 2022

Grey Alien Games is delighted to announce that
it launched **Jewel Match Solitaire X Collector’s Edition **on behalf of
Suricate Software today on Steam for PC.

![](../../assets/89a31eb9fafb37d7.jpg)

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic casual titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match titles to Steam.

This new Jewel Match Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version.

Players will explore a fantastical world in this Solitaire adventure and restore magnificent castles to their former glory. The game boasts over 1000 levels, including the new Sliding level type, while unlocking classic and additional solitaire variants such as Carlton, Fanny, or Daisy Garden. There are even Supersize and Challenge levels, as well as Mahjong and Match-2 levels included.

‘Diehard solitaire fans will love the huge array of levels and the generous helping of extras included in this bumper Collector’s Edition,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett. ‘There’s just so much gameplay to enjoy.’

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
