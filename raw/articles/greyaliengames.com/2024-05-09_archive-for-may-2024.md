Grey
Alien Games is delighted to announce that it launched **Jewel Match Origins 3
– Camelot Castle Collector’s Edition** on
Steam for PC today.

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European casual game developer behind numerous classic titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match games to Steam.

This new Jewel Match Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version.

Players explore expansive levels full of treasure, and enjoy bonus mini-games with hidden object scenes and puzzles to gain additional resources. Unleash that inner architect to solve match-3 puzzles and unlock the power to build and design the fabled Camelot Castle!

‘Match-3 fans are going to absolutely love this game set in the world of knights and castles,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett.

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
