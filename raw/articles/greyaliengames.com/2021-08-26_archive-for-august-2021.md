The latest title in the bestselling Clutter franchise launched today on Steam. UK-based Grey Alien Games is publishing Ki11er Clutter on behalf of Puzzles by Joe, the Atlanta, GA-based game developer behind the best-selling casual game series.

Ki11er Clutter is the 11th
installment of this ever-popular hidden object/puzzle hybrid game series.
Thanks to its old-school magic, addictive gameplay, and high replayability –
Clutter games are a firm favourite in the casual gaming world, and have generated
over $3.5 million in sales to date.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey
Alien Games said: “We are absolutely delighted to add Ki11er Clutter to
Steam. Puzzle game fans, get ready. You’ve never seen Clutter
this fast, furious and challenging!”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy

The 11th installment of the ever-popular Clutter franchise offers many new Clutter variations, along with old favorites for a truly unforgettable quest.

Features include:

new object sets and many new gameplay modes;

over 1700 levels and a very challenging 144 level Main Quest;

over 100 picture puzzles and box quotes puzzles;

and randomly generated levels for endless replayability.

Grey Alien Games is delighted to announce that it will publish Jewel Match Solitaire Summertime on Steam on behalf of Suricate Software later this summer.
