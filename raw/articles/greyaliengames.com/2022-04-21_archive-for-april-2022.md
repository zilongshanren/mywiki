**Press Release**

Bridport, UK. April 21st 2022

Jewel Match Origins 2 – Bavarian Palace Collector’s Edition launched today on Steam for PC. UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic casual titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match titles to Steam.

Set in the magnificent Romantic Era, Jewel Match Origins 2 – Bavarian Palace Collector’s Edition is a regal Match-3 adventure. Players will explore 180 huge levels and collect resources to help architect Daniel van Arras build a majestic palace for the Bavarian King. The game also features 60 additional bonus levels, and replayable mini-games games, including jigsaw puzzles and hidden object scenes.

‘Match-3 fans are going to love this building adventure set in historical Bavaria,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett. ‘Along with the glorious palaces, there’s hours of gameplay to enjoy.’

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
