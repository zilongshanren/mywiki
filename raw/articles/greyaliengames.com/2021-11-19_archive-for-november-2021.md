**Coming soon…**

Grey Alien Games is delighted to announce that it will publish [Jewel Match Atlantis Solitaire 3 Collector’s Edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1764240/Jewel_Match_Atlantis_Solitaire_3__Collectors_Edition/) on Steam on behalf of Suricate Software on Dec 20th, 2021.

This underwater solitaire adventure features:

– More than 800 Solitaire levels!

– Discover the new supersize, challenge and match-2 levels.

– 6 classical Solitaire games such as Klondike, Spider or Freecell.

– 18 additional Solitaire variants including Sir Tommy and Double Wasp.

– 120 replayable bonus Mahjong levels!

– 10 beautiful underwater scenes to build.

– Unlock 40 ocean fishes to populate the waterscapes.

– Solve dozens of quests.

– Choose between multiple card decks to play with.

– Tons of game play variations and power-ups.

– Play 4 different game modes: Normal, Hard, Relaxed and Timed.

– Unlock wallpapers for every seascape!

– Soothing instrumental soundtrack.

The game will be priced at $12.99 with a 15% launch week discount.
