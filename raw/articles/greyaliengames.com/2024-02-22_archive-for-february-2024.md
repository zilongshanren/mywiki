Grey Alien Games is thrilled to announce that **Regency Solitaire II** launched on Steam and Itch.io for Windows/PC today. The game is priced at US$14.99, with a 10% launch week discount.

![](../../assets/9b27abf4434cca63.jpg)

UK-based Grey Alien Games’ beloved casual hit, Regency Solitaire, garnered numerous accolades, securing a spot among PC Gamer’s ‘Top 100 PC Games.’

Now
fans can revisit romantic Regency Britain to build a glorious garden, in
charming solitaire sequel, **Regency Solitaire II**. Players will tour
historic hotspots, silence gossips, and host the season’s most anticipated soiree.

This extended, collector’s edition also features bonus levels and an extra love story, which will unlock after completing the main game.

‘This game has generated a stir among solitaire afficionados, and fans of Regency romance,’ says designer, Helen Carmichael. ‘We’ve poured a lot of love into this game, and we can’t wait to share it with you.’

Features to look forward to:

- Smooth, satisfying solitaire gameplay.
- Transform Bella’s garden and costume.
- Sumptuous art and romantic storyline.
- 160 unique levels spread over 18 chapters plus 66 bonus levels!
- Unlock dozens of exciting gameplay upgrades.
- Normal and Hard modes.
- Specially-composed, classical music.
- Extra Regency romance story to unlock.
