Due to popular demand, Suricate Software has created an enhanced version of Jewel Match Solitaire – the Collector’s Edition. UK-based Grey Alien Games will publish the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic casual titles.

Jewel Match Solitaire Collector’s Edition boasts 320, all-new levels and 18 solitaire variants such as Klondike, Spider, and Freecell. This enhanced version also includes a strategy guide on how to succeed at solitaire, a music player for tune selection, exclusive wallpapers, concept art, and more!

Players have eight beautiful castles to build. Varied gameplay, dozens of trophies and a choice of card decks all add to the game’s appeal.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “We are delighted to bring Jewel Match Solitaire Collector’s Edition to Steam. This is a great solitaire game, and we think fans will love the thoughtful new additions, which add hours of fun.”

Grey Alien Games has several of it’s own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire and solitaire-driven RPGs, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy.

Jewel Match Solitaire is due to launch on 19th November, 2020.
