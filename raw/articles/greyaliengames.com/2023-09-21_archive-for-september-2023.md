Bridport, UK. September 21st, 2023

Grey
Alien Games is delighted to announce that it launched **Jewel Match Atlantis
Solitaire 4 – Collector’s Edition **on Steam for PC today.

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European casual game developer behind numerous classic titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match games to Steam.

This new Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version.

Players explore the enchanting depths of the mystical underwater realm of Atlantis Solitaire, delving into myriad intricate underwater levels and building stunning aquatic landscapes.

‘The Jewel Match Solitaire games are a firm
favourite among card game players,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett. ‘**Jewel
Match Atlantis Solitaire 4 – Collector’s Edition **is
beautiful, relaxing and fun
– why not dive in?’

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
