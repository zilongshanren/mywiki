Grey Alien Games now has a dedicated [Developer Homepage on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/dev/GreyAlienGames/). You can follow us here and find out about all our games easily in one location.

This is a response to Steam’s recent launch (in beta) of Publisher and Developer Homepages. The new pages mean Steam users can follow individual developers and publishers, and are automatically notified when they release new games, while studios can better showcase their games.
