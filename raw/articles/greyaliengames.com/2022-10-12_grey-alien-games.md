## Publishing

Grey Alien Games has helped other indie creators to bring their games to Steam.

We handle setting up the Steam page for your game and gather wishlists from our extensive fanbase and beyond. We also help with marketing both within Steam, and via social media, press releases and running promotions. Once your game launches we send you monthly revenue reports.

Please [contact us](http://www.greyaliengames.com/contact.php) for more information.
