Sink your teeth into a gothic Match-3 this spooky season!

![](../../assets/6b50ee4f9ad947ef.jpg)

Grey
Alien Games is delighted to announce that it launched **Jewel Match Dracula
Collector’s Edition **on Steam for PC today.

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European casual game developer behind numerous classic titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match games to Steam.

The Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version. The latest installment of the Jewel Match series allows players to become a powerful vampire lord, tackle eerie match-3 puzzles and resurrect and refurbish the legendary Castle Dracula.
