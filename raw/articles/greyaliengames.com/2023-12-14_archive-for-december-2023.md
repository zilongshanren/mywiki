Grey
Alien Games is delighted to announce that it launched **Jewel Match
Solitaire Winterscapes 2 – Collector’s** **Edition** on Steam for PC today.

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game – with a special 15% launch discount – on behalf of Suricate Software, a European casual game developer behind numerous classic titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match games to Steam.

This new Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version.

With
over 1000 levels to explore, players will embark on festive quests, collecting
exclusive winter decorations to bring frozen scenes alive and enhance their
beauty. The snowy serenity of **Jewel Match Solitaire Winterscapes 2** **– Collector’s** **Edition** embraces 6 classical Solitaire
games including Klondike, Spider and Freecell, plus 18 additional Solitaire
variants including Somerset and Council.

‘Jewel Match Solitaire Winterscapes proved a firm favourite,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett. ‘We are delighted to bring its even bigger sequel to Steam in time for the Holiday season.’

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
