Today saw UK-based Grey Alien
Games publish **Clutter 1000** on behalf of Puzzles by Joe, an Atlanta,
GA-based game developer behind the best-selling casual game series.

**Clutter 1000** is the latest iteration of
this popular hidden object/puzzle hybrid game franchise. Thanks to its
old-school magic, addictive gameplay, and high replayability – Clutter games are
a firm favourite in the casual gaming world, and have generated over $3.5
million in sales to date.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey
Alien Games said: “Many fans of the series are saying this is their favourite
Clutter game yet. If you enjoy puzzle games, then we think you are going to
love this!”

Grey Alien Games has several
of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand and Ancient
Enemy.

**Clutter 1000** launched today, 1st
June 2021, with a 15% launch discount.
