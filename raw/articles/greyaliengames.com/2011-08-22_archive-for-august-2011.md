I recently attended [Casual Connect](http://greyaliengames.com/blog/casual-connect-summary/) 2011 in Seattle where I spoke on a panel about being indie hosted by Mark Baxter. Fellow panelists were Ola Rogula ([Doll Divine](http://dolldivine.com/)) and Shane Neville ([Ninja Robot Dinosaur](http://nrdland.com/)).

The panel was called “DIY: Indie Game Dev from Napkin to Profitability” and you can watch the whole video [here](http://casualconnect.org/lectures/indie/diy-indie-game-dev-from-napkin-to-profitability-birkett-rogula-neville-baxter/).

The sound volume is a bit low but if you turn it up it’s clear enough. Enjoy!

Doing the panel was a lot of fun, plus I got a free pass to Casual Connect, which was awesome. Next week I’m on a panel at [PAX Dev](http://dev.paxsite.com/) about being indie.
