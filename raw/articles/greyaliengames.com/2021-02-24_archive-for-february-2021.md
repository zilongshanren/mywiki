[Jewel Match Atlantis Solitaire 2 – Collector’s Edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1543750/Jewel_Match_Atlantis_Solitaire_2__Collectors_Edition/) by Suricate Software is coming to Steam for PC.

UK-based Grey Alien Games will publish the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic casual titles. Grey Alien Games has already published several of Suricate’s other Jewel Match Solitaire titles on Steam.

![](../../assets/9899ee463cd439fa.jpg)

The new game features 600 levels and 10 beautiful underwater scenes to build, with 24 additional Solitaire variants such as Klondike, Spider or Freecell, and new variations such as Gretchen, The Tudors or Rose Garden. Players will also be able to enjoy 100 bonus Mahjong levels.

![](../../assets/7265f4d384bc081e.jpg)

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy.

Jewel Match Atlantis Solitaire 2 – Collector’s Edition is due to launch on **18 March, 2021**.
