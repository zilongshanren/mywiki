Bridport, UK. April 18th, 2023

![](https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/2313420/header.jpg?t=1677831208)

Grey
Alien Games is delighted to announce that it launched **Jewel Match Aquascapes
Collector’s Edition** on Steam for PC today.

UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European casual game developer behind numerous classic titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match games to Steam.

This new Jewel Match Collector’s Edition is packed with exclusive extras that you won’t find in the standard version.

Players will make matches across sprawling coral reef levels, and collect sparkling treasures to restore magnificent maritime mansions to their erstwhile splendor over 220 huge levels.

‘Match-3 fans will love the plentiful levels and the generous extras included in this undersea-themed Collector’s Edition,’ said Grey Alien Games’ Jake Birkett. ‘Jewel Match Aquascapes is packed with relaxing puzzle fun – why not dive in?’

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand, and Ancient Enemy.
