A couple of months ago I did my no.2 speech at Toastmasters and it was called “How I changed my Financial Blueprint” and it went pretty well. It was about a book I read called “[Secrets of the Millionaire Mind](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0060763280?ie=UTF8&tag=httpwwwgreyal-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=0060763280)” by T Harv Eker, and the effect that it had on my finances.

I posted the text for the speech [here](http://greyaliengames.com/blog/how-i-changed-my-financial-blueprint/) and now I have a video of it kindly recorded by another Club 59 member, [Steve Cosmic](http://www.bushpilotproductions.com/).

Watching yourself speak is very educational. You can see all the little mistakes you made and improve upon them next time.

Let me know what you think.
