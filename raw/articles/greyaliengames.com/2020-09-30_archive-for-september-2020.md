## Archive for September, 2020

The bestselling Clutter franchise is coming to Steam. UK-based Grey Alien Games will publish Clutter Evolution: Beyond Xtreme on behalf of Puzzles by Joe, an Atlanta, GA-based game developer behind the best-selling casual game series.

[Clutter Evolution: Beyond Xtreme](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1425120/Clutter_Evolution_Beyond_Xtreme/) is the latest iteration of this popular hidden object/puzzle hybrid game series. Thanks to its old-school magic, addictive gameplay, and high replayability – Clutter games are a firm favourite in the casual gaming world, and have generated over $3.5 million in sales to date.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “We are absolutely delighted to introduce players on Steam to Clutter. If you enjoy puzzle games, then this one’s for you.”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy.

Clutter Evolution: Beyond Xtreme is due to launch on 15th October, 2020.
