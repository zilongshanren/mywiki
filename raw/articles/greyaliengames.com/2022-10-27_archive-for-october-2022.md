**Jewel Match Twilight** and **Jewel Match Twilight
2** launch today on Steam. UK-based Grey Alien Games is publishing the games
on behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous
classic casual titles.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “Players on Steam can now enjoy not one, but two spooky match-3 games from Suricate Software. With haunted castles and no end of spine-tingling gameplay, we think these are ideal entertainment in the approach Halloween.”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire and solitaire-driven RPGs, Shadowhand and Ancient Enemy. Grey Alien has brought a number of Suricate Software’s titles to Steam previously.

Jewel Match Twilight Solitaire
launches today, 27th October, 2022, at 4PM UK time.
