**Jewel Match Solitaire Winterscapes 3 – Collector’s Edition** by Suricate Software launched today on Steam for
PC. UK-based Grey Alien Games published the game on behalf of Suricate
Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic casual titles. This
follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match Solitaire
titles to Steam.

Jewel Match Solitaire Winterscapes 3 – Collector’s Edition includes a thousand levels, including Klondike, Spider, and many more solitaire variations. Players journey through beautiful winter scenes transformed by the magic of the season — from a cozy cottage to a grand snow palace — where they collect winter decor and bring each backdrop to life. Standard, Challenging, Relaxed, and additional modes are available.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “The festive sounds and visuals throughout make this highly-anticipated casual game the perfect holiday solitaire adventure.”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own solitaire titles on Steam, including Regency Solitaire, Regency Solitaire II and Shadowhand Solitaire.
