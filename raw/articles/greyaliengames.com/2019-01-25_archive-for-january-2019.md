[Jewel Match Solitaire L’Amour](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1009700/Jewel_Match_Solitaire_LAmour/) by Suricate Software is coming to Steam for PC. UK-based Grey Alien Games will publish the game on behalf of Suricate Software, a European game developer behind numerous classic casual titles. This follows a similar agreement to bring Suricate’s other Jewel Match Solitaire titles to Steam last year.

Jewel Match Twilight Solitaire L’Amour includes 200 solitaire levels, five beautiful scenes to build and multiple card decks. Players will can enjy tons of gameplay variations and power-ups, along with 50 replayable bonus mahjong levels.

Jake Birkett, owner of Grey Alien Games said: “We are delighted to publish Suricate Software’s romantic themed game on Steam. Jewel Match Solitaire L’Amour is a truly charming game and we think it’s just right for Valentine’s Day.”

Grey Alien Games has several of its own titles on Steam, including [Regency Solitaire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/351090/Regency_Solitaire/) and solitaire-driven RPG, [Shadowhand](https://store.steampowered.com/app/427490/Shadowhand/).

Jewel Match Solitaire L’Amour is due to launch on 14 February, 2019.
