At Google I/O 2016, Francisco Souki and I got to speak about developing for the new Google Daydream. After working with Google on a handful of prototypes designed to showcase what the new platform was capable of we joined Google's Basheer Tome and Bruno Oliveira on stage to talk about it.

Basheer and Bruno cover the hardware and API design and then at around the 23 minute mark Francisco and I go over the prototypes we built and what features they use.

PLATENS– The mounting plates of a press on which the mold halves are hooked up. PERMANENT SET– The increase in length expressed in a proportion of the original length, by which an elastic materials fails to return to authentic length after being careworn for a standard Stylish Bow Shower Caps time period. PARTING LINE– Mark on a molding or casting where halves of mold met in closing. MOLDING PRESSURE– The strain applied instantly or indirectly on the compound to allow the complete transformation to a strong dense part.
