Empowering Content Creators was my session at Unite 2013 in Vancouver.

My focus here is to highlight some of the systems and tools we made when building The World of Lexica that allow artists and designers to take as much control of the development as programmers. I cover modular skinned meshes to dynamically build characters, piecing multiple animations together in the editor for cinematics, and scripting branching dialogue using custom tools.

[The video of the talk is on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmu8D2j1_os)
