As a **computer scientist**, I strive to understand visual perception and build machines that can see. To do so, I leverage tools from differential geometry and algebraic topology, in conjunction with deep neural networks. My research focuses on:

- 3D Computer Vision
- 3D/4D Generative Priors
- Geometric/Topological Deep Learning
- Statistical and Topological Learning Theory
- Quantum Computer Vision

As a **science advocate**, I am committed to breaking down barriers to education, making knowledge accessible to all—especially those from underrepresented and underprivileged communities. Through initiatives like [Bilimler Koyu](https://bilimler.org) and [Nesin Maths Village](https://nesinkoyleri.org/en/main-page/nesin-maths-village/), I actively contribute to this mission. I envision the possibility of an alternative, transformative academia. #baskabirakademimumkun

As an **entrepreneur**, I have co-founded multiple startups, including [BeFunky](https://www.befunky.com), an online photo editing platform that empowers creativity worldwide.

As a **music aficionado**, I am immersed into drums & percussion. I particularly appreciate blues, jazz, funk as well as folk, ethnic and experimental forms of rhythm and music holistically.
