Code: [github.com/ssell/LITL](https://github.com/ssell/LITL)

Developed using Angular and .NET for tracking and sharing your *Magic: The Gathering* collection, decks, and stats. Partial feature list includes:

- Large collection support allowing seamless filtering and browsing of collections with tens-of-thousands of cards.
- Import and export collections across a range of formats.
- Create your own decks, or effortlessly copy existing decks (including precons), or import from other sites.
- View deck statistics such as mana curve and color coverage, as well as drawing sample hands.
- Add friends to compare collections, or to share content only with them.
- Powerful search to find cards by name, release, artist, or preconstructed decks.
- Track various statistics about your collection including value over time, release completion percentage, and more.
- Robust collection history auditing to easily rollback unintended changes.
- Automated task runner that ensures all data stays up-to-date.
- And much more!

Code: [github.com/ssell/OcularEngine](https://www.github.com/ssell/OcularEngine)

A web-based coherent noise generation tool, implemented using JavaScript and HTML5.

- Coherent noise algorithms (Perlin, Simplex, Wavelet, Worley, Diamond-Square, etc.)
- Pure noise algorithms (XorShift32, XorShift128, WELL512, CMWC131104, MT128, MT19937, etc.)
- Grayscale and color palette manipulation
- Multi-threaded using workers for maximum* performance.

Website: [vertexfragment.com/noisegen](https://www.vertexfragment.com/noisegen/)

Code: [github.com/ssell/OBJParser](https://github.com/ssell/noisegen)
