The object above is commonly referred to as a Shader Ball, which are used to test shaders on.

This one in particular was created using [signed-distance fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signed_distance_function) and rendered with raymarching and physically-based lighting. It was originally made for my cleverly named [ “PBR Shader Ball”](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/MtXczf) shadertoy, and I have since come to use it as my standard icon/avatar on various sites.
