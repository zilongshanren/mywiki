**Other Publications**
In addition to the tutorial resources, the following may be of interest:
[Texture Filtering](OtherPubs/Texture_Filtering.pdf)
(pdf). A short talk I gave to a computer graphics class in 2015, covering some
basic anti-aliasing techniques for textures.
[The Future of Curved Surfaces](OtherPubs/FutureOfCurvedSurfaces.pdf)
(pdf). From GDC 2005. Summary of roundtable sessions discussing future
directions in curved surfaces for the games industry.
[Vector Units and Quaternions](OtherPubs/VUQuats.pps) (pps). From GDC
2002. Discusses how to implement standard quaternion operations on the
PS2 vector units.
[Formation-Based Pathfinding for
Real-World Vehicles](OtherPubs/vanverth00formationbased.pdf) (pdf). From GDC 2000. Describes a pathfinding
system that manages objects driven by a physical simulation.
[Writing Java Games: How We Did It](OtherPubs/JavaGame.pdf) (pdf). From
GDC 1998. A case study of the first commercially published Java games:
Tom Clancy's Politika and Ruthless.com.
