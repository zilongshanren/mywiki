Uncover the mysteries of the Wither Storm Mod tailored for Minecraft Java Edition, inspired by Minecraft: Story Mode’s epic narrative. This article, purely a fan-made endeavor, offers insights into progressing within the modification, from the initial summon to the climactic victory over the Wither Storm.

## The Genesis

Upon discovering a dilapidated abode at coordinates x: 0 and z: 0, an intriguing platform appears showcasing a unique wither configuration. The distinct Command Block sits at its heart. To activate the Wither Storm, position the final wither skull adjacent to the formation atop the Command Block. Initiating this sequence will bring forth the Wither Storm at phase 0, granting you a mere 50-second headstart to escape.

- Once the Wither Storm is active, its pursuit begins. Ensure you’ve put ample distance between yourself and it; else, strategize defensively to stay out of harm’s way. Your subsequent 30 minutes will encompass the essence of survival gameplay. Equip yourself with food, gear, transportation means (like boats or equine companions), and ranged weaponry. Put as much space as possible between you and the entity. Before long, the Wither Storm escalates to phase 4, magnifying its size and threat;
- As the creature intensifies its hunt, stay vigilant. On occasion, it may divert its attention elsewhere. Seize such moments to increase the distance or establish a fortified shelter. Refrain from elaborate constructions; its might can obliterate them. Consider underground bunkers for safety and resource replenishment;
- Invest time in forging an amulet (refer to your crafting directory). This magical accessory aids in discerning the Wither Storm’s proximity and direction. The amulet’s subtle blue pulse reveals the entity’s direction. Upon examination in your inventory, it indicates the distance from the menace.

Moreover, the amulet has the ability to tether other beings, facilitating tracking. A specialized version even allows monitoring specific creature types, like endermen.

## The Enchanted Command Block Chronicle

Enter the Withered Symbiont, a formidable, afflicted variant of the wither zombie. Commanded by the inner Command Block of the Wither Storm, it’s the sole source of the coveted enchanted command block tome, essential for creating the ultimate weapon against the Wither Storm. However, a Withered Symbiont manifests only during the climax of phase 5.

During this phase, closeness to the Wither Storm may summon the Symbiont. But beware, proximity risks being devoured by the Wither Storm. Equip yourself adequately, keep shields, tools, ranged weapons, and fall-prevention tools on hand. Invisibility elixirs can be invaluable, making you undetectable. In the dire event of being captured, fight back, targeting its head, and employ fall damage prevention if released.

When the Withered Symbiont emerges, its presence overshadows the Wither Storm’s menacing aura. Engaging the Symbiont assures that the Wither Storm remains passive. As the battle ensues, be alert to the creature’s array of abilities:

- Evoker Fangs: Mirroring the Evoker’s ability, it sends forth a cascade of fangs in your direction;
- Shulker Bullets: Orbiting projectiles that target you after a brief pause;
- Arrows: Projectiles conjured above, then released towards you;
- Smash: A devastating ground attack following a leap;
- Fireball: Revolving fireballs that are successively shot at you;
- Wither Skulls: Projectiles launched in a circular pattern;
- Pull: A magnetic ability drawing you closer.

Master these nuances, strategize your approach, and be one step closer to defeating the Wither Storm.

### Facing the Withered Symbiont

Understanding the Attack: The Withered Symbiont will throw various spells in a random order. The most perilous is the ‘smash’. Post several spells, it summons mobs:

- Mob Summoning and Vulnerability Phase: When the Symbiont summons mobs, they’ll come armored and might be placed between you and the Symbiont. After this, the Symbiont becomes vulnerable. Remember, it’s only open to attacks from its glowing purple gash on its back;
- Health Drop Alert: At below 50% health, its attack frequency rises, but the power remains the same. More armored mobs will be summoned;
- The Reward: Upon defeating the Symbiont, a floating enchanted command block book will appear. Grab it quickly as the Wither Storm will target you soon after;
- Respawn Tips: If you’re defeated and respawn at a distance, the Symbiont may have despawned due to the absence of the Wither Storm and players. Keep your spawn point nearby but safe from the Wither Storm;
- Utilizing the Command Block Book: The book enables crafting super TNT or creating command block tools. Crafting tools consumes the book, and it might take a while to get another. Withered Symbionts have specific summoning restrictions, especially in multiplayer.

### Wither Sickness

Identifying the Disease: Mobs you face may exhibit a purplish disease, indicating wither sickness. Players can contract it too.

- Effects and Cure: Wither sickness reduces health over time and the effect is long-lasting. Golden apple stew is the remedy. You can also heal mobs with this stew;
- Nether Preparation: Blaze rods from the Nether are essential for crafting formidibomb. Be swift; the Wither Storm will follow you through dimensions.

### Using the Formidibomb

- Crafting and Detonation: Crafting the formidibomb starts its fuse, risking an explosion in your inventory. Approach the Wither Storm, craft it, place it, and ignite with flint and steel. The Wither Storm will attract and activate the bomb;
- Post-Explosion Scenario: After the blinding explosion, the Wither Storm will appear dead. Approaching its command block reanimates it, and it’ll split into multiple entities;
- Endgame Strategy: In phase 6, the Wither Storm evolves with up to 9 heads. To defeat it, procure another enchanted command block book and craft a command block tool. This tool is the only way to obliterate the Command Block and eliminate the Wither Storm for good.

## The Belly of the Beast

Venturing into the Wither Storm, adventurers await an entrance, which becomes apparent when a noticeable hole forms above the creature’s primary head. The hole’s purple film is visible even from far away. But patience is required, as this entrance can take hours to manifest. During this time, prepare your gear to enhance survival chances.

Key Preparations:

- Armor & Enchantments: Prioritize diamond armor and imbue them with protective enchantments. Ensure the boots have maximum feather falling enchantment;
- Essential Equipment: Command block tool (for the Command Block), other tools (like pickaxes, shovels), ample food, regeneration potions, water bucket, shield, ender pearls, and a bow loaded with arrows;
- Blocks & Respawn: Carry a stack of blocks and a respawn anchor, along with glowstone blocks. This allows you to set your spawn within the Wither Storm’s bowels;
- Enter the belly of the Wither Storm by either throwing an ender pearl, using a crossbow loaded with an ender pearl, or simply making contact with the entrance hole.

## The Dark Bowels

Inside, you’ll find the Wither Storm’s dark intestines, plagued with tainted flesh, glowing mushrooms, and the remnants of consumed structures. These structures, referred to as bowel caves, can be riddled with dangers like sickened mobs and the looming threat of wither sickness.

Navigating through the vast corridors of the beast will lead you to the main chamber, where the humming of the Command Block grows louder.

## Facing the Command Block

The central room, devoid of mob activity, houses the Command Block atop its pedestal. Several dormant tentacles and two inactive Wither Storm heads surround the area. Once you approach the Command Block, it awakens, leading to a series of confrontations. Here’s a breakdown of the encounters:

- First Strike: After striking the Command Block, it summons tentacles to knock you away, elevates its pedestal, and sends waves of sickened mobs;
- Second Confrontation: On your next approach, more tentacles emerge and a faster-casting Withered Symbiont joins the fray;
- Final Showdown: The third strike awakens the Wither Storm heads. This time, the summoned mobs are more potent and armored. The Command Block remains impervious to damage until the two heads are injured;
- Falling into the central hole during these confrontations will transport you back to the base of the Wither Storm, granting temporary immunity. To ensure revival within the beast’s bowels, set up a respawn anchor.

### The Triumph

On the final blow to the Command Block, you’re whisked away to witness the majestic end of the Wither Storm. As a token of your victory, you receive a withered nether star. The Wither Storm has been vanquished, and you emerge as the triumphant hero.

## Life Post-Storm

The fallout from the Wither Storm’s rampage is evident in the scarred landscapes left in its wake. This altered world is now the canvas on which survivors must rebuild. However, from the debris arises a beacon of hope, quite literally, in the form of the Withered Beacon.

### Crafting the Withered Beacon

The Withered Beacon is born from the Withered Nether Star—a souvenir from your clash with the Wither Storm. To decipher its construction, refer to your recipe book. Powering this beacon necessitates a pyramid of beacon-compatible blocks, mirroring the architecture of standard beacons. A unique twist, however, is the mandatory 3×3 lapis lazuli layer crowning this pyramid.

With the beacon set atop, it springs to life, unveiling a menu of effects upon interaction. The Withered Beacon stands out with its hassle-free activation: simply pick an effect and validate your choice, no additional offerings required. The potency of its influence, covering a 128-block expanse, scales with the pyramid’s height, peaking at 4 layers.

### Harnessing Support Beacons

For those desiring amplified power, Support Beacons are the answer. Every Withered Beacon can synchronize with up to four of these, differentiated by the colors of the amulet’s exterior. Crafting them demands the coveted Nether Stars, speaking to their enhanced capabilities.

### Activating a Support Beacon is straightforward:

Foundation: Lay out a 3×3 platform of iron, redstone, diamond, or emerald blocks.

Connectivity: If it lies within the Withered Beacon’s domain, the Support Beacon springs to life, signaling its connection with a luminous beam.

However, bear in mind:

- Each Withered Beacon can bond with only one variant of a Support Beacon color;
- The effects from these supportive counterparts vary based on their color;
- There’s no obligation to construct a pyramid for Support Beacons—they inherently adopt the Withered Beacon’s tier.

These beacons not only augment power but also open up strategic avenues to manipulate the environment to your advantage.
