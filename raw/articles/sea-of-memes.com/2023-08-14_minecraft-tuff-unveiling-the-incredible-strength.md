## What is Tuff in Minecraft?

Introduced during Minecraft’s “Caves & Cliffs” update, Tuff — also termed “Deepslate Tuff” — was brought into the game’s universe to enrich its subterranean elements. This update wasn’t just a mere addition; it was a transformation aimed at enhancing the intricacies of the underground, introducing novel geologic facets, innovative blocks, and mechanics, all to amplify the thrill of exploration and mining.

Boasting a singular texture, Tuff mirrors the appearance of rugged, aged stone. This distinctive block comes in two forms: the standard Tuff and its refined counterpart, polished Tuff. Venturing deeper than the conventional stone layers, one would encounter these Tuff blocks nestled in the world’s deepslate strata. Notably, deepslate is recognized for its superior durability compared to the usual stone, necessitating more potent tools for effective mining.
