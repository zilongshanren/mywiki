## Acquiring Axolotls: A Comprehensive Guide to Starting Your Breeding Journey

To pave the way for a seamless and rewarding experience, let’s delve into the intricacies of acquiring these fascinating creatures. The process of obtaining Axolotls differs based on the version of the game you’re engaged with.

This phenomenon transpires under specific conditions: the luminosity level must be nil, and the substrate beneath the spawning location should comprise stone, deepslate, andesite, diorite, granite, or tuff. These nuances imply that these creatures could be found within subterranean pools in caverns or nestled in the depths of expansive oceans. The journey to secure a few Axolotls, then, shouldn’t be a strenuous one. A simple yet effective approach involves employing a water-filled bucket to encase the Axolotl, thereby making it portable within your inventory.

With the advent of Minecraft 1.18 and its subsequent iterations, Axolotls adopt a more exclusive habitat: the lush caves biome. Within this distinctive environment, the foundational block beneath the spawning vicinity must consist of clay, refining the parameters of their occurrence. This heightened level of specificity might render the quest to locate them a tad more challenging, yet within the lush caves biome, numerous water pools harbor the potential to yield these captivating creatures.
