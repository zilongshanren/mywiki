Minecraft is the most famous sandbox game known for its abundance of content. It invites players to create their own extensions to the universe of the game, showing imagination and creativity. Minecraft maps are no exception, and every day there are more and more of them.

## Why do we need maps for Minecraft?

Particularly popular with players enjoy Minecraft card walkthroughs. They allow you to plunge into a world of stories created by other users. In story maps there are certain tasks, the implementation of which is mandatory for further progress. For example, in a particular location must collect a certain number of well-preserved items, to bypass cunning traps or defeat powerful opponents. Many of the maps of the passage of Minecraft shine with their originality. Their action can unfold in confusing mazes, the exit from which is not easy to find, a huge open spaces, the study of which takes some time or even on islands floating in the air. Creator of maps for Minecraft player can set their own rules, to complicate or facilitate the passage for other users.

## What Minecraft maps are worth paying attention to?

If you like big bright worlds, the action of which unfolds on vast locations, you can install the map “Fantasy Landscape”. It is distinguished by its beautifully recreated nature and unique views. For players who want to tickle their nerves, you can offer to download the survival map for Minecraft – “SkyWars Castle”. It includes several castles, which will have to explore the user. Their peculiarity is that they float in the air, which means if a great risk of falling into the abyss. Medieval maps are a popular theme among modders. Another interesting example of a survival location is “RPG Island”. At first, this place seems like a corner of paradise, but this impression is deceptive. The island hides a large number of traps that only experienced gamers can bypass. Such maps Minecraft you can download from any thematic resource.

In addition to locations for research and survival, there are many other types, no less popular among fans of the game. For example, there are Minecraft maps with interesting quests. An example of them is “The Reawakens – Days of Creations”. Here before the player opens up beautiful and mysterious world of ancient ruins. Once upon a time an ancient civilization lived in these places, but later vanished without a trace, leaving a field of secrets and traps. Fulfilling the story missions, our hero will uncover all the secrets of this place.

A separate category of maps can be allocated for jumping or parkour. Usually they are located at a huge height with separate islands or platforms floating in the air. Download maps for Minecraft with dizzying jumps is not difficult, because they are very popular. Literally every day there are new, more complex and intricate variations. One example of such a map is the location with the unpretentious name “Jump”. It is a good way to train and test your reaction time.

Minecraft 1.5.2 maps also have a lot of success.

They are based on the famous update “Redstone”, which brought new elements to the game. Snow crafting has become available and along with it appeared beautiful snow-white locations. Many of the custom maps successfully recreate locations familiar from other games, movies or even real cities. Get in the middle of dynamic battles can be on the map “Dust 2”, well known to fans of the series “Counter Strike”. There are also maps of Minecraft for 2 – and more players to complete the task in cooperative mode.

You can see that the game is incredibly diverse. Thanks to the gamers themselves, it is expanding every day. Any Minecraft maps can be downloaded absolutely free, because they are freely distributable user content.
