The modes in Minecraft are a certain setting, the choice of which will determine the nature of the game.

What kinds of modes there are:

Observation – a mode in which the player does not interact with the world, but observes it from the outside. It allows you to freely pass through blocks and entities, being in a state of continuous flight. At the same time the player, not visible to others, can not have any effect on the mobs, but they can not hurt him. Damage can only be sustained by falling into the Void. The participant of the game can open the inventory window, but will not be able to modify it. Observation mode is not available to choose in the process of creating the world. Minecraft servers with modes allow you to set this and other modes by default for all players.

Creativity – the name speaks for itself – this variant of the game is designed for those who love to create and build. For this purpose, the developers of Minecraft have provided the creative mode with a countless number of things, blocks, ore. In the inventory window, you can find a lot of items that help in the construction. The possibility of even a single block is not limited to anything and allows you to build a castle. Here the player is not threatened by disease, hunger and death. Such a peaceful life allows to functionally compare Minecraft creative mode with multiplayer editor of the three-dimensional world, in which the reign of creation.

Adventure – a mode that appeared in version 1.3.1. It was originally created for custom maps, based on which provided cunning tasks, for example, to survive in an extremely limited resources or pass a confusing maze filled with traps. In this virtual space, the player must have special tools to move towards the goal, as the hands are not able to do anything. Therefore, you should use the bonus chest. Minecraft servers with different modes make it possible to organize a multiplayer version of the game.

Survival – Survival mode is traditionally one of the most interesting and popular modes of Minecraft, which allows you to show your gaming qualities at any level of difficulty. But nothing is given for free, and the materials for construction must be obtained by yourself, for example, baking in an oven or digging up ready-made blocks. Participant of the game is available inventory to store the necessary items. Not forbidden to construct their own tools, combining items in recipes. From time to time will appear monsters that can manifest themselves at the most inopportune moment. Therefore, the survival mode of the popular game Minecraft is something akin to the action.

Hardcore – something like Survival mode, but with its own characteristics. Here the player is endowed with only one life and the death of the character ruins the whole created world. After the map is created, the game takes place only on the maximum difficulty level. Most suitable for experienced players and professionals.

Modes in the game of Minecraft allow you to customize your virtual space, in which the player can show his best qualities.
