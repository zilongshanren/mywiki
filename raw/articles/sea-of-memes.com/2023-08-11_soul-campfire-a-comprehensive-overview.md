## What is a Soul Campfire in Minecraft?

Within the realm of Minecraft, a “soul campfire” emerges as a distinct campfire variant, meticulously crafted through the combination of soul soil and wooden planks. This innovation echoes the functionality of conventional campfires, offering illumination and culinary applications. Yet, its uniqueness is accentuated by its capacity to yield a captivating “soul fire,” an augmented luminance source that possesses the remarkable ability to empower specific tools, like the smithing table. The genesis of soul campfires traces back to the Nether Update (1.16), and their presence is confined solely to the enigmatic dimensions of the Nether.

## Creating Soul Fire in Minecraft

In the realm of Minecraft, crafting soul fire entails the collection of soul sand and wooden logs. Once in possession of these components, the process involves situating the soul sand on the terrain and subsequently positioning the wooden logs atop the soul sand. This fusion begets the mesmerizing soul fire, a perpetual blaze that serves as an enduring illumination source. Furthermore, the versatility of soul fire extends to the crafting of a soul fire lantern – an illuminating fixture capable of adorning surfaces or suspending from ceilings.

Follow these steps to craft your very own Soul Campfire:

- Collect the Ingredients: Venture into your Minecraft world and gather Sticks, which can be obtained by placing two Wooden Planks vertically in a crafting table grid. Obtain Soul Sand or Soul Soil by mining Soul Sand blocks in the Nether;

- Gather Wood: You’ll need any type of Log, Stem, Stripped Log, Stripped Stem, Wood, or Hyphae. Chop down trees to collect the appropriate material for this step;

- Access the Crafting Table: Open the crafting table interface by right-clicking on a crafting table block;

- Crafting Recipe: Combine the gathered Sticks, Soul Sand or Soul Soil, and the chosen Log, Stem, Wood, or Hyphae in the crafting table according to the following recipe:
