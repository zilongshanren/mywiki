### How many people play Minecraft in Asia?

It’s hard to provide stats on the exact number of Minecraft users in Asia. The game is very popular in the region, however, most countries don’t collect and don’t share the data about users.

The data from Newzoo showed that the Asia-Pacific region constituted the largest market for PC games in 2020. More than 40% of all PC games revenue was generated there. Minecraft is definitely one of the biggest contributors to it.

If we consider the copies sold, over 400 million copies were sold in China alone in 2020, while the rest of the world counted 200 million copies. Most Minecraft hours played are also recorded in China. In general, a player spends around 5 hours a day in the game.

While some people say that playing games is only for kids and condemn this activity if the player is an adult. Users also have to learn some basic programming and improve their analytical skills if they want to play. So Minecraft can hardly be considered a stupid time-killer, it’s more a virtual hobby.
