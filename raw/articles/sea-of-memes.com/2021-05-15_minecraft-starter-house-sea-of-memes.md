Many people like the game Minecraft. It is so popular that it has been added to the school curriculum in more than a thousand schools around the world. The first thing the player thinks about when starting the gameplay is how to build a house in Minecraft. This is one of the primary tasks, because in the house you can hide at night, during an attack by evil monsters, and the house can be used as a storehouse for valuable items. Let’s try to understand step by step how to build a house in Minecraft.

## How to build your first house

Before you build something in Minecraft, you need to decide what material to use for it. Typically, most players build their first house out of wood. To get it, you need to cut down trees. When enough resources are collected, you can start building directly.

To install a block, you need to select it in the inventory list and right click on the place where you want to install it. This will be our foundation. To install the second row of blocks you need to stand on the first one. Next, install the blocks in the same way as in the first row. You can immediately leave room for windows and doors.

After the walls are built, it is necessary to install windows. They will protect us from winds and rain. For the windows, we will need glass. To make it, we need a stove. In the middle of the bottom cell there is coal, and above it sand. In recent versions of the game you can use a special workbench from six cells to make a glass window.

After making the door. Material for the door is suitable almost any. But if our house is made of wood, it is better to make the door of this material.

The last step is the roof. It can be anything, depending on your imagination.

Often experienced players first make a small outbuilding, then build on it. We can add a few rooms, and then the second floor, and we can build a small cellar from below. Later we can build a new house made of stone.

## Building other houses

After we have built a beautiful house made of wood we can consider other options on how to build beautiful houses in Minecraft from other materials.

### Earth cave

Builds in a few minutes. This is a great option if the night catches you away from home. You need to dig a cave at least three blocks down and close the hole up with opaque glass. You can try to find a ready-made cave on the map and just equip it a little bit. Then it will be a great shelter at the time of the construction of this house.

### House of stone

This type of house is the most common in Minecraft. All because it is the most practical and reliable. To build it you need to have some experience. The very construction is not difficult and takes no more than 10 minutes, you only need to know how to obtain the necessary materials.

Stone construction is superior to the wooden fireproof. The main advantage of this house is that later it can be turned into a mechanical house. It can be built approximately the same way as a wooden one, but using stone instead of wood.
