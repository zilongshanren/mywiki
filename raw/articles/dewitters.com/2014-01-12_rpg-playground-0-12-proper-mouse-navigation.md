Time for a small update of your [favorite RPG creation tool](http://rpgplayground.com). The most important fix is that you can now control the hero properly with the mouse. I implemented A* pathfinding for this. So now he moves to the place where you clicked, not in the direction of the click.

Other things also improved:

- NPC’s say “…” by default.
- Tilemap and entity editing are now completely separated.
- Fixed a nasty glitch where the grass is gone, and images are copied all over the place.
- Better logging on the client side, to discover bugs.

Have fun!
