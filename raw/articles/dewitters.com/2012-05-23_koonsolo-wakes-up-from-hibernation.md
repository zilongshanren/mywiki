It’s been a very long time since I updated anything related to Koonsolo. Having a demanding full-time job, 2 young kids and building a house, doesn’t leave much time to work on spare time projects. But since the kids are getting a bit more independent, and we finally moved into our new home, I managed to squeeze some Koonsolo time back into my schedule. Oh yes, that’s right, you can expect more to come soon!

I left off working on the RPG Editor, and I will pick up from there. Although there is a slight shift in focus: It will be an online web RPG that you can edit on the fly. This means that all editing and playing will happen in your browser. And since I love my new android tablet, I will also support easy RPG editing on tablets. More info on this will definitely follow.

I’m glad to be back! 🙂
