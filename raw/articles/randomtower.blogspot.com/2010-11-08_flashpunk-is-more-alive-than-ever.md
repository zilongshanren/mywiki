[Flashpunk](http://flashpunk.net/)is a really good library to develop games (see my

[AirHeat](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2010/11/airheat-out.html)and

[Zombie Employee](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2010/04/zombie-employee-v05.html)) with flash,

[I've talked a lot before](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/search/label/framework)and develop some games with it:

- Flashpunk

[useful classes](https://github.com/RichardMarks/FPX): sitelock, show static and moveable messages, starfield. A good idea with lot of potential, works fine with 1.4+ version of Flashpunk- Flashpunk

[JRRPG Engine](https://github.com/dolgion1/FlashPunk-JRPG-Engine): impressive engine to build in simple way games like Final Fantasy 6 and Lufia. There are some useful[on-going blog tutorial](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/search/label/framework), to understand concept behind it.- Flashpunk

[MicroRPG Engine](https://github.com/RichardMarks/MicroRPG): I've not try it, but seems to be a good starting point to developm a little rpg :D- Flashpunk/Box2d

[integration for physics](https://github.com/pdyxs/Box2FP): do you want physics in your game? Come here! With great[Box2d](http://www.box2d.org/)behind scenes, with good performance and scalability.- Flashpunk

[MMORPG Engine](https://github.com/prsolucoes/evangel-playerio): a massive work, just try it! Actionscript 3+[PlayerIO](http://playerio.com/)works together to help you in developing new great games,- Flashpunk

[Advanced Platform Engine](http://flashpunk.net/forums/index.php?topic=738.0): my favorite fp-side project. A powerful set of classes to develop platforms games with flashDo you want to develop with Flash? Try Flashpunk :D

Have you thought about porting APE to Slick and Marte Engine? Should be fun and a great test run for Marte Engine and Slick ;-)
