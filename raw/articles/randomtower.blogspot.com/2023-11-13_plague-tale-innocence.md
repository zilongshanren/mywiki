Not only Plague tale: innocence is a good game, has a good story and is enjoyable.

Is not perfect, is not too much innovative (even if the swarm of rats are terrifying to see on screen).

It's a good mix of all of this and centered around the relation between a sister and a brother. Something really valuable. A message so powerful can be viewed as "eternal". We are not only a family, we choosed to be a family and help each other.

No matter what.

Go and play it, worth your time!
