I don't play too often games where there are no conflicts, no strategy, not even a clear "enemy".

But this game surprise me, and no image can explain how much good is this game, in terms of narrative, immersion and story.

Not a single word of a story that you as player has to understand: the fox is the character, but player is also part of the game, working on the backstory, understanding the story.

I've found this game easy to play, with okay graphics, but more important it's really, really relaxing and let you think while you play. It's like a friend, you can share and listen, laught and cry, yell and play.

Worth play, it will catch you!
