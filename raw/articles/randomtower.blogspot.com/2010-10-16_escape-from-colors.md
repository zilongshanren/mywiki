"In a world of squares only one uncolored square could survive against colorful squares: be unique, be different and survive!"

Use arrows or WASD keys to move and try to avoid colorful squares as soon it's possible! Press R to restart..

Keep one eye on strage messages on screen, there is a story behind..

I've post some screenshot of Escape from colors before (

[here](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2009/11/escape-from-colors-screenshots.html)and

[here](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2010/01/escape-from-colors-screenshots-again.html)), and as you can see I used this little game for

[Toadfrogs' "Escape" Contest on Gamejolt](http://gamejolt.com/community/forums/topics/toadfrogs-escape-contest/652/): this is not my best game, but I like it anyway.
