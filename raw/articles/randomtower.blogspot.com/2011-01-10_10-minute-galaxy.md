10 Minute Galaxy is a fast-browser-based game that you must play: started as

[Ludum Dare 19 entry](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-19/?action=preview&uid=2925), now his creator have made and extended version.

Rules are simple (but no tutorial are in so, be patient and read): you have one ship to move using mouse try to explor all galaxy. Every planet you visit give you resources you can use to upgrade your ship and your attack firepower, because there are enemies around!

Game is simple as soon you don't make stupid moves and have a random generator for planets, so every game is different.

Beauty of this game is simple design, rules and scan idea: let player decide to see before do is a key point for every game, but here is a point of gameplay, so player must decide what to do and when :D

[Try now](http://vacuumflowers.com/10MinuteGalaxyLatest/10MinuteGalaxy)!
