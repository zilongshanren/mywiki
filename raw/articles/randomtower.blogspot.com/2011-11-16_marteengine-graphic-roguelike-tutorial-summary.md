In this new series of tutorial my plan is to design, develop and share with anyone my experience in building a

[roguelike](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike)with

[MarteEngine](http://github.com/Gornova/MarteEngine),

I will use this post as summary for all the series, for now I have planned this tutorials for:

[Project setup, HelloWorld, distribution](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2011/11/marte-engine-graphic-rouge-like.html)[Resources, Menu and Game worlds, Hero and Walls](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2011/11/marte-engine-graphic-rouge-like_30.html)[Camera and random caves](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/03/marte-engine-graphic-rouge-like.html)[Creature and CreatureAi, Hero and digging](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/04/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like.html)[Stationary monsters!](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/04/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like_11.html)[Hitpoints, combat, messages](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/04/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like_18.html)[More levels](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/04/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like_25.html)[Concept, Line, Field of view, Fog of war](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/05/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like.html)[Wandering monsters!](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/05/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like_09.html)[Items, inventory and inventory screen](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/05/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like_16.html)[Hunger and food](http://randomtower.blogspot.it/2012/05/marte-engine-graphic-rogue-like_23.html)

FAQS

**Why this series?**

Because I've learned so much from

[Trystan's series](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2011/09/trystans-rougelike-tutorials.html)and I want to expand his work using my library, MarteEngine

**Why use Java, Slick, MarteEngine and not another language or game development library?**

Because I've a lot of experience with it and I think this is a great learning experience



**Your english is n00b!**

I know that, but most parte of this series will be code, so anyone can follow me!



**I like code, where is it?**

Any steps of this series will have a downloadable content

**Why game development?**

Because force programmer to think. Develop a game is not a easy task and in my experience you can learn a lot and adapt in another part of your professional life and .. wow, you can play a game in the end!

**Why roguelike, try a 3d mmorpg instead!**

I'm not a professionist in game development and I haven't so much time. You must always remembeer this: it's better use my time in develop (and learn) from a little project that starts a big project will never ends!
