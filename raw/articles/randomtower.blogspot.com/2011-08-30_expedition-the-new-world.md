One of the most impressive things of rougelike is that they are complex, very complex, but fun., very fun!

This time my review is for

[Expedition: the new world](http://slashware.net/blog/expedition-the-new-world/). In this game player have some ship, Atlantic ocean to cross and a new world to conquer!

Graphic is simple but effective and find the right path through ocean is not so easy. Beauty in this game is explore, find new places, but there are also some problems: you cannot speaks with natives and what you can do is "only" establish come colonies and continue in your exploration.

Keep eyes on this little gems because have many interesting ideas!
