You know, games are strange. So strange that sometime one guy from a contest could make a great game.

[Man in the Mirror](http://www.users.on.net/%7Erossn/nreactornew/mirror.html)is one of that game.

You are insane ... like Batman's Joker? I don't know. But there is someone else in your mind, a "demon". Two minds and two worlds.. in one there are evil creatures.. but into "real world" there are only guards and civilians.. and only a little mirror keep away demon from "real" world!

Words cannot explain this little perl, made in only 7 days for

[7DRL](http://roguebasin.roguelikedevelopment.org/index.php?title=7DRL_Contest_2011)... and made with

[Slick](http://slick.cokeandcode.com/)!

A part lack of music and sfx, it's a really little and incredible game.. just keep in mind that is a rougelike, so press F1 and use your keyboard!
