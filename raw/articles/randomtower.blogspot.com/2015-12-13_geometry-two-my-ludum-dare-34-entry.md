Game is really simple: you have to control a space station growing into space and fighting with enemies. You have the power to get smaller!

How many levels can you do ?

![](../../assets/3812d3a99f9010b2.png)


![](../../assets/3812d3a99f9010b2.png)

Controls:

- Left mouse click: attack

- Right mouse click: get smaller

Good luck! I'm glad for any feedback and in particular.. your maximum level :D
