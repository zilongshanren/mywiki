Save trees in desert, throw barrells full of water on dead tree and bring them back to life!

Airheat is my new game made with

[Flashpunk](http://flashpunk.net/)for

[Heat Contest on Gamejolt forum](http://gamejolt.com/community/forums/topics/heat-contest/664/): made in 4 days, is really "casual", one play and go. But I've decided to build it to think about game mechanics and prove how simple is to build a videogame.

I've also used

[Ogmo editor](http://ogmoeditor.com/)to build levels: nice to use!

It's not perfect but I really enjoyed to build it, in particular design gameplay: decide what barrells have to do, storm and so on :D

[Play on Kongregate](http://www.kongregate.com/games/Gornova81/airheat)- and challenge your friend with Highscore!
