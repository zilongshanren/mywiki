A zombie employee wants to escape from a bad boss and cumbersome and tedious tasks. Do you wanna to help him?

Following

[this tutorial](http://flashpunk.net/forums/index.php?topic=42)from Drazzke (thanks man!) i was able to build a little platformer with

[Flashpunk](http://flashpunk.net).

My idea is to

[follow my online-design experiment](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2009/12/game-design-approach-online-mindmap-3.html)and give some feedback from my readers!

Just click on image to try it!


![](//lh3.ggpht.com/_D9FyA78ffcA/S17e2YV7n5I/AAAAAAAAAUU/dVtHn9R99po/flashpunkPlatformer01.PNG)



It's a just a demo, like

[TonyPa Platformer](http://randomtower.blogspot.com/2009/10/tonypa-platformer.html)with Java or

[Flashpunk Hello World Shooter](http://gamejolt.com/online/games/shooter/flashpunk-hello-world-shooter/1416/)(, but is fun see things moving on screen quickly!

[Gimp](http://www.gimp.org/)it's easy and free draw a little sprite of a strange zombie-like employee jumping around :D
