For this cast, I spoke with Derek and Clint of Thorium to discuss the roguelike Undermine that is out now.

We started with talking about their backgrounds at Relic before moving to the indie space. Designing an action roguelike is not easy, and we spent the bulk of our time going over the balancing and gameplay of Undermine.
