For this part, things got very painful for my party as it turns out my A-Squad wasn’t up to snuff to deal with veteran missions. Lots of deaths peppered this play through including a few stupid mistakes by me and a very dangerous second boss fight. The big point about this run is that you really can’t start caring about your people until they are at least level 3 as it’s just too costly in the beginning to keep someone in high spirits.
