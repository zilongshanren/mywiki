This week on the cast, I sat down with Andrew and Thomas from Pixelated Milk who are finishing up their first project Regalia of Men and Monarchs.


After introductions we talked about the development of Regalia. The team wanted to put their own spin on RPG design and mix things up compared to traditional games. One of the unique aspects of the game would be the chapter structure and we discussed how they designed the winning conditions. We also spent some time talking about how the art of the game came about.
