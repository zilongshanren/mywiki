There’s no telling how many games have been created so far. Then again there won’t be an exact number any time soon as many more are on the way. However, the industry has produced a couple of titles that have gathered a mass of players. Some of them might be newer while others are a bit older. Nevertheless, these are online and offline titles that have millions of players and in their essence, they’re amazing games. In that regard, here are some examples.


**DOTA 2**

DOTA or Defense of the Ancients is a game that started as a map on Warcraft III. It took some of the characters in the game and made Ancients or heroes of them. Each one has distinct abilities and belongs to a different class of heroes. Some of them are more strength oriented while others are more agile or more intelligent.

Either way, this map evolved into DOTA and eventually into DOTA 2. The goal of the game is simple. You need to pick a hero and together with your team, destroy the opposing team’s base. You’ll level up throughout the game and the higher your level is the easier the game will be.

**League of Legends**

This is a game that’s similar to DOTA 2. The concept is pretty much the same, but the design is vastly different. Also, the selection of heroes differs significantly as does the look of the maps. It’s a game that came out in 2009 and by today it has a mass of players from all over the world. The players are divided into ranks and the more games you win in League of Legends the better you’ll become and the better your competition will be.

**Hitman**

The Hitman franchise has been alive and kicking since the 2000s. The bald and deadly assassin called Agent 47 has become an icon of gaming and each title presents a benchmark for stealth games. Hitman has been moving with the trends and the graphics and gameplay have improved significantly as a result.

The current Hitman game came out in parts and [Hitman III](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/hitman-3-review/) was released in January 2021. It was an episodic approach to the franchise and the way you could go about the missions had evolved. The freedom of your choices has improved substantially.

**The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim**

Skyrim is a game that came out in 2011 and it has millions of players to this day. That’s because it offers a superb story and excellent side quests. You also get to create your character and pick out a class for it. Finally, you can mod the game however you want which is why gamers won’t stop playing Skyrim any time soon. In other words, it’s a masterpiece.
