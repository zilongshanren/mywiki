Do you feel as though your gaming ability isn’t as good as it could be? If so then now is the time for you to do something about that. This guide will show you what steps you can take to try and become a better player overall, so you can have more fun with your friends while gaining confidence along the way.


**Break up your Day**

It’s all very well gearing up for battle, especially if you are having an all-day session with your friends. That being said, burnout is a big deal. You need to take regular breaks from anything that is causing cognitive stress. This allows your brain to unpack itself and it also allows you to process everything that you have learnt. When you have to balance team play with your individual play, you may get to the point where you end up practicing too much. To combat this, you need to try and take a more relaxed approach. Fatigue of the mind is something that’s very real so you need to make sure that you don’t play too long before you change things up a little. Weaker teams will lose focus and start overthinking things, so make sure that you are keeping your team nice and strong by having regular breaks when you need to. If you need a break then you can always play a simpler, or less stressful game such as [winter solitaire](https://www.wintersolitaire.com/).

**Mentor Someone New**

If you want to become a better player then one thing you can do is find the Luke to your Yoda. Find someone who isn’t as good at gaming as you and then teach them how to become better. This is called the [protege effect](https://blog.innerdrive.co.uk/the-protege-effect#:~:text=The%20Prot%C3%A9g%C3%A9%20Effect%20is%20the,others%2C%20it%20reinforces%20their%20understanding.). If you can adopt this then you will soon find that you become a better player by simply tutoring other people. The best thing about doing this is that it will help you to recall things and you will also be forced to apply new things. Another fresh pair of eyes may even help you to highlight the weaknesses within your strategy which is great. It will force you to think about the aspects of an outcome and what you could have done differently.

**Open a Window**

Nature is one of the easiest ways for you to restore your attention. Even if you cannot get up to walk around, the simple act of cracking open your window can give you a boost of natural light. At the end of the day, your body expects to get Vitamin D during the day and if you aren’t getting any then this can be very detrimental overall. You may feel drained and you may also find that you are not able to react as quickly as you could. This can also give you the fresh air you need so be mindful of that.

**Analyze your Play**

Streaming services are huge right now and highlight reels are available in nearly every single [game](https://game-wisdom.com/general/five-reasons-stop-playing-bad-games-dont-like) out there. Watching your gameplay back is now easier than ever. With that in mind, if you are not actively trying to better yourself then this could work against you. Go back and rewatch your performance, as your brain will still be processing what happened and it will also need time to log any changes that have to be made.
