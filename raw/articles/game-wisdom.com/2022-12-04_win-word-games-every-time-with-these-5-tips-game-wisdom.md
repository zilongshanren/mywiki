Word games are a great way to stay sharp and challenge your vocabulary. They also come with an added benefit: They’re a great way to meet people! Whether you’re playing with a group of friends or family, word games are ideal for stimulating the social part of your brain. But that doesn’t mean they will be easy—the key is in approaching them with a positive mindset and self-confidence. Here are some tips on how to win at word games every time:


## Play To Your Strengths

When you go to pick a word game, pay attention to what your strengths are as a player. For example, if you have a good vocabulary, you’re more likely to take home the victory at Scrabble. If you’re great at math, you could play a strategy game that uses math rules on top of the word game to gain an advantage over your opponents. A good way to find out what your strengths are as a player is to [try many word games](https://www.dailywritingtips.com/list-50-word-games/).

## Be Patient and Keep Questioning

While playing, be patient and keep trying to find the best answer. It’s easy to get frustrated and impatient when you’re losing, but this will get you nowhere. Even if you feel stuck or think you have no winning moves, keep looking! There always might be a word you missed or a phrase you didn’t think of when you’re playing a word game. Not getting frustrated and continuing to question will help you win.

## Use Your Resources

You may not be able to use resources during the game, but there are great resources that can help you improve your skills before and after the game. For example, you can keep track of your letters throughout the game, then enter them into a [word unscrambler](https://www.wordunscrambler.net/) after the game to see if there were any words you missed. You can also ask the people playing with you for tips after the game to get better.

## Build Your Vocabulary In Other Ways

Word games can be a great way to build up your vocabulary, but they should not be your only source of expanding your vocabulary. There are many [other ways to build your vocabulary](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/how-to-improve-your-vocabulary) so that you can win word games every time! You can read a book, listen to a podcast, or watch TV. You can also go through a dictionary or find resources made especially to teach you unique words that you might not have another opportunity to learn.

## Play With Friends

Word games can be a lot of fun when you play with a group of friends, and they can also be a great way to meet new people. For example, if you’re playing a word game with math rules, you can invite people who don’t normally play your game to join your team. This way, you can meet new people while playing with people who you know. You can also invite your [kids](https://game-wisdom.com/podcast/teaching-kids-games) to play with you to teach them more vocabulary words!

Word games are great ways to challenge your vocabulary, meet new people, and build friendships. They can also be a good way to build up your vocabulary and have fun! If you don’t think you’re good at word games, it just takes a little time to get better. With these tips, you’ll be winning word games in no time!
