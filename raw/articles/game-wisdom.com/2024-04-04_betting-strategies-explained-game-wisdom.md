**What are Betting Strategies?**

The first question on most people’s mind would be; “What is a betting strategy?” As some of you may have already figured it out, a betting strategy is a plan focusing on the act of wagering cash, rather than on a gameplay plan.

Professional gamblers throughout the years have figured out that casino games are largely games of chance. That means, no amount of planning can alter the outcome. But, if one were to plan out how and when to wager what amount of cash, one could maximize a win, or at least minimize a loss.

It is important to note that betting strategies are not created to alter the outcome of a game. Indeed, as we’ve repeated throughout the article, it is impossible to alter the outcome of most casino games. With that in mind, let’s look at some of the most popular betting strategies.

**Progressive Betting Strategies**

Without a doubt, the most popular betting strategy category is the progressive betting strategy. As the name suggests, a progressive betting strategy relies on progressively increasing or decreasing the amount you’ve wagered.

Most gamblers who use progressive betting strategies, tend to focus on the ones in which the player progressively increases their bet. The idea here is that, in the case of an eventual win, the player would not only earn back the cash they’ve lost, but also a little something extra.

**Martingale Strategy**

The Martingale strategy is the most popular progressive betting strategy used by gamblers in the 21st century. While incredibly popular today, the Martingale strategy is also one of the oldest. Gamblers have been using it since the 19th century, which points to its effectiveness.

In essence, the Martingale system is quite simple; players start the wager with a pretty low bet, and then simply double it each time they don’t win. In the case of an eventual victory, the player earns back the cash they’ve lost, and also a bit of extra cash.

**Other Strategies**

Apart from Martingale, the most popular progressive betting strategy is the Fibonacci system. Named after the [mathematical sequence](https://www.techtarget.com/whatis/definition/Fibonacci-sequence) of the same name, the Fibonacci largely is based on betting to the pattern of the Fibonacci sequence.

For those who want to try something new, there is the reverse-martingale. As the name suggests, The Reverse-Martingale demands that a gambler starts with a higher sum, and then reduce the wager whenever they lose, and double it when they win.
