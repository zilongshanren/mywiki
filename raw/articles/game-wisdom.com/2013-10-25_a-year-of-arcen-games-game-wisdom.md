This week on the Perceptive podcast, owner and lead designer for Arcen Games: Chris Park joined me to discuss how 2013 fared for his company.

We started talking about how Arcen’s focus on smaller, more focused design has helped them ship six different products for 2013 and how that has effected Arcen both from a business and design standpoint. We also talked about the importance of when to have game sales for your titles.

Moving on we discussed his latest game — Bionic Dues, from design to talking about how classic games influenced certain aspects of it. Lastly we spoke briefly on Arcen’s next game, due out early 2014 : *The Last Federation*

We had some technical issues this week, apparently my mic picked up a lot more of my heavy breathing. And when I edited most of it out, it lowered the overall volume of my voice, but I figured that it’s more interesting to hear what Chris has to say.
