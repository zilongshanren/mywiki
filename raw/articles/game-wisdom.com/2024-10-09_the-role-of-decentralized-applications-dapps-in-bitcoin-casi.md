**What are Decentralized Applications (dApps)?**

Decentralized applications, or dApps, are digital applications or programs that run on a blockchain or peer-to-peer network instead of a centralized server. Unlike traditional apps, dApps are not controlled by a single authority or company. Instead, they leverage blockchain technology to ensure that every transaction is transparent, immutable, and auditable.

In the context of online casinos, dApps allow players to access games and features that are verifiably fair, meaning that no third party can manipulate or tamper with outcomes. This level of transparency is one of the key advantages that Bitcoin casinos offer compared to traditional online casinos.
