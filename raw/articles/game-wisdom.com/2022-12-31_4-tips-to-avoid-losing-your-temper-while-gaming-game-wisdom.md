It’s common for gamers to feel bad after a loss. Feeling bad is absolutely nothing wrong, but you shouldn’t be tempted to lose control of yourself. In fact, you’re doing more harm than good when this happens. When your game, it’s important to[ improve your experience](https://game-wisdom.com/general/how-to-improve-your-gaming-experience) and allow this free time to be enjoyable. So, here are some tips to help you stay calm while you play.


**Game in moderation**

Is the anger you’re getting mostly coming from gaming? In this case, it may be a good idea to just game in moderation. Games can sometimes cause anger. Some games are loved and praised for rage-baiting. Either play these games in moderation, or at least just don’t play any games as much if you notice your mood is shifting.

**Train your temper**

If you are an avid gamer, you may have noticed that playing your favorite games can sometimes make you lose your temper. The difficulty of the game, the content of the game, and the dynamics of the game can all contribute to angry outbursts. But the good news is that you can train your temper while gaming. With the right tools, you can control the rage and calm down without resorting to punishment.

One of the easiest ways to control your anger while playing a video game is to take deep breaths. You can do this by holding your breath for three seconds and then releasing it. This will help you calm down and focus on numbers instead of being angry. Remember that this is just a game; games are meant to be fun and relaxing, not anger-inducing. You can even consider[ anger management](https://www.betterhelp.com/) if you feel like it’s getting out of hand.

**Understand that anger is normal but needs to be controlled**

Learning to control your anger will help you enjoy your gaming experience and reduce your stress. Ultimately, rage reactions while gaming is something everyone must learn to manage. Whether you are a child or an adult, it is best to take a break and consider other aspects of your life if you become upset about a game. Getting angry is a natural reaction but not the most enjoyable. When you rage, it is important to stop and consider the situation so that you can avoid acting out in a way that might hurt your friends and family.

**Don’t rage-quit**

Rage quitting is also common, especially in team-based multiplayer games. These games are designed to encourage player cooperation, but when a rage quitter causes the game to end, it is often viewed as bad sportsmanship. But it’s still important to try your best to avoid rage-quitting. You’ll still be mad and potentially turn hostile if you quit due to rage. Sometimes, it’s not the game but the people you’re playing with or the server you’re on. If you’re playing Minecraft and you just have a terrible experience, then instead quit playing it (calmly) or instead Look into the[ Minecraft survival server list](https://minecraftbestservers.com/minecraft-survival-servers), for a server that may be less anger-inducing.

The best way to avoid rage-quitting is not to let your ego get the best of you. Instead, take time to enjoy yourself and your friends. Also, ensure that you do not allow a game to define you. If you find that your game skills are slipping, take a break to recover. There are several things you can do to prevent rage-quitting while gaming. You can take breaks to relax, pause to recharge or play other games.
