This week on the cast, Chris Bourassa of Redhook Games stopped by for a post mortem talk about developing the Crimson Curse DLC for the Darkest Dungeon.


We jumped right into our talk on the DLC, with Chris going into detail about where the design came from and what they wanted to add to the game with it. I asked him about balancing the new additions and class. We avoided spoilers on the story, but we did talk about the new enemies and challenges that were added in.
