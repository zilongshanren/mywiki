It’s easy to get lost in the sea of incredible game titles that have come out in the last couple of years. With tech advancing rapidly, and gaming becoming one of the most popular hobbies worldwide, we’ve been seeing some incredible innovation when it comes to the field of gaming, and great improvements in the overall gameplay of what’s out there. However, this brings with it some struggles to gamers on a budget who can’t afford the expensive equipment needed to run these masterpieces. If you find yourself struggling with this issue, here are some alternatives you can try.

As we have in the past, we keep underestimating the entertainment potential that comes with these fun mini-games. Browser games may seem like small fry when compared to the giants that have come after them but they still have a lot to offer and can be loads of fun if you give them a try. From classics like poker and blackjack to hundreds of slots inspired by pop culture, you’ll surely find something that suits your tastes. If casino games aren’t your thing though, you can always turn to websites that host retro arcade games. Taking a trip down memory lane could be exactly the kind of fun you’re looking for.

**Older Single-Players**

No matter how big of a gamer you consider yourself, there are always a couple of excellent titles throughout the years that you might have missed. While online multiplayer games have taken over the world of gaming as top dogs, there’s nothing quite like a good single-player game that immerses you in the story and has you hooked with its gameplay.

Do some thorough exploring on the web and see if there’s something interesting you haven’t played. Some popular older titles like the first games in the Batman: Arkham Series, Assassins Creed, and Devil May Cry 3 are great options if you’re a fan of some fast-paced action, that doesn’t require a gaming beast of a PC.

**Indie Games**

When they’re being pushed out by the thousands yearly, indie games might not seem as original as they once did, yet this can be a good thing for gamers playing on budget PC’s as these games don’t usually require much on the power front. Fans of the strange and unusual will no doubt have a blast exploring the mystical worlds that indie games often depict.

Indie games mainly focus on the story and visuals, so you should buckle in and get ready for [a wild ride](https://www.thegamer.com/indie-games-underrated-better-than-triple-a/) when choosing to play some of these. While the gameplay might seem simple, there’s usually a lot more than meets the eye, and some recent titles such as Katana Zero, Oxenfree, and Owlboy are the perfect proof.
