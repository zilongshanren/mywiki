Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality are changing the face of gaming. **Evolving Consumer Preferences**

**Mobile Gaming**

The shift to mobile gaming continues to grow. Gamers are relishing the convenience and ease of playing their favourite casino games on the go via smartphones and tablets. It has now become a necessity for a casino operator to have a mobile platform or app. The increasing Internet bandwidth and penetration of sophisticated mobile devices continue to drive the growth trend in mobile gaming.
