When it comes to first-person shooters, we all have our preferences. *Rainbow*, *Fortnite*, *Halo* – everyone has a preferred FPS.

But when a new kid hits the block, it’s easy to get interested, and even a little bit excited about the latest release. While many try and fail, there’s a new game in town that’s grabbing the attention of even the most devoted gamers – *Valorant*.

Still in its BETA stage, *Valorant *is already luring gamers away from their much-loved FPSs and is already earning some big fans.


## What is Valorant?

*Valorant *is the latest first-person shooter on the horizon. Developed by Riot Games for Microsoft, it will be a free to play online game, and is set to be rolled out widely this summer.

From April this year, *Valorant *was launched as a closed-access BETA via Twitch. Those who have been lucky enough to access the game so far have been giving it rave reviews, with [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/2020/3/2/21158401/valorant-how-riot-games-made-something-new)’s Austen Goslin describing it as “one of the most fun tactical shooters I’ve played”. It’s proving difficult to access for many, which is making the appetite for it even greater.

Set in the not-so-distant future, *Valorant* is a tactical/first-person shooter game where players play as multiple characters from all over the world. Players are given the choice to play as agents on either attacking or defensive teams. In the game, the attacking team have to work to plant a bomb, named ‘the Spike’. If they are able to protect it within the 100-second time limit, they keep the point, while the defensive team will earn the point for defusing the bomb successfully. Teams will also earn points from eliminating members of the other team. There are 24 rounds in total, and teams will switch sides after 12 rounds. The team who win the best of 24 games wins the overall match.

*Valorant* has a simple concept, but it’s the cast of characters, choice of weapons and settings that make it an exciting new contender in the world of FPS.

## How can you access the closed BETA?

If you’re already intrigued by *Valorant*, you’re probably wondering how you can start to play it? To be among the first to access the BETA, you’ll need to sign up to Riot and connect your account to your Twitch account. You’ll need to watch *Valorant* streams to be in with a shot of getting access to the game via a BETA key.

While this might sound simple, many people have been trying to access a BETA key for weeks with no luck. Judging by recent streaming figures (which could also be down to coronavirus), there is a lot of demand for *Valorant*. So [how do you get your Valorant access key](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-get-your-valorant-beta-key-watching-twitch-/1100-6475366/)?

At first, the codes were only available for certain, selected streamers to issue to their viewers. This has now been changed so that all streamers who have access to the BETA are able to distribute access.

Viewers must watch at least two hours of streams to be eligible to receive a code. Your odds increase as the number of hours watched goes up. There are also some other ways to be rewarded with a code, including being highly engaged with those streams.

However, there are no guarantees for being able to access the streams, as explained in Riot’s [post](https://beta.playvalorant.com/en-us/news/announcements/day-1-closed-beta-in-eu-na-and-moving-forward/), so you’ll simply need to be patient and wait.

## How does it compare to other FPSs?

When a new first-person shooter is released, it’s easy to start making comparisons with what’s already out there. Some articles are describing *Valorant* as a [‘ Rainbow Six/CS:Go mashup’](https://www.polygon.com/2020/3/2/21156352/riot-valorant-project-a-shooter-csgo-overwatch-rainbow-six), with fast gameplay and only one life to play with, it’s an action-packed game that will suit many gaming styles. While things might seem fairly calm at first, things become more hectic as players start getting eliminated and the counter starts counting down.

The gameplay is also smart, which makes things a little more interesting and ends some of the possible frustration from your turn ending early. Bringing together the best out of popular shooter games, it seems that *Valorant* is the change that many have been hoping for.
