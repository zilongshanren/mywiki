For this week’s Perceptive Podcast, I sat down with Vertigo Games’ David Galindo to talk about the Cook Serve Delicious series became a surprise hit, and his thoughts on taking things further with the Barista update.


We started off talking about David’s background when it comes to game design, and his previous titles before Cook Serve Delicious blew up. From there, we talked about the balance and design of it, and the plans for the Barista update.
