For this cast, I spoke with Glen of the studio Sprite Wrench to talk about his first game Grimm and Tonic, and what it’s like to be a game designer in a third world country.


We talked about Glen’s background with learning game design and what the game dev scene is like in Jamaica. For the second part of our cast, we spoke about him developing Grimm and Tonic, storytelling in video games, and his plans for the game.
