Casinos set maximum bet limits when you play with bonus funds. The typical cap sits around $5 per spin or $0.50 per bet line. Exceeding this amount, even accidentally, can void your bonus and all winnings attached to it. Check the terms before placing any bet.

Deposit match bonuses sometimes carry withdrawal restrictions, too. Read the terms for any mention of maximum withdrawal amounts tied to bonus winnings.
