This week was a special podcast as Ken, returning guest Lorson Poirier and I celebrated the two year anniversary of Game-Wisdom.


Instead of a regular cast schedule, we decided to talk about the past, present and future of the site. This also gave me the chance to talk a bit about the behind the scenes of what’s been going on with myself and keeping the site going over the last two years. We also talked about some of our favorite guests, podcasts and more. And it wouldn’t be a cast looking back at Game-Wisdom without talking at length about Ken’s Steam trading card addiction.

And as I mentioned on the cast, we are looking for fan questions that you would like us to talk about on an upcoming cast. Thanks for everyone who reads the site and listens to these casts and I hope that we continue to grow over 2015.
