2012 is coming to an end and that means it’s time to discuss the best games of the year. Continuing my tradition from my previous blog: it’s time for a top ten list.


As always the conditions for a game making the list are: The game must have been released in the current year and ports of games or DLC are ineligible (sorry Dark Souls).

As with my old blog, only games that I’ve played will be considered. For those wanting a specific game to be picked, there’s a pay pal link to your right, fair warning that all bribes take at least 50 years to be considered.
