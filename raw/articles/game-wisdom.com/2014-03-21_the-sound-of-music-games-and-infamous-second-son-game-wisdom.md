This week, Ken and I were joined by freelance composer Danny Barkley to help us talk about composing music for games with composer Marc Canham, a veteran composer who recently did the soundtrack for *Infamous Second Son* as well as *The Secret World*.


Despite me being back to my old headset while I wait for a new one to get here, we had a great cast. We began with introductions as Danny and Marc talked about their backgrounds. We had a very interesting discussion on the differences between composing music for video games and movies.

With Ken and I not versed in the area of music, Danny took the lead to ask Marc some specific questions about how he composes music. Finally we spoke about Marc’s work on the *Infamous Second Son* soundtrack.
