**Understanding the Basics: Random Number Generators (RNGs)**

At the heart of online slot machines and all other computer-generated online casino games lies a technology called Random Number Generators (RNGs). These are sophisticated mathematical algorithm programs designed to produce unpredictable and unbiased results/outcomes with no discernible patterns.

Here is a quick look at the fundamentals of how the technology works:

– **Random Outcome Generation**: RNGs constantly generate numbers at an incredibly rapid pace, even when the game is not in active play.

– **Game Result Determination**: When a player spins the reels, the RNG halts at a particular moment, determining the outcome based on the generated number.

– **Advancement Over Time**: The latest RNG technology ensures that the numbers produced are not only random but also adhere to a set standard of fairness.

Despite being pre-determined, the outcomes remain fair. They are often programmed to pay back between 93.00% to 98.00% for every C$1.00 wagered. The payback percentages vary depending on the specific game and can significantly influence player experiences.
