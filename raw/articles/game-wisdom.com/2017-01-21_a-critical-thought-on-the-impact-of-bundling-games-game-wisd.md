The bundle promotions have become another facet of selling games and the subject for today’s Critical Thought. I talked about the Humble Bundle and what it means as a developer when you want to put your game up. While it can earn you a lot of fans, this is definitely something that you don’t do if you’re looking to earn a profit on your game.
