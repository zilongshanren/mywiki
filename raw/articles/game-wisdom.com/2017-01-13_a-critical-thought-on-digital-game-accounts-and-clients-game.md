Today’s Critical Thought is about the use of digital game accounts thanks to the rise of Steam. Despite Steam’s popularity, it hasn’t stopped major publishers and smaller studios from wanting to set up accounts to play their games. I talked about why consumers prefer one unified account and what this will mean going forward hopefully with the Nintendo Switch.
