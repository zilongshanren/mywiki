This week we welcomed from Blue Manchu, designer and writer on the upcoming game *Card Hunter*: Joe Mcdonagh to talk about, game development. the industry and about *Card Hunter*.


This may be one of my favorite casts we’ve done to date and the longest as we went on more random tangents than ever before. From talking about the game industry, Joe’s worse game pitch of all time and more that I can’t remember. We also pitch what could be the next greatest kickstarter to Joe and I expect a credit mention if that ever happens.

And besides all that, we talked about *Card Hunter* (starting at around 50 minutes in): From design questions, game balance and what Blue Manchu has in store after the game is released.
