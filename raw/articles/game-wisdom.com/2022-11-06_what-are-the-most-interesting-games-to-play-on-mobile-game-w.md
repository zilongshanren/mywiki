## Why People Like Mobile Games?

Every year, mobile games become more popular, while the requirements of users for them become higher. Due to this fact, game developers have to work hard in order to satisfy gamers and attract the audience to their products.

There’re many reasons for people to like mobile games:

- There’re many free games, which can be downloaded from Google Play or App Store
- There’re diverse types of games for both amateurs and professional players
- There’re games of different genres
- They are accessible on various devices (compared, for instance, to the console-oriented titles)
- They help relieve stress
- They are accessible anywhere (people can play them on their way to work, in a queue, etc.)
- They allow communicating with other people with the same interests
- They make feel accomplished when a certain task is completed (there might even be leader boards or shareable achievements)
- They are regularly updated, which makes old players return to the game even after they left it
- They usually have very interesting content.

Many experts speak about the benefits of mobile gaming:

- Brain power boost
- Better mood
- More social connections
- Positive influence on health and development (for instance, such games are useful for children with autism)
- The improvement of dexterity
- Improvement of problem-solving skills
- Better concentration
- Development of new skills and knowledge (there’re, for example, games, which teach coding).

## Types of Games

Gamers are an enormous group of people who have different interests and preferences in what concerns mobile games. For the mobile gaming industry, it’s important to cover a wider audience, so the companies create diverse games for mobile devices. First, it’s required to determine two main categories:

- Online games (which require an Internet connection to play)
- Offline games (they can be played even without the Internet).

Each group is represented by a wide variety of genres. Let’s now consider the main ones:

- Action. Such games are focused on physical challenges. They presuppose sharp and fast movements and good reaction. This genre also includes subgenres, such as fighting, shooting, etc.
- Casual. Such products represent the largest share of the mobile gaming market. They are aimed at a mass audience and include products with simple rules and short gaming sessions. In addition, users don’t need special skills to play them. For example, we can speak about puzzles, card games,
[free slot machines](https://www.slotsup.com/free-slots-online), etc. - RPG. Role-playing games are extremely popular among PC and mobile users. Such products are able to create a new world and make a player plunge into the atmosphere & forget about problems. Modern mobile RPGs are quite advanced. They offer amazing gameplay and high-quality graphics, which is close to that of PCs and consoles. In RPG, a gamer chooses a certain character and fulfills diverse quests in accordance with a storyline. Besides, players can explore the large world of the game.
- MOBA. It’s a mixture of a real-time strategy and RPG. The groups of players fight against each other. The game allows developing skills and becoming stronger. And still, it’s necessary to make a plan for how to win the opponents.
- Sports. There’re many players who prefer games devoted to various types of sports. It might become a great alternative to a real-life activity. Such games might be complicated. However, users can gradually make accomplishments.
- Strategies. This genre is also extremely interesting and includes various subcategories. Players can develop armies, take part in combats (either in real time or in turn), seize lands, etc. Such games presuppose planning and tactics.
