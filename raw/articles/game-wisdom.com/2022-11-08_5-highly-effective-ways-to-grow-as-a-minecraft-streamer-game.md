Minecraft is among the world’s most popular games, popular among gamers of all ages. Players create buildings and items using the various resources available in pixelated blocks throughout the game. Despite the game being over a decade old, it retains an active audience numbering in millions.

It is a favored game for streamers as people can watch them and enjoy a wholesome time. This also means that there is already strong competition for you to get yourself out on the scene and promote yourself and your stream. Fortunately, there are a number of things that you can do that can give you the edge and ensure a high viewership.


## Selecting The Right Platform

Twitch is the leading platform when it comes to streams and streamers. There are millions of streamers from all around the globe streaming a variety of content at any given moment. However, in recent times alternatives, such as YouTube Gaming and Facebook gaming, have popped up on the scene.

Each option offers something unique and incentivizes streamers differently, so you have to see which platform appeals to you the most. One platform may be better known but the other may offer better monetization rates. So keep these factors in mind before you start.

## Active Presence On Social Media

An active social media presence is crucial for content creators on the internet, especially streamers. This is where your audience will know about your upcoming streams and will engage with you regarding off-streams to discuss various topics.

As such, you should create accounts and profiles on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Twitter. Facebook and Twitter can be used to offer updates to your fans while Instagram and YouTube can be utilized to share short clips from your streams and Videos on Demand. Montages from your game, highlights, and such can be shared which will make you go viral and bring more people to your streams.

## Interact With Your Audience

As a streamer, you must always engage with your audience and interact with them to make them feel wanted and appreciated. They will be more likely to return and become long-term viewers of your streams.

You can reply to comments in the chat in your streams and give people shout-outs, especially those who give you subs and donate. This will make your streams feel more lively instead of just you talking to your camera as you play your game.

## Collaborate With Established Streamers

A great way to increase your viewership numbers is to collaborate with streamers who are established with bigger audiences. Doing so will bring the streamer’s viewers over to your stream which you will then have a chance to retain. Your collab partner can also benefit as well because they get access to your viewer base in return.

Initially, you probably won’t be able to collab with some of the bigger names in the industry and will only stream alongside others who are only slightly bigger than you. But with time and effort, you will grow and soon will be able to reach out to the big wigs and collaborate with them as well.

Popular streamer and VTuber Gawr Gura [collaborated with another streamer](https://twitter.com/gawrgura/status/1410997965901209607) Takanashi Kiara for a Minecraft stream where they planned on building a particular statue. By collaborating, the viewers for both streamers would overlap. If the viewer for one streamer would also likes the other streamer as well, and vice versa, then both of them would gain new viewers and increase the size of their audience.
