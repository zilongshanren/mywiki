**Dell U2723QE (2022)**


[Dell](https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoinegara/2021/08/03/deal-of-the-century-how-michael-dell-turned-his-declining-pc-business-into-40-billion-windfall/) is one of the most reliable and trustworthy companies when it comes to PC monitors — and the Dell U2723QE ticks all the right boxes. From a beautiful 27-inch 4K display to an HDR color gamut, you can’t ask for much more. Plus, the Dell U2723QE’s current retail price is around $550, which is an absolute bargain for what you get in return.


-
**BenQ PD2725U (2022)**


Don’t be deceived by the simplistic design of the BenQ PD2725U monitor. Underneath the minimalism is a monitor with an ultra-crisp 4K display and plenty of connectivity ports for all of your gaming needs. Not to mention, the bezels are also super-thin, which is always a nice added bonus.


-
**LG 27GN950-B (2020)**


The LG27GN950-B is one of the fastest 4K monitors at 144 Hz around. Designed specifically for gamers, the red and black colors look truly awesome. Make no doubt about it, if you’re looking for a new PC monitor to add to your [gaming setup](https://reviewed.usatoday.com/tech/features/everything-you-need-perfect-gaming-set-up), this is *definitely* one to consider.


-
**Gigabyte M32UC (2022)**


Thanks to its 4K monitor and high refresh rate, the Gigabyte M32UC is currently one of the hottest PC monitors on the market in the $500 to $1,000 price bracket. It currently retails at around $530, which is incredibly cheap when you consider the fact that you get a 1ms response time thanks to the SuperSpeed VA technology in the monitor.


-
**Philips 243B9H (2021)**


Seen as one of the best budget options on the market for both work and gaming, the Philips 243B9H is a respectable PC monitor. It isn’t flash or extravagant — but it gets the job done. The only downside is that the speakers, which are low-standard when compared to other premium monitors on the market. However, if you’re looking for a nice monitor to use when gaming in your free time or working from home, the Philips 243B9H is more than up to the job!
