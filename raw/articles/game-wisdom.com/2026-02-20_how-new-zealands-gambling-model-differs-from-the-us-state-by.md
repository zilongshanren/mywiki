**How Gambling Is Regulated in New Zealand**

New Zealand has established a straightforward regulatory model for online gambling, and it is primarily governed by the Gambling Act of 2003. This legislation is aimed at promoting [responsible gambling](https://responsiblegambling.org/for-the-public/safer-play/safer-gambling-tips/) while providing clear licensing guidelines.

The Department of Internal Affairs (DIA) is the main regulatory authority overseeing all gambling activities, ensuring that players are protected and that operators adhere to strict standards.

Under this framework, only the New Zealand-based TAB (the government-run betting agency) and Lotto New Zealand are allowed to operate legal online gambling operations. These entities offer a range of services, including sports betting and traditional gaming.

As a result, the New Zealand market is relatively stable and transparent, allowing for consistent player experiences.
