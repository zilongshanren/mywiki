## Puzzle Games

Puzzle games such as* Sudoku* or *Tetris* are classic puzzle games for a mental workout. Wordle is another popular option that challenges players to solve problems quickly and efficiently. These types of games improve one’s ability to problem-solve, enhance pattern recognition, and sharpen spatial reasoning.

The added benefit of puzzle games is that they serve as a reflective way to unwind, offering a pleasant balance between simplicity and challenge. Studies have shown that problem-solving games stimulate the [creative and logical thinking parts of the brain](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/could-digital-puzzle-games-help-improve-memory) simultaneously.

## Strategy Games

Strategy games often demand players to be adaptable, plan carefully, and manage their resources well. Games like *StarCraft, Sid Meier’s Civilization,* or even staple family board games like *Risk *or *Chess* develop players’ ability to adjust to new challenges, think critically, and plan moves well in advance. Games can even teach players skills that they can apply to everyday life, such as anticipating and planning for future outcomes in real life or managing resources.

## First-Person Shooters and Action Games

Action games require players to react quickly and accurately. Games such as *Call of Duty* or *God of War* heighten your situational awareness, sharpen reflexes, and improve hand-eye coordination. It can lead to the development of superior multitasking abilities as well as elevate attention spans. Science Daily discusses a [2017 study done by Université de Genève](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2017/12/171212102158.htm) which demonstrates that action games expand the cognitive abilities of the brain.

## Role Playing Games (RPGs):

The deep narratives created by games such as* Witcher, Final Fantasy, *or *Skyrim* engage players in creative problem-solving and critical thinking. Players are challenged to manage intricate storylines, solve complex problems, and even make ethical decisions. The extended engagement of RPGs helps players to think strategically over an extended period of time while building patience.
