## Responsibility towards the well-being of the player

Gambling addiction is one of the most pressing issues that has hit the industry ever since it existed. To mitigate the effects of prolonged gaming sessions on both mental and physical health is important for gambling ethics and guidelines to be established.

Software game developers have the responsibility to promote balanced play by integrating responsible gambling features, such as session limits, reality checks, take a break, and other tools.

## Inclusivity and Diversity

The ethical scope of the online gambling industry also comprises the portrayal of various social groups. A lack of diversity in characters can perpetuate stereotypes and keep some players on the sidelines.

It is important for social game developers to create games, themes, and characters that are inclusive and that reflect a wide range of identities. This ensures that players from all walks of life, genders, and ethnicities are both represented and valued.

## Online Interactions

Online gambling communities often mirror the various social dynamics and nuances of the real world, but with the added complexity of anonymity, which can at times be a breeding ground for toxic behavior.

Hate speech, cyberbullying, and harassment are common issues that can impact negatively players’ mental health as well as their gaming experience. This is quite common in online bingo rooms and live dealer games, where players have access to a live chat box. It is quite common for players to harass the dealers or others via live chat.

To combat this, gambling ethics should give priority to creating a safe and more inclusive space. This can be done by the successful implementation of reporting systems, which allow players to flag any inappropriate behavior that they may be subject to or exposed to.

In addition to this measure, various moderation tools should be also adopted to quickly address and eradicate any toxic interactions. Beyond the technical measures, various educational initiatives are important to promote a culture of respect within the gaming community.
