For this cast, I sat down with returning guest and owner of Psychology of Games Jamie Madigan for another discussion about psychology and game design.

After trading war stories about our books, we spoke about different topics in game design. One of our main ones was discussing the frustration factor in games, and what is considered to be the breaking point of people and they decide to quit.
