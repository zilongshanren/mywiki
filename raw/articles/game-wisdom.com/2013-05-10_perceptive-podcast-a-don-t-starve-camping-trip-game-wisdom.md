This week Ken and I welcomed Kevin Forbes from Klei Entertainment to the cast to discuss all things *Don’t Starve* related. From art design, mechanics, some of the criticisms against the game and more. Kevin also talked a little about the general plan for the new content coming to *Don’t Starve* in the coming months.

Allergy season is only getting worse and I’m afraid that my heavy breathing is going to be standard for some time. I also made a small error discussing the tree guard and said that I planted seedlings, when I meant pine cones.
