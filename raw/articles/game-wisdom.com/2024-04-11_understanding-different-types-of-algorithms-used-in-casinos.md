## The Role of Random Number Generators (RNG)

RNGs are the backbone of fairness and unpredictability in online casino games. These sophisticated computer programs generate random numbers to determine game outcomes, ensuring that each spin, card deal, or dice roll is entirely independent and free from influence.

To uphold trust and fairness, reputable online casinos regularly inspect and certify their RNGs by independent bodies such as eCOGRA and Technical Systems Testing (TST), ensuring that the games operate correctly and fairly.

The essence of these algorithms lies in the Random Number Generators (RNGs) that determine the outcome of each game action, such as a spin in slots or a card deal in blackjack. These RNGs ensure that every outcome is independent of the previous.
