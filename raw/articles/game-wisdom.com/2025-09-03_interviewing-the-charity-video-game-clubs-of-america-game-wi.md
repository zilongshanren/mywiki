For this podcast, I was joined by Josh Bound and Max Jamelli from the charity VGCUSA, or the Video Game Clubs of America, about their mission to fund and develop library clubs for kids. As a point of transparency, I am working with them with some projects in the pipeline. We spoke about how it got started, misconceptions about game clubs, and more.
