## Minecraft

Exploring, collecting resources, and engaging in Warfare are all possible in the game, in addition to constructing with a wide variety of [3D building pieces.](https://architizer.com/blog/practice/tools/architecture-building-blocks-3d-models-photographs/)

## Fortnite

Fortnite is an electronic battle royale game developed and published by Epic Games. You can choose from three game modes: Battle Royale, Save the World, or Creative.

Most Fortnite gamers now choose between the first two game types. After only a year of release, Battle Royale has amassed 125 million gamers. Fortnite took home the “Greatest Multiplayer/Competitive Game” trophy at the Webby Awards in 2019.

## Rocket League

The goal of this game is straightforward: earn points by driving your [rocket-powered vehicle](https://newatlas.com/automotive/aussie-invader-land-speed-rocket-car/) into the net of your rival. The video game may be played online and offline, and each team comprises four people. Rocket League is the follow-up to the 2008 PlayStation 3 game Supersonic Acrobatic Rocket-Powered Battle-Cars.

## Playerunknown’s Battlegrounds (PUBG)

PlayerUnknown’s Battlegrounds (often known as “PUBG”) is the most epic battle royale game ever created for online multiplayer gaming. The rules of this game are easy to grasp. There can be as many as a hundred players in a game, all of whom parachute in and fight to the death.

You, the player, can improve your odds of survival by scavenging for tools and weapons. The “safe region” is constantly shrinking due to the game’s design, leaving little choice except to fight. It is one of the most popular video games, and for a good reason!
