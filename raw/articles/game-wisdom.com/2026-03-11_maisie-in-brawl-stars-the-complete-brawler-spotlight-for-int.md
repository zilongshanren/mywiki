If you’ve been playing Brawl Stars for a while, you already know that climbing the trophy ladder requires more than just raw mechanics; it takes smart brawler selection and deep game knowledge. Maisie is one of those brawlers that rewards players who take the time to understand her. She may look tricky at first, but once it clicks, she becomes one of the most satisfying picks in the game.


## Who Is Maisie?

Maisie is an Epic Marksman brawler who wields a pressure rocket launcher. She’s a futuristic safety coordinator who, according to her lore, secretly loves the dangerous situations she’s supposed to prevent. Her kit revolves around long-range pressure, crowd control, and punishing enemies who dare get too close.

Her stats at a glance:

**Class:**Marksman (Epic)**Health:**3,600 (base)**Movement Speed:**Normal**Range:**Long**Attack Damage:**~3,000 per rocket (at max range)

## Understanding Maisie’s Kit

**Basic Attack – Pressure Rocket**

Maisie fires a rocket from her pressure launcher. Here’s the key mechanic that sets her apart: **the projectile starts slow but accelerates as it travels**, dealing more damage the farther it goes. This means enemies can’t simply outrun the shot, the further they are, the more it hurts. Mastering this quirky projectile is what separates good Maisie players from great ones. Never use auto-aim unless an enemy is directly in front of you, since the rocket’s variable speed makes manual aiming a must.

**Super – Shockwave**

Maisie unleashes a powerful shockwave in all directions, dealing damage and **pulling enemies toward her** while also being able to interrupt certain abilities (like Frank’s and Bibi’s attacks). It charges up quickly, especially with the right Star Power, giving Maisie strong and consistent area control.

**Gadget 1 – Disengage!**

Maisie dashes forward and stuns nearby enemies for 0.5 seconds. This gadget is a Swiss Army knife — use it to escape dangerous situations, close the gap on a fleeing target, or cancel an enemy’s ability. It’s the go-to gadget for most situations and is chosen by the vast majority of Maisie players.

**Gadget 2 – Finish Them!**

Instantly reloads one ammo and boosts the next rocket’s damage by 30% of the target’s missing HP. It’s situationally useful for finishing off a low-health tank, but Disengage! is generally the better all-around pick.

**Star Power 1 – Tremors (Recommended)**

Enemies hit by Maisie’s Super are slowed for 2 seconds. This is her best Star Power by far. The slow allows you to land follow-up rockets with ease, charge your Super faster, and maintain pressure on both tanks and long-range brawlers. A deadly combo: activate your Super, then immediately use Disengage! to dash onto the slowed enemy for an easy finish.

**Star Power 2 – Pinpoint Precision**

Increases Maisie’s rocket damage and Super charge rate when hitting enemies at maximum range. It’s a strong pick specifically in **Heist**, where hitting a stationary safe at max range is easy. For most other modes, Tremors wins out.

## Best Game Modes for Maisie

Maisie is a versatile pick, but she truly shines in objective-based modes where area control matters:

**Gem Grab**– Her Super and Tremors Star Power make her exceptional at controlling the center. Push enemies off gems and deny advances.**Heist**– Since the safe is stationary, every rocket is a guaranteed hit. Her Hypercharge (Aftermath) is especially broken here, dealing massive damage to the safe per Super.**Hot Zone**– Great for zoning enemies off the capture point. Use Shockwave to keep opponents out.**Brawl Ball**– Her Super can force ball carriers to drop the ball and push enemies away from the goal. She pairs especially well with teammates who can tank near the goal while she provides long-range support.**Bounty**– Long-range poke and area denial make her a reliable pick here too.

## Leveling Up Your Maisie Game

If you’re an intermediate player looking to push higher with Maisie, keeping your overall account progress in check matters too. Many players looking to experience top-tier brawlers like Maisie explore [brawl stars accounts](https://gameboost.com/brawl-stars/accounts) that already have Epic and Legendary brawlers unlocked — a great way to experiment with different builds before fully investing your own resources.

Back to Maisie — here are the intermediate-level tips that make the real difference:

**Lead your shots.**Because the rocket accelerates, you need to aim slightly ahead of moving targets. Practice this in lower-stakes matches until it feels natural.**Use the Super + Disengage! combo.**Activate Shockwave, then dash with Disengage! onto the slowed enemy. They won’t see it coming.**Corner enemies before using your Super.**Walk toward a brawler cornered by a wall, then activate Shockwave. They have nowhere to go.**Never auto-aim**— unless you’ve just used your Super with Tremors active, as the slowed target is much easier to hit.**Manage your positioning.**Maisie struggles against long-range snipers like Piper and Brock. Dodge their shots by predicting them and slowly close the distance.

Who Counters Maisie (And How to Deal)

Maisie struggles against brawlers who can outrange or out-maneuver her:

**Piper, Brock, Mandy, Colt**— They can poke safely at distances where Maisie’s accuracy suffers. Tip: Use walls and bushes to approach safely, then burst them down.**Assassins (Mortis, Edgar, Buzz)**— They can rush in fast. Use Disengage! to stun them and create space, then follow up with your Super.

On the flip side, Maisie is excellent against tanks, as her rockets deal massive damage from a safe distance and her Super keeps them from closing the gap.

## Final Thoughts

Maisie is the kind of brawler that punishes lazy play — both from her opponents and from her own player. She demands precision, timing, and game sense. But when played well, she offers an incredible mix of damage, crowd control, and survivability that few brawlers can match. If you’re ready to put in the practice, Maisie will take your game to the next level.

Now get out there, and remember: aim manually, play the range, and don’t let them breathe.
