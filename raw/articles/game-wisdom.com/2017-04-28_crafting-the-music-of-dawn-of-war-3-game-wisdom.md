This week on the cast, I sat down with composer Paul Leonard Morgan to talk about the work he’s done composing the music for Warhammer 40K Dawn of War 3.


We started with talking about his background when it comes music before jumping into sound design with Dawn of War 3. We only had about a half an hour to talk, but we discussed the challenges of crafting the score for a strategy game compared to other genres.
