For this perceptive podcast, I sat down with creator Demon Chuckle to talk about making the visual novel Pirates of Gensokyo and their thoughts on the visual novel genre as a whole. We spoke about how it is a lot harder to stand out and the differences between good and bad examples while talking about the work that went into Pirates.
