For Ghostbusters who can’t get enough of ghostbusting, there’s one way you can satisfy that desire or craving even when you’re not watching the movies. Since the Ghostbusters franchise became a pop culture icon back in the ’80s, it became a phenomenon and expanded into comics, toys, cartoons, and video games. After all, we’ve all dreamt of becoming a Ghostbuster and using all the cool tools we’ve seen in the movie.

If you’re looking for Ghostbuster games to try, we’ve got an exciting list for you. Playing these games may even feel like you’re [ TV streaming](https://planetdish.com/) and being in the plot itself. Let’s start!



**Ghostbusters: The Video Game (PS3)**


If you’ve ever wanted to suit up and bust ghosts with the original Ghostbusters team, you will love this game. Ghostbusters: The Video Game on PS3 feels like a love letter to the movies, like seriously, with voice work from the original cast and a story that plays out like a proper sequel. Many people found the gameplay to be a bit tricky at times, but that’s the fun part, right?

The graphics are from 2009, so those who like modern graphics may find it hard to play this game, but it still looks great in our opinion. There’s also a surprising amount of detail that fans will appreciate. It’s a solo-player game, so you don’t have to worry about needing to have a fast fiber or [satellite internet connection](https://satelliteforinternet.com/hughesnet-gen5-internet-plans/) for a multiplayer game.


**Ghostbusters: Spirits Unleashed (PS4)**


If you’ve ever wanted to bust ghosts…. or be one, well, you can’t go wrong with Ghostbusters: Spirits Unleashed. This is a multiplayer game where you can team up with your friends and catch ghosts together. The twist is that you can also play as the ghost and try to scare everyone before they catch you.

Since this can be played online and offline, if you’re planning to play this with your friends, you will need a fast internet connection. Whether you’re connected to your home WiFi or your mobile data, you’ll need a fast and stable connection. **Extreme Ghostbusters: Zap the Ghosts! (PC)**


If you like puzzle games, you’ll enjoy Zap the Ghosts! It was released in 2001, so yes, it’s a bit dated already. In this game, you’ll play as one of the new Ghostbusters, either Eduardo, Roland, Kylie, or Garrett. The main goal of the game is to clear the screen of ghosts by matching three or more of the same color. If you’re familiar with the game Bust-a-Move, it’s similar to this.


**Ghostbusters: Rise of the Ghost Lord (PS5)**


If you’re looking for a newer Ghostbusters game and you have a PS5 console, you can give this game a try. This is a first-person VR game that lets you put on the proton pack, hold a PKE meter, and trap ghosts like a real Ghostbuster. You’ll be in San Francisco, where you can play by yourself or team up with other players online (up to three). Everything feels super interactive so you’ll really have the best time.

**Final Thoughts**

There are still a lot of Ghostbusters games out there, but these are our top picks because of the reasons we’ve mentioned above. If you’re looking for something to try, you can’t go wrong with these four games. It’ll just depend on which device you have right now, but trust us when we say that these games are all worth your time. Happy playing!
