This week I sat down and played Prison Architect. Originally I was going to play Invisible Inc but I thought that the recent update to it would have made it a great contender.

However lesson learned : Management style games aren’t the best for watching as too much of what makes the game interesting goes on in your head and not on the screen. For next week I think I’m going to go with Invisible Inc as that game is easier to see what’s happening and comment on.

I’m also noticing some of the technical limitations of using an older monitor setup and I hate to bring this up, but this would be one of the upgrades that the Patreon campaign was set up to cover. Eventually I will buy a new monitor but I can’t justify it without having either another contract or the campaign picking up.
