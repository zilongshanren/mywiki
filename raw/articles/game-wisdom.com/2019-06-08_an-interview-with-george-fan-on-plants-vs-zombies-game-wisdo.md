For this cast, I finally had a chance to sit down with the creator of Plants vs. Zombies George Fan to discuss game design.


We were going to originally talk about both PvZ and Octogeddon, but we spent the majority of our time just talking basic game design while breaking down the design of PvZ. Hopefully we can get him back on for another cast in the future.

You can find [Plants vs Zombies](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3590/Plants_vs_Zombies_GOTY_Edition/) and [Octogeddon](https://store.steampowered.com/app/525620/Octogeddon/) on Steam
