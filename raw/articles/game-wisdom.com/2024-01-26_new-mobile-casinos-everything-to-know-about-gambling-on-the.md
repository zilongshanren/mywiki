## Things to Remember While Gambling On the Go

While we’ve already mentioned that you should avoid unlicensed casinos, there are definitely a few other things you’re going to want to keep in mind. First off, make sure that you’re taking advantage of any bonuses and promotions the casino offers. Always take advantage of welcome bonuses and reload bonuses. Another thing to remember is to practice safe gambling habits. One problem new players make is not understanding proper bankroll management techniques. Your bankroll is the total amount of money you’ve decided to set aside for wagering. This is essentially money you can spare and doesn’t cause any hardships if you lose it.

It’s not enough to simply have this number set aside; you also want to keep your bets proportional to your bankroll. The main idea is that your bets should never exceed 1-5%—depending on the game and odds. Safer bets allow you to wager more of your bankroll, while riskier bets should be smaller. The idea behind this is that if you go on a losing streak, you still have a large chunk of your bankroll left and can recuperate your losses with a little luck.
