For this week’s cast, Dave Gilbert of Wadjet Eye Games joined me in finally celebrating Unavowed’s release this week with a discussion on the game, writing and more.


We got right into things with talking about the work that went into the development of Unavowed. Dave discussed some of the challenges of elevating his design with multiple characters. We also spoke about writing the four main characters, and discussing the future of Unavowed.

There are very minor spoilers mentioned, but nothing related to the main plot of the game.
