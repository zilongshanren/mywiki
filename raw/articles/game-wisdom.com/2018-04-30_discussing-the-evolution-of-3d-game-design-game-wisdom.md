This week on the cast, I welcomed owner of Super Jump James Burns to discuss the Game Industry, Game Journalism, and what it’s like to grow up through the 2D and 3D era.


We started sharing our war stories when it came to reviewing and covering video games. James talked about how he got started and what kind of games he likes to play. After that, we moved on to discussing some of the trends we’ve seen lately out of the Indie space and that led into talking about 2D and 3D design.

Both designs have their own considerations and challenges to deal with, and we analyzed them both from a AAA and Indie developer perspective.
