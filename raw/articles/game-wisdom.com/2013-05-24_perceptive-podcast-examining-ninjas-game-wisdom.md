This week on the cast, Nels Anderson from Klei Entertainment, who was the lead designer of *Mark of the Ninja,* joined us. We talked about the stealth genre and the design of the game. Besides getting on the topic of the oculus rift, I found a kindred spirit who has also watched the two Lawnmower Man movies.

As always my allergies were still getting in the way, but as a coincidence they would prevent me from not only being a ninja, but surviving in the wilderness world of [Don’t Starve](https://game-wisdom.com/podcast/perceptive-podcast-a-dont-starve-camping-trip).
