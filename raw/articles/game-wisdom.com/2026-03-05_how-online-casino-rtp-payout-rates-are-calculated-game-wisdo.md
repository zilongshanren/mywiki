**What is RTP?**

RTP, or Return to Player, is a percentage that indicates how much money a particular game returns to players over time. For example, if a game has an RTP of 95.00%, this means that, theoretically, for every $100 wagered, players can expect to receive $95 back in winnings over a significant number of spins or hands.

Most online casino games generally have RTP rates that range from 93.00% to 98.00%. Although higher RTPs are favorable for players, it’s important to remember that this statistic is based on long-term statistical averages and does not guarantee short-term outcomes.

With millions of players possibly engaging with the same game simultaneously, results can vary significantly in the short term.

**How is RTP Calculated?**

RTP is calculated using extensive data from countless games played over time, combined with mathematical models that simulate various scenarios. Game developers analyze these scenarios to identify how much of the wagered amount should be returned to players.

The mathematical algorithms that generate these calculations are formulated based on statistical principles and provide the fundamental framework that ensures fairness and randomness.

These calculations are often verified by independent testing agencies. Organizations such as:

-
[eCOGRA](https://www.mga.org.mt/malta-recognises-ecogra-standards-testing/) - Technical Systems Testing
- Global Lab Limited

These internationally accredited, non-profit testing agencies frequently evaluate online casino games to ensure they meet fairness standards.

These organizations employ rigorous testing methods and random sampling techniques to validate the accuracy and integrity of the RTP percentages claimed by the online casinos.

**The Role of RNGs**

[Random Number Generators](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/standards/remote-gambling-and-software-technical-standards/rts-7-generation-of-random-outcomes) (RNGs) are crucial in ensuring fairness in online casino games. These sophisticated algorithms generate results that are random and unpredictable, which provides each player with a fair chance of winning each time they hit the spin button on their favourite video slots, when they’re dealt a new hand of cards, receive a new scratchcard and so on.

The RNGs used in online casinos are routinely tested and certified by independent agencies similar to those that assess RTP percentages.

It’s essential to note that while RTP percentages apply to a specific game over a prolonged timeline, individual outcomes can still fluctuate dramatically, but it’s always influenced by the randomness produced by RNGs.

The outcomes are designed to be fair and without discernible patterns, ensuring that each spin or shuffle is an independent event.

Live dealer games create a different gaming environment. Here, the results are not determined by RNGs but by actual dealers and physical game equipment.

However, in computer-generated live dealer slots or during certain computer-generated bonus rounds found in live TV game show formats, RNGs do play a role, which effectively means that while live dealer games offer an interactive experience, certain elements may still rely on random number generation to ensure fair play.
