## Wheel of Fortune

The Wheel of Fortune is a symbolic attribute widely known in various cultures and mythologies. It denotes fate, randomness and uncertainty. Many believe that it was the Wheel of Fortune that served as the idea for the creation of roulette.

The connection between the Wheel of Fortune and roulette is manifested in an element of randomness and uncertainty. As in the Wheel of Fortune, where the result is determined randomly, when spinning the roulette, there is also no way to accurately predict which cell the ball will fall into.

## Biribi

Other historians believe that the Italian game Biribi is the historical predecessor of modern roulette. This entertainment was popular in Italy in the XVIII century and was often called a “lottery game”. Biribi had similar features to roulette, including the use of a wheel and the execution of bets on numbers.

The game Biribi used a wheel on which various numbers were presented. Each number was marked with a chip. Players placed bets on the selected numbers by placing chips on the respective areas. After all the bets were placed, the Biribi wheel was launched and the ball, similar to the roulette ball, rotated around it. Eventually, as the wheel slowed down, the ball would stop at one of the numbers, determining the winning bets.

The similarities between Biribi and roulette lie in the general concept of the game, the use of the wheel and betting on numbers. However, over time, as historians assure, Biribi gradually evolved, and became the basis for the creation of modern roulette.

## Blaise Pascal

Many believe that Blaise Pascal, a French physicist, is the creator of the famous game. Adherents of this theory say that in the course of studying cycloids, Pascal developed a theory of probability and applied it to rotating wheels. Roulette subsequently became a kind of by-product of his research.

However, primitive versions of roulette were known long before Pascal. It is more likely that Pascal was not the direct inventor of roulette, but his research in the field of probability made a significant contribution to the development of this game.
