Did you know that [both laughter and exercise](https://www.webmd.com/balance/features/give-your-body-boost-with-laughter) offer some of the same health benefits? Because of this, it’s a good idea to try and make yourself, and everyone around you, smile and laugh as much as possible.

One of the best ways to do just that is to create funny memes for your friends and family to enjoy. Also, if you’re a brand or business, funny memes can help humanize your brand and make it that much easier for people to connect to you.

Regardless of your intentions, it’s never a bad thing to put smiles on people’s faces. In this article, we’ll give you a few tips you can use to create the best, most effective memes possible.

That way you can go viral and make literally millions of people smile and laugh! Now then, let’s get started!


**Don’t Be Afraid to Remix a Meme**

If you know anything about memes, you know that, for the most part, nobody is creating and sharing original ideas.

Sure, it’s possible to come up with some unique and funny and start a trend yourself. But most people are taking inspiration from preexisting memes and tweaking them to fit their own jokes.

So, don’t be afraid to remix a meme that is already out there online and make it your own. Just be sure to change enough about the meme so that you’re not completely ripping off someone else’s idea.

**Stick with What’s Hot Online**

When looking for memes to remix, try to stick with memes that are current and new, here’s where you can find the [latest source of new memes](https://www.kumospace.com/blog/goals-memes).

Of course, there are tons of older memes that have been around for years. But if you want to get lots of shares online, sticking with newer memes is the best way to go.

**Use Quality Photos and Editors **

If you’re making your own memes or tweaking pre-existing ones, there are a few things you need to know in order to get good results. The biggest tip is to use high-quality images that are clear and easy to see.

Along with high-quality images, make sure you use a meme maker that doesn’t over-compress your images, making them blurry. [Adobe Spark’s memes generator](https://spark.adobe.com/make/meme-maker/), for example, works the best and is easy-to-use, which is a huge plus.

**Try Before You Share**

Nobody wants to post and share and unfunny meme. Doing so too many times can be quite damaging to your brand’s online repetition, which isn’t what you want.

That’s why we suggest that you try before you share. In other words, have a small group or team that approves your memes before you post them for the entire world to see.

That way you can make sure you’re meme is up to par before going live with it online.

**Tips and Tricks for Making Funny Memes**

Well, there you have it! Those are a few tips and tricks you can use to make funny memes! So, as long as you keep these ideas in mind when making your memes, you should be more than happy with your results after you post them online!
