With the rapid rise in popularity of online games, it’s important to stay safe while playing. Finding a safe place to game can be difficult in these times of endless threats and attacks. From phishing to malware, there are countless forms that digital dangers can take. It is important that everyone be aware of these threats and understand how to avoid them.


As a new industry, online gaming is growing every day. New sites, apps, and games pop up every week. As with any other industry, however, there are also risks attached to online gaming. It’s important to remember that while many of these sites and apps offer games that are fun and safe (and fair), some do not have your safety as a priority.

There are many industries that can benefit from online gaming and, along the way, these companies can also end up benefiting the whole gaming industry. This article will help to explore what strategies you can take to make sure that your gaming experience is both safe and fun:


**Download the Right Security Software**


You have probably heard the old saying that an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. That saying is especially true when it comes to online gaming. The first step in defeating online dangers is to stop them from getting into your computer in the first place. Do not fall prey to infected links and phishing sites, because they are what cause most problems for gaming enthusiasts. You can protect yourself with anti-malware software and security suites, which can be downloaded for free or at a low cost from any number of online vendors. This software will keep you protected from unsafe links, dangerous sites, and other threats that could steal your information or harm your computer by installing malware or viruses on it.


**Use VPNs and Multi-factor Authentication**


The second step in preventing online gaming dangers is to protect your information. This means using multi-factor authentication on sites like those offered by Steam, Blizzard, and others. However, it also means keeping your passwords safe. You should not use the same password on different sites. You should also use a unique and strong password on each site as well as a password manager to keep track of them all for you.

As an additional layer of security, some gaming sites and apps allow you to use Virtual Private Networks, or VPNs, to secure your data. A VPN acts like a tunnel that you and your information can travel through to get from one place to another. Unlike a browser’s built-in security features, VPNs are stronger and more flexible tools for protecting yourself on the web because they can block malicious data from getting into your computer altogether while also stopping it from leaving your computer once it’s inside.



**Always Check Your Reviews and Read the Fine Print**


It’s important to read reviews of any site or app before you play on it. Look carefully at star ratings and comments from players who have played on the site in the past. You want to make sure that you are playing on a site that has a record of being secure. You can do this by checking out reviews and ratings on sites like The Online Gaming Review or another trustworthy site. You should also check out the gaming site or app before you download anything from it, as most apps and sites are now available in both their own native app stores and Google’s App Store for Android.

If you decide to download an app or game from an online vendor, it is important that you read the fine print first, before hitting the “download” button. Terms of service agreements and privacy policies can be lengthy documents, but they are vital to your safety online.


**Use secure passwords and never share them**


Your passwords should be a strong and unique password. You may want to use a password manager to help you remember a bunch of different passwords. This will keep your accounts secure, and it can also help you keep track of them all in one place. As long as you do not give out this identity information, no one will ever know that your account information has been compromised by hackers or other online adversaries.


**Do not give out your personal information**


You should never give out your personal information online. If you are talking to someone in a game or on a different app, you may have to share your cell phone number or other basic information about yourself. Be very careful how much personal information you give out online. Remember that no one is going to come after you if they have the wrong cell phone number, so do not share it if you are worried about privacy issues.
