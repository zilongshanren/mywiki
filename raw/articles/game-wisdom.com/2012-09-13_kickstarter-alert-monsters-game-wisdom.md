A friend of mine who is a local designer has a kickstarter up for a game idea that he has been working on. He wants to create a different take on the RoboTron, in which each time the player attacks the player is drained of health.

He’s looking for enough money to finish development as he’s almost finished. It’s great to see more local developers working on labor of love projects and I hope he gets the funding. You can find his kickstarter page [here](http://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1935836322/monsters-a-2d-shooter-for-win-mac-linux).
