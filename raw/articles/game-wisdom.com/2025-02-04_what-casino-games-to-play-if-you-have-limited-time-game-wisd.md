## Games with High RTP

RTP refers to the Return to Player and is the percentage of the fund deposited in a game that is eventually returned to the players. Some games have a lower RTP and some have as high as 99%. If you don’t have a lot of time to spend on your casino game, you’re advised to choose the ones with a high RTP. Finding out the RTP of a game is as simple as a search engine query and will make your experience that much better.
