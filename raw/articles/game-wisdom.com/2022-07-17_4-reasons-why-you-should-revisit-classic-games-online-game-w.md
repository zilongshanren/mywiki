As society moves further into the digital age, it can be harder for the older generation to keep up with the latest innovations. **Nostalgia**

There are many cases where nostalgia can be considered something negative. It can ruin some modern films and video games, while also holding back the progression of popular culture. However, nostalgia isn’t something that should immediately be dismissed as there is evidence to suggest that it can [have positive effects](https://www.spring.org.uk/2021/09/nostalgia.php) on your health.

Engaging in activities that remind you of a happier time can make you more optimistic in general, studies have shown. Optimism is a crucial factor when it comes to maintaining positive mental health, and poor mental health issues can cause damage to your physical health over time. Therefore, playing some classic games online could give you the jolt of positivity that you need in your day. It isn’t an instant cure, but we all need something comforting when going through troubling times. Why not make that comfort the games from your youth?

**Conclusion**

It shouldn’t take a lot to convince you of the benefits that can come from playing classic games online. While you will find a place to relieve stress and feel good about yourself, the most important thing is that you have fun.

If today’s modern school of video games does not interest you, take a step back in time to find some of your favourite childhood classics online.
