This week Ken and I had the pleasure to talk with famed adventure game designer and writer Jane Jensen about her path as a game designer, adventure game design and her upcoming game– *Moebius: Empire Rising*.


We started off talking about Jane’s background and her thoughts on adventure game logic and design philosophy. Also how the genre has grown, the shift to narrative focused titles and more.

Moving on we talked about her kickstarter and the challenges that went with it. Finally we spoke spoiler free about *Moebius* and how she is trying to bring classic adventure game design to a modern audience.
