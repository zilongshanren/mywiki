This week we had an extra long cast, as James and I sat down with returning guest Chris Park of Arcen Games. It’s been awhile since we spoke and a lot has been going on with Arcen; including the kickstarter for AI Wars 2.


There were too many topics to break down from the cast. We spoke about the changing market, the difficulties that have been going on with Arcen and a lot more. For people who missed the news, we discussed the recent problems with their last two games: Stars Beyond Reach and In Case of Emergency, Release Raptor.

This cast was so big, it had to be split into two parts. This part focused on Arcen Games and design thoughts, [part 2 is all about AI Wars 2 and the kickstarter](https://game-wisdom.com/podcast/arcen-games-ai-wars-p2).
