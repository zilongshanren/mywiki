For this cast, I spoke with Steven Spohn, COO of the organization Ablegamers to discuss what accessibility means to him, and how developers can make their games better for everyone. We spoke about what the charity and organization does and Steven talked about some of the major myths and criticisms surrounding accessibility in the game industry.
