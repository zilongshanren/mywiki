This week, Ken and I decided to try something different and have an informal debate. Last week we had an heated discussion on gameplay vs. narrative in relation to Bioshock Infinite and [my analysis](https://game-wisdom.com/analysis/bioshock-infinite-sky-dive) and we wanted to expand on it.

Like in the analysis our conversation will go into some detail about Infinite’s game mechanics, but we did our best not to go into any plot spoilers for any of the games we talked about.
