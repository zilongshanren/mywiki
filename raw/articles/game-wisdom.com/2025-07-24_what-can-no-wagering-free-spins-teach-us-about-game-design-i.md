**What wagering requirements?**

Most online casino operators these days offer new and existing players free spins bonuses to try out some of their selected online slot machines in the real money mode, meaning they can use those free spins to try and win more money, no strings attached.

However, most free spins bonuses come with what many people refer to as wagering requirements, which are also known as rollover, turnover, or playthrough requirements. It all means the same thing.

The wagering requirement is there to protect operators and prevent players from receiving free spins, landing a huge win, and then simply withdrawing that money straight away and spending it elsewhere.

If every player who received free spins ended up winning big and withdrawing straight away like this, [iGaming operators](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce90k034mzvo) would quickly go out of business.

Therefore, instead of being able to do this, players must wager their winnings X amount of time first, which is typically anywhere from 20x to 50x times (always remember, the lower the better).

If you end up winning $/€/£20 from your 10 free spins for, say, the Book of Dead online slot machine, and each free spin was valued at $/€/£0.10 (or equivalent currency value), then instead of being able to simply withdraw your $/€/£20 winnings, you must first complete the wagering requirements.

If the bonus comes with a 20x wagering requirement attached, and you win $/€/£20, then you would need to wager $/€/£400 before you could withdraw, but if you win $/€/£100 with a 20x wagering requirement, then you would need to wager $/€/£2,000, and so on.

If you have any winnings left over in your account AFTER the wagering requirements have been cleared within the time given (can be anywhere from 24 hours up to one month), then you are now free to withdraw the winnings or spend as you please on the website.
