This week, we welcomed back Zach Barth : head of Zachtronics Industries to talk about his latest game — *Ironclad Tactics*.


We talked about how the concept came about, the design process of combining CCG and strategy gameplay and more. Also we got into a small discussion on Free To Play and micro transactions and what Zach’s thoughts are on both. Lastly we touched on what Zach has planned for *Ironclad Tactics* including if the game does take off.

Zach’s call quality was a bit iffy in some spots but nothing too bad. And I should have my analysis of *Ironclad Tactics* sometime next week.
