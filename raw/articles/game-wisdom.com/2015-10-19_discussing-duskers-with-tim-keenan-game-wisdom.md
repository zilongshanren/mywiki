This week on the cast, I had a great time talking to owner of Misfits Attic Tim Keenan about his studio and the work being done on their current game: Duskers.


We started with introductions and how Tim started Misfits Attic. We talked about connection to Positech Game’s Cliff Harris and about some lessons on Indie Game Development. After a great discussion on game development, we moved on to talk about Duskers and how development is going. We touched on enemy design, future content and a lot more.

While it may not seem like it, this cast was the perfect storm of technical issues which unfortunately got in the way of an amazing conversation. Knock on wood, but I may have gotten them fix so it won’t happen again.
