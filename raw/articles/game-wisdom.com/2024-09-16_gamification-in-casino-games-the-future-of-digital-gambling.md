## What is Gamification in Casino Games?

Gamification in general is the integration of [game elements into non-game contexts](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221518895_Gamification_Using_game_design_elements_in_non-gaming_contexts). For casino games, gamification practices make ordinary gambling more interactive and entertaining. These may be in the form of:

**Rewards and Badges**: These are virtual trophies, badges, or prizes which players get when they complete certain tasks or achieve milestones.

**Leaderboards**: Competition is driven by the display of rankings of top players, which would urge users to strive and perform better.

**Tiers**: Like levels in playing, the player earns the right to progress from one tier to another as the progress of playing continues, opening new games or benefits.

**Daily/Weekly Challenges**: The casinos provide challenges or missions available for a limited time, which keeps the player returning regularly.

Gamification can reach bigger audiences while newer generations grow older and become familiar with how video games work, thus becoming more involved in online casinos.

## How Gamification Improves Player Retention

One of the major reasons casinos incorporate gamification into their platforms is because of the ability it gives them to retain players more successfully. In this competitive market, where online casinos fight for the attention of players, it is important to keep them engaged. Gamification does this by:


**Increasing Emotional Investment**: The more a player feels he or she is achieving something, working toward some kind of goal, or gathering some sort of achievement, the more likely they are to come back. A sense of achievement pushes further engagement.

**Long-term Motivation**: Instead of gaming for an immediate win, gamified elements tend to extend their session durations. Whether by unlocking new levels or through participating in tournaments, there is every reason for players to continue playing over a longer period of time.**Building Loyalty**: A casino can reward players for winning but also for other activities-anything from daily logins and referring friends to completing certain tasks. This creates loyalty and encourages repeat visits.
