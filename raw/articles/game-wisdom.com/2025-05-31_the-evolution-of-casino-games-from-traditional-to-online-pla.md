## Land-Based Gambling: Popular Games and Past Trends

Il Ridotto, also known as Casino di Venezia, began operating in 1638 and still welcomes players with its luxury ambience and amazing spirit. In the 17th century, risk lovers didn’t have the chance to enjoy innovative content. Lotteries were increasingly common during that time, so most visitors could enjoy them in land-based casinos.

Card games were also in demand: Primero is one of the titles we know about. Even though modern gamblers don’t know its exact rules, its essence is similar to the blackjack we all know. Players had to get the highest possible hand. Bluffing was already common in the oldest brick-and-mortar casinos, and this tradition is kept in the contemporary gambling world.

The appearance of the gaming house in Venice gave rise to the industry’s development on the European continent, and many other casinos were opened in the following decades. Casino de Monte Carlo, known for its amazing game choice and opulent atmosphere, was launched in 1856. It became a haven for wealthy people and royalty. At the beginning of the 1900s, it became one of the best casinos in the world, where players could enjoy multiple games.
