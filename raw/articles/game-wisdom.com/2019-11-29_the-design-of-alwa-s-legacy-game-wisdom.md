For this cast, we sat down with Mikael of Elden Pixels who created the modern-retro game Alwa’s Awakening to discuss the kickstarter for their sequel Alwa’s legacy.


We spoke about the history of the company and design that went into Alwa’s Awakening. After that we spoke about Alwa’s Awakening and what the company has planned for that. We spoke a lot about modern retro design and what that means for the market.
