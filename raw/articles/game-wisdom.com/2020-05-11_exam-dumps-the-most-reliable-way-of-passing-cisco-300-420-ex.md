**CCNP Enterprise Certification Overview**

The CCNP Enterprise credential validates a candidate’s knowledge and skills in implementing and troubleshooting Cisco enterprise advanced routing and services. This certification is appropriate for those who desire to be network engineers, network design engineers, and system administrators. It requires knowledge in technological areas such as network design, enterprise networking, and routing and switching.

The Cisco 300-420 ENSLD (Designing Cisco Enterprise Networks) test is one out of two exams required to earn the CCNP Enterprise badge. The first is the core exam (350-401 ENCOR) and then the candidate should choose a concentration exam from the six options and one of which is 300-320 ENSLD test.This Cisco assessment is also required in order to earn the Cisco Certified Specialist — Enterprise Design credential.

**Cisco 300-420 Exam Details **

This test, offered in English and Japanese, is supposed to be done in a duration of 90 minutes. It validates the candidate’s knowledge of enterprise design especially advanced addressing and routing solutions, WAN, advanced enterprise campus networks, and security services. The main topics tested include:

- Advanced addressing and routing solutions — 25%

This part involves creating structured addressing plans for IPv4 and IPv6 and creating stable and secure routing designs for IS-IS, EIGRP, OSPF and BGP.

- Advanced enterprise campus networks- 25%

This section incorporates designing campus networks for high availability, campus Layer 2 infrastructures, and understanding SD-access architecture.

- WAN for enterprise networks — 20%

This one includes understanding SD-WAN architecture, designing site to site VPN and understanding WAN connectivity alternatives.

- Network services — 20%

As for this topic, it implies choosing the most appropriate QoS strategies to meet client needs and designing end to end QoS strategies and network management procedures.

- Automation — 10%

The last part involves selecting the most appropriate YANG data model depending on the client’s requirements.
