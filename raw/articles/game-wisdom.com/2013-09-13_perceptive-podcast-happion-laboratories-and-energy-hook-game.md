This week we welcomed to the cast Jamie Fristrom from Happion Laboratories who prototyped the original web physics system seen in *Spiderman 2* and is taking it a step further with his upcoming game: *Energy Hook*


During the cast we talked a little about how the physics system was developed, starting with its first iteration for the first Spiderman game by Treyarch and how it was expanded and refined for the open world sequel.

I didn’t get a chance to try out Energy Hook until after the cast but look towards a spotlight sometime next week. We did have some call quality issues near the end of the cast so fair warning there.
