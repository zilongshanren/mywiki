For this special livecast, I hung out with tabletop game designer John Brieger to talk about the importance of playtesting and the user experience.


We spoke about little things that can improve your game, and how to understand and gather feedback from people playing your game. While John is a tabletop designer, the lessons we talked about can be applied to video games as well. The cast starts about four minutes in.
