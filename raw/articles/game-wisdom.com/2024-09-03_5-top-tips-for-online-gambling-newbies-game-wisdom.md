## 2. Warm up Without Money

Rare are people who start playing ice hockey immediately, without learning how to ice skate first.

The same analogy can be applied to online gambling. If you haven’t gambled before, it’s recommended to spend some time playing without real money, i.e., playing casino games that let you check out what such games look like. Making a spin, playing a hand of cards, or placing a bet just for fun or practice will give you the gist of iGaming, and you’ll quickly see whether you’re a natural or not.

## 3. Set a Reasonable Budget

Once you’re done playing iGames for practice, without investing real money, it’s time to spice things up and go real.

Since online gambling and betting are a type of online gaming, every responsible player will want to [set their gaming budget](https://budgetbakers.com/gaming-budget/). This is the amount of money you decide to spend monthly on your iGaming activities. In the beginning, you’ll need a computer and/or smartphone to access such games in the first place. Likewise, it’s wise to get a pair of headphones because they can improve the overall gaming experience. Unlike traditional video games, where sound and images are complementary, in most iGames, the gaming experience won’t suffer if you switch off the sound.

Still, you might want to play your favorite playlist in the background; imagine playing online slots while listening to the GTA Vice City soundtrack – sounds exciting, right?

Once you’ve purchased the equipment, decide how much money you can spend monthly on your iGaming activities. While the Internet is brimming with different tips thereof, using common sense is a safe lane. Start with small amounts that you won’t regret spending; for instance, make coffee at home instead of going out and redirect this budget to your gam(bl)ing sessions.

## 4. Find Your Game(s)

One person’s poker is another person’s roulette; different people have different preferences when it comes to gaming in general, iGaming included.

As a rookie, you might feel overwhelmed with the number of games and the abundance of incentives on hundreds of iGaming platforms. At this point, you shouldn’t scatter your time and energy on too many things because that will make you inefficient. While any form of gambling and betting should be fun, it’s more interesting if you keep improving your skills, to win some of the games in question.

For instance, if you’ve always liked to play cards and you’re good at such games, focusing more on blackjack, online poker, and other card games makes more sense. When you find the right game for your affinities and existing knowledge, your iGaming sessions will be more amusing.

## 5. Upgrade the Gaming Skills

We get better at everything we do with the hours we invest in that activity. But does this principle apply to iGaming? Yes, it works in this gaming subdivision, as well, but not entirely because the outcome of gambling games doesn’t depend only on our skills.

Still, [improving your gaming skills](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/how-to-get-better-at-gaming) is necessary if you want to go through the rites of passage from a novice to a sophomore iGamer.

Keep an eye on your stats from day one. Every reputable iGaming platform will let you dive into your stats (usually in the profile or account section). Use these features to check out how you’ve been doing; for example, take a look at this data at the end of every gambling session.

Find the relevant online players and streaming sessions and watch how they play their games. It will help you notice and apply some details that otherwise you might not figure out all by yourself.

Concentrate on the financial aspect of your iGaming sessions as much as possible. In addition to setting the budget and its limitations, work on your bankroll. Building self-discipline will help you gain wider knowledge from your gambling and betting investments. The skills you gain here might even help you shape your professional career; a [study revealed by the Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2023/03/your-gaming-skills-can-help-you-shape-your-career) has shown that gaming can do that.
