This week on the cast, I sat down with more of the great folks at Failbetter Games. This time I spoke with Narrative Editor Olivia and Writer James to talk about the work that goes into their writing.


We started with talking about their roles at Failbetter before discussing story writing. We talked about the challenges of writing stories for situations that you’re not used to. Controversial topics can be polarizing to write about, and we talked about what it means to write them.

While they couldn’t talk much about Sunless Skies, I tried to get them to give out any teasers for future content.
