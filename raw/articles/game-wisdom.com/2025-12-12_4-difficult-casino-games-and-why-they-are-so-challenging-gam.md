Then there’s the famous poker face. Rules, math, and strategies can be learned, but controlling your emotions and maintaining a poker face even in dicey situations is a whole different challenge.

Over time, one develops a sense of who is “sitting” in front of them, how they think, and what tactics they prefer. The reverse is also true. ## 2. Craps

With its many rules, craps is arguably one of the most complex casino games. Added to this is the speed of play and the need for a well-developed strategy. Although every dice combination can theoretically be determined mathematically, the betting options are so varied that a game can quickly become expensive. Avoid relying on questionable tactics such as dice control techniques. Most casinos have rules governing how and where the dice must be thrown. Instead, focus on understanding the game and placing sound bets, such as odds bets, and avoid high house-edge traps.

While the rules are relatively simple once understood – if the dice fall on 7 or 11 it’s automatically a win; if it’s 2, 3, or 12 it’s automatically a loss – the biggest challenge in craps remains probability.

## 3. Blackjack

Blackjack seems simple until players understand how much a small difference in the rules can change the math, depending on the version. A blackjack house edge calculator can help compare variants: For “optimal results,” players play with a perfect strategy, and the dealer shuffles after each hand. This is most favorable for the player. In the “basic strategy with cut card,” players use the normal basic strategy, but with a cut card, as is used in real casinos. This gives the dealer an advantage. In the “basic strategy with constant shuffling,” players also play according to the basic strategy, but the dealer automatically reshuffles after each hand.

Knowing both offensive and defensive strategies is a must, but ultimately, luck also plays a big part in determining whether one wins or loses. The challenge in this game is that players play against the dealer and have to beat their hand without going over 21. However, players can’t influence the cards they are dealt.

## 4. Mahjong

Although mahjong is not a casino classic, it is becoming increasingly popular worldwide and has found its way into casinos. Before mahjong became a gambling game, it was and still is primarily a [social game in China](https://www.ancientgames.org/history-of-mahjong/), and is still played at big events like New Year’s. The game became famous in gambling culture because it is particularly challenging due to the combination of complex rules, the strategic depth required, and the need for a good memory.

Players can only see part of the tiles, but must recognize patterns, assess so-called dead tiles, and guess their opponents’ hands. Playing tactics must be constantly adapted to the dynamics of the table and to each variant and set of rules. Ultimately, a good assessment of probabilities and tile efficiency determines victory or defeat.

What makes Mahjong unique and difficult at the same time is that it requires both intuition and precise planning as well as a strong memory, something that not many games can offer.
