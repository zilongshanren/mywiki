## Which casino games are better in terms of RTP?

The mathematical model when [playing casino games](https://www.casinonic.com/en-AU/games) always made in a way with the slight advantage over the player to casino. (Otherwise, what would casinos make money on). Gambling establishment has no need to cheat or deceive players whatsoever, due to the average percentage casino has with each bet laid in the mathematical model of the game.


Let’s take roulette and color bets as an example, as a simpler example to understand. The roulette wheel has 18 black numbers, 18 red numbers and one zero (0). If you guess the color, the winnings are 1:1. So, if you bet 1000 USD on red, and the bet played, then you will get your bet back plus your winnings of 1000 USD, which equals to a total of 2000 USD.

The probability of such win can be calculated as:

- Number of cells and market sectors + zero (green) = 18 /(18+18+1) = 18/37= 48.65% or 1:2.05

If there was no zero, the chances of the player and the casino would be equal (18/36 = 50%). On average, the players and the casino will stay in this particular casino game on their own money. The presence of the extra number slightly reduces the player’s odds and gives the casino a slight advantage. Although you can bet and even win on a zero, it doesn’t change the fact. From each game, the casino will have 1/37 = 2.7% of the bet.

Of course, the situation on the table may change. At some point, players may be lucky and they will win more often, or on the contrary, most players will lose. But at a long distance, the [law of large numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_large_numbers) comes into play. And with a lot of bets, surprisingly, the amount the casino will win is very close to 2.7% of all bets.

This indicator is called an RTP (Return to player). So, at roulette RTP will be = 100%-2.7% = 96.7%. In every game RTP is different and is determined by the math of the game. Let’s see what casino games are better in terms of RTP below:

- Slot machines 85-98%
- Poker 95-99%
- Baccarat 85.6-98.6%
- Craps 86.1-98.7%
- Video poker 92-99%

The situation with slot machines or slots is interesting. Unlike table games, the percentage of returns in some of slot games can be adjusted by casino staff. However, the law states that the RTP of any casino game cannot be less than 85%.

No game can have a return percentage greater than 99,9%. Otherwise, casinos would be at a loss and players would be winning all the time. By the way, you can play [Casinonic slots](https://www.casinonic.com/en-AU/games/slots) since they have the largest RTP among all other games.
