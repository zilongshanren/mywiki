This week on the podcast, I sat down with Harry Tuffs from Pixel Trickery who is currently working on the kickstarter for A House of Many Doors.


We started with talking about Harry’s background and how he is the first person to take part in Failbetter Game’s Incubation program to help new developers get started with their projects and then Harry’s start in the Game Industry with Pixel Trickery. Then we dove into A House of Many Doors; talking about the gameplay and what Harry has planned for the game now that the kickstarter is a success.

Our final topics was more about crowdfunding and early access with Harry’s thoughts on kickstarter and then talking about Early Access.
