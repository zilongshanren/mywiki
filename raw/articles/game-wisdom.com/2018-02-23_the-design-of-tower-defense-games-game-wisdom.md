This week on the cast, I spoke with Tikiri Wicks whose team ChillX is trying to fund an elevated take on Tower Defense design with the kickstarter for Defense Task Force.


We started off talking about Tikiri’s background and the state of the Game Industry in New Zealand before going all in discussing tower defense design. We talked about the best examples of the genre and some of the complexities of design. To wrap things up, we discussed Defense Task Force and how its trying to do something different with the genre.
