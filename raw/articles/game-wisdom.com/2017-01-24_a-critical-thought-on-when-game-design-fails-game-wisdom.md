Today’s Critical Thought focuses on one of the hardest parts of game design: Building and Designing a Game at the same time. Video games are a combination of programming and game design, and sometimes these two elements don’t come together. I talked about some of the big ones for me over the last few years, and how crowdfunding has in some cases made things harder for game developers.
