## What is 5G?

The term 5G stands for the fifth generation of networks. It is faster than 4G and LTE. With this, users get quicker downloads and uploads. It also reduces delays, making things happen almost instantly. Thanks to its larger capacity, more data can flow simultaneously. For users, 5G technology means smoother play and quicker responses on their screens. This network upgrade helps improve the overall experience on mobile devices. It makes things like game optimization possible, allowing more detailed and interactive activities.
