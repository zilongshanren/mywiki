This week for our podcast we went live with the guys over at Yacht Club Games to discuss game design and the past, present and future of Shovel Knight.

As standard for our live casts, we went long talking about the development of Shovel Knight and how Yacht Club expanded on the game with two, and eventually three expansions. We also talked about how Yacht Club works as a studio and the lessons they’ve learned over the years.
