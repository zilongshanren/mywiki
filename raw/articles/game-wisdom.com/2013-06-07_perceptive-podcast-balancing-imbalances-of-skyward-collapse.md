This week on the cast, Ken and I welcomed back Chris Park from Arcen Games. For the first part of the cast we talked about Arcen’s new development strategy and the state of Indie and AAA developers. Around the 35 minute mark, we transitioned to talking about Arcen’s latest hit: *Skyward Collapse*.

From how the game came to be, to what is coming with the first free content patch and paid expansion. This week was also my first time editing the cast and I hopefully caught most of my heavy breathing. Ken may sound a bit louder than normal as he was not using his regular headset.
