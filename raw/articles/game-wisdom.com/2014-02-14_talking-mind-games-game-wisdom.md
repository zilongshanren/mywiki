This week on the cast I was joined by psychologist and Game Industry analyst Jamie Madigan for a discussion of recent industry topics as well as his upcoming book on psychology and the Game Industry.


After introductions we talked a little about the console market and what our thoughts are on Valve’s foray into it with the Steambox. We also cracked the code and managed to deduce what the next *Half Life* game will be, even though it may not come within our lifetimes.

News this week, we discussed EA’s continued use of monetization with the recent scandal over the *Dungeon Keeper* mobile game. Jamie brought up *Flappy Bird* and how it seemed to drive the internet crazy… or crazier than usual. I brought up the recent F2P game *Loadout* which Jamie and I have both started playing this week.

For our main topic we discussed Jamie’s upcoming book and how it will use psychology to examine a variety of topics regarding game design, consumer behavior and game development. I also picked Jamie’s brain on the topics of sexism in the Game Industry as well as the always popular topic: Are video games making people violent?
