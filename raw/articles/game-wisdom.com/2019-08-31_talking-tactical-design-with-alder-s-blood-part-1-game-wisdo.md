We spoke with Michal of Shockwork Games about the development of Alder’s Blood and designing an original tactical strategy game.

We started off with discussing how the project came to be, as well as the original concept of the game. We talked about our thoughts on tactical strategy design and some of the major names of the genre. Finally, we talked more about the kickstarter itself and this was before they hit their initial funding. The cast was so long that it had to be split into two parts.
