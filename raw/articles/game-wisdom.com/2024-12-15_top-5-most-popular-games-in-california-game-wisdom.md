## The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Released in May of 2023, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is the latest installment from the Zelda franchise which dates back to 1986. The plot of this game has been hailed as one of the best of any Zelda game, and players search for Princess Zelda as Link and battle against Ganondorf.

Reviews have been varied, but that hasn’t stopped diehard fans from enjoying the much-loved characters from the classic game.

## Minecraft

While many new games focus on graphics to dazzle their fans, Mincraft’s blocky, retro style has seen it become the best-selling game ever with [more than 300 million units](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/technology-67105983) being sold since it was initially released in 2011.

The game focuses on exploration and creation but also offers various different modes where players can fight, team up with, or compete against other players.

Defined as a sandbox survival title, Minecraft has been near the top of the charts for over a decade now, and things don’t look to be changing any time soon with new audiences being drawn in and investing in the extensive selection of merch that goes along with it.

## Fortnite

One of the biggest appeals for fans of Fortnite is that the game is free to play. Of course, players can spend money on the game, but since its 2017 release, its accessibility has been a driving force.

Several different versions have been released since with players collaborating in open-world environments to survive against computer-generated characters or other live players.

Players can also choose to compete in single-player mode, and its versatility has been key to its prolonged success.

## League of Legends

Since 2009, League of Legends has been one of the most popular multiplayer games with players attempting to destroy the base of their opponents. The game combines battle, RPG, and strategy aspects to create an immersive and exciting gaming experience.
