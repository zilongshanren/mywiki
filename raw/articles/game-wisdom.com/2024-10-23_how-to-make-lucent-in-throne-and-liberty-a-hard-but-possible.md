## Trait Extraction: A Profitable but Challenging Method

Earning Lucent through **Trait Extraction** is a viable option, but it’s far from a quick or guaranteed way to build wealth. Every piece of gear in *Throne and Liberty* comes with random traits, and extracting and selling these traits on the auction house can sometimes bring in Lucent. However, the process relies heavily on luck, patience, and careful market research, making it more difficult than it initially sounds.

For example, when you acquire a new piece of gear, you’ll need to check if it has a valuable trait—like **Heavy Attack Chance**. If it does, you can extract the trait and list it on the auction house. However, many traits are not highly valued, and spending time extracting and listing those can result in very little profit, making this method far from foolproof.

## The Slow Process of Extracting and Selling Traits

The process of extracting traits requires time and resources:

- First, you need to find an item with a valuable trait.
- Use
**Trait Extraction Stones**to extract the trait from the item. - Head to the auction house to sell the extracted trait.

One of the challenges is that **Trait Extraction Stones** are not easy to come by, and you can run out quickly if you’re extracting traits from several items. Furthermore, the auction house takes a 22% cut from your sales, so even if you manage to sell a trait, you’ll end up with less than you initially expected.

In some cases, it can take a long time for your items to sell, especially if you’re trying to sell traits that aren’t in high demand. So while Trait Extraction can generate Lucent, it’s a slow and often frustrating way to do so.

## Obtaining Trait Extraction Stones: Limited Availability

The bottleneck for making Lucent via Trait Extraction often comes from the scarcity of **Trait Extraction Stones**. You need these stones to extract traits from gear, and obtaining them can be quite challenging:

**Exploration Codex**: Completing certain exploration milestones will reward you with a small number of extraction stones, but this is far from enough for regular extraction activities.**Contract Merchants**: You can also get Trait Extraction Stones from completing**contracts**. However, completing contracts daily can be repetitive and time-consuming, and the stones you earn are limited to 420 per week.

Given the limited availability of these stones, it can take weeks before you accumulate enough to regularly extract and sell traits. This means that earning Lucent in large quantities is likely out of reach for most casual players, making this method particularly difficult to sustain over time.

## The Auction House: An Unforgiving Marketplace

Selling traits on the auction house is not as straightforward as it seems. The *Throne and Liberty* marketplace is competitive, and prices can fluctuate depending on the trait’s demand. For example, traits like **Heavy Attack Chance** or **Crit Chance** are often sought after, but it can be hard to predict when and how much they will sell for. If you misprice your item or the market becomes saturated with similar traits, your item could sit unsold for days, or even weeks.

Additionally, the 22% tax on every sale makes it harder to turn a profit. If you sell a trait for 100 Lucent, you’ll only receive 78 Lucent after the auction house fee. Over time, this can significantly cut into your earnings, making the process of building Lucent a grind.

## Monster Farming: Low Rewards, High Time Investment

Another method some players try is **monster farming** in areas like the **Shadowed Crypts**. Farming monsters can sometimes drop gear with traits that can be extracted, or items like **Precious Rubrics** that can be sold. However, the drop rates for valuable items are extremely low, and many of the drops—like rubrics—have fallen in value. In the past, rubrics could sell for hundreds of Lucent, but today they might only fetch 30-40 Lucent per piece.

Given the amount of time needed to farm monsters and the low chance of getting high-value items, this method is not particularly effective for most players. While it can be used as a supplement to Trait Extraction, it’s far from a reliable way to earn significant amounts of Lucent.

## Guild Gear Sales: A Method Reserved for the Few

Another way players can make Lucent is through **guild gear sales**, but this method is only available to those who are part of an active, high-ranking guild. Participating in **guild events** like boss farming can result in valuable gear drops that can be sold on the auction house for thousands of Lucent. However, these items are often shared among guild members, and you need to be in a guild with a fair distribution system to benefit from these sales.

If you’re not in a top-tier guild or you don’t have a leadership position, this method is likely out of reach. Even if you do get access to high-value items, guilds often split profits among members, reducing your overall earnings.

## Understanding Trait Values: The Importance of Market Research

Before you sell any trait, it’s essential to understand its value. Some traits, like **Heavy Attack Chance**, **Crit Chance**, and **Hit Chance**, tend to be more valuable and sought after by players looking to optimize their gear. However, many other traits are much less valuable and may not sell for much, if at all.

Researching the auction house prices for each trait is key. If you list a trait without checking the market, you risk either underpricing it—missing out on potential Lucent—or overpricing it, leading to it sitting unsold for weeks. The unpredictability of the market makes this process even more challenging for players trying to earn Lucent.
