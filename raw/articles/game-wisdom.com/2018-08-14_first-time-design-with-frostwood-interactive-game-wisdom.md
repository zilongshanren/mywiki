For this cast, I spoke with Armaan who is a first time developer currently trying to get his game Rainswept funded on IndieGogo.


We talked about Armaan’s background and why he wanted to create Rainswept as his first game. We discussed adventure and storytelling design.

As of today, Rainswept has one more day left for its Indiegogo campaign, and we spent the back-half of the cast talking about it.
