## The top 7 AWP skins in CS:GO

## Dragon Lore

The Dragon Lore is one of the most iconic skins in CS:GO. It displays an intricate dragon design and is based on a real-life design of a firearm. The skin is highly valued for its rarity and unique appearance, making it one of the most expensive skins in the game. The skin’s popularity is above 90%, according to the anonymous surveys conducted [among CS:GO players](https://www.esports.net/news/csgo/top-5-csgo-players/).

## Medusa

The Medusa skin is another valuable skin in the game. It has a striking serpent design that covers the entire weapon. The skin is also among the most expensive CS:GO weapon skins. The rarity, popularity, and availability of the skin can be the main factors, affecting the price of the skin.

## Asiimov

The Asiimov skin is a futuristic-looking skin that has a digital camouflage pattern. The demand curves for this skin are upward since its release. The skin is also among the oldest AWP skins in the game. With limited availability, the price and popularity of the skin will, most likely, increase in 2023, as well.

Hyper Beast

The Hyper Beast has a colorful, abstract design with an intimidating beast-like creature emblazoned on the side. The skin is moderately priced and is quite popular among players, particularly those who enjoy bold and eye-catching designs.

## Lightning Strike

The Lightning Strike has a sleek, black design with a lightning bolt graphic on the side. While not as commonly used by professional players as the Hyper Beast, the Lightning Strike AWP has been used by players such as kennyS and Stewie2k. It’s possible that this skin will continue to be popular in 2023, particularly among players who want clean and simple skin for their AWP.

## Fade

The Fade AWP skin has a rainbow-colored gradient that fades from one color to another. The Fade has been used by players such as kennyS and FalleN, and in fact, these players have contributed to the popularity of the skin among the CS:GO community. The skin has a moderate price, and taking the detailed design, popularity, and limited availability into account, we can expect it to be trendy in 2023.

## Desert Hydra

The Desert Hydra skin is a detailed skin that was painted with a desert-like color scheme with a serpent design. Comparing it to the other skins from the same series, we can say that the skin is undervalued, and will probably have an increase in price in 2023.

## How to obtain AWP skins in CS:GO

There are several methods for obtaining an AWP skin in CS:GO. Each method has its advantages and disadvantages, and players can choose the method that best suits their needs and preferences.

## Trading

One way to obtain an AWP skin is by trading with other players. Trading allows players to exchange items in their inventory for other items of equal or similar value. The advantage of trading is that it can be a cost-effective way to obtain skins, especially if the player has items in their inventory that they no longer want or need.

## Buying from the Steam Community Market

Another way to obtain an AWP skin is by purchasing it from the [Steam Community Market](https://steamcommunity.com/market/). The advantage of buying from the market is that it offers a wide range of skins at different prices, making it easier to find the desired skin. Additionally, purchases are made with real money, so players can choose to spend as much or as little as they want.

## Opening weapon cases

Players can also obtain AWP skins by opening weapon cases. Weapon cases can be purchased or obtained as drops during gameplay, and they contain a random skin of varying rarity. Opening cases is the excitement of potentially obtaining a rare skin, which can be a thrilling experience for some players. However, the chances of getting the desired skin can be low, and the cost of opening cases can add up quickly.

## Obtaining skins from in-game drops

Lastly, players can obtain AWP skins from in-game drops. Drops are awarded to players for participating in matches and are random. Drops require no additional effort on the player’s part.
