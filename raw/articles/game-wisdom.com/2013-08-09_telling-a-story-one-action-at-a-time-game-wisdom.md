This week on the Perceptive Podcast we welcomed Chris Gardiner who worked on the browser based game: *Fallen London* and is currently developing his own game — *Below*.


We discussed the challenges of free-to-play games and the devaluing of video games that has been happening. Also what it meant to write for the browser game *Machine Cares,* that was made to help talk about teen suicide.
