**Popular Esports Games by Country**

**United States**

In the United States, games like “Fortnite,” “Call of Duty,” and “League of Legends” dominate the esports scene. The diversity of gaming preferences reflects the country’s broad interest in both traditional and innovative gameplay mechanics.

**South Korea**

South Korea is a powerhouse in the esports industry, with “StarCraft II” and “League of Legends” as major titles. The country’s infrastructure and cultural embrace of gaming have propelled these games to the forefront.

**China**

In China, “Honor of Kings” (known as “Arena of Valor” globally) and “Dota 2” are incredibly popular. The vast player base and investment in esports infrastructure have made these games central to the country’s esports narrative.

**Europe**

Across Europe, “CS:GO” and “FIFA” are staples, reflecting a strong preference for shooter games and sports simulations. The continent’s diverse gaming culture supports a wide range of esports activities.

**Brazil**

In Brazil, “Free Fire” and “League of Legends” are exceptionally popular, driven by a vibrant community and the accessibility of mobile gaming. Brazil’s passion for esports is evident in its enthusiastic fanbase.
