**Write Amazing Music**

This may sound obvious, but you have to make sure that your music is absolutely amazing. People will often tell you that your music has to stand out, and it really does, but the main and most important thing would be for you to get the quality right. Even if your music is good, you still have to make sure that the mix is right and that the production is on-point. If you don’t, then you may find that you end up struggling to really connect with your audience. The best video game music has the ability to move people, so keep this in mind. If you want to benefit yourself here, then you may want to have some good monitoring speakers. Playing around with EQ and compression will also help you out here. When you have a basic level of compression, you can then begin to experiment much more.

**Distribute**

When you have produced some music that you are well and truly happy with, you then need to work on getting it out there. Make it available for free, if you really have to. The main thing that you have to do is just get yourself out there. The more available you make your music, the better. Far too many musicians neglect marketing, and if you have something that you believe is really good then you need to get as many people to notice as you can. Going to industry events really will help you out here, especially if there are some aimed at indie developers. This will help you to get a handle of what is going on and it will also help you to really focus on what you need to be doing in order to get noticed.

**Think about the Atmosphere**

If you ask any gamer what they believe is the most important thing in a video game soundtrack, they will probably tell you that it is the atmosphere. They want you to try and convey an emotion, so that they can become engrossed in the game and so that they can also invest in the game. One of the best games out there right now is the Last of Us, on the[ Playstation](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/) console. The story is an emotional rollercoaster as you follow the two lead characters who unexpectedly meet during a zombie apocalypse, but it’s the soundtrack that makes this game stand out. It’s rare that a soundtrack matches a story so perfectly, but in this instance, it worked wonders.

**Optimization**

Whenever you find yourself working on a commercial project, you may find that you just feel rushed. When you are working on your own music, you need to find patterns that will help you with what you are doing. If you are always using the same EQ settings for the sub-bass or if you use the same compressor then save them so you have a preset. This will help you to make sure that you have consistency throughout the entire soundtrack.

**Let the Game choose the Score**

If you are working on a huge commercial project, then there is a high chance that the brief will be tight. If you are working on a game made by a small indie creator, then you will have a lot more leeway. At this point, you need to put your ego to one side and really look at the game that you are working on. Again, it helps to think about the audience here, and what they are looking for when they are playing the [game](https://game-wisdom.com/videos/makes-game-frustrating-play) at that moment. If you do this then there’s no reason why you shouldn’t be able to get the best result out of your project.

**Work with the Sound Design**

There will have to be sound other than just your music. Where possible, you need to try and work with your audio technicians so that you can make sure that you are not actually competing with them at some of the key moments in the process. At the very minimum, it’s worth talking to them to make sure that you go over the sonic concepts for the game. If you do this then you will be able to get everyone on the same page and you can also make sure that everyone is comfortable with the concept overall.

**Mix and Match**

One thing that will help your game soundtrack to stand apart is mixing and matching what you love. If you love classical music, then don’t be afraid to intertwine this with a romance scene in the game. If you love heavy metal, then you can incorporate this into a battle scene to get across the aggression that you are trying to set in the game. Little things like this can help you to unite two different concepts together beautifully, so keep that in mind. Of course, you need to make sure that you are appropriate with your music, but at the same time, you also need to forge your own path and you need to try and identify what you love. If you are able to do this, then it will almost certainly come across in the music and it will also help you to create something really special.

Another thing that you need to do if you want to become a better video game composer is focus on the character. You have to make sure that you channel what the character is feeling or thinking.
