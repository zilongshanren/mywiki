For this cast, I spoke with Michel who is the lead designer of the game Mondo Museum for a talk about bullfrog sims, management games, and how we’ll be running a museum.


We started off talking about Michel’s background and the management sim genre as a whole. After that, we focused more on what he has planned for Mondo Museum; this podcast was originally recorded in September and some details may have changed since then.
