In this episode of my live play through the Darkest Dungeon, I managed to get my first hero up to level 3 and at the same time take him out of the running for lower level missions. With an Occultist finally in my group I began to assemble my A-Team while using a powered up Leper to start taking charge. In this run I also fought the very first boss of the game and managed to deliver one hell of a fight.

But after that I got too cocky and the game hit back with a few missions I failed. Now with a lot of people stressed out, I need to figure out a way to get some money fast and start preparing a B-Squad.

Please be aware that starting with the next episode, all live-plays will be happening at 10 EST not 9. I did this to make it easier for me schedule wise.
