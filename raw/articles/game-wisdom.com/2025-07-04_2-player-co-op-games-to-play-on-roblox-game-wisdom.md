If you have that one person you play with every single day, chances are, you have looked for every 2-player co-op game out there that you can play with your friend. Most of us have been there, time and time again, and it’s actually one of the best feelings you can have. There are lots of different co-op games to play: survival, obby, tycoon, roleplaying, and even adventure games. Roblox is home to some of the best games, where you can spend hours and hours [playing Roblox](https://kick.com/category/Roblox) with your friends or by yourself.


As someone who has been playing 2-player co-op games recently, I have found the best 2-player co-op games on Roblox that I think you and your duo will have a blast playing. Just keep in mind that you both will need to have [ fast internet](https://golatinotv.com/en/dish-internet/) to enjoy these games. Let’s begin!

**Pull a Friend**

Pull a friend is one of the first games we’ve played together, and it was so chaotic. Well, it was at first, especially if you’re playing with lots of people on the server. The game basically works by having one player sit in a cart where they control the brakes. The other player pulls the cart in different directions to navigate through different obstacles. There are several checkpoints throughout the game, and some of them are really challenging to reach. However, there are free spins where you can get revives and stronger brakes. But if the free ones aren’t enough, you can always purchase them using Robux.

**Chained Together **

Yep, Roblox also has its own version of Chained Together. It’s not exactly the same, but the mechanics are completely similar. It’s not exactly a 2-player game because the game itself allows up to 6 players. However, it can be played by duos. Chained Together literally chains you and your partner together as you try to escape hell. You will go through different obstacles. Some are easy, while others are just completely challenging. A [ fast internet provider](https://planetdish.com/dish-network-internet/) is required for this game.

**Carry Me**

Carry Me is a game that requires you to carry each other to progress throughout the map. If you’ve ever watched a movie or TV show while [ TV streaming](https://godirectv.com/streaming-packages/) about a person carrying another person like a sack of rice, that’s how this game works. The core mechanic is simple: one player carries the other to reach higher platforms or cross dangerous areas, then you switch roles to progress further. It’s a really good game for duos who love testing their coordination and communication skills while having a good laugh.

**Together**

Like Chained Together, Together is not an exclusive 2-player game. However, you can play it with only two players. It’s a side-scrolling game that requires you and your teammate to work together to go through different maps and levels. Some are easy, while others are just really challenging. There was one level that literally look us 30 minutes to finish, although that was partly my fault because I kept falling. Regardless, it’s a really good game to kill time. And if you ever want to add more players in the future, you can do so as the game supports up to 6 players.

**The Bottom Line**

These are just some of the best 2-player co-op games that you can play on Roblox with your friend. There are actually a lot more, but it will depend on your game preferences. If you’re just looking for games that would kill time while leaving you and your friend laughing until you cry, you’ll enjoy these games we’ve shared. Happy playing!
