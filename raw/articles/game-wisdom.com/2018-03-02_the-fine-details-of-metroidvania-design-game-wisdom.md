For this week’s podcast, I spoke with Indie Game Designer “Shield” who is working on the Metroidvania Stonicorn about his thoughts on the genre.


We started off with introductions and how Shield got started with Indie Game Development. From there we discussed our thoughts on Metroidvania design and the difference between good and bad versions of it. That also inferred on Shield’s idea for his game Stonicorn.
