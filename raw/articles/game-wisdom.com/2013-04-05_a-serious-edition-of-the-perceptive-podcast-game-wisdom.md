This week Evan, Ken and I talked about *Rock Band*, Star Trek vs. Star Wars, the closing of Lucasarts and about the right and wrong ways to write “dumb humor.” For the second half of the cast we unintentionally got very serious, as we discussed everything from education, childhood development and 9/11, while talking about historical accuracy in games. The extra seriousness equated to a longer than normal cast.
