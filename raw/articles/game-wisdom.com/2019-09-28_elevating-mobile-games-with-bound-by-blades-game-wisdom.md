For this cast, I sat down with Justin Thompson who has been working on the game Bound by Blades for the past few years, and is now going on kickstarter to try and sell the concept of an elevated mobile game.


We started with Justin’s background in the mobile space and how that inferred on his vision for Bound by Blades. With the game on kickstarter, we didn’t have a lot to talk about in terms of the content there, but we discussed what he and his team have in mind for making an elevated mobile game.
