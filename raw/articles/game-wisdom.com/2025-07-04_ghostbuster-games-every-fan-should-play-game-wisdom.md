A true Ghostbusters fan has always dreamed of suiting up, grabbing a proton pack, and catching ghosts themselves. But since it’s only a movie, most of us just had to imagine what it would feel like to hunt ghosts across New York City. But that’s before these games started popping up and giving us the firsthand experience of what it’s like to chase down ghosts right from our own living rooms. So if you like to explore haunted buildings and fight paranormal and supernatural forces, you’ll love the games we’re about to share with you.


So if you’re looking for an experience beyond [ TV streaming](https://godirectv.com/streaming-packages/) Ghostbusters, you gotta try these Ghostbuster games. Ready? Let’s start!

**Ghostbusters: The Video Game Remastered**

This game is probably the closest thing fans can get to being in an actual Ghostbusters movie. It was originally released in 2009 (which is considered one of the best Ghostbusters games) and remastered in 2019. It’s known for having the real voices and looks of the original cast, such as Bill Murray, Harold Ramis, Ernie Hudson, and Dan Aykroyd. In this game, you’ll play as a new rookie recruit who joins the team during a new wave of ghost activity in NYC.

You can get the remastered version for $9.74 on Steam. It’s smoother and more visually appealing than the 2009 version, but of course, nothing beats that classic, still. It’s also a single-player game, so you don’t need high-speed internet when playing this. However, when you install the game, you’ll need a decent connection, regardless of whether you have fiber, cable, or [ satellite internet](https://satelliteforinternet.com/).

**Ghostbusters: Spirits Unleashed Ecto Edition**

If you’re looking for a multiplayer Ghostbusters game, you need to try Ghostbusters: Spirits Unleashed Ecto Edition. You can get this game on Steam for $7.99. In this game, you can either be a ghost or a Ghostbuster in a 4v1 setup. That means four players will team up as Ghostbusters to catch one player-controlled ghost. The entire game is easy to pick up, especially if you like team-based games. In this game, you’ll need a fast internet connection. If you’re existing internet connection isn’t up to the task, it may be time to look into better internet plans like [ Boost Infinite deals](https://wireless.planetdish.com/boost-infinite/deals/), for example.

**GhostControl Inc.**

If you like strategy games, you might be able to enjoy GhostControl Inc. It’s a fun strategy game where you are tasked with running your own ghost-hunting business. All you have to do is hire a team, buy equipment, upgrade your gear, and respond to ghost sightings around a haunted city. Just like any business owner, you need to keep your customers happy and grow your business while catching ghosts for a living. Also, the game has a retro-style look, which means you need to plan your moves carefully. You can get GhostControl Inc. on Steam for $18.99.

**New Ghostbusters II**

New Ghostbusters II is an old-school game that lets you bust ghosts the easiest way. It’s based on the Ghostbusters II movie and was made for the NES and Game Boy. You pick two characters from the team and go around New York to catch ghosts. One Ghostbuster will zap the ghost, while the other traps it. It’s easy to play and has that classic retro game vibe we used to enjoy way back. The levels are based on scenes from the movie, so it’s a cool throwback for fans.

**The Bottom Line**

That’s all we’ve got for you right now. If you’re looking for your first Ghostbusters game to play, we can swear by these games. Just make sure that you’re choosing one that’s aligned with your preferences. These games are paid, so you wouldn’t want to waste your money buying the wrong game. Have fun playing!
