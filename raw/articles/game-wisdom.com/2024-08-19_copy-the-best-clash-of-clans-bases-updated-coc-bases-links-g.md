## Base Design Strategies for Each Townhall Level

### TH3 to TH6 – Early Game Base Design

- In initial levels, your home village requires a base design to protect townhall so it must be placed at the center of the base and secured by defenses around it.
- Dividing the base into compartments to slow down enemy troops.
- Place resource storages inside the walls and surround by defenses to prevent easy loot.

### TH7 to TH9 – Mid-Game Base Design

- At these levels, air troops are unlocked so your base must be designed strategically to prevent air attacks.
- Place the advanced defenses like x-bow and hidden tesla near townhall and resources.
- The bases must have wisely placed traps like giant bomb and seeking air mines.

### TH10 to TH12 – Advanced Base Design

- Place inferno towers in the center of the base to protect the townhall and key buildings.
- Position the multi-target and single-target defenses to protect the base from heavy troops.
- At TH11 you have unlocked the eagle artillery and it must be placed inside the base walls to maximize its effectiveness.

### TH13 to TH16 – End-Game Base Design

- Upgrading to level 13, townhall can heavily defend itself with giga inferno so it should be at the center of the base.
- Place powerful defenses like scattershots to cover large areas of the base.
- At these higher townhall levels, create multiple layers of defenses to weaken the opponents’ attacks.

## Builder Hall Base Design Strategies

As compared to the home village, designing an effective builder hall base requires a different approach. Here are some key strategies:

- It’s important to design a base that can defend against common attack strategies while being difficult in one-on-one attacks.
- Centralized defenses is another key to master in designing COC builder bases. For this, place strong defenses like crusher, giant cannon, and mega tesla in the center of the base.
- Reaching the builder hall level 6 opens doors to a new underwater base known as multistage base or base 2.0. Now you have to design more challenging COC BH bases.
- Wise trap placement like push traps, mines, mega mines and spring traps to distract the attackers.
- Since builder base battles are timed, it’s important to design a base that prevents your opponent from achieving a higher percentage of destruction than you.
