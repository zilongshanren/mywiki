For this cast I spoke with Matt who is the owner of Alderon Games about his upcoming game Path of Titans, and their decision to make their own platform/store for the game as opposed to using Steam.


We spoke about the design of the game and the work that has gone into it for the first part. In the back half we focused more on the storefront itself and some of the lessons Matt has learned about developing the architecture for it.
