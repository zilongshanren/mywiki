Every online slot game has an important key element: volatility. This measures how often a game pays out. It also determines the size of these payouts. High-volatility slots offer larger, less frequent wins. These are for risk-takers. Low-volatility slots give smaller, more frequent payouts. So choose these if you prefer steadier gameplay. In all cases, whether you prefer high or low volatility, you should understand its concept. Why? Because this will help you tailor your slot game experience to your preferences.


**How RNG Ensures Fair Play**

Random Number Generators (RNGs) ensure fairness in online slots. But the big question is, how do they ensure that? RNGs, in fact, simply produce entirely random outcomes for each spin. These systems continuously generate numbers. They do so even when no one is playing. This makes every result entirely unpredictable. The size of your bet? Doesn’t matter. How many times did you play? Also, doesn’t matter. This is because the RNG guarantees that each spin is independent. This keeps the gameplay fair for all players. The randomness also keeps the experience fresh. This applies whether you’re new to online slots or a seasoned player.

**The Importance of RTP in Slot Games**

Return to Player (RTP) is another important aspect of slot machines. This one indicates the percentage of wagered money a game will return to players over time. An RTP of 96% means that for every $100 wagered, players could expect a return of $96. However, this is calculated over millions of spins. No slot can guarantee a win. So, you need to understand RTP for a simple reason. It helps you as a player [make more informed decisions](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/informed-decision) when you choose a game.
