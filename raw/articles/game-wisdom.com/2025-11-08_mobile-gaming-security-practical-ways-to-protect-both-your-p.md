Mobile gaming is no longer a niche pastime – in fact, it’s the fastest-growing segment of the gaming industry, with players spending hours immersed in titles like the popular Call of Duty: Mobile or Genshin Impact*.* But with a bigger audience comes a wider threat landscape. From phishing attempts disguised as tournament invites to malicious app downloads, cybercriminals are increasingly targeting unsuspecting mobile gamers. Read on for some practical tips on how to protect your phone, data, and gaming accounts – whether you’re competing for tournament glory or just passing time in the bus queue.


**Play it safe: How to protect your connection on public Wi-Fi**

It’s easy to ignore network risks when all you want to do is immerse yourself in a game when you’re out and about. Public Wi-Fi is convenient, but it’s a notorious trap for hackers looking to intercept login details or payment data. It’s best to stick to your mobile data plan or use a private network whenever possible. If you must connect through public Wi-Fi, route your traffic through a [VPN for iPhone](https://protonvpn.com/download-ios) or Android device to encrypt your connection and mask your location. A VPN adds an essential layer of protection – especially critical if your game automatically syncs sensitive account information.

**Choose trustworthy apps and manage permissions**

Vet your games before you play. Only download games from official app stores, and double-check developer names and reviews before installing any updates. Shady apps often request excessive permissions (such as access to your microphone, contacts, or camera) which they then exploit to harvest data. Whatever you do, never sideload games from unofficial sources – those ‘early access’ [Android Package Kits can hide malware](https://www.darkreading.com/endpoint-security/hook-android-trojan-ransomware-attacks) designed to drain your battery, mine crypto, or steal credentials.

**Keep your game accounts safe from scams**

Most compromises happen when players reuse passwords or click unverified links. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible, and use [strong, unique passwords](https://www.forbes.com/sites/technology/article/how-to-make-strong-passwords/) for every game or platform. Be aware than scammers increasingly mimic mobile game payment pages or send phishing links promising rare skins or in-game bonuses. Use the built-in app store protections to keep track of purchases, and turn off one-tap payment authorization if kids will be sharing the device with you.

**Update, secure, and back up your device**

Always keep your phone’s operating system and games fully updated – vendor patches are designed to fix vulnerabilities that hackers exploit. Install a trusted mobile security app to detect risky downloads and monitor unusual behavior, such as odd battery spikes or unauthorized data use. Regularly back up your saves to cloud storage or your computer – players who maintain update routines and backups recover faster from attacks and data loss.

**Play smart, play secure**

You want mobile gaming to be fun – not a cyberhazard. The good news is that it takes just a few steps to prevent big losses later. Just secure your connection, choose apps wisely, and treat your phone like a gaming console to protect your data and enjoy your sessions worry-free.
