For this cast, I spoke with author and designer Matt Thomas about translating his fantasy novel series “Imperium Omni” into a videogame and going to kickstarter to fund the first part.


Despite having some problems with his voice, Matt was happy to talk about his time in the game industry, and his interest in RPG design. We spoke about some of the gameplay elements of the first game in the series and talked briefly about the kickstarter which should be up on the 28th. We’ll be doing a follow up cast with Matt at the end of January with the kickstarter going on and that will be announced formally soon.
