This week on the cast, I spoke with co-founder of Indie Bros and long-time member of the Indie Game scene Erik Johnson about what it’s like to be an Indie developer in the market, and how things have changed over the decade.


We spent our time talking about how things have changed for Indie development since the start of the decade and Erik’s main job at Arcen Games. Being an indie developer requires a lot more work these days, and it can be demanding for new developers.
