**Martingale Strategy**

The Martingale strategy is what you would call a progressive betting strategy. As you might gather based on the name, a progressive strategy includes placing a higher wager each time you lose a bet. The trick is to lower your wagers once you win. The idea is simply to come out on top at the eventual victory.

The Martingale strategy is pretty simple in theory. After every loss, a gambler should double their bet. The idea is that the wagers will make up for the losses at the eventual win. Martingale, as well as the next strategy we will discuss, can both be applied to slots, roulette, and even blackjack.

**The Fibonacci Strategy**

Like the Martingale strategy, the Fibonacci strategy is a progressive betting practice. Named and inspired by [the mathematical sequence](https://www.livescience.com/37470-fibonacci-sequence.html), the Fibonacci strategy dictates that every subsequent bet should total the sum of the two bets that preceded it. The following is a practical example of how the Fibonacci system would look, if you started with a hypothetical bet of $5:

$5 – $5 – $10 – $15 – $25 – $40 – $65 – $105

As you can see, the system is pretty simple. Though most gamblers favor the Martingale strategy, Fibonacci also gets quite a bit of play.
