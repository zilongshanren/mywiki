Recent statistics suggest that 201 million people in the US alone engage in gaming of some kind. With that in mind, here are four facts about crypto gaming that you have to know:


## Real Life Rewards

Crypto games are a new and innovative form of gaming that, by incorporating cryptocurrency, allows players to purchase in-game assets and extras. This is an excellent way to fuse the popular world of gaming with the rapidly developing realm of cryptocurrency. Such games give real-world value to various in-game assets and allow for a deeper level of immersion into their gaming worlds. They also ensure absolute ownership for the individual who purchases these assets using cryptocurrency, as opposed to previous in-game transactions that saw assets still belonging to the developers of the games. Such a move also allows for the games to affect the users outside of their play, as players themselves can sell their in-game assets for cryptocurrency, making a profit while gaming. All of this takes gaming away from its previous existence as a solely recreational activity, and instead allows individuals to play games for real-world rewards, also known as Play-to-Earn.

## Genres


In the world of gaming, there really is something for everyone, and this is true also when it comes to crypto gaming. Here I’ll go over some of the common genres of games, and provide examples of ones that use crypto.

- Massively Multiplayer Online (MMO)–These are the kinds of games perfect for playing with a large group of friends or trying your hand against strangers online. MMO games offer servers in which hundreds of different people across the world can play together in real-time.
[Popular MMO](https://www.pcgamesn.com/10-best-pc-mmos)games that incorporate crypto include Delysium and Undeads Games. - Role-Playing Games (RPG)– This is another popular genre in gaming. Some popular RPG crypto games right now include Gabby World and Knights of the Ether.
- Sandbox Games– These are games that provide users with a large amount of freedom and creativity as they play. These games often encourage players to explore the world and create as they go, making their own buildings and structures. These games allow for a lot of creative freedom and some really impressive results. The most famous sandbox game that incorporates crypto into its gameplay is The Sandbox.
