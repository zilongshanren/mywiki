For this cast, I spoke with Peter from Blackfox Studios who is currently working on the game Dreadlands. We discuss making a PVP focused tactical strategy game, and the lessons he learned from other examples in the genre when coming up with the different systems of Dreadlands.

This cast was recorded a few months ago and may not represent the current version of the game.
