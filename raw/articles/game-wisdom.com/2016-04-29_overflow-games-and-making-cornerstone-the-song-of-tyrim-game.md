This week on the cast, I sat down with Jonathan and Sam of Overflow Games to talk about their first Kickstarter completed game Cornerstone The Song of Tyrim.



We started off with introductions and how Jonathan and Sam got started and came up with the idea of Cornerstone. We talked about the state of the Game Industry and what’s going on in Sweden with game development.

We spent some time talking about the kickstarter itself and how the original one for Cornerstone failed and they went back to the drawing board for a success. I also asked them about their thoughts on both kickstarter and early access as a relatively new studio. There were some call quality issues possibly due to the connection that I tried to get rid of, but couldn’t get rid of it all.
