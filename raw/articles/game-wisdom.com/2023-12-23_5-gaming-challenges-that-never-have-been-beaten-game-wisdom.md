**3.** **The HALO Three-Peat**

HALO is tough, but HALO sessions in LASO mode are close to impossible. LASO rules make the notorious skulls so fierce that they become super-resilient or even invisible. The first time someone managed to complete the whole game without dying a single time was in 2022. In case you don’t know, that’s the year when the [HALO](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/halo) trilogy officially turned adult.

The HALO impossible challenge takes the task to a higher level – you must go through three games in a row without losing a single life. Of course, you need to do it according to the LASO playbook.

**4.** **The TMNT Jump**

Back in 1989, NES published its Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles for a couple of platforms (Amiga and DOS). The game features many entertaining elements, at least from the 20th century perspective. However, one of them is truly unbreakable.

There’s a situation in which players have to jump over the sewer in order to cross one particular street in NY. This happens at the initial level, so you’d expect the jump to be routine business for most users. However, there’s an issue – the ceiling isn’t high enough for TMNTs to jump over the hole, so the actual task becomes impossible.
