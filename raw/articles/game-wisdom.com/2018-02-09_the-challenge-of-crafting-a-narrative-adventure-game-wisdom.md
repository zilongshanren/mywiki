For this recorded developer interview, I spoke with Ben Wander who has developed the game A Cast of Distrust. We talked about his time in the industry, as well as the work that goes into writing a narrative for a video game.

We spoke about the “walking simulator” genre and what it means to write a video game where the story is the focus. Be sure to check out my [first look on the game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i2kFeSf5rA0), and you can [pick it up on Steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/717610/A_Case_of_Distrust/).
