## What Factors Affect CS2 Skin Prices

Several key factors influence skin prices, including the following:

**Rarity**

Rarity is one of the most important elements affecting skin prices. Skins are grouped into tiers based on how likely they are to drop, ranging from common (like Consumer Grade) to extremely rare (such as Covert or Contraband). The rarer the item, the fewer of them exist in the game, which increases their market value. Players are often willing to pay a premium for rare skins simply because they are harder to get, making them more exclusive and desirable for both use and trading.

**Design**

The overall look and artistic quality of a skin can have a huge impact on how much players are willing to pay for it. A skin’s color scheme, visual theme, detailing, and animation (in the case of special finishes) can significantly affect its value. Players often want skins that reflect their personal taste or help them stand out in-game. As a result, even among skins with similar rarity, those with more attractive and appealing designs often sell for higher prices.

**Popularity**

Even if a skin isn’t extremely rare or detailed, its popularity can cause a major spike in its price. Skins that are regularly used by well-known streamers or professional players tend to become trendy. Social media, YouTube content, and esports events can all influence how desirable a skin becomes. Once demand increases, prices typically follow. In some cases, a skin can remain popular for years simply because of its reputation, even if newer and flashier alternatives become available later on.

## Everything You Need to Know About Skin Conditions

We intentionally left out one factor above, perhaps the most important one: skin condition. This will be discussed in the following section:

**What Is the Condition of a Skin**

Every CS2 skin comes with a specific condition that reflects its [CS2 skin wear level](https://bountystars.com/cs2-skin-wear-levels-float-values). These conditions range from Factory New to Battle-Scarred and determine how clean or worn a skin appears. A Factory New skin looks almost untouched, while Battle-Scarred skins show significant scratches and fading. The condition doesn’t impact gameplay, but it strongly affects the skin’s visual appeal and market value. Players often prefer skins in better condition, especially when collecting or showcasing high-end items.

**What Are Float Values**

Behind each skin’s condition is a hidden number called the float value. It ranges from 0.00 to 1.00 and shows how worn a skin actually is within its condition range. For example, a Minimal Wear skin can have a float closer to Factory New or closer to Field-Tested, and this small difference can greatly influence price. Traders and collectors often check float values to find the best-looking versions of a skin, especially for rare or expensive items.

**Does Skin Quality Worsen with Use**

A common question among players is whether skins degrade over time with use, and the answer is no. Once a skin drops or is unboxed, its condition and float value are permanently set. No matter how many matches a player uses the skin in, it will always look the same. This fixed wear system adds stability to the market and gives players confidence that their purchases or trades won’t lose value simply because they’re used frequently in-game.

## Best Skins with Desired Float Values

There are three specific skins we want to highlight, along with their respective conditions and float values:

**M4A4 | Neo-Noir**

The M4A4 | Neo-Noir is a popular Covert skin, perfect for players looking to add a unique touch to their loadout. On third-party sites, the standard version usually sells for around $50, while the Factory New [StatTrak](https://counterstrike.fandom.com/wiki/StatTrak%E2%84%A2) edition can reach up to $106. Considering its rarity and artistic style, it offers great value for the price. For this skin, we recommend the Minimal Wear StatTrak edition, which typically costs about $40.

**AWP | Sun in Leo**

If you want a stylish but budget-friendly AWP skin, the AWP | Sun in Leo is a great pick. Its rich purple color and zodiac-themed patterns give it a sleek and elegant appearance. Prices usually fall between $17 and $33, depending on the condition. Although it’s an Industrial-Grade skin, it is fairly common and easy to find on the market. For skins like this, it’s worth going for the highest quality, as the price difference between Battle-Scarred and Factory New isn’t that big. So if you’re comfortable with it, you can grab the Factory New version for just $34.

**Falchion Knife | Freehand**

Let’s take a look at a more affordable yet highly popular item: the Falchion Knife | Freehand. This knife stands out not only among other knife skins but across all skins due to its unique street art-inspired design. It features a dark purple blade adorned with white hand-drawn illustrations of small figures, arrows, lightning bolts, and wavy lines. Classified as a Covert skin, it is also available in a StatTrak version. Prices vary based on condition but generally start at around $135. If your budget allows, we recommend the Factory New non-StatTrak version, which usually costs about $168.
