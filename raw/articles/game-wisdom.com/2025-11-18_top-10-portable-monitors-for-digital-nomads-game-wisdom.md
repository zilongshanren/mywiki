Working away has altered our thinking on productivity. Since digital nomads seem to spend most of their time in airports, cafes, or co-working centers, having a single screen is insufficient. That is where portable monitors come in. Small, lightweight, and powerful, portable monitors have become an absolute necessity for people who need additional screen space when needed. Having a good portable laptop display is always important, whether you are editing a photo, calculating numbers, or just struggling to keep multiple tabs open.

There are so many available in the market that one would be lost on where to start. We have done the research and compared the best to save you time. These are the 10 best portable monitors that combine performance, portability, and cost.


# 1. UPERFECT 24 Inch Portable Monitor 4K

No, you need a large screen with a good ability to travel, but you are hard-pressed to find a better monitor than the UPERFECT [ 24 inch portable monitor 4k](https://uperfect.com/products/24-inch-4k-monitor). This very high-definition resolution exposes all the details and transforms them into the dream of creative people and specialists. Editing videos to switch between several spreadsheets provides sharp images and plenty of space.

What is unique about this perfect monitor is its flexibility. It can also be connected to laptops, tablets, and even game consoles with USB-C and HDMI connections. It also pulls power right out of your device, which means you do not have to carry additional adapters. Combine that with a spectacular color gamut, and one can understand why it is popular with digital nomads seeking desktop-like performance in a package lightweight enough to travel with.

# 2. ASUS ZenScreen MB16AC

ASUS has been pioneering portable displays, and the ZenScreen MB16AC is a perfect example. It is only 15.6 inches, which is slender enough to fit in most backpacks and provides ample space to multitask. It has a simple, hybrid-signal USB-C connection and is sleek and professional in its design.

It is lighter and smaller than the UPERFECT, and it is easy to transport, but it is more for everyday work than for serious creative work.

# 3. Lenovo ThinkVision M14

Lenovo ThinkVision M14 is designed to be portable. It weighs less than two pounds, and at only 14 inches, it is one of the lightest monitors that you can carry. Dual USB-C ports are easy to set up, and the fold-out stand is effortless yet efficient.

This model is perfect if you want a monitor that won’t weigh down your bag. While it’s not designed for high-resolution media editing, it’s more than capable for productivity tasks like emails, documents, and spreadsheets.

# 4. ViewSonic TD1655


Would you like to be a bit more interactive? ViewSonic TD1655 provides a touchscreen of 15.6 inches, where you can sketch, write, or draw on the screen or project your presentation. It can be linked to laptops and smartphones, which is a significant advantage for people using several devices on the road.

This monitor is really sensible if you are a creative or an educator who appreciates touch capabilities. It is a digital whiteboard in your bag.

# 5. AOC I1601FWUX


Affordable, simple, and reliable, the AOC I1601FWUX is a great option if you just need an extra screen. Its 15.6-inch Full HD display connects through USB-C, keeping cables to a minimum. It also comes with a smart cover that doubles as a stand.

It doesn’t offer 4K resolution or fancy features, but if your goal is productivity on a budget, it gets the job done.

# 6. Dell C1422H

The Dell C1422H is literally business portability. The Full HD display of 14 inches is very small, and it is very easy to carry with only 1.3 pounds. Anti-glare, also known as anti-reflective, finishing gives you clear visibility even when you are working in the open or in front of a bright window.

This is an intelligent design of a monitor that can be moved around by a professional who may need those other features to be usable.

# 7. HP EliteDisplay S14

HP made an early entry into the USB-C-powered [ portable laptop monitor](https://uperfect.com) market with the EliteDisplay S14, and it is also a solid option. It is small (14 inches) and business-friendly.

It is not equipped with all the recent features, but it is very easy to use and reliable, and is a good option when the professional just needs a trustworthy traveling companion.

# 8. LG Gram +View

The LG Gram +View adds a little additional resolution to the table. It has a 16-inch, WQXGA (2560×1600) display, which is sharper than standard Full HD displays. The aspect ratio of 16:10 also provides more space vertically, which is ideal when typing code, writing documents, or researching.

This one is perfect when you need something bigger than the normal portable monitor, but do not want to go into a larger size.

# 9. Cocopar 15.6 Inch Portable Monitor.

The 15.6-inch portable monitor of Cocopar is a relatively cheap device, but it does not sacrifice any of the convenient features. Its edges are thin, and they have built-in speakers, which makes them a convenient option both at work and for entertainment.

This is a good monitor to consider when you want an affordable monitor that still feels modern.

# 10. GeChic On-Lap 1503H

GeChic has long been producing portable monitors, and the On-Lap 1503H is a solid product to consider in situations where a wide range of devices is necessary. It allows VGA and HDMI connections, so it can be useful when you need to connect old hardware to new devices.

It is not the thinnest or the slickest, but it has the advantage of being versatile and reliable.

# Conclusions: How to choose the right monitor.

The right portable monitor is truly a matter of the way you work. The UPERFECT 24-inch portable monitor 4k is difficult to miss, should you require the largest display possible with gorgeous clarity. It provides both performance and portability in a smooth package.

If you want a smaller and extremely lightweight one, then the Lenovo ThinkVision M14 or Dell C1422H is a great choice. ViewSonic TD1655 will be a favorite among touchscreen users, but the AOC or Cocopar might be considered by the low-end traveler.

Finally, any digital nomad will need to reflect on their workflow, budget, and traveling preferences to decide on which portable laptop monitor to buy at the end of the day.
