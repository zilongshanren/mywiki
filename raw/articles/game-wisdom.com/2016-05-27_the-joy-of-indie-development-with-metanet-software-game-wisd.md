This week on the cast, I sat down with co-founder of Metanet Software Mare Sheppard to discuss her company, N++, and what it’s like to have been working in the Indie market for over a decade.


We started as we always do with introductions and how Metanet Software was founded. After that, we began a massive talk about the Indie market and some of Mare’s thoughts on how things are going, what goes into making a great game and a lot more.

Eventually, we turned to the N series and how Metanet Software developed and refined it. With N++ coming to the PC soon, I asked Mare what makes this version different from previous ones. It was a great cast and I definitely want to have Mare back on most likely around the time N++ is released.
