This week on the cast, Logan from Nintendo Wire joined me for our first official recording in 2017 to talk about Nintendo and what we think about the Switch.


We started off with talking about Logan’s background and what we enjoy about Nintendo. This cast was recorded just before the big Switch announcement, but we did share our thoughts and opinions on what we were thinking about it. We talked about some of the major criticisms of Nintendo in the past and what we’re hoping to see with the Switch.

The other topic we talked about was the divide between reviewers and consumers in terms of what’s important to a game.
