## Starburst – Top rated game

It would be challenging to find a player that has not at least dabbled in this online slot, this popular game skyrocketed to the top shortly after its release. This cosmic themed slot is a straightforward and direct online slot. The simple gameplay is a big factor that contributes to Starburst’s popularity as it is an uncomplicated road to fun.

## Fortunium – One of the best Slots

This slot is considered unique, even groundbreaking, as it challenges the way in which online slots are usually played. Fortunium contains the standard features of typical online slots; however, it also has innovative features such as the win booster feature, free spins, and wild symbols. The most unique special feature of this slot is the Mystery Symbols feature; certain symbols can change into any symbol, even into the wild. Fortunium transports you to a golden city of opportunity and sports a steam punk theme.

## Jumanji

By collaborating with the entertainment industry, this popular slot was brought to life. Jumanji is based on the TV show that was on air during the 90s. This fun-filled game has special features that are certain to keep you entertained. The RTP is an impressive 96.33% and displays 5 reels and 36 pay lines. This high number of optional pay lines gives players many ways to win.

## Dead or Alive 2

This sequel comes over a decade after its predecessor; it seems worth the wait if its popularity is anything to go by. Its commercial success and well-received release prove just that. With similar settings, this game will have players of the original game fitting right in. Set in the wild-wild west, Dead or Alive 2 is action-packed and excitingly themed online slot.

This adventure slot is quaintly themed and has immersive gameplay with an interesting narrative. Gonzo’s Quest is an online slot with a goal of helping the character Gonzo to find the legendary Eldorado, the city of gold. With the standard 5reels and 20 payline setup, this slot lets its features speak for themselves without any complicated gameplay needed. The promise of many free spins makes this slot’s winning potential substantial. This adventure slot’s popularity has withstood the test of time and continues to go strong as players take up the mission to help Gonzo on his quest.
