## Get Distracted From Learning

The human brain should be engaged in various activities to remain productive. In other words, learning day and night will make you feel exhausted and overloaded by too much information. To study in the most effective way, it is important to switch to something else. For example, you can go for a walk, communicate with friends, or go in for sports.

However, playing video games is one of the best activities to distract from constant learning. The reason is that gaming requires being extremely focused, noticing even minor details, and keeping in touch with the game every second. Consequently, you forget about all your college topics and subjects while staying focused on your game.

When you are all in the video game, you don’t think about low grades, failure to meet deadlines, and other common college issues. As a result, your study stress levels will significantly decrease.

## Boost Your Social Skills

There is a common myth that all students who enjoy playing video games are boring nerds with plenty of communication problems. The truth is that many of these fellows have many friends and easily meet new people. So, what is the secret?

When playing video games, you will likely get acquainted with many other players. You communicate, cooperate, choose the most winning game strategies, and do plenty of other things together online. Sometimes gamers have huge communities where they discuss hundreds of different questions. In many cases, communication might move from online to offline. In other words, you can meet many like-minded people who share your interests and gaming preferences.

Not only you can get more friends among other gamers. Improving your social skills means you will likely become more communicative in college, and find new mates there. Furthermore, communicative students might become more active during the lectures and get higher grades!

## Bring Your Creativity To A Higher Level

Playing video games is not as simple as many people think. You need to be creative, use unusual approaches, and find new ways to win the game and move to the next level. Therefore, gaming significantly develops your creativity. If you want to stand out from the crowd and choose out-of-the-box solutions, playing video games can help you achieve these goals.

All in all, playing video games has countless benefits. You can become more creative, improve your problem-solving skills, get distracted from constant learning, and significantly decrease your study stress.

However, to get all these advantages, it is crucial to set up certain limits for playing. Most experts recommend limiting your gaming activity to two hours on weekdays and four hours on days off. This approach will allow you to become more productive, reduce your anxiety levels, and have time for family, friends, and other hobbies.
