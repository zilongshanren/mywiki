## Forging innovative and unique features

Gamers expect familiar features in slots, such as scatter and wild symbols and free spins. How these options are implemented can make the difference between highly entertaining and less exciting gameplay.

Implementing creative bonus features like charging meters or collecting points to increase levels and opening up mini-contests or bonus awards elevates the fun in base gameplay. Developers can also include prize wheels, mini video games, or betting on wins to create diversity.

Free spins are an all-time classic and something most players will expect from a slot title. In most cases, this unique feature is triggered by landing scatters on the playing grid, but there is room for improvement in how this feature can work.

Another way to improve gameplay is to use different mechanics for each spin. Some titles have cascading reels, where multiple wins can happen on one spin. Others have cluster mechanics combined with cascades, and Megaways mechanics are among the most popular slot types.
