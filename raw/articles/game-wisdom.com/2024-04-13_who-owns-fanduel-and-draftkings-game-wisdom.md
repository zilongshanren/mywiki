**What is FanDuel? **

*FanDuel* is officially the number one gambling company in the US. It’s a sportsbook, fantasy sports platform, and online casino rolled into one, with over 17 million users in the US and a 43% market share. Thanks to its “same-game parlay” bet type and excellent range of bonuses, *FanDuel* has been able to reach the top. And just for creating an account, *FanDuel* offers new users up to $150 in bonus bets. All you have to do is make their first deposit ($10 or higher), place a bet, win that bet, and then receive the bonus. This is an excellent offer that highlights why *FanDuel* is one of the best gambling companies around.

**What is DraftKings? **

*DraftKings* is the second most popular gambling company in the US and currently holds a 25% market share. Like *FanDuel*, *DraftKings* offers sports betting, daily fantasy sports, and an online casino. However, *DraftKings* is primarily focused on daily fantasy sports, which has been the key driver behind its popularity.
