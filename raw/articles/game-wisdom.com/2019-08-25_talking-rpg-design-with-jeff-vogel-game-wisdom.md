For this cast I finally sat down with the “old man of the game industry” himself: Jeff Vogel, for a cast on RPG design, life, and some of the weirdest tangents yet.


We started off with talking about Jeff’s history in the game industry and the work done at Spiderweb Software. After that, things got weird, and I’ll leave you to figure out what that means. For the final part of the cast, we talked more about Queen’s Wish the Conquerer and how it breaks tradition from his previous games, and we talked about his style of game design.
