After last week’s incredibly long cast with Jon Shafer, I wanted to keep things on the quicker side this week… well quick in terms of our usual lengths at least.


Joining me this week, returning guest and co-host for the Quarter To Three cast : Jason Mcmaster. Even though I wanted to keep things light, that didn’t stop us from talking about a variety of topics. From the recent Valve announcements, *GTA 5′*s release and more.

Our big topic for the week was talking a bit more about narrative games vs. gameplay heavy games. Both Jason and I were on the same side but I would be interested in coming back to this topic perhaps as a full cast.
