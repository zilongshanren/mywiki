An interview with Sandy Petersen of Petersen Games. For this cast, we spoke with the veteran designer about his lifetime in the industry, his work in the tabletop market, and his amazing house and walls of games. We discussed what he’s currently working on and his thoughts on the design and manufacturing of tabletop games.
