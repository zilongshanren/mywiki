## How Do Withdrawals Work

There are a few steps in the process once you click withdraw. While things are usually fast and smooth, you have to keep in mind that these processes are all taking part in the background. First, there is the **verification **of the transaction. Blockchain needs to ensure that the movement of funds is verified and legit. This is the prime safety characteristic of the technology but it can take up to a few minutes.

Next comes **casino processing **the said transaction. While most casinos today are aiming for almost instantaneous withdrawal times, there are also ones where it can take up to a full day if there is a review that has to be done. Once that step is through, blockchain will once again confirm that the transaction is done. The speed of this, again, depends on the cryptocurrency of your choice. BTC and ETH are quite fast if there is no [network congestion](https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbestechcouncil/2024/10/01/understanding-the-challenges-of-data-congestion-on-decentralized-blockchain-networks/), so the average time is a few minutes. Altcoins are usually not as crowded, giving almost instant transactions.

After all that, your wallet will finally get the funds you won. This takes literally no time as your total will increase the same moment the blockchain network confirms the transfer.

## What Can Influence Withdrawal Speed

Even in the same casino you’ll notice that you’ll need different amounts of time depending on some factors. A lot of those factors are actually dependent on the casinos. Surprisingly, time of day can be one of the biggest influences on the speed of the transaction. It’s not just about the congestion of the network, but there are actually online casinos that will only process these transactions during their appointed **business hours**. This is not very widely spread but it’s something to keep in mind. Other casinos will have internal policies about mandated withdrawal wait (such as up to 10 minutes, e.g.) because of their **anti-fraud policies**. These are very important with the rise of [hi-tech cyberfrauds](https://www.unodc.org/roseap/en/2024/10/cyberfraud-industry-expands-southeast-asia/story.html) even in the blockchain world.

Still, the biggest factor for the speed of the actual transaction will definitely be the current status of the blockchain network, i.e. the **network congestion**. When things are busy on the blockchain, you can expect some delays with your withdrawals – but this also depends on the type of cryptocurrency, as previously mentioned.

If you’re just starting out and cashing out your first win you might get an extra layer of delay because of the **Know Your Customer** (KYC) or [ Anti-Money Laundering](https://complyadvantage.com/insights/online-gambling-money-laundering/) (AML) procedures. This is especially true for transactions with bigger amounts of crypto
