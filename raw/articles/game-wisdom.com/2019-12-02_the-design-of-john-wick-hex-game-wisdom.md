For this cast, I spoke with Nic Tringali who works at Bithell Games and was on the design team for John Wick Hex.


We started with introductions and spoke about how the team pitched John Wick Hex to Lionsgate. From there, we spoke about balancing the design and trying to stay true to the John Wick movies. I spoke about some of my issues with the game and Nic talked about the challenges of designing the timeline system. I did ask him to see if we could get Keanu Reeves on, but unfortunately, I just missed him.
