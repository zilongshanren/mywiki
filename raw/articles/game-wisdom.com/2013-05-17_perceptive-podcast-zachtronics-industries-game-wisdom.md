This week on the cast, we welcomed Zach Barth: creator of *SpaceChem* and the upcoming *Ironclad Tactics*. Besides talking about *SpaceChem’s* design, we also talked about the educational aspect of it and edutainment games in general.


We spent the remaining time discussing *Ironclad Tactics*: a CCG tactical strategy game taking place in a steam-punk version of the civil war. If that last sentence wasn’t enough to peak your interest then I don’t know what to say.

And as what is sadly standard for at least until winter time, my allergies were incredibly bad and I’m still sorry for the heavy breathing. Maybe someone can invent a steam-punk breathing helmet for me.
