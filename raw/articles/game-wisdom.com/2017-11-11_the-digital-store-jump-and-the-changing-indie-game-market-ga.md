This week on the cast, founder of the indie store Jump — Anthony Palma joined me to discuss how the indie game market is doing, and what led to entering the digital store market.


We started with Anthony’s background and his thoughts on the indie market today. The market has certainly changed over this decade, and he discussed what led to the creation of Jump.

From there, we talked specifically about the Jump platform and what it offers to fans and indie developers. I asked Anthony about some concerns that indie developers have and the details of being on the platform. We also went into detail about some of the risks of having a digital store and how Jump is trying to navigate around them compared to other digital stores.
