An interview with Andrew Swihart of Arion Digital to talk about what he learned going from the AAA space to being an indie dev. We spoke about Andrew’s background and going from working at a AAA studio to being an indie developer.

His indie games: [Remnants of the Dawn](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1271510/Remnants_of_the_Dawn/) and [Death of the Rising Sun ](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1483990/Death_of_the_Rising_Sun/)
