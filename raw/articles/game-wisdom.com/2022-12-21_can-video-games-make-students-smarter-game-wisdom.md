For sure, you have heard an opinion that playing video games gives you nothing good and is just a waste of time; many parents and teachers believe that computer games can only make children violent, cruel, or spoil their eyesight and distract them from important things like studying. Unfortunately, they don’t understand that there can’t be 100% useless things and that, in some way, video games can make your kids smarter.


## Concentration

Have you ever tried to hit some target with a small ball or do any other task on the screen of your computer using just a mouse or a keyboard? It’s not easy at all and requires a lot of concentration. And thanks to video games, children can easily develop this useful skill and will be able to use it at school, in college, and even at work. Almost all tasks you are going to have in your future life will be based on your ability to concentrate and fulfill tasks.

## Getting experience

The development of a person is getting new experiences all the time. And how can children do that if most parents are overprotecting right now and are trying to control every move they make and every breath their children take? And the answer is, once again, video games. There is such diversity of games right now, with different plots and strategies, that any child can find something interesting that they have never experienced before. When playing, children learn to anticipate, analyze and build logical connections, which helps them to fulfill the main aim of the level or the game. And for sure, your kid’s science teacher will be grateful to you as a parent who managed to develop such skills in a child. If you are unable to do this in the game, you can use bonuses or extra moves. ## Special video games

If you have doubts about whether video games can make your child smarter, just remember that there are special games designed to fulfill exactly this task. There is plenty of educational games where you need to solve a simple mathematical problem or where you get to know some basic rules of physics or biology. These games are mainly for the little ones and can be used in kindergartens, but it‘s exactly the time to develop intellectual abilities in your children. You can spend just 20 minutes per day playing these games with your children, but it will give great benefits in the future.

## Competitive spirit

Being smart means being successful, and being successful means achieving a lot in your life. In order to achieve something, you must have the desire to be the leader and to be the best. Video games can develop the wish and necessity to reach a certain level and win a prize or to be at the top of the ranking. These qualities are indispensable at school, it helps a child, in a good way, never be satisfied with their achievement, not to stop and be able to set new goals when they’ve succeeded in the previous ones.

## The way to relax

Video games are also considered to be a great way to relax and help to deal with constant stress during studies. From time to time, our brain needs a stop, a couple of moments when it can concentrate on collecting puzzles, for example, and not on some math problems. Even during the most important meetings on a high level, people have coffee breaks in order to relax. And if it’s too early for your eight y.o. child to drink coffee, just give them a Play Station, and the result will be the same. They will relax, gain some positive emotions, and will be able to return to their studies.

There will always be arguments regarding video games, whether they are helpful or useless. The only thing you have to remember is that only your child can decide what influence the games have on him. And if it benefits him by making your kid smarter, then let them play, and you can even do it together.
