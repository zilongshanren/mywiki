We usually relegate browser games to the dustbin. We consider them lesser than other games, perhaps because they are more accessible, so you never know how seriously people will pick them up. However, it is time to take a second look at browser gaming. You may be surprised by what you find and all the benefits of this type of gaming.

Here are the benefits of playing games directly on your browser and how to find the best ones on the web.


**Browser Gaming Comes with Many Benefits**

Playing games right in your browser has many advantages. Most of these advantages have to do with accessibility and ease of use, which makes it better for you and more accessible to create a community in general.

For example, you can find lots of free browser games out there. While some of them will have a paywall, many will not. This is a noticeable difference from the classic PC games from companies like [Blizzard](https://www.blizzard.com/en-gb/) that you have to purchase even to download or install on your computer. This is in addition to the monthly fees.

Another great benefit is that you can play them from more places. You do not need to have your computer or one with the program installed. Because it is a browser game, you play it on any computer with the right browser. This is much less demanding on a computer compared to big games that might require lots of random-access memory and an advanced video card. Also, you can sign in from a new computer, and your account information and history will be right there with you.

Similarly, it is nice to play a game without downloading anything. Traditional games can clog up our computers and slow everything down. This can be a big commitment, along with the cost, when we are not even sure if we want to play the game for a long time.

In this sense, browser gaming allows us to try the game before we spend too many resources on it. This is useful both for specific titles and also for genres. Maybe it has been a long time since you played a side scroller, so you want to try it, but you are concerned it will be too simple. Browser games allow us to dip our toes in the water.

**Catan Universe**

Perhaps you have played the board game previously known as The Settlers of Catan? Now called Catan, this popular board game is available in your browser. And, of course, this comes with many advantages. A big one is that you do not have to organize with several people to enjoy the game. Online, you can start playing with players worldwide at your level.

Plus, this is for free! So even if you want to play with friends in real life, you can all play this version without spending a dime. You can coordinate with your friends if you are in the same space or each one from their house. This is also an excellent way to connect with friends in different cities.

**Agar.io**

This game initially seems simple but ends up being quite the strategic puzzle. In Agar.io, you will see several colored circles on top of a gridded background. Your circle will get bigger and bigger as it consumes the other ones.

As your circle gets bigger and bigger, it will also move slower and slower. This means that the tiny dots can escape you, but you can always split yourself in two, launching half of yourself towards the other smaller dot!

**Wilds.io**

If you have never played a hack-n-slash, you should try it. It is an incredible rush unlike anything else, as you run around destroying anything that gives you a funny face.

In the most popular version of this game, you play as a member of one of three teams. You can slash up the other team members, break boxes and collect loot. As you slash up the other people, they leave their bones on the ground. Collecting these bones is your main objective in the game.

This game has other playing modes, including soccer or versions with more manageable objectives. It is a fast-paced good time!
