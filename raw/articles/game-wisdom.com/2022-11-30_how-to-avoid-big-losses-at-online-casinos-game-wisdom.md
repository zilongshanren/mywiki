## Be aware of losing

Don’t treat playing at an online casino as a way to make money or multiply your savings. This way of thinking is doomed to failure in advance. Remember that no matter what the tips and advice from the experts say – the casino always wins. While winning small jackpots at casinos is possible, winning large life-changing jackpots is unlikely. The same goes for setting yourself up for a regular income from casino games – such a situation is downright impossible due to the very nature of casino games.

The results in online casino games at honest operators are created by special computer algorithms called random number generators. It is therefore impossible to influence the outcome in any way. Moreover, these algorithms already have an implicit casino advantage at the start, which is usually between 2% and 10%. This means a certain revenue that casinos can count on after thousands of game rounds played, once all winnings made by players have been taken into account.

## Choose games with a low casino edge

The most popular games in online casinos are slots, the results of which are always 100% random. Nonetheless, the games that have the lowest casino advantage, and therefore a higher probability of satisfying returns, are table games, including blackjack, poker or baccarat, in which the decisions made by players and the strategies they use play a major role.

Learning the rules of card games and mastering them to perfection is time-consuming, but well worth the effort. There is a reason why the world’s best poker players, who win international poker tournaments thanks to their skills, are able to make a living from their passion. However, these are the few exceptions that confirm the rule that playing for money is not an easy or good way to make money.

## Treat betting on games like a non-refundable expense

If you decide to make a deposit to an online casino and use it in a game, treat it as a non-refundable expense. This will make it easier for you to devote money to the game that you are sure to lose. You can treat such a deposit as part of a movie ticket or dinner out. If you manage to win anything, you will be able to treat it as part of additional funds, for example, to continue playing.

Remember never to spend money in a casino that you need to pay other expenses, including bills and building up savings. You will most likely not get back most of the amount you deposit at the casino, so it is not worth risking the serious consequences you may face if you do not have the funds to live on.
