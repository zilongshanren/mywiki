## Luck-Based Casino Games: The Thrill of Chance

Luckily-based casino games like slots or roulette are on the other side of that spectrum. The concept for these games is to be unpredictable; they have very little control over what happens in the game. For example, the result of a spin in a slot machine is dictated by a random number generator (RNG), meaning every spin is completely independent of the one before. The unpredictability of this makes the luck-based games so exciting because a big win is always possible no matter how skillful a player is.

## Game Design Philosophy: Keeping Players Hooked

As a game design thing, balancing luck with skill is about fairness as much as anything else. The game has to give players a feeling that they have an equal chance of winning whilst still making it exciting to play with the random factor. This balance is delicate: a game is boring if it’s too predictable or if it’s too random, and players feel frustrated for the lack of control.


Online casino games are constantly trying to strike this balance. They will introduce new mechanics, update existing games, or adjust algorithms to keep the experience fresh for both new and skilled players.
