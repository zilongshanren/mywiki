This week on the cast, we sat down with Martin and Andrej of Poetic Studio to talk about RPG design and how they’re planning on developing their title Sacred Fire if it meets its Kickstarter goal.


We started with talking about the studio before getting into both general game design and RPG design. There is a lot that goes into making a RPG, and we tried to cover as much as we could. From there, we moved on to Sacred Fire and what they’re trying to achieve to with it. After talking about Sacred Fire, we focused more on RPG design in general and their kickstarter strategy.
