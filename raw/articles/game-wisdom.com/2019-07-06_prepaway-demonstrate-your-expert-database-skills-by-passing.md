They use their specialized skills to carry out roles like installation, upgrading, and management of database servers. Professionals who carry out these tasks are called Database Administrators (DBAs) or database developers. Due to an accelerated rate of data production, there’s a high demand for such experts.

**Oracle 1Z0-061 Exam Details**

The exam tests your hands-on skills in performing complex database tasks. 1Z0-061 test is suitable for both database administrators and database developers. The exam has 75 multiple-choice questions and lasts 120 minutes. To pass 1Z0-061, you have to attain a passing score of 65%. This test is included in the first stage of the following certification paths:

- Oracle Database 11g Administrator Certified Associate (1Z0-061/1Z0-071 and 1Z0-052)
- Oracle PL/SQL Developer Certified Associate (1Z0-061/1Z0-071 and 1Z0-144)
- Oracle Database 12c Administrator Certified Associate and (1Z0-061/1Z0-071 and 1Z0-062)

It means that to attain any of these credentials, you will have to pass 1Z0-061 test first. Once you clear two required exams, you’ll earn your Oracle certification.
