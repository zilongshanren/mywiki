This week on the cast, Yaniv Nizan of Soomla joined me for another talk on mobile analytics and the state of the mobile market.


We started off with introductions before diving into Super Mario Run. Nintendo’s first official foray into mobile gaming caused a lot of buzz, and I asked Yaniv what he thought of it given his company’s experience with analytics and the mobile market.

We also talked about the state of mobile games and what are some important analytics developers are paying attention to today.
