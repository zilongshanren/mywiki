For this week’s podcast, we went live on Twitch with Nelson who is a writer and game designer. We spoke about what it means to adapt video games to other mediums and taking other mediums and adapting them to games.


Please note that the cast starts at 1:34. There was a lot to talk about when it comes to adaptations. Just because something works in one medium, doesn’t mean that it will work in others; as evident by movies based on video games.
