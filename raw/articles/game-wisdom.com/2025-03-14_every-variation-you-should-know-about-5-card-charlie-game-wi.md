### What Is the 5 Card Charlie Rule?

The **blackjack 5 Card Charlie rule** is a lesser-known but thrilling variation of traditional blackjack. All the core rules remain the same — the goal is still to hit 21 without going over. But here’s the twist: if you manage to collect five cards in your hand without busting, you automatically win, regardless of the dealer’s hand.

Imagine this: you’re holding four 2s, totaling 8. You draw a fifth card — a 4 — bringing your total to 12. Even if the dealer has 20, your five-card hand secures an automatic win. It’s a rare but powerful way to claim victory.
