## Cleopatra in Detail

The complexity of this game will have you develop a perfectly timed action plan in your quest to win your missions. Cleopatra is set up in a way that makes the player build infrastructure for the game’s city and ensures the game’s people get water, food, and the luxuries they want. These things must be accomplished by hunting, farming, fishing, and distributing nutritious necessities through markets. The player will also be asked to make beer, linen, pottery, and other society-important things, even taking into account aspects like importation and exportation of goods for your world’s well-functioning.

With all these aspects to keep in mind, the player might wonder how a mission can be achieved or won. By achieving a set rating in game categories, the player can win a mission, whether it’s related to your kingdom’s wealth, quality of life, or even culture. Constructing monuments is another effective way to win a mission, as it adds to your overall ratings. With complex industries and sectors to think about, this game adds a real-life aspect to your experience, forcing you to think about things such as paint making, oil, farming, and much more. Remaining in its ancient Egyptian setting, it also has missions involving tombs and their construction, decoration, and luxuries.

Interestingly, Cleopatra’s missions are put in the form of campaigns, full of challenges and plot twists. Firstly, you have to build the Valley of the Kings to win your first mission, with all its tombs and building structures. Although it is a difficult game that requires a quick mind and patience, the infrastructure construction is worth it once you have successfully tackled your objectives. You can extend the game with the more challenges you win, which are never the same and are always exciting. Some include gods, and others include plagues, so you constantly progress to make your quest and build your vast kingdom.

Make sure to keep the Egyptian gods satisfied as you develop your world because the game also includes monsters, and attackers will try to bring down what you’ve built. However, keep in mind that as an advanced strategy game, you can build your army to fight your battles by your side. Although [Egypt is a popular theme](https://www.criticalhit.net/gaming/the-enduring-legacy-of-ancient-egypt-in-video-gaming/) for games, it also means that you’ll have to fight the unwanted insects and dangerous animals that appear. Additionally, there may be some annoying factors to consider when playing. Ensure you finish your missions properly before starting new ones, as if you forget some luxuries for the tomb you are building, the whole production could be stopped. These sometimes-frustrating details of the game will make sure you are completing every mission according to the requirements because the risk of losing all your progress is too dangerous.

Despite some aspects of the game, which can seem annoying or time-consuming, its complexity and fun make it all worth it. Even 22 years after its release, the game still holds a 77% good rating on Metacritic and is reported to be liked by 96% of its users. This longevity in its success can only be explained by the incredible immersion you experience in this ancient Egypt-set video game. Whether you are a fan of strategy, military, or building games, Cleopatra includes all the exciting aspects of a video game. The game’s innovative features, like the inclusion of city-building, floods, farming, and destruction, and its vast levels, make it an unforgettable experience for the player. Despite its serious content, it never stops being fun and exciting, with constant new opportunities arising with every new mission.
