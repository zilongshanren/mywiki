For this perceptive podcast we recorded during turn-based thursday fest, I sat down with Guillaune who is the lead designer and founder of Nine Dots Studio to talk about the company and their upcoming roguelike Lost in Prayer. We spoke about some of the challenges in the industry today and then discussed the plans and development of [Lost in Prayer](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2849500/Lost_in_Prayer/).
