This week on the cast, it was all about Dark Souls 3, as my friend Alex and I went over as many points on the game as possible in one of the longer casts in awhile.


Obviously, this was a spoiler cast, and we jumped right into the game. I don’t need to write a lot of notes this time, as everything and anything Dark Souls 3 related was on the table. Some points we discussed: The new poise system, enemy design, world design, favorite/least favorite parts and more.

Alex spent some time talking about the lore of the game and his theories on what’s been going on, thanks to the research done by the community.
