For this episode of the perceptive podcast, I’m speaking with Celia Schilling who is the marketing manager at Yacht Club Games to talk about modern retro game marketing, Cyber Shadow and more. We spoke about how Cyber Shadow was picked up by Yacht Club Games, and what they did in terms of assisting with the project. For the final part, we talked about how to position a modern retro game in today’s market.
