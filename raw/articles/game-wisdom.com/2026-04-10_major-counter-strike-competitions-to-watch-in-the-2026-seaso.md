CS2 is a tactical first-person shooter from Valve Corporation. With millions of fans globally, it is considered to be the most popular video game these days. No wonder it represents the biggest part of modern esports. Its busy schedule doesn’t let teams and their fans get bored. Apart from watching a great number of esports tournaments, there are multiple opportunities for[ CS betting](https://gg.bet/counter-strike), which can keep the audience engaged for hours. Let’s see the major competitions to follow in 2026.


## PGL Bucharest 2026

April is a busy time for CS2 teams, with several major competitions taking place throughout the month. PGL Bucharest 2026 invites 16 teams to compete for a massive prize pool of $625,000. The top 12 teams will be selected from the VRS Global, while 4 teams were qualified through regional tournaments in Europe, North America, South America, and Asia. All participants will go through the group stage to let eight teams get to the playoffs. The winner will be determined throughout the Quarters and the Grand Final.

PGL Bucharest 2026 will take place at Studio Environment at PGL Studios. A friendly atmosphere will allow app participants to focus on the gaming process. CS2 fans will be able to watch the live event on Twitch and YouTube.

## PGL Astana 2026

PGL Astana 2026 is an annual event to take place at the Barys Arena. With strong infrastructure and great capacity, it is quickly becoming one of the most anticipated esports tournaments in the world. This year, PGL Astana invites 12 teams based on the Global VRS and 4 teams qualified through open and closed online qualifiers worldwide. FURIA, MOUZ, PARIVISION, Team Falcons, and Team Spirit have already secured their places in the tournament. The seeding for the main competition will take place on April 6.

PGL Astana 2026 has $800,000 in a prize pool. The whole amount will be split among all participants, with the major payouts being made to the first four standing in the final leaderboard.

## CS Asia Championships 2026

CS Asia Championships 2026 will take place in Shanghai, China. Organized by Perfect World, it will consist of several rounds, including quarterfinals, semifinals, and a grand final. The competition is going to have 16 teams, with 14 teams selected from the Global VRS and 2 teams advanced through the closed qualifier. The CS Asia Championships will involve Ancient, Dust II, Mirage, Nuke, and other major maps. The streaming will be made in English and Chinese, while the event itself will be available to watch on Twitch and YouTube.

All participants will compete for $400,000. The winner will be eligible for $150,000, while the rest of the prize pool will be split among other teams based on their final result.

## IEM Cologne Major 2026

IEM Cologne Major 2026 will be held at the LANXESS Arena. The tournament will invite the world’s top teams based on the Global VRS. They will go through three elimination stages, with the top teams proceeding to the playoffs. In the extended format, all games during Stage 3 will be Bo3. This means that teams will have one extra day of competition. The best two teams will meet each other at the Grand Final.

IEM Cologne Major has a well-balanced qualification system, clear invitation rules, and a large prize pool, creating a great environment for competitive play. Teams will fight for a massive payout at the heart of Europe. IEM Cologne Major came back with a prize pool, reaching up to $1,250,000. The winner’s going to receive $500,000, which is one of the biggest payouts in professional esports.

## Esports World Cup 2026

Esports World Cup 2026 is a highly anticipated tournament among CS2 fans. It brings 32 teams to the table to let them compete for $2 million. The lineup will include the best teams from the Global VRS, the winner of the Asian Champions League, and four teams advancing through the open qualifiers.

Esports World Cup will be held in Riyadh at Boulevard Riyadh City. In the initial stage, four groups of eight teams each will compete in the double-elimination format. The top four teams from each group move to the playoffs. The participants join the single-elimination bracket before reaching the Grand Final.

## BLAST Open Fall 2026

BLAST Open Fall 2026 is one of the biggest CS2 tournaments in the autumn. Organized by BLAST, it will take place in Copenhagen, Denmark. Fans will enjoy the competition between 16 teams fighting for the title. The tournament involves a group stage that will let the top teams from each group move to the playoffs. All matches are based on the best-of-three format. After the quarterfinals and semifinals, the winners will meet in the grand final to determine the champion.

BLAST Open Fall has $400,000 in the prize pool. The winner can get $150,000, while the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th places get $60,000, $40,000, and $40,000, respectively. BLAST hasn’t revealed all the information about the future tournament. Fans are still waiting for more details, including the final list of participants.

## Thunderpick World Championship 2026

Thunderpick World Championship 2026 is a relatively small but popular esports tournament to be held in Malta. With 8 teams on board, it is based on the classic format, including the group d stage and playoffs. In the group stage, the top two teams from each group will qualify for the playoffs. All matches will take the Bo3 format, except the grand final, which is based on Bo5. The whole event will be live-streamed on Twitch, YouTube, and Kick in English.

Thunderpick World Championship invites players to compete for a $500,000 prize pool. The reward is split among all participants, with the biggest piece of pie going to the winner.
