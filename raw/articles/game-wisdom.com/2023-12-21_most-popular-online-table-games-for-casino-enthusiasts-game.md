**An Overview of Most Enticing Digital Table Games**

When it comes to [table games online](http://www.cafecasino.lv/casino/table-games) there are not many that are as popular as blackjack. This classic does have plenty of variations, so you won’t ever feel bored by the game in question. There are too many to list but let’s stick to the most popular ones, like Classic Single Deck Blackjack. This latter version has the highest potential when it comes to RTP, which is up to 99.69%, making it very attractive for players.

Another option is Perfect Pairs Blackjack, ideal for the skilled player filled with interesting twists. For starters, when you split your hand, you also have the option to double down, and the dealer has to draw on 16 but can’t go over 17.

Right after blackjack, the next popular version is poker, another delightful card game. This game heavily relies on strategy and skill, so make sure your knowledge is up to par. Here your goal is to build the [best hand](https://upswingpoker.com/poker-hands-rankings/) you can with the cards you have.

If you’re looking for a poker game with a progressive jackpot, then Let ‘Em Ride will be the game for you. It’s another one that starts with three cards, but here you just need to get a pair of tens to win. If you opt-in for the progressive jackpot, you can win a minimum of $75 with a Flush, and the full pot with a Royal Flush.
