For this cast, I was joined by Ryan Matejka who joined Vertigo Gaming Inc. to help the story of Cook Serve Delicious 3 to talk about making a surprisingly dark story for a lighthearted game.


We started off discussing his background and how he met David (the designer/creator) and came on board as one of the writers. The story of the game has certainly taken some weird and dark turns, and we discussed how all that came about.
