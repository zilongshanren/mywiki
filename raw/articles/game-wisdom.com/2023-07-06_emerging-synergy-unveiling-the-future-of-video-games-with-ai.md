Changes in technology and innovative ideas have always been a part of the video game industry. In the past few years, artificial intelligence (AI) has helped in shaping the industry. It opened up new possibilities and broke barriers.

This article looks at how AI and video games are starting to work together and what this new alignment means for the future of gaming.


## AI-Driven NPCs

Gone are the days when non-playable characters (NPCs) are boring and predictable. Now, NPCs can learn how players interact with them and adjust to new situations. It can even create their own characters and goals in the game world. This upgrade takes games to a new level whether through using natural communication abilities or understanding complex patterns of behaviour.

## Dynamic Storytelling

AI is also change the way games tell stories. Basically, AI-powered stories don’t have to toe a straight line. Instead, they can easily adapt based on what players do and consider the choices they make. In addition to changing the story, it can add new characters and even change their behaviours. Thus, providing an unprecedented depth of involvement and immersion.

## Procedural Generation

Advances made by AI are noticeable in the field of procedural generation. This process involves creating game elements like maps, levels, and missions. These are done automatically using algorithms rather than working on them manually.

## Player Analytics

Another fascinating use of AI in gaming is in the realm of player analytics. AI programs can help developers to study player behavior, tastes, and skill levels. This can be done by collecting and analyzing a huge amount of player data.
