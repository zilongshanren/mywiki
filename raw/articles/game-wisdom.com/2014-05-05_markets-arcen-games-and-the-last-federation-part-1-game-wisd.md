This week, returning guest Chris Park from Arcen Games joined me for one of the longest discussions I think I’ve ever had.


We first started talking about our thoughts on the Souls series, with my experience playing Dark Souls 2 and Chris’s time with Demon’s Souls. That led to a discussion on how the video game market has changed and the problems we felt with sequels.

Chris used his time spent playing Super Mario 3D World with his son as a starting point to talk about Nintendo. We talked about what we enjoy from Nintendo, some of our favorite games in recent years along with that something special that keeps us coming back to the house of Mario.

The cast was so big that I had to split it into two parts, with part two focusing exclusively on The Last Federation.
