## New vs older online casinos: What are the differences?

Older casinos, also called ‘veteran casinos,’ have been operating for more than three years. Some have been on the market for 10+ years, making them household names in the online gambling community.

One of the biggest advantages of older online casinos is their unprecedented level of safety. They’ve built up quite a reputation for themselves, with hundreds of people leaving reviews that can vouch for their security.

New online casinos, on the other hand, can feel like an unfamiliar territory. They’ve been on the market for less than three years, with many of them being up and running only within the last 12 months. That’s what makes them scary for many people, especially [those who get scared by novelty and change](https://www.health.harvard.edu/mind-and-mood/overcome-your-fear-factor).

On the bright side, new online casinos usually come with many revolutionary changes. Where older online casinos prefer to stick to tried-and-tested methods, new sites don’t mind thinking outside the box. That’s why you’ll usually find a longer list of accepted payment methods, along with a wider range of lucrative casino bonuses.

## How do new and older online casinos differ in terms of bonuses?

With reputation being the primary difference between new and older online casinos, the newer sites typically have to fight more to attract new customers. This means coming up with new methods to pique players’ interest enough to make them want to join and create an account. Most of these new sites will try to do that through casino bonuses and promotions.

Casino bonuses are offers that online operators provide for players, and they come in many shapes and forms. Thus, you’ll be able to see:

- Free spins
- Welcome bonuses
- Cashback offers
- Free credits

And these are just a few instances of what awaits the most loyal casino players. While the main purpose of these offers is to attract new players, they are also a great tool at keeping the existing ones active and engaged.

Hence, you’ll see that many online casinos offer special VIP bonuses, which are reserved for the most loyal players. You can climb the VIP ladder by spending more money on games which, in turn, results in better prizes and special offers.

One study conducted among Brazilian online gamblers showed that bonus size is the most important consideration for 68% of players, with wagering requirements taking the second spot. That’s why VIP offers have such special appeal, as they can be very lucrative once you get to the final levels.

If you choose older online casinos, you can expect to encounter tried-and-tested types of bonuses. This means accepting classic welcome bonuses—claimed by 15.10% of users, according to a survey—which usually entail making a deposit before being able to use the money from the bonus or free spins.

Newer online casinos are more liberal with their offer. Therefore, it’s not unusual to find no-deposit bonuses that let you go straight to your favorite game without investing any money beforehand. Keep in mind that they also have terms & conditions in place – but they might be less demanding.

Generally speaking, it is possible to find a no-wagering bonus that doesn’t require playing through your winnings before withdrawing money; however, these bonuses are scarce and easy to miss.
