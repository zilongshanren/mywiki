## The Main Principle of Progression: The Town Hall Is Not a Goal, But Part of a Strategy

Most players’ mistakes begin with upgrading their Town Hall too quickly. This leads to an imbalance: defenses are weak, so is the army, resources are constantly being stolen, and matchmaking becomes increasingly difficult.

To Grow Your Clash of Clans Account Correctly:

- Don’t upgrade your Town Hall until your key buildings are upgraded. These include barracks, camps, laboratories, storage facilities, and basic defensive structures.

## Economy – The Foundation of Account Progression

You won’t get far in Clash of Clans if your storages are half-upgraded or your mines barely produce anything. Progress depends on having a steady income, and when your resource flow is reliable, everything else in your village develops much faster. A strong economy keeps your upgrades moving and prevents those long pauses where you’re waiting for gold or elixir to catch up.

What you need to do daily:

- Keep your builders busy 24/7 – there shouldn’t be a single idle moment.
- Develop mines, collectors, and storages before defenses.
- Use runes, books, and hammers only at the most expensive stages.
- Monitor your resource limits – overcrowding = loss of efficiency.

The Best Ways to Farm Resources for Accelerated Account Development

Farming is a key element of Clash of Clans account development. There are several strategies that can significantly speed up progress.

Primary farming methods:

## Proper Defense: The Part of Progress Everyone Underestimates

A good defense not only protects your resources but also ensures stable growth. A weak base leads to constant losses, which slows down account development.

Rules for a strong defense:

- Place storages deep in the center of the base.
- Pay attention to mage towers and trap placement.
- It’s important to keep your layout current – the meta is changing.
- Don’t copy random bases; choose layouts that suit your skill level.
