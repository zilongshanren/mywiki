For this cast I spoke with veteran game developer and owner of Game Refuge Brian Colin who is also the creator of the hit arcade game Rampage among other titles.


We spoke about the past and how he got started in the arcades, what it was like to be in the game industry in the 80’s and more. We then talked generally about what he has learned from over 20 years in the game industry about designing a game and running a studio.
