This week, Ken and I were joined by friend and programmer Will Luong to discuss several topics relating to how the game industry is shifting.


First we spent some time talking about the latest Tropes vs. Women video series, focusing on the Ms. Male character. Then after a brief kickstarter update from Ken, we took a look back at how much GOG.com has changed and had an impact on the Game Industry.

Finally, we talked about the changing state of pre-orders and whether or not the industry still has a use for them. Sound wise, Will’s mic was having some problems like mine before and it picked up a lot of his breathing which I edited out the best I could.
