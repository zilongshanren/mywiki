For this Perceptive podcast, we spoke with Al who is the lead designer on Skald Against the Black Priory, a modern retro RPG designed to emulate the look and feel of the commodore 64.


We talked about his background with wanting to make the game as well as his thoughts on RPG design in general. We spoke about the kickstarter and his plans for Skald, along with the modern retro market for these kinds of games.
