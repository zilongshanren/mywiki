For this cast, Linden Labs’ CEO Ebbe Altberg returned to the cast to discuss how the hit MMO Second Life has grown, and what it means for the future.


We talked about Ebbe’s thoughts on Second Life still going strong after 15 years. We spent some time discussing the MMO market, and how the game managed to sidestep the crash of the mid 00’s. We’ll also be having a cast with a member of Project Sansar in a few weeks to discuss more about user created content.
