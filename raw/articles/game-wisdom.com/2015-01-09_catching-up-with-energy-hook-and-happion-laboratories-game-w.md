This week,I spoke with returning guest and lead designer behind the game Energy Hook, Jamie Fristrom to catch up about the game’s development and what’s going on at [Happion Laboratories](http://www.happionlabs.com/).


We started off talking about what Jamie has been up to over the last year, including working on the game Sixty Second Shooter Prime and his updates to Energy Hook.

We talked about how the game’s development is going and what his fanbase is thinking of the title. In terms of new content we talked about him finalizing the player’s avatar with a new model. Discussing adding a female character to the game also brought up a brief tangent on the whole Gamergate debacle and we spent a few minutes on it.
