What a cast, for this week Ken makes a return along with freelance designer and writer Chris Gardiner who is currently working on [Below](http://www.belowgame.com/) for what is the longest cast we’ve had since starting Game-Wisdom.


Starting out we discussed the concept of re-reviewing games thanks to the recent second review of *Bioshock Infinite* over at Gamespot. Then a brief discussion on *Knock-Knock* and how much different failure states can have an affect on the player’s frustration level. Due to the size of the cast, I had to split it into two parts, with the second part focusing on examining storytelling in the video game industry.
