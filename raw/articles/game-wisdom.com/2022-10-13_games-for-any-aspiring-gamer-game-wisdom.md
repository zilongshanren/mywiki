Gamers nowadays are always looking for the next best title to enjoy. So, it makes sense for them to wait for new titles to come out. But they can always go back and try some older ones. These might not be as good-looking as the ones today, but they can pack a punch. In that regard here are some suggestions:


**Ryse Son of Rome**

This is a relatively new game when it comes to visuals. It’s a hack and slash title and you’ll take the role of a Roman Legionary named Marius. After Barbarian tribes assault Rome and murder your family, you’ll take up arms against them and take the fight back to them. As this is a hack and slash title you’ll get to perform various combos and finishers as you take the fight back to Britannia as well as other interesting locations. It’s a visual masterpiece even though it came out some time ago, which is why you should give it a shot.

**KOTOR**

The world of Star Wars is vast and you’ll get to visit it in Knights of the Old Republic. You’ll get to face the Sith and all sorts of other enemies in the game. You will go from planet to planet completing various missions and side quests, or you could scope out the cantinas and get in for a round of Pazaak.

Pazaak is a card game in the KOTOR universe and it’s similar to blackjack. You’ll get to beat opponents and earn prizes in the game. Blackjack is mostly enjoyed online nowadays and so are sports. Similar to the swoop races in KOTOR, people enjoy sports like cricket, boxing, darts and more at online sportsbooks like [www.betstation.com](https://www.betstation.com/sports-betting-sites/) and others. Enjoy them responsibly. Set your limits and respect them.

Another great thing about this olden RPG game is that you’ll get to influence the story in the game with your decisions and actions. So, the light side or the dark side will rule in the end depending on what you choose. This title is also getting a remake so, you’ll be able to enjoy it with modern visuals too.

**The Assassin’s Creed Franchise**

This franchise can be considered modern as it’s still spanning with a future title in the making. It’s based on various periods in history and you’ll get to play in these periods with various characters in them. You’ll be taken to the Crusades and even the Renaissance. These are the first couple of titles and when you get to [the third one](https://www.metacritic.com/game/switch/assassins-creed-iii-remastered) you’ll get to take an active part in the Liberation of America.

Additionally, you’ll play as members of the Assassin and Templar orders in the game. These fictional orders have been going at it for centuries, but one has never completely defeated the other. You’ll get to visit Ancient Egypt and even climb the pyramids if you want to. Additionally, you’ll get to sail ships as their captain and do so much more. This is the ultimate franchise for any true gamer out there.
