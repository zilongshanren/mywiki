This week on the cast, Z from Serenity Forge returned for a chat about what’s been going on with the studio, and their next published game Mystic Melee.


We started off with catching up and talking about some of the industry trends that have happened in 2017; including Steam Direct, VR and more.

Our main topic was talking about Mystic Melee, and how it’s a departure from the studio with regards to multiplayer, early access and speedrunning.
