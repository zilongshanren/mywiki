For this cast, we sat down with Jan Taaks of Overhype Studios to discuss the development of their first major game — Battle Brothers.


We did this recording just after the game’s release and my review. We started off with how development started and the original concept for the game. Jan talked about the never-ending battle of balancing the mechanics. I also asked him some questions from my review of the game.
