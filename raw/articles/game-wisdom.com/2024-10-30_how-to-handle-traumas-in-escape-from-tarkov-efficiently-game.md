The game becomes really realistic in terms of combat and injury, making players familiar with using medicines to recover after having injuries and also keeping their health in order. Knowing the many types of injuries and how one must treat them could mean the thin line between life and death, which makes medical knowledge just as important as combat skills. With that said, this guide will help explain essential tips for injury management and proper usage of medical supplies during your raids in Escape from Tarkov.


**Understanding Injury Types**

First of all, let me explain what kinds of damage you can receive in the game Escape from Tarkov. The game has several types of injuries: bleeding, fractures, and destruction of body parts-each in their way of treatment. The light bleeds involve slow health depletion over time and, while not immediately life-threatening, can get dangerous if untreated. Contrary to this, heavy bleeds cause fast blood loss and will kill your character unless attended to with expedition. Fractures ruin movement speed and/or aim such that you are a highly advisable target. Blackened limbs result from too much damage and render a body part completely useless, returning some serious penalties, such as reduced stamina and weapon handling. Knowing these types of injuries means the difference between successful recovery and survival in the game.

**Healing During Combat**

In actual combat, the task of healing becomes very different. Timing and knowing how to heal can mean life and death. It is important that you use some form of cover while healing, as attempting to heal injuries in an open area can quickly result in death. First of all, stop the heavy bleeds as they are the most immediate threat, then treat fractures and blackened limbs. Painkillers will temporarily numb your pain and make you able to keep up the fight or escape. While doing this, assign medical items to hotkeys for quick access to rapid healing in the heat of battle without fumbling through your inventory.

**Must-Have Medical Supplies**

To manage injuries properly, one will need to have the right medical supplies. Medical items within Escape from Tarkov vary between the simple bandage to high-grade kits capable of healing many injuries at once. Bandages are a must-have for light bleed stops; inexpensive and compact, they become a raid necessity. Stashed in large numbers, bandages can quickly stop small blood loss before it gets out of hand. Medkits – AI-2, CAR medkit, and higher-tier IFAK and Salewa kits – can heal injuries and stop bleeds. The Salewa kit is especially great because it heals all injuries and stops all bleeds with one usage.

**Severe Injuries**

To cure the more grievous wounds, hemostats and tourniquets come into play. These goods are designed to deal with heavy bleeding, that obviously is far more life-threatening than lighter forms. In any case, the Esmarch tourniquet or even the CALOK-B hemostatic agent is the standard equipment to halt heavy bleeding. Without this equipment, players can easily bleed out; hence, it is essential to include at least one in your configuration. Splints are also quite vital, which will be used to deal with fractures that can significantly hinder mobility. Immobilizing splint is the most straightforward method of fracture treatment, and Alu splint is more advanced, serving for several fractures before it gets used up.

**Pain Management**

The painkillers like Analgin or Ibuprofen are applied to handle pain and blackened limbs. For the cases of blackening of a limb, painkillers offer some relief that enables a person to move around and fight despite their injuries. But remember: using painkillers, you will get dehydrated, so hydration items like water bottles or Aquamaris will help you preserve your health for a long run. Also, CMS and Surv12 kits will deal with blackened limbs and restore functionality to any particular part of your body. It’ll restore blackened limbs with the CMS kit, but lower the maximum health of the treated limb, while the advanced Surv12 kit restores the limbs without such a high penalty in maximum health. These kits are crucial for longer raids, where extensive damage is possible.

**Conclusion**

Good health and injury management are crucial to raid survival in Escape from Tarkov. Knowing how to treat different types of wounds and having proper medical supplies at hand often means the difference between life and death. This will put you far ahead in the art of mastering medical knowledge and healing strategies, increasing your chances of survival even on a very dangerous raid. Be it the staunching of a heavy bleed, using a CMS kit to restore a blackened limb, or knowing when to step away from using cheats that would undermine the integrity of the game, knowing how to react quickly and treat injuries will make all the difference in mastering Escape from Tarkov.
