In this cast, I spoke with returning guest and Game Economist Ramin Shokrizade about the recent proposed bill to regulate loot boxes and what it could mean for game developers.

The plans for the loot box bill would basically destroy the free to play market as it stands, and Ramin and I went over the copy of it available for everyone. We spoke about what ethical F2P design looks like, and whether loot boxes have a place in it.
