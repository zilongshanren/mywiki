GTAV comes with stunning visuals (4K resolution, 60 frames per second, HDR options, and more) and a new career builder option that allows players to choose their path (biker, nightclub owner, executive, or gunrunner).**Death Stranding 2****– On The Beach**: This sequel is Hideo Kojima’s latest game. ### 2. Fresh New Titles

**Avowed**: If you’re into fantasy games, then this is for you. Developed by Obsidian Entertainment, Avowed is set in the universe of the Pillar of Eternity. From spellcasting to mysterious islands, the game remains true to Obsidian’s action signature.

**Clockwork Revolution**: This title is an action role-playing game made exclusively for Windows and the Xbox Series X/S. Unlike other games, players can alter the timeline of the game allowing them to replay it for different outcomes. This level of time manipulation also makes it appealing to players who love adventure games that require strategy and skill. The game is developed by inXile Entertainment.

## Technology in Adventure Games

New games like Avowed and Clockwork Revolution will use AI to create dynamic storylines. Game characters will no longer have to follow rigid scripts written by developers, instead, they will respond to whatever decisions the players make. The effect this will have is to create a storyline driven by consequences, which is the reality of life.

In 2025, we can also expect to see VR/AR-exclusive adventure games that allow players to explore their physical environment to solve puzzles or to go on a citywide adventure. This kind of realism cannot be matched by traditional video games no matter how advanced the visuals are.

Another trend to look out for is cloud gaming. Many games are going up in the cloud to improve gaming experiences for players. Cloud games do not take up storage space, as such, players do not need to purchase expensive consoles to buy more storage. For developers, this allows them to seamlessly fix bugs and perform upgrades without interrupting players or requiring their input (or permission) to do so. Examples of these platforms include [LG TV packed with Xbox Cloud Gaming](https://www.theverge.com/2025/1/6/24337366/xbox-cloud-gaming-lg-tvs-ces-2025), GeForce Now, and other similar trends that will come along in 2025.
