While Virtual Reality (VR) might seem like a fairly recent technological development, it dates all the way back to the 1960s. But it wasn’t until the 1990s that VR gained traction in the gaming industry thanks to the introduction of innovative, yet still primitive arcade games and home consoles. The technology continues to evolve throughout the following decades and, nowadays, VR includes high-resolution displays, sophisticated motion tracking systems, and haptic feedback devices.

2023 has been an exciting year for VR games enthusiasts, with the release of titles such as Resident Evil Village, Firmament, Horizon Call of the Mountain, and Vertigo 2. So, what other VR titles can gamers expect in 2023 and what’s the [future of the technology](https://game-wisdom.com/critical/4-concerns-vr) looking like?


## Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

An VR, open-world Grand Theft Auto was announced at Meta’s Connect in October 2021. The game, which was said to have been in development for several years, is expected to be released in 2023. Mark Zuckerberg himself promised that players would be able to experience the 2004 classic Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas “in a new way”. The original game is regarded as one of Rockstar’s greatest titles, lauded for its missions, characters, music, weapons, vehicles and open world.

## Assassin’s Creed Nexus

Announced back in September 2020, Assassin’s Creed Nexus is a highly anticipated VR title from developers Ubisoft for Meta Quest headsets. While not much has been revealed about the game, leaks have suggested that players will be able to take on the role of beloved characters such as Cassandra, Connor, Ezio and Haymat in 20-minute linear missions.

Assassin’s Creed Nexus will also bring anything that’s expected from an entry in the franchise, including the ability to climb, pickpocket, fight with precision and do the Leap of Faith. According to recent reports, Ubisoft is expecting the game to be a hit and is allegedly [discussing a sequel. ](https://mixed-news.com/en/assassins-creed-nexus-vr-almost-done/)

## Hellsweeper VR

Hellsweeper VR is confirmed to be released for Quest 2, PC VR and PlayStation VR later this year. In this rogue-like, first-person combat game, the player takes on the role of an undead immortal who is tasked with the mission of entering hell and eliminating the twisted entities that inhabit it. Developers Mixed Realms are promising the “craziest moves in VR,” with plenty of backflips, bullet-time, wall-running and power-sliding.
