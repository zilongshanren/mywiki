A roundtable cast with adventure game designers discussing design. We talked about adventure games, design, and a lot of tangents. If there’s interest we are also talking about doing a part 2 at some point in the future.

Our panel included:

- Corey and Lori Cole who worked on Hero-U and the Quest for Glory Series: https://store.steampowered.com/app/375440/HeroU_Rogue_to_Redemption/ https://www.hero-u.com/ https://twitter.com/transolargames
- Dave Gilbert of Wadjet Eye Games who made the Blackwell series, Unavowed and more http://www.wadjeteyegames.com/ https://store.steampowered.com/app/336140/Unavowed/ https://store.steampowered.com/app/1221950/Nighthawks/ https://twitter.com/WadjetEyeGames
- Francisco González of Grundislav Games who made Lamplight City and currently working on Rosewater https://twitter.com/GrundislavGames https://store.steampowered.com/app/761460/Lamplight_City/ https://store.steampowered.com/app/1226670/Rosewater/
- Julia Minamata who is currently working on the Crimson Diamond https://www.thecrimsondiamond.com/ https://store.steampowered.com/app/1098770/The_Crimson_Diamond/ https://www.youtube.com/c/JuliaMinamata https://www.twitch.tv/a_maplemystery
- And Nathan of Sick Chicken Games who just released Guard Duty https://twitter.com/sickchickstudio www.sickchicken.com https://store.steampowered.com/app/872750/Guard_Duty/
