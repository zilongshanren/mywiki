Every year, world-famous video game developers create new, more and more twisted plots that gamers go headlong into. An enthusiastic player may forget what day it is, what time it is, and how long ago he ate or slept. This is especially true for open-world games, where there are practically no boundaries – you explore the space and interact with it at your discretion, as well as choose how to achieve goals yourself.

Open-world gaming on PS4 is an endless adventure that you just can’t stop playing. It all depends on your actions as if you yourself are writing a gameplay script. There is a continuous interaction with the outside world, you go through quests, and find tips. So, are you ready to check out our selection?


## Red Dead Redemption 2

The sequel to the action-adventure from Rockstar Games saw the world on October 26, 2018. The events unfold in the Wild West and are a reference to the past of the protagonist John Marston. This is a real living world in which your actions determine the further development of events. Each step matters and influences the plot. GTA fans will get a similar experience, only in the style of Wild Wild West.

Despite the length of the story and the number of challenges, you will be able to complete it in an average of 60 hours. It is possible to play mini-games such as poker and feel the whole atmosphere of Wild West. Unfortunately, the developers did not include an [online baccarat game](https://nationalcasino.com/live-casino/live-baccarat) or other table games in Red Dead Redemption 2, but still, it is a fantastic game.

## The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

The third part of the series was released on May 19, 2015, and developed by CD Projekt RED. The plot is based on the series of novels “The Witcher” by Andrzej Sapkowski. The main character Geralt is the witcher fighting monsters. We can note obvious improvements in the series – the combat system has been simplified and thought out.

If you decide to complete all the tasks, you may need about 200 hours.

## Assassin’s Creed Odyssey

On October 5, 2018, Ubisoft Entertainment released a new part of the Assassin’s Creed series. The action takes place in Ancient Greece during the Peloponnesian War. This is a real role-playing story with long dialogues. During the passage of the game, you can choose which character to play. You can explore 27 zones and even have romances with any characters.

The open-world gives a lot of opportunities, which can be enjoyed for at least 100 hours.

## Horizon Zero Dawn

This open-world game was developed by Guerrilla Games and released on March 1, 2017. Imagine life after the apocalypse – there is no trace of civilization left, people are forced to survive and fight robots that have turned into animals. The main character, Aloy, explores a post-apocalyptic planet, fights with opponents, and therefore needs to constantly upgrade her skills.

Horizon is fairly easy to complete, and if you’re not in a hurry, it will take 60 hours.

## Kingdom Come: Deliverance

The open-world role-playing game saw the world on February 13, 2018. Events unfold in Europe during the Middle Ages. The Papal Schism happened and the struggle for power began. There is not a single fantasy element here – the gameplay is as realistic as possible. The main feature is that you decide who to be – a hero or a villain. The plot of the game directly depends on this.

Depending on how quickly you pass the quests, the process can take from 45 to 100 hours.
