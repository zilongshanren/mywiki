This week, returning podcast guest Zain of Ogrehead Studio came back on to discuss what’s been going on with Asura, and the challenge of growing the game post-launch.


We started with catching up with Zain about Ogrehead Studio and how things are going. For the cast, we spoke about what happened after the launch of Asura and the steps they took to continue growing and changing the game.

Zain talked about balancing the difficulty of the game and trying to get it just right. We spoke about the Vengeance expansion and the additions that came with it. As a special point, Zain talked about porting Asura to Japanese and some of the challenges that went with that.
