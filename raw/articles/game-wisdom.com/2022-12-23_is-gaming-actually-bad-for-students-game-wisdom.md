## Escapism

All students experience certain stresses in school, so having a break from negative emotions, school pressure, and anxieties of the real one can be a good thing. But, of course, as usual, moderation is the key here. The virtual world won’t solve all your problems or help you deal with real-life situations. It’s just a controlled escape that can become quite a joyful, sometimes even productive, experience. Yet, it is dangerous to treat games as a constant escape from problems. Such a tendency can lead to negative consequences, such as falling out of social circles and being behind in studies.

When approached wisely, though, gaming can really help students cope with school stress. It can charge young people with positive emotions, fun experiences, and confidence. ## When is gaming a bad thing?

As it turns out, gaming can greatly impact students’ lives and even their school performance. So, why are adults, particularly parents and teachers, so against it? In other words, when can gaming turn into a bad thing? A simple answer will lie in how big of a habit gaming has become for students. Thus, when such a pastime exceeds the reasonable amount of hours per day or week, gaming can turn into a problem. Then, it will start to affect one’s productivity, school performance, social connections, and other spheres of life.

A young person needs to have a well-rounded life. It means experiencing enough of everything over certain time periods. They should be physically active and eat well to stay healthy. They need to have real-life communication and relationships to fulfill their social needs. They should also work on their school progress and reputation to achieve their dreams in real life. However, gaming addiction can take away all those elements from one’s life, replacing them with the virtual world. At this point, one’s love of gaming is unhealthy and requires changes and possible interventions from outside parties. ## Final thoughts

Gaming can be a great, fun hobby for a student. It can give them a temporary escape from their daily stresses and troubles while helping them build motivation and courage to face their real-life fears. Of course, gaming will remain a harmless hobby as long as young people treat it that way, stay responsible with their time, and know the difference between the virtual and the real world.
