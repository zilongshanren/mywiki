Among the other variants of poker that may be worth considering are:

- Omaha – This is a form of poker that is very similar to Texas Hold’em, therefore it may suit players who are seeking a slightly alternate gameplay experience. Instead of being dealt with two hole cards at the beginning, players are dealt with four. Two of these cards must be used when forming a hand of five that involves three community cards.
- Seven Card Stud – In this popular online poker variant, players are dealt seven cards in total, but they need to make the strongest five-card hand that they can if they are to win.
- Razz – An alternative variant to Seven Card Stud, Razz is a game that requires players to make the lowest five-card hand that they can with the cards that they have been dealt. Essentially, it’s the complete opposite.
- Five Card Draw – A game that gives players the chance to continually discard cards until they have the best hand possible, Five Card Draw has become a very popular online poker variant for many.
