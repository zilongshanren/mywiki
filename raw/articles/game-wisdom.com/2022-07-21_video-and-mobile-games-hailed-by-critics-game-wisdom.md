The gaming sector has seen many changes throughout the years, not just from a technological standpoint but also by the sheer number of video and mobile games that are produced on a large scale every year.

Some of them are outstanding, unique, and provide you with hours-on-end entertainment, while others are less than decent. So, if you’re looking to avoid time-wasters and jump into action-packed gameplay, here are some suggestions that need to be part of your bucket list.


**Red Dead Redemption **

Red Dead Redemption is a popular action-adventure game, a successor to 2004’s Red Dead Revolver. It was released back in 2019, but it’s still holding up as a high-quality and well-written video game even with the new modern titles from the same genre.

The game is set in 1911 in the Wild West and follows John Marston, an outlaw whose wife and child are kept hostage, and he is basically threatened to murder or capture his former gang members. The game was praised by critics for its well-developed characters, storylines, and beautiful visuals. It’s an outstanding game by every standard and even features mini-games like poker and blackjack, which you can participate in.

**Florence **

Florence is a unique, heart-warming interactive video game (with a high-quality mobile version) that follows Florence Yeoh, who feels a bit stuck with her day-to-day routine. But, one day, her life changes when she meets an attractive cellist in the park Krish, who turns her world upside down for better or for worse.

They eventually fall in love, but it’s up to you to find how the story ends. The simple game does require interaction. For example, on the first date with the choice, you will need to take some photos or so small puzzles to keep the conversation going and help Florence have a successful date. So, you’re not just tagging along; you actually need to make some decisions that have an impact on the story.

**Assassin’s Creed **

Assassin’s Creed is a famous, critically acclaimed, and widely popular franchise in the gaming community. It is an action-packed game set in different time periods, perfect for history buffs and sci-fi fans. It begins with Altair, who is a skilled young Assassin who needs to retrieve a sacred artifact by Robert de Sable, a Templar leader, but encounters many dangerous opponents and makes horrible mistakes along the way.

Then, in Assassin Creed II, you’ll step into the shoes of Ezio, who decides to work as an Assassin after a personal tragedy leaving him bereft of his family. He is set to murder dastardly templars while his story ends in Brotherhood and Revelations. After Ezio’s story ends, you’ll meet the third Assassin in the [American Revolution](https://www.bbc.co.uk/history/british/empire_seapower/american_revolution_01.shtml). The exciting questions juxtaposed with the open-world game design along with the beautifully depicted characters and environment make the franchise fan-favorite among critics and gamers.
