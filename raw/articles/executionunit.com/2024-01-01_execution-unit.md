An [Execution Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_unit) is part of a CPU that does a well defined bit of work. This Execution Unit makes games.

![](../../assets/42d9efdac16b3adb.jpg)


Dazza has worked in the video games industry for over 25 years starting at System 3 (Constructor: Mob Rules, Bloodlust), moving to Argonaut (Disney’s Aladdin - Nasira’s Revenge, Reeco, Carve and Catwoman) and then on to *PKR - Poker without the vowels* before founding Execution Unit to make games for himself.

Cutting his teeth from a young age on the C64, Atari ST, Amiga and Apple Mac Dazza graduated from Imperial College with a degree in Software Engineering but despite being trained to design software to control nuclear power plants all he wanted to do was make games where you could destroy nuclear power plants! A career in games beckoned.
