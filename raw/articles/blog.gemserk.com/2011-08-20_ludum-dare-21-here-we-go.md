As we did for the previous fourth Ludum Dare (or at least we tried), we joined [Ludum Dare 21](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/) (page is down right now). The theme in this case is Escape.

Based on my lack of imagination I am creating a game similar to [Canabalt](http://www.adamatomic.com/canabalt/) where you play as a Vampire escaping from some vampire hunters named [Vampire Runner](https://github.com/gemserk/vampirerunner). I have almost nothing right now but I am making the effort. If you want to follow my progress, click [here](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/author/arielsan/).

Ruben is also joining, if you want to follow his progress, click [here](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/author/ruben01/). His game is a platformer game trying to add some interesting behavior with physics and gravity initially the idea is that you can turn the room 90 degrees, but could be interesting to add levels without gravity, with gravity inversion like VVVVVV and stuff like that.

Hope we finish something in time.
