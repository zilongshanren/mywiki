### Basic Instructions

Make groups of 3 or more balls of the same color to explode them. Eliminate all balls before they reach the end of the path to win.

- LEFT MOUSE BUTTON - Shoot selected ball
- RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON - Switch balls
- ESCAPE - Pause the game

### Advanced Instructions

If you explode balls in the middle of the chain, they create two chains. The one in the front and the one in the back, the one in the front stops moving and the other one keeps rolling. If the first ball from the back chain and the last ball of the front chain are of the same color, the front chain moves backwards until it hits the back chain. If they form a group of 3 or more balls of the same color, they explode and the process is repeated.
