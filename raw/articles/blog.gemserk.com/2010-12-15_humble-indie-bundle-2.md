The [second Humble Indie Bundle](http://www.humblebundle.com/) is released, this time containing: [Braid](http://www.braid-game.com/), [Cortex Command](http://www.cortexcommand.com/), [Machinarium](http://machinarium.net/), [Osmos](http://www.hemispheregames.com/) and [Revenge of the Titans](http://www.puppygames.net/revenge-of-the-titans/), all DRM free and for Windows, Mac and Linux.

As well as the first HIB, you can pay as you want. The money you pay goes for the developers, for charity ([Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org/) and/or the [Child’s Play Charity](http://www.childsplaycharity.org/)) and for the HIB itself, you decide how much for each one.

By the time this post was written, they had a revenue of $470,446.62 made in only one day. They have 6 days left to keep making money.
