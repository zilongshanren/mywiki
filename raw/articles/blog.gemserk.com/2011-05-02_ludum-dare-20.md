This weekend was the [Ludum Dare](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/) contest, and as you may already know, we always try to participate. It was the Ludum Dare number 20 and the theme was [It’s Dangerous to go Alone! Take this!](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ld-20-voting-round-6-of-6/).

I was the only one who entered the contest this time, my game is named [Codename: Taken](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-20/?action=preview&uid=2034). The idea is to survive as long as you can, there are red flying robots which fires at you and you have a green flying robot which protects you. The defender robot is a bit slow, so as you move away from him you become vulnerable to enemy robots then it is dangerous to go alone.
