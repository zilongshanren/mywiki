This [Ludum Dare](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-21/) was a bit hard, I got a zero originality idea and that demotivated me over the process, so I tried to finish it quickly but without love.

The game is named Vampire Runner and here is the [LD21 entry](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-21/?action=preview&uid=2034). It is about a guy with super powers to move through walls for a limited time. The idea, as I said the previous post, is a game like a [Canabalt](http://www.adamatomic.com/canabalt/), the guy runs forever and runs faster and faster, in the case of [Canabalt](http://www.adamatomic.com/canabalt/) you have to jump to avoid obstacles, in the case of [Vampire Runner](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-21/?action=preview&uid=2034) you have to activate your super skill to move through obstacles, but the main objective remains.
