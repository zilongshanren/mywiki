Auditorium clone is a simple prototype based on [Auditorium](http://www.playauditorium.com/). We chose this game because we considered that its physics based gameplay was easy to replicate with our current engine. Our prototype is a puzzle-based game that consists of modifying a flow of particles to make them reach some targets (colored squares). In order to do that you have space modifiers that can bend it using effects like gravity, forces, reflections, etc.
