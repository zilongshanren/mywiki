As we did for [Ludum Dare 17](https://blog.gemserk.com/2010/04/24/ludum-dare-17/), we decided to participate to [Ludum Dare 18](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/2010/08/20/ludum-dare-18-has-begun/). In this case, there were two options, the classic : one developer has to develop a game in 48hs from the scratch; and the jam which allows multiple developers to develop a game in 72hs reusing any code and assets they want.

We tried to make the games for the main competition but as we ran out of time we decided to continue and participate to the jam instead.

Our entries are [Dude, that’s my bullet](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-18/?action=preview&uid=1929) from [ruben01](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/author/ruben01/) and [Grappling Hookus](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/ludum-dare-18/?action=preview&uid=2034) from [arielsan](http://www.ludumdare.com/compo/author/arielsan/).

You can try our games and make comments if you want.
