Codename: Taken was made for Ludum Dare #20 where the theme was “It’s dangerous to go Alone, take this”. The idea of the game is to survive from the attack of enemy robots, to do that you have a friendly robot which will defend you from their attacks. You can improve your robot by taking upgrades.
