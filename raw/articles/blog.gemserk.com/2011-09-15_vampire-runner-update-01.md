We have published [Vampire Runner on the Android Market](https://market.android.com/details?id=com.gemserk.games.vampirerunner) and added a [dedicated page](https://blog.gemserk.com/games/vampirerunner/) on the [Games](https://blog.gemserk.com/games/) list.

As [introduced earlier](https://blog.gemserk.com/2011/08/22/vampire-runner-my-game-for-ludum-dare-21/), Vampire Runner is a Canabalt like game where you have to run as far as you can, in this case as you are a Vampire you have to run from the sun light.
