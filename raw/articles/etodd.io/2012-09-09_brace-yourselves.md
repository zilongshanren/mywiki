# Brace yourselves

Everyone pretend this blog didn't die for six months, okay? Okay. Here's a brain-dump of random things I've learned while not blogging.

- Distributed programming is tough but really, really fun.
[gevent](http://gevent.org/),[Couchbase](http://www.couchbase.com/),[Fabric](http://fabfile.org), and[pyzmq](http://zeromq.github.com/pyzmq/)are awesome.- Django's admin interface can be twisted fairly easily into a CMS for a
[dynamic website](http://reallifeosu.com). - Bed bugs + week-long power outage + another week with still-broken A/C = fun summer.
- Leaky washing machine + computer in basement directly beneath = new computer.
[A bike](https://etodd.io/assets/pRox8.jpg)is the best thing ever when you're in college.- Spotify is the best thing ever regardless of time or space.
- Batman was eh.
- Bourne was great.
- Game of Thrones, holy crap! Both the books and TV show. Amazing.
- Buy a
[Mosin-Nagant](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosin%E2%80%93Nagant). Best $100 you'll ever spend. - Girls, how do they work?
- Writing HTML5 games is a
[thing which I can do](http://et1337.net23.net/the-easy-life/). - Don't submit your
[cover design contest entry](https://etodd.io/assets/EBOz9.jpg)a month early, they might totally forget you. - Tribes is fun and free.

- In December, Lord willing I'll be graduated, living in a new apartment, working full-time for an iPhone game studio. Holy crap.
