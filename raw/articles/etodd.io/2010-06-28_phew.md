# Phew

Well, I submitted A3P to IndieCade on Friday. It's also currently in QA testing for an online gaming portal ([BigPoint](http://bigpoint.com), still free of course).

I won't say that A3P is done, but I think it's pretty stable. I need to do a bit more work on the connection establishment process (it seems a little fragile), but other than that, there's not much else to do. I also packaged the game into a standalone version available here: [Windows (68 Mb)](http://sourceforge.net/projects/a3p/files/a3p-1.0.exe/download) and [Debian Linux (77 Mb)](http://sourceforge.net/projects/a3p/files/a3p-1.0.deb/download). Speaking of packaging, if anyone with a Mac is interested in creating a Mac installer, please contact me. It should be pretty easy, the only reason I haven't done it is because I don't have a Mac.

I took some delicious screenshots for the BigPoint promotional material. Enjoy.

Anyway, it's not over quite yet! I'll keep you posted.
