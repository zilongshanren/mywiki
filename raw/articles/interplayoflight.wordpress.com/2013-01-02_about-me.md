I am a Lead Rendering Engineer at Playground Games working on an unannounced title. Previously, I was the Lead Graphics Programmer at Unit2Games working on Crayta and at Radiant Worlds working on SkySaga:Infinite Isles, in the UK. Further in the past I was at Blitz Games Studios working on a range of PC, Xbox360 and iOS projects. I was also a rendering engineer at Rare taking part in the development of the in-house rendering engine as well as a few Xbox360 and Xbox games. I did a PhD in Computer Graphics at the University of Warwick.

Computer Graphics and rendering is my passion, starting at high school using GW-Basic. Making them fast is my second passion. I can still remember the awe I felt when I managed to implement hidden line removal on a wireframe, CPU rendered, cube innumerable years ago.
