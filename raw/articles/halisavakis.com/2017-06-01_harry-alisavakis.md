Berrynauts Entertainment is a small studio dedicated to creating simple, fun and addictive games for mobile devices. You can see all the games for android devices in [Berrynauts Google Play page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Berrynauts).

Games by Berrynauts Entertainment:

## Lane Slide

![](../../assets/3f06752118b4f062.png)


Infinite runner game where the player must switch lanes in order to avoid to get hit by spikes!

Features:

- Simple gameplay mechanics
- 100 Unlockable characters
- Leaderboards and achievements

You can give it a go here: [Lane Slide on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Berrynauts.LaneSlide)

## Sling Toss

![](../../assets/5908679cc8390279.png)


Sling your character from one sling to the next! Seems simple enough, but it will probably frustrate you a lot!

Features:

- Simple slinging mechanic
- Google Play Games integration with leaderboards and achievements

You can check it out here: [Sling Toss on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Berrynauts.SlingToss)
