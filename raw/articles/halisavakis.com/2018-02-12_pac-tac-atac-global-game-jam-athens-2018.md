Our entry for the Global Game Jam [Athens] 2018. It is a VR game where you have to fight waves of aliens (called pac-tacs) using a weapon you paint with spray cans while you supposedly wait for a space tow-truck to pick you up.

Features:

- Ability to draw your own unique weapon
- Procedurally generated enemies by a combination of different body parts
- Unique art style made through Google’s Tilt Brush

The team:

- Harry Alisavakis – Programming
- Sotiris Gyftopoulos – Programming, team coordination
- Spiros Koutsourelis – Art
- Chris Day – Art, voice acting, video production
- Akis Pavlidis – Music, VFX
- Renaldas Zioma – Programming, optimization support
