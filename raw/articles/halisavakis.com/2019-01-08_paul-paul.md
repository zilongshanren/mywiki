Paul is a VR narrative experience where players try to figure out their identity through exploring surroundings and events in a tech-noir, futuristic and often changing environment.

The game is developed for most major VR devices including:

- HTC Vive
- Oculus Rift
- Oculus GO
- Google daydream
- Windows Mixed Reality

You can learn more about the project in the [project’s website](http://paul.anothercircus.com).
