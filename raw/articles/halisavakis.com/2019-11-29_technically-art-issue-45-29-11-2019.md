Value of PI using the Monte Carlo sampling.

— Shahriar Shahrabi (@IRCSS)

A circle with a radius of r. A square with length of 2r.

Relationship between area of circle and square:

p = PI *r*r/4r*r

PI = 4p

you can calculate p with monte carlo:

p = samplesInCircle/ TotalSamples
