Clean 10×10 grid

— aVersionOfReality (@AversionReality)[#UVmap]! Made with UV transfer.

– Snap a grid to the face

– Snap the face to that grid (shrinkwrap, project, etc)

– Tweak with shapekey

– Transfer UVs from the grid to that face

– Transfer from that face to the original

– Apply, clean by hand if needed[#b3d][#bnpr][pic.twitter.com/7caTVgAymM][January 28, 2023]
