## Introduction

The theme for this [Technically Speaking](https://discord.gg/e9XnPFv) challenge was once again suggested by [Simon](https://twitter.com/simonschreibt) and this time it was: No textures allowed.

Pretty simple concept; the participants could create anything they wanted around tech art (shaders, vfx) but not use any texture files whatsoever! That being said, render textures etc were ok to use!
