I’ve just released a new update for an [old blender addon](http://www.boristhebrave.com/2014/05/07/celtic-knots/), bumping the version to 1.0.

The addon can now generate a much wider variety.

Check it out on ** github**.


And you generate “twills”, a fancier sort of weaving.

![](../../assets/50e2394639321315.png)


The coloring and UV options have also been improved, as you can see above.

Thanks to the following papers which helped me generate these additional features:

* [Single-Cycle Plain-Woven Objects (2010) Xing, Akleman, Chen, Gross](https://www.viz.tamu.edu/faculty/ergun/research/topology/papers/smi10.pdf)

*

[Cyclic Twill Woven Objects (2011)](https://www.viz.tamu.edu/faculty/ergun/research/topology/papers/smi11.pdf)

*Akleman, Chen, Chen, Xing, Gross*
