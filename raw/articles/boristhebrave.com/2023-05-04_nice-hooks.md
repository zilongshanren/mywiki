I’ve created a new open source library.

I’ve been learning quite a bit about AI and AI Alignment recently. A few weeks ago I joined the [Interpretability Hackathon](https://itch.io/jam/interpretability-hackathon). Sadly my contributions were minimal as I had to leave halfway through, but doing it made me appreciate how bad the tooling is in this area.

So I’ve created [nice-hooks](https://github.com/BorisTheBrave/nice-hooks), a library for working with pytorch hooks and activations more effectively.
