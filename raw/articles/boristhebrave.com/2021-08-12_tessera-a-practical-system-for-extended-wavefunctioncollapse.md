I’ve been working a lot on Tessera. I presented a paper at the most recent [PCG Workshop](http://pcgworkshop.com/) of [FDG](http://fdg2021.org/), where I explain how Tessera makes WaveFunctionCollapse somewhat less daunting, and go into some of the details of its features.

That may not be news for users of the software, but here I explain *how* things work, and what parts work well / I’m especially proud of.
