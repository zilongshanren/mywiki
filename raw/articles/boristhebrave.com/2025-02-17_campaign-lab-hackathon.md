I did a short hackathon “Building Tools for Community Organisers with Matrix” sponsored by [Campaign Lab](https://www.campaignlab.uk/), working in a team with [Ben Dunn Flores](https://www.dunnflores.com/).

Our tool is a prototype for summarizing a chat log, and publishing the result to Matrix (an instant message platform) and gitbooks.
