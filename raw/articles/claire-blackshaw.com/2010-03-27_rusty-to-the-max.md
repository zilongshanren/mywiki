Rusty to the Max

So I’ve been moddelling most of this afternoon and remembering how rusty I am at this stuff. Trying to decide on the work flow path to take. Part of me wants to work on the modelling to the extreme (barring pipes) another part of me wants to work a rough animation together to play around with the camera angles and timing.

After the horrible IK mistakes I’ve been having I think I might focus on texturing and modelling the scene then come back to the nightmarish problems involved with the see through pipe IK problems.
