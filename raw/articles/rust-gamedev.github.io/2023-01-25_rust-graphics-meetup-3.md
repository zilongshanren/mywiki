The third Rust Graphics Meetup will take place on [Jan 28th at 16:00
UTC+0](https://everytimezone.com/s/0d2e3d68). This meetup is a chance to see what others have been
working on in the Rust graphics community. You can see videos from the [previous
meetups here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yzr9va5UtiE&list=PLYiOdhpKxxXJwaocrJcOCoBhlV6foaO8F).

The meetup will take place on the Rust Gamedev [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=63dnzjw4azI) and
[Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/RustGameDev).

## Schedule [#](https://gamedev.rs#schedule)

### Introduction 👋 | [Dzmitry Malyshau](https://github.com/kvark) & [Connor Fitzgerald](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald) [#](https://gamedev.rs#introduction-wave-dzmitry-malyshau-connor-fitzgerald)

**16:00 - 16:05**

### Hello, Blade! | [Dzmitry Malyshau](https://github.com/kvark) [#](https://gamedev.rs#hello-blade-dzmitry-malyshau)

**16:05 - 16:40**

Introducing a lean and mean graphics library ‘Blade’, for fun and no profit.


### Implementing an Extensible Renderer | [Philip Degarmo](https://github.com/aclysma) [#](https://gamedev.rs#implementing-an-extensible-renderer-philip-degarmo)

**16:40 - 16:55**

Quick introduction to Rafx, and some ideas for making more extensible renderers.


### Rend3: High Performance, Cross Platform, GPU Driven Rendering in wgpu and WebGPU | [Connor Fitzgerald](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald) [#](https://gamedev.rs#rend3-high-performance-cross-platform-gpu-driven-rendering-in-wgpu-and-webgpu-connor-fitzgerald)

**16:55 - 17:25**

An overview of the current state of rend3: new data and gpu driven rendering model, performance improvements, and a look into the future.
