The 32nd Rust Gamedev Meetup took place on November 11th. You can watch the
recording of the meetup [here on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MadryxOwjb8). The meetup took
place on the Rust Gamedev [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@RustGameDevelopment) and [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/RustGameDev).
The meetups take place on the second Saturday of every month via the [Rust
Gamedev Discord server](https://discord.gg/yNtPTb2) and are also [streamed on
Twitch](https://twitch.tv/rustgamedev).
