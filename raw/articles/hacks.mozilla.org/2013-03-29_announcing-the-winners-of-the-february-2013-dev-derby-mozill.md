Last month, some of the most creative web developers out there pushed the limits of [touch events](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/DOM/Touch_events) and multi-touch interaction in the [February Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/february) contest. After looking through the entries, our [three expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby#tab-judging)–Franck Lecollinet, Guillaume Lecollinet, and (filling in for Craig Cook this month) yours truly–decided on three winners and two runners-up.

Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting things you can do with multi-touch today.

![Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## The Results

### Winners

- First place:
[TouchCycle](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/touchcycle)by newcomer[Koen Kivits](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/koenkivits) - Second place:
[Multitouch Boids](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/multitouch-boids)by JavaScript artist[Jaume Sánchez](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/spite) - Third place:
[Last Blob Standing](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/last-blob-standing)by the always creative[Nikhil Suresh](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/nklsrh)

### Runners-up

[Polyball](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/polyball)by newcomer[Josh Freeman](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/freejosh)[Multi-touch Timer](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/multi-touch-timer)by newcomer[Emmanuel Pelletier](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/Leimi)

Naming just a few winners is always difficult, but the task was especially hard this time around. Just about every demo I came across impressed me more than the last, and I know our other judges faced a similar dilemma. Our winners managed to take a relatively familiar form of interaction and create experiences that were more creative, more fun, and more visually stunning than we would have ever predicted. Congratulations to them and to [everyone else who amazed us last month](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/february).
