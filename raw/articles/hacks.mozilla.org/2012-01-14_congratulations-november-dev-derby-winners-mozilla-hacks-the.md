Canvas is a new HTML5 element which creates a digital “drawing board.” A web developer can use one of these drawing boards along with some JavaScript to create simple shapes, graphs, animations, interactive games, and more. Recently, eighteen creative minds showed us just how powerful and important Canvas is by sharing their work in the [November Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2011/november/).

Once again, this was a fairly close race, so join us in congratulating the winners:

![devderby-november-2011-winners](../../assets/3d8e90c9ff18132e.png)


**1st Place:** [Bouncy and the Apple – Canvas](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/bouncy-and-the-apple) by [nklsrh](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/nklsrh/)

**2nd Place:** [Rob in Soundland](https://developer.mozilla.org/hu/demos/detail/rob-in-soundland) by [michal.b](https://developer.mozilla.org/hu/profiles/michal.b/)

**3rd Place:** [Vandalism](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/vandalism) by [tuxie](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/tuxie/)

**Runners-up:**

[Jump the Wall](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/jump-the-wall) by [avnishkgaur](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/avnishkgaur/)

[Cirplosion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/cirplosion) by [AmaanC](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/AmaanC)

Thanks to everyone who participated in the November and December Dev Derbies! We love your demos. In the days ahead, we’ll be rounding up the votes on December’s [IndexedDB submissions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2011/december/). Now’s the time to get your January Orientation demos built and submitted. Can’t wait to give them a spin (or a tilt).
