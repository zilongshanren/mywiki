If you’ve been here last week, you might have seen the [webinar and geolocation Q&A with Remy Sharp](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/09/webinar-geolocation-with-remy-sharp/). Sadly enough, we had a problem recording the screen so we recorded this replacement screencast yesterday night to give you a quick introduction to the Geolocation API.

Once you are up to speed (or refreshed your memory) why not go and [build something for this month’s Developer Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby)?

You can [see the video on any HTML5 enabled device here](http://vid.ly/6b3p7k) (courtesy of vid.ly).
