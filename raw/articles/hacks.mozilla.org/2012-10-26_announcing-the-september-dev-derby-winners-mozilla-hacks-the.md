Last month, web developers young and old showed us what they could do with [Geolocation](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Using_geolocation) in the [September Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2012/september) contest. After looking through the entries, our [three expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby#tab-judging)–David Walsh, Joe Stagner and John Hammink–have decided on our top picks.

Not a contestant? There are many other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting things you can do with Geolocation today.

![Dev Derby logo Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## Winners

- First place:
[GoGeoTweet](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/gogeotweet)by newcomer[Giovanny Andrés Góngora Granada](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/Gioyik) - Second place:
[Around the World](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/around-the-world)by the always creative[guska076](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/guska076) - Third place:
[Meet me there](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/meet-me-there)by August winner[Patrick Wied](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/pa7)

## Runners-up

[Map My Day](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/map-my-day)by[Patrick Wied](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/pa7)[Neighborhood Hangouts](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/neighborhood-hangouts)by newcomer[Massimiliano Marcon](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/mmarcon)[Geolocation love](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/geolocation-love)by sophomore[Tomasz Kołodziejski](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/Tomasz_Kolodziejski)

You may have noticed that *Meet me there* also ended in the top three [last month](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2012/09/announcing-the-august-dev-derby-winners/). This marks a first for the Dev Derby. Because Patrick used both the Camera API and Geolocation in his demo, it was eligible for both contests. As if that weren’t interesting enough, this was one of the closest Derbies we have ever held, with *GoGeoTweet* and *Around the World* ending in a virtual photo finish.

Deciding on just a few winners is always difficult, so let’s not forget about our [other amazing contestants](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2012/september). Every one of these participants has pushed this important mobile technology forward in ways we never imagined, and deserves a great deal of praise for doing so. I look forward to seeing what they create next.
