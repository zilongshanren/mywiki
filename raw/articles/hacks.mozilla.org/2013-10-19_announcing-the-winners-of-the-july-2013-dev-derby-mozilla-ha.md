This past summer, some of the most passionate and creative web developers out there innovated with the [File API](http://dev.w3.org/2006/webapi/FileAPI/) in our [July Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/july) contest. After sorting through the entries, an all-star cast of former judges–[Peter Lubbers](https://twitter.com/peterlubbers), [Eric Shepherd](http://www.bitstampede.com/), and [David Walsh](http://davidwalsh.name/)–decided on three winners and two runners-up.

Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting things you can do with the File API today.

![Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## The Results

### Winners

- First place:
[miniPaint](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/minipaint)by newcomer[viliusl](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/viliusl) - Second place:
[Tutorializr](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/tutorializr)by newcomer[M.G.A.](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/M.G.A.) - Third place:
[Slide My Text !](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/slide-my-text)by the creative[Arun Kumar](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/palerdot)

### Runners-up

[TopoPhoto](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/topophoto)by the always incredible[Sevastos](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/seva)[imageviewer.js](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/imageviewerjs)by newcomer[Alexey](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/marwinxxii)

Congratulations to these winners! As always, this represents only a small portion of the impressive work submitted to the contest. After you have finished playing with these winning demos, be sure to [check out the rest](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/july). You will not be disappointed.

The Dev Derby is currently on hiatus, but will be back before long. In the meantime, head over to the [Demo Studio](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/) to see some general-interest demos and submit your own.
