HTML5 [orientation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/detecting_device_orientation) allows web developers to read the motion and orientation data of devices to create more engaging and more interactive web experiences.

![](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


Recently, creative developers from around the world demonstrated just how powerful orientation can be in the [January Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2012/january/). After careful consideration, our [three new judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby#tab-judging)—Remy Sharp, Chris Coyier, and Chris Heilmann—are proud to announce that they have decided on three winners and two runners-up. Please join us in congratulating these contributors!

**First Place: **[Help UFO](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/help-ufo) by [michal.b](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/michal.b/)

Second Place: [Pinball Maze](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/pinball-maze) by [CarsonMcDonald](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/CarsonMcDonald/)

Third Place: [Orientation Tetris](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/orientation-tetris) by [nestoralvaro](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/nestoralvaro/)

**Runners Up**

[Exploration](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/exploration) by [seva](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/seva/)

[Street Orientation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/street-orientation) by [nestoralvaro](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/nestoralvaro/)

And let’s not forget about our [other exceptional contributors](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2012/january/). Each and every one of these participants is is pushing the web forward and deserves a great deal of praise as a result.

Want to see your name here next month? We are now accepting demos related to [CSS 3D Transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/Using_CSS_transforms) (March), [HTML5 audio](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Using_HTML5_audio_and_video) (April), and [Websockets](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/WebSockets) (May). Get an early start by [submitting today](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby)!
