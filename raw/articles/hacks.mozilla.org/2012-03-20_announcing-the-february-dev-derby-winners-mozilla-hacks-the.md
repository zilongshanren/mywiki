[Touch events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Touch_events) help you make [websites](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/learn) and [applications](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Apps) more engaging by responding appropriately when users interact with touch screens. A user touching a screen is very different from a user clicking a mouse button, so special care must be taken to ensure that [touch-enabled](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Touch_events) Web applications respond to touch screen interactions in ways that users expect.

![](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


Last month, creative developers from around the web competed in the [Touch Events Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2012/february/) to show off just how powerful [touch events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Touch_events) are. We had some really great entries (many so much fun I could barely stop playing with them). Our [expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby#tab-judging)—[Remy Sharp](http://remysharp.com/), [Chris Coyier](http://chriscoyier.net/), and [Chris Heilmann](http://christianheilmann.com/)—looked over the entries and are proud to announce three winners and a runner-up. Please join us in congratulating these outstanding contributors!

**1st: **[Pinch That Frog!](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/pinch-that-frog) by [Danny.D](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/Danny.D/)

2nd:[Kite Flying](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/kite-flying) by [ltch](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/ltch/)

**3rd: **[The Face Builder](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/the-face-builder) by [boblemarin](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/boblemarin/)

But let’s not forget about [everyone else](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2012/february/) who submitted to this Derby. Each and every one of these people is pushing the web forward and deserves a huge amount of praise for doing so.

Want to see your name here next month? We are now accepting demos related to [CSS 3D Transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/Using_CSS_transforms) (March), [HTML5 audio](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Using_HTML5_audio_and_video) (April), and [Websockets](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/WebSockets) (May). Get an early start by [submitting today](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby)!
