Welcome to a quick round-up of what Mozillians have been talking about at events in and around June.

[Frédéric Harper](https://twitter.com/fharper)spoke at

[Devoxx UK](http://www.devoxx.co.uk/)about “

[Getting the best out of your design with responsive web design](http://outofcomfortzone.net/2014/06/13/getting-the-best-out-of-your-design-with-responsive-web-design-at-devoxx-uk/)“

[Robert Nyman](https://twitter.com/robertnyman)spoke at

[JSCamp Romania](http://www.jscamp.ro/)about “Five stages of development (

[slides](http://www.slideshare.net/robnyman/five-stages-of-development)–

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lG474sQedI4))”

[David Baron](https://twitter.com/davidbaron)spoke at

[CSS Day](http://cssday.nl/)about “Efficient CSS Animations (

[slideshow](http://dbaron.org/talks/2014-06-04-cssday/slide-1.xhtml)–

[all slides](http://dbaron.org/talks/2014-06-04-cssday/master.xhtml))

[Chris Heilmann](http://twitter.com/codepo8)visited

[Campus Party Mexico](http://www.campus-party.com.mx/2014/index.html)to deliver the keynote “The Future of the Open Web (

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8cZ-rPu7oxQ),

[slides](http://www.slideshare.net/cheilmann/campus-party-mexico-the-future-of-the-open-web))”. There were also various talks by local Mozillians.

[Nick Desaulniers](http://nickdesaulniers.github.io/)spoke at

[HTML5DevConf](http://html5devconf.com)about “

[Raw WebGL (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H4c8t6myAWU)” whilst

[Chris Heilmann](http://twitter.com/codepo8)delivered the “

[Write less, achieve meh (notes)](http://christianheilmann.com/2014/06/04/write-less-achieve-meh/)” keynote.

[Soledad Penadés](https://twitter.com/supersole)covered an inordinate amount of amazing things at

[Goto Amsterdam](http://gotocon.com/amsterdam-2014)in her “Invest in the future: build for the web! (

[notes and screencast](http://soledadpenades.com/2014/07/02/invest-in-the-future-build-for-the-web/))” talk.

[Open Source Bridge](http://opensourcebridge.org)in Portland had a whole bunch of Mozillians presenting (the site also has lots of notes of what happened where and when and is worth visiting):

[Lukas Blakk](https://twitter.com/lsblakk)talked about “[Explicit Invitations: Passion is Not Enough for True Diversity (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ypU1U4MBgM)“[Larissa Shapiro](https://twitter.com/larissashapiro)covered “[Badging and Beyond: Rubrics and Building a Culture of Recognition as Community Building Strategies](http://www.slideshare.net/larissashapiro/recognition-osb-36447243)“[Ben Kero](https://twitter.com/bkero)covered “[Modern Home Automation](http://opensourcebridge.org/sessions/1328)“[Erika Owens](https://twitter.com/erika_owens)talked about “[From navel gazing to ass kicking: Building leadership in the journalism code community (ODP slide deck)](https://docs.google.com/file/d/0ByNClvIX143uSHhYTlljXzNoQ0U/edit)“[Lars Lohn](https://twitter.com/2braids)gave insight into “[When Firefox Faceplants – what the fox says and who is listening](http://uncommonrose.com/presentations/pytn14/)” and “[Nest + Pellet Stove + Yurt]( http://www.twobraids.com/2014/01/hacking-pellet-stove-to-work-with-nest.html)“[Liz Henry](https://twitter.com/lizhenry)partnered with[Sumana Harihareswara](https://twiter.com/brainwane)to explain “[The Outreach Program for Women: what works & what’s next (PDF)](http://opensourcebridge.org/wiki/File:Opw-works-next-small.pdf)“[Eitan Isaacson](https://twitter.com/eeejay)talked about “[Making your mobile web app accessible (notes by Carson Shold)](http://www.carsonshold.com/2014/06/open-source-bridge-making-mobile-web-app-accessible/)“[Sucheta Ghoshal](http://mikipedian.blogspot.in/)and[Niharika Kohli](http://niharika29.roon.io/)covered “[Making language selection smarter in Wikipedia (slides)](https://docs.google.com/file/d/0ByNClvIX143uM0hqeG1uNzk4MVE/edit)“[Netha Hussain](https://twitter.com/nethahussain)shared “[The joy of volunteering with open technology and culture](http://opensourcebridge.org/wiki-raw/images/b/b0/The_joy_of_volunteering_-Netha_Hussain.pdf)“

[Dale Harvey](https://twitter.com/daleharvey)attended

[Scotch on the Rocks](http://2014.sotr.eu/)and talked about “The offline Web (

[slides](http://daleharvey.github.io/Presentations/SOTR-Edinburgh-2014-06-06/#/)–

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nnLBdFLo2fc))” and took part in the panel discussion on “

[When is Enough Enough (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ai4y-Iecfw)“

If you want to know where we will present, check the [Where is Mozilla?](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/events) page. If you are interested in getting a Mozilla presenter for your event, [why not tell us about it](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/form.dev-engagement-event)?
