Just a quick tip that if you are a developer and you lately upgraded Firefox4 to Firefox5 (or why not give [Aurora](http://www.mozilla.com/en-US/firefox/channel/) a try?) it tells you that Firebug is not compatible and can’t find an upgrade.

However, there is a compatible version of Firebug (v1.8) available for you: [get it here](http://getfirebug.com/releases/firebug/1.8/).

Now you can get the latest Firefox goodness and still debug your code.
