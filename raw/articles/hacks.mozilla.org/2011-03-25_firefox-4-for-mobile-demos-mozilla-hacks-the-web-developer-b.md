The [Release Candidate for Firefox 4 for mobile](http://www.mozilla.com/en-US/mobile/4.0/releasenotes/) (Maemo and Android) is out. If you want to see a quick overview of Firefox for Mobile, look at [Madhava’s post](http://madhava.com/egotism/archive/005055.html).

**Firefox 4 Desktop, Firefox 4 Mobile: same engine!**

And this is awesome! It means you will find the same feature in mobile and desktop: HTML5, CSS3 and modern JavaScript APIs. And this is what we want to show you in our new round of demos in [Web O Wonder](http://webowonder.org) (*This new round also includes more 3D Demos, see this dedicated blog post)*.
