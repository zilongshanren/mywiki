Virtual reality (VR) seems complicated, but with a few JavaScript libraries and tools, and the power of [WebGL](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API), you can make very nice VR scenes that can be viewed and shared in a headset like an Oculus Go or HTC Vive, in a desktop web browser, or on your smartphone. Let me show you how:

In this new YouTube series, [How to make a virtual reality project in your browser with three.js and WebVR](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLo3w8EB99pqKLF6R8eAaleDeS-vUqOzin), I’ll take you through building an interactive birthday card in seven short tutorials, complete with code and examples to get you started. The whole series clocks in under 60 minutes. We begin by getting a basic cube on the screen, add some nice 3D models, set up lights and navigation, then finally add music.

All you need are basic JavaScript skills and an internet connection.
