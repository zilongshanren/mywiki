Today the Developer Engagement Team has launched the [Evangelism Reps program](https://wiki.mozilla.org/ReMo/SIGs/Evangelism_Reps) – a special interest group within [ReMo](https://wiki.mozilla.org/Remo). Each year, we get thousands of requests to send Mozilla speakers around the world to talk about HTML5, new web technologies, Mozilla’s mission, our projects, products and more. Now, we would love for you to join the effort and become a Mozilla speaker too!

This program is open to paid staff and Mozilla Reps of all skill levels and capabilities. If you are a new speaker and have always wanted to represent Mozilla at events, you can take advantage of our advanced speaker training where you can learn from people like Christian Heilmann and Robert Nyman on how to give effective presentations and get access to their best practices. People who are veteran speakers can also benefit by having the tools and resources available to host events, prepare stunning screen casts and be mentors to new Evangelism Reps.

We encourage all current Mozilla speakers to please join our [Speaker Database](https://wiki.mozilla.org/ReMo/SIGs/Evangelism_Reps#Speaker_Database) even if you don’t join the Evangelism Reps program. This will help us know who you are and match you up with the speaking opportunities that fit you best.

The Evangelism Reps program information is on the wiki: [https://wiki.mozilla.org/ReMo/SIGs/Evangelism_Reps](https://wiki.mozilla.org/ReMo/SIGs/Evangelism_Reps)

Please contact us at make-me-a-speaker@mozilla.com if you are interested in joining the team. We anticipate our first training to be held in May so stay tuned for this exciting opportunity.
