We are currently in Tokyo, Japan for the [Mozilla Vision 2012](http://mozilla.jp/events/vision/2012/) conference and hack day. For two days Mozilla Japan with friends from the other locations are putting up an amazing effort to encourage people to help us [educate the next generation of web makers](http://commonspace.wordpress.com/2011/11/22/mozilla-2012-plan/).

![Chibi and Jono showing off a platform game](https://farm8.staticflickr.com/7175/6739121745_5252129f52.jpg)


Being in Japan, all of this is of course wrapped in quite some amazing tech like [voice activated robots with emotional responses](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EtqEY5GII_w), but also good old dinosaurs you can take your tourist shots with:

![Chris Heilmann, Mozilla Godzilla and Mark Finkle](../../assets/743f76a9c42e6a84.jpg)


My part of the play was to give a talk about HTML5 and how we can use it now to give our users a better web experience. The [slides are available](http://icant.co.uk/talks/mozillatokyo/).

There is an [audio recording of the talk on archive.org](http://www.archive.org/details/TheFutureOfHtml5AndWebTechnologies):

The demos I showed in the talk were:

[A morphing cube in CSS](http://jsfiddle.net/codepo8/NvTuf/45/)[An HTML5 rotating logo](http://thewebrocks.com/demos/html5-3d-css/)with CSS3 and JS[Progressively enhancing “box rollovers”](http://www.netmagazine.com/tutorials/create-progressively-enhanced-3d-css-rollovers)[The 3D tester](http://thewebrocks.com/demos/3D-css-tester/)[The beanie maker](http://thewebrocks.com/demos/beaniemaker/)[Cropping images in the browser](http://thewebrocks.com/demos/crop/)[Building a game from HTML](http://thewebrocks.com/demos/html5catcher/)[The Mouselock API](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/12/paving-the-way-for-open-games-on-the-web-with-the-gamepad-and-mouse-lock-apis/)[The Gamepad API](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/12/paving-the-way-for-open-games-on-the-web-with-the-gamepad-and-mouse-lock-apis/)

Today we are at a hack day – stay tuned for some more of the information on that soon.
