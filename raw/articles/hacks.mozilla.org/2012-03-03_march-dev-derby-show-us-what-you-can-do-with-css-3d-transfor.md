The March [Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby) begins today! A monthly contest hosted by the [Mozilla Developer Network](https://developer.mozilla.org/), the [Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby) allows creative web developers to push the web forward and compete for fame, glory, and prizes too.

![](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


This month, we want to see what you can do with [CSS 3D transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/Using_CSS_transforms). New to the topic? That’s okay. We have a great article on [CSS 3D transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/Using_CSS_transforms) over on the [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/) that should help you get started. Additionally, Mozilla principal evangelist Chris Heilmann has contributed three great resources on the topic: an article on [getting started](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/10/css-3d-transformations-in-firefox-nightly/), an article on [progressively enhanced rollovers](http://www.netmagazine.com/tutorials/create-progressively-enhanced-3d-css-rollovers), and a [screencast on rollovers](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/12/screencast-3d-css-rollovers-and-3d-css-tester/). With all this great documentation, you’ll be an expert in no time.

**The Prizes**

- First place: An Android mobile device from
[Motorola](http://www.motorola.com/Consumers/US-EN/Consumer-Product-and-Services/Mobile-Phones/)or[Samsung](http://www.samsung.com/us/mobile/cell-phones)and an article featuring your work here on[Mozilla Hacks](https://hacks.mozilla.org/). - Second place: A hand-crafted laptop messenger bag from
[Rickshaw](http://www.rickshawbags.com/bags/laptop-messenger-bag/standard/standard-commuter-laptop-bag-black.html). - Third place: A limited edition
[MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/)t-shirt to show off your geek cred.

You might have noticed that the March Derby already has three entries. Want to get a head start like these people did? We are also accepting entries related to [HTML5 audio](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Using_HTML5_audio_and_video) (April Derby) and the [Websocket API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/WebSockets) (May Derby). [Submit today](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby)!
