Good Thursday, dear Mozilla Hacks readers! The Mozilla Developer Engagement Team has a new fresh list of reading suggestions for you!


## Weekly links September 29th 2011

If there is anything you think we should read or know about, don’t hesitate to post a comment, contact us on Twitter or through any other mean.

The picks this week are:

### Christian Heilmann

![A picture of Christian Heilmann](../../assets/73c300a3a9b62fcb.jpg)

[tutorial series on Box2D and JavaScript](http://creativejs.com/2011/09/box2d-javascript-tutorial-series-by-seth-ladd/)should get you on the way to write the next Angry Birds.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Christian, he’s available on Twitter as [@codepo8](http://twitter.com/codepo8).

### Havi Hoffman

![A picture of Havi Hoffman](../../assets/b2d68053fc6219be.jpg)

[Marketing your startup with a steady drumbeat of data](http://kellynford.com/2011/09/27/marketing-your-startup-with-a-steady-drumbeat-of-data/)by Kelly Ford of Hunch.com, offers great suggestions for building reputation and credibility by sharing authoritative insights and crafting interesting stories and infographics.

Havi can be found on Twitter as [@freshelectrons](http://twitter.com/freshelectrons)

### Jeff Griffiths

![A picture of Jeff Griffiths](../../assets/6eccd82dcc467937.png)

[Coffeescript in Firebug console with Acebug](http://spin.atomicobject.com/2011/09/23/coffeescript-in-firebug-console-with-acebug/)Lots of people complain about Firebug being sluggish, but lots of other people keep hacking cool stuff into it!

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Jeff, he’s available on Twitter as [@canuckistani](http://twitter.com/canuckistani).

### Joe Stagner

![A picture of Joe Stagner](../../assets/322282a9790b0827.gif)

[OpenStack Diablo Open Source Cloud Platform Accelerates High-Availability](http://www.serverwatch.com/server-news/openstack-diablo-open-source-cloud-platform-accelerates-high-availability.html)– A serious Open Source PaaS / Cloud option lets you develop Cloud Based applications your way!

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Joe, he’s available on Twitter as [@MisfitGeek](http://twitter.com/MisfitGeek).

### Rob Hawkes

![A picture of Rob Hawkes](../../assets/bf1d2a9fda8c5762.jpg)

[Slideshare has finally ditched Flash for HTML5](http://engineering.slideshare.net/2011/09/slideshare-ditches-flash-for-html5/). This is a great move by the main player in the slide-hosting scene. Still, I’m interested to see how newcomer

[Speaker Deck](http://speakerdeck.com/)improves in the near-future.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Rob, he’s available on Twitter as [@robhawkes](http://twitter.com/robhawkes).

### Robert Nyman

![A picture of Robert Nyman](../../assets/520e1af8dc948590.jpg)

[The makeup of the Open Web stack](http://dstorey.tumblr.com/post/10657722675/openwebstack)– Comprehensive & accurate listing of the APIs & specs by David Storey

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Robert, he’s available on Twitter as [@robertnyman](http://twitter.com/robertnyman).
