This past March, some of the most creative web developers out there showed us what they could do for the mobile Web in the [March Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/march) contest. After looking through the entries, our our [three expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby#tab-judging)–Craig Cook, Franck Lecollinet, and Guillaume Lecollinet–decided on three winners and two runners-up.

Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting mobile experiences you can create today using only the skills you already have.

![Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## The Results

### Winners

- First place:
[Memory Cube](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/memory-cube)by sophomore[Jesus Perez](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/tx2z) - Second place:
[Writerrific](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/writerrific)by designer and developer[Laurens Debackere](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/laurensdebackere) - Third place:
[Loc Tag](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/loc-tag)by newcomer[bzub](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/bzub)

### Runners-up

[Paint my dash](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/paint-my-dash)by mobile Web lover[Giovanny Andrés Góngora Granada](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/Gioyik)[Ubercalc](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/ubercalc)by the always impressive[JoanC](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/JoanC)

The March Derby saw some great entries, from games to productivity applications to wonderfully useful location-based services and more. Please join me in congratulating these winners and [all of our contributors](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/march) for making the wireless world a better and more exciting place.

*Want to get a head start on an upcoming Derby? We are now accepting demos related to getUserMedia (May) and WebGL (June). Head over to the Dev Derby to get started.*
