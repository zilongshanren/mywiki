This past April, some of the most creative web developers out there showed us what they could do with [Web Workers](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/Guide/Performance/Using_web_workers) in the [April Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/april) contest. After looking through the entries, our [expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby)–James Padolsey, Janet Swisher, Maire Reavy, and Randell Jesup–decided on three winners and two runners-up.

Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting things you can do with Web Workers today.

![Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## The Results

### Winners

- First place:
[Zen photon garden](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/zen-photon-garden)by newcomer[scanlime](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/scanlime) - Second place:
[Hot-cold Typing !](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/hot-cold-typing)by newcomer[Arun Kumar](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/palerdot) - Third place:
[Photo editor](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/photo-editor)by the always impressive[Ondřej Žára](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/ondras)

### Runners-up

[Fractal Explorer](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/fractal-explorer)by newcomer[calvin](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/calvin)[Professor Sudoku](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/professor-sudoku)by newcomer[tleunen](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/tleunen)

April was the month of the newcomer. If you have ever doubted your ability to make a difference on the web, just look at these results and think again. As always, it was difficult to name just a few winners, so please join me in congratulating [all of our competitors](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/april) for making the web a more exciting and a more responsive place.

*Want to get a head start on an upcoming Derby? We are now accepting demos related to WebGL (June) and the File API (July). Head over to the Dev Derby to get started.*
