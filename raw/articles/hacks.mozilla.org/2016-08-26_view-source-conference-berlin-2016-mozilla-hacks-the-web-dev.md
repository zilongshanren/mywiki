### An overview

[ View Source](https://viewsourceconf.org/) is an intimate, single-track conference for web developers, now in its second year.

View Source 2016 takes place in Berlin, Germany, September 12-14, beginning with Ignite lightning talks on Monday evening, followed by two full days of great presenters, curated conversations, and sociable evenings. Tickets are still on sale.

Here’s a quick look at our lineup of main stage speakers — 16 great reasons to go! Discounted tickets are still available if you [ register now](https://ti.to/mdn/view-source-berlin-2016/discount/MOZHACKS). (Note: this link applies the MOZHACKS discount. Bring a friend!). We’d love to meet you there.
