Last month, you pushed the envelope with the [Camera API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Using_the_Camera_API) in the August Dev Derby competition. After looking through the entries, our [three expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby#tab-judging)–David Walsh, Joe Stagner and (filling in for John Hammink this month) yours truly–have decided on our top five picks.

Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of the entries are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in all of the exciting things you can do with the Camera API today.

![Dev Derby logo Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## Winners

[Φotos](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/otos)by the always determined[Sevastos](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/seva)[Meet me there](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/meet-me-there)by newcomer[Patrick Wied](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/pa7)[Daily Snapshot](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/daily-snapshot)by newcomer[Colin](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/Colin)

## Runners-up

[Photo Shake](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/photo-shake)by newcomer[James Doyle](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/james2doyle)[Shot & Filter](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/shot-filter)by newcomer[dmolin](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/dmolin)

Congratulations to all of our winners. And lets not forget about our [other amazing contestants](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2012/august). This contest was similar to the [June Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2012/june). The number of entries was relatively low, but the quality of each one was exceptionally high. I personally had more than a few “wow” moments, and not all of them were about the winners. Keep up the great work — the open web is better because of it.
