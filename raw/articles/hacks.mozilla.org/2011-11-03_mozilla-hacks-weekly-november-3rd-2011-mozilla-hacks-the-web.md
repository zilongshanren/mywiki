Thursday again, and we in the Mozilla’s Developer Engagement Team has our weekly reading tips for you.


## Weekly links November 3rd 2011

If there is anything you think we should read or know about, don’t hesitate to post a comment, contact us on Twitter or through any other mean.

The picks this week are:

### Christian Heilmann

![A picture of Christian Heilmann](../../assets/73c300a3a9b62fcb.jpg)

[Animatable](http://leaverou.github.com/animatable/)(not to be mixed up with the tool powering

[Madmanimation]) is collection of CSS animations for different attributes. See all the possibilities.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Christian, he’s available on Twitter as [@codepo8](http://twitter.com/codepo8).

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Havi, she’s available on Twitter as [@freshelectrons](http://twitter.com/freshelectrons)

### Rob Hawkes

![A picture of Rob Hawkes](../../assets/bf1d2a9fda8c5762.jpg)

### Robert Nyman
