As you might know, each month we have a [Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby) as part of [Mozilla Developer Network (MDN)](https://developer.mozilla.org/), and each of them are focused on a certain technology where people can compete with their submissions.


![](../../assets/97bf675924639c06.png)

[Detecting device orientation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/detecting_device_orientation).

![](../../assets/c1d0800a0cae1cf9.png)


## Prizes

- Android mobile device
- Winner gets an Android mobile device from Motorola or Samsung.
- Rickshaw laptop bag
- Runner-up gets a hand-crafted laptop messenger bag from Rickshaw.
- MDN t-shirt
- 3rd place gets a limited edition MDN t-shirt to show off their geek cred.

And as if that wasn’t enough, your demo will be showcased in [Mozilla’s Demo Studio](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/), and we would like to feature you in an article here on Mozilla Hacks as well!

So, what are you waiting for? :-)
