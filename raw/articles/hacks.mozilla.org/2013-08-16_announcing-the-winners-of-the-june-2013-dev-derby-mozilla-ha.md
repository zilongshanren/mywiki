This June, some of the most creative web developers out there pushed the limits of WebGL in our [June Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/june) contest. After sorting through the entries, our expert judges–James Padolsey and Maire Reavy–decided on three winners and three runners-up.

Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting things you can do with WebGL today.

![Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


## The Results

### Winners

- First place:
[Falling in Circles](http://developer.cdn.mozilla.net/media/uploads/demos/r/o/rossmckegney/0e2e691e2d9617d5566e52eb1dfdd0b0/falling-in-circles_1371497189_demo_package/index.html)by newcomer[rossmckegney](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/rossmckegney) - Second place:
[Simple 3D Snake Game](http://developer.cdn.mozilla.net/media/uploads/demos/s/a/samsol/a3c128454fe73e10bc9bb1ecb6a1322b/simple-3d-snake-game_1366720299_demo_package/index.html)by sophomore[Samir Solanki](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/samsol) - Third place:
[Cross-hatching GLSL shader](http://developer.cdn.mozilla.net/media/uploads/demos/s/p/spite/35a3de017b01ae2e91d928a979a8703f/cross-hatching-glsl-_1372642697_demo_package/index.html)by the always incredible[Jaume Sánchez](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/spite)

### Runners-up

[Bricks 3D](http://developer.cdn.mozilla.net/media/uploads/demos/r/o/robinmitra/134652472d518862ec7f91314220d138/bricks-3d_1372657066_demo_package/index.html)by newcomer[robinmitra](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/robinmitra)[One Whale Trip](https://developer.cdn.mozilla.net/media/uploads/demos/t/h/thp/0a4a91baf8d77286cba1b48facff403f/one-whale-trip_1368462068_demo_package/index.html)by newcomer[Thomas Perl](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/thp)[Running image triangulation and extrusion](https://developer.cdn.mozilla.net/media/uploads/demos/w/i/wizgrav/f232e401f674086f9aae99b474e27ed0/running-image-triang_1372626220_demo_package/index.html)by newcomer[Yannis Gravezas](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/wizgrav)

Congratulations to these winners and to everyone who competed! The Web is a better, more expansive place because of their efforts.
