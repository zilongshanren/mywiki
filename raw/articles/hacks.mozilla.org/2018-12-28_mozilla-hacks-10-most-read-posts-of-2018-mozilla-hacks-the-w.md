Must be the season of the list—when we let the numbers reveal what they can about reader interests and attention over the past 360-some days of Mozilla Hacks.

Our top ten posts ranged across a variety of categories – including JavaScript and WebAssembly, CSS, the Web of Things, and Firefox Quantum. What else does the list tell us? People like [ code cartoons](https://hacks.mozilla.org/category/code-cartoons/)!

I should mention that the * post on Mozilla Hacks that got the most traffic in 2018 was written in 2015*. It’s called [Iterators and the for-of loop](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2015/04/es6-in-depth-iterators-and-the-for-of-loop/), and was the second of seventeen articles in an amazing, evergreen series, [ES6 In Depth](https://hacks.mozilla.org/category/es6-in-depth/), crafted and written in large part by Jason Orendorff, a JavaScript engineer.
