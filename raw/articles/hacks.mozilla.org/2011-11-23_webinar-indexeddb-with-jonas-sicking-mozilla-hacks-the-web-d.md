IndexedDB is the [emerging standard](http://dvcs.w3.org/hg/IndexedDB/raw-file/tip/Overview.html) for structured client-side data storage. The IndexedDB standard is supported by current versions of Firefox and Chrome, and support for it is expected in Internet Explorer 10.

With this growing maturity and support, it’s time to start experimenting with what IndexedDB can do for Web applications. Working with IndexedDB requires a shift in mindset for many Web developers, as it is more similar to “NoSQL” systems like [CouchDB](http://couchdb.apache.org/) or [MongoDB](http://www.mongodb.org/) than to traditional relational databases.

IndexedDB will be the theme for December’s MDN [DevDerby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby). And to kick off the theme for the month, on **December 1st**, we’re offering an [MDN Webinar](http://hacks.mozilla.org/category/webinars/) on IndexedDB with **Jonas Sicking**, one of the editors of the IndexedDB draft standard and one of the implementors of IndexedDB in Gecko.
