Firefox 4 is just around the corner and the updated version of the Firefox API has been frozen for some time now. The Add-ons blog is reminding add-on developers to [Update your add-on for Firefox 4](http://blog.mozilla.com/addons/2011/02/22/update-your-add-on-in-time-for-firefox-4-2/). Add-ons that are compatible with Firefox 4 are eligible for a competition to be featured in a collection to showcase the best Firefox 4 add-ons. So, if you’re an add-on developer, [check out the details of how to update your add-on](http://blog.mozilla.com/addons/2010/11/11/making-add-on-compatible-firefox-4/), [read the contest rules](http://blog.mozilla.com/addons/files/2011/02/Fx4_Contest-Rules_final_updated.pdf), and enter the competition.
