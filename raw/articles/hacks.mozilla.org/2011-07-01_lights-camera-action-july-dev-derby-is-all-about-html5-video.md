Show off your coolest video hack in July’s [Mozilla Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby)!

Moving pictures have always fascinated people. From the first [zoetropes](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kN9DD8jtXAo) to the multi million dollar blockbusters of today – seeing things move grabs our attention as humans much better than any clever copy or imagery could. Video was alien to the web for a long time. Well, not alien, but we needed plugins to make them play. With newer browsers and HTML5, we now have the `<video>`

element to natively embed video in a web page. This allows us to, amongst other things:

- Style video elements with CSS and SVG
- Make the video interact with other elements of the document and vice versa
- Use Canvas to grab frames from videos and manipulate them
- Create accessible video as the controls can be used with a keyboard without us having to write that functionality

Using this arsenal, you can do pretty cool things. You can:

[write a player that rotates and zooms video](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/01/zooming-and-rotating-for-video-in-html5-and-css3/)([source on GitHub](https://github.com/codepo8/rotatezoomHTML5video))- Build your own
[Indiana Jones style maps](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2010/12/spirit-of-indiana-jones-syncing-html5-video-with-maps/)([GitHub](https://github.com/codepo8/HTML5---map-and-video-syncing)) [Put your colleagues in a washing machine](http://people.mozilla.com/~prouget/demos/round/index.xhtml)[Simulate a green screen](http://people.mozilla.com/~prouget/demos/green/green.xhtml)[Apply CSS3 and SVG filters and transitions to video](http://people.mozilla.com/~prouget/demos/mashup/video.xhtml)[Inject other content into videos](http://people.mozilla.com/~prouget/demos/DynamicContentInjection/play.xhtml)[Stretch a video over the whole screen](http://html5doctor.com/video-canvas-magic/)[Use video in a 3D environment](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sSLcsHAuiF8)[Allow users to take screengrabs of videos](https://github.com/codepo8/videograbber)([source on GitHub](https://github.com/codepo8/videograbber))

..and many more things.

Now it is your turn to show us what you can do with HTML5 video! We don’t expect you to build another [Wilderness Downtown](http://www.thewildernessdowntown.com/) or [3 Dreams of Black](http://ro.me), but we are very excited to offer you the chance to shine in July’s [Mozilla Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby).

Why not use CSS3 and Canvas to redesign the [Outer Limits intro](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8CtjhWhw2I8)? Why not make the video do the things Diana Ross talks about in [Upside down](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4GtyMeEcPPE)? Why not build a video cropping tool in Canvas? How about you have a crack at making closed captioning and subtitling work with HTML5 video? There are many ideas floating about – it just needs a bit of drive and a text editor to go for it. So, show us what you can do!

There are a lot of tools out there to make your life easier with HTML5 video. [Vid.ly](http://vid.ly) makes it easy to convert videos, [Popcorn.js](http://popcornjs.org/) is a library to sync page content and video and there are many [tutorials on video](http://html5doctor.com/tag/video/) available.

To get you into the mood and see some examples in action, we are running an [HTML5 video webinar](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/06/webinar-deconstructing-html5-video/) on the 14th of July and you can ask some questions you have upfront [using Google Moderator](https://www.google.com/moderator/#16/e=9ae94).

Bring the web screen alive, I dare you! I am off to watch [Cinema Paradiso](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0095765/) again.
