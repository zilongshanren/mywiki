Update 2011-08-19:We had intended to record this webinar and make it available for those who couldn’t attend. Due to a convergence of technical difficulties, the recording was not successful. Apologies for failing on that promise. You can see Syd’s[slides]and a[recording]of a similar talk Syd gave at the[Heart & Sole conference]earlier this year.

On Thursday, August 18th at 16:00 UTC, the latest installment of the [MDN webinar series](http://hacks.mozilla.org/category/webinars/) will be presented by [Syd Lawrence](http://sydlawrence.com/), on the topic of using the [History API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Manipulating_the_browser_history) to make AJAX sites more friendly. The History API is also the topic for the August [DevDerby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby) and this week’s [Ask MDN](http://hacks.mozilla.org/category/ask-mdn/) session on Twitter (which Syd will also be helping out with). So you can get your head full of History API know-how, whip up an amazing History API demo, and [submit it for the DevDerby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/submit?tags=challenge%3A2011%3Aaugust)!

Syd Lawrence is a freelance front-end web developer, living in Winchester UK, who [speaks at a variety of conferences](http://lanyrd.com/profile/sydlawrence/) and is also a part-time lecturer at Winchester University in digital media development.

This is the third MDN webinar, and our third experiment in webinar technology. This time around, we’re using [BigBlueButton](http://bigbluebutton.org/), an [open-source web-conferencing](http://bigbluebutton.org/components) system (thanks to our friends at [Blindside Networks](http://blindsidenetworks.com/)). It was used for the [Knight-Mozilla News (MoJo) Learning Lab](https://drumbeat.org/en-US/journalism/process/) in July, and worked pretty well for that. Since it’s open source, if there’s something about it you don’t like, you can [do something about it](http://code.google.com/p/bigbluebutton/).
