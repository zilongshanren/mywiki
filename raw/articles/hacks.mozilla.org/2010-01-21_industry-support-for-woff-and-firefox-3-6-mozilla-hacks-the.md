Today we announced the release of Firefox 3.6 and users are starting to upgrade. One of the more important features we included for developers was support for a new font standard called [WOFF](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2009/10/woff/).

WOFF has received wide support from the type community and we’re starting to see the results of that. There are a couple of specific instances that we’d like to point out on day zero of our release:

1. FontFont, one of the largest type foundries in the world, announced a free-as-in-beer demo font in the WOFF format called [FF Nuvo Medium Demo](http://www.fontfont.com/downloads/index.ep#FFNuvoMediumWoffDemo). This is part of FontFont’s commitment to support the new format and to start to give people the tools to start to adopt it.

2. Typekit, a commercial hosting provider for fonts, [announced that they will start serving up WOFF fonts to Firefox 3.6 users](http://blog.typekit.com/2010/01/21/typekit-supports-woff-in-firefox-3-6/).

It’s great to see commercial support already ramping up to support the new format. It’s going to really improve the experience for users on the web.
