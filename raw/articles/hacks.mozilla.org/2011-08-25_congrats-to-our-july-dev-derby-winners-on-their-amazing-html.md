We shifted gears for July and invited Web developers to have some fun with [HTML5 video](https://developer.mozilla.org/En/HTML/Element/Video) for Dev Derby.

![Mozilla Demo Studio presents Dev Derby on MDN](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


We had [15 awesome demos submitted](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/tag/challenge%3A2011%3Ajuly/) and while there was a lot of entertainment and innovation happening, we had to narrow them down to 5 finalists:

[Facial Recognition and Analytics with HTML5’s Video Tag](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/facial-recognition-and-analytics-with-html5s-video)

[HTML5 Video Voting](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/html5-video-voting)

[HTML5 VJing Tool](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/html5-vjing-tool)

[jQuery VideoBG](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/jquery-videobg)

[Punch the TV!](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/punch-the-tv)

There are so many cool things you can do with HTML5 video on the Web, and our winners circle definitely reflects the possibilities. So join us in congratulating our [July Dev Derby winners](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/tag/challenge%3A2011%3Ajuly/)!

**1st Place:** [HTML5 Video Voting](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/html5-video-voting) by [Jordan Humphreys](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/jordan/)

**2nd Place:** [HTML5 VJing Tool](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/html5-vjing-tool) by [spite](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/spite/)

**3rd Place:** [Facial Recognition and Analytics with HTML5’s Video Tag](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/facial-recognition-and-analytics-with-html5s-video) by [dsg](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/dsg/) *(@dsg: Please update your profile with an email address so we can contact you! Thanks.)*

**Runners-up:**

[jQuery VideoBG](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/jquery-videobg)

[Punch the TV!](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/punch-the-tv)

Thanks to everyone that submitted their HTML5 video demos for the July Dev Derby. On to the next race!

Right now, there are only a few days left to submit your [History API demo for August](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby). And coming up next we have **Geolocation for September** and **CSS Media Queries for October**. So start experimenting and hack on.
