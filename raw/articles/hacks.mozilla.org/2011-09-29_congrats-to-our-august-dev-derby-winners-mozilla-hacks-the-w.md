For our August Dev Derby, we decided to see what developers could do with the [History API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Manipulating_the_browser_history).

![Mozilla Demo Studio presents Dev Derby on MDN](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


We had [11 demos submitted](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/tag/challenge%3A2011%3Aaugust/) and it was interesting to see how the History API creates new ways to interact with Web content.

After all the votes were in, we had our August Dev Derby winners!

![augderbywinners August Dev Derby Winners - History API](https://hacks.mozilla.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/augderbywinners-500x197.png)


**1st Place:** [HTML5 Drawings](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/html5-drawings) by [Cory Gackenheimer](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/cgack/)

**2nd Place:** [Fly, fly!](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/fly-fly) by [Michal B.](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/michal.b/)

**3rd Place:** [Media Memory](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/media-memory) by [Ian Lunn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/IanLunn/)

**Runners-up:**

[History Image Gallery](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/history-image-gallery)

[HTML5 Address Bar Video Status](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/html5-address-bar-video-status)

Thanks to everyone that participated in the August Dev Derby… if you want to experiment with Geolocation, **you have one more day** to [join the September Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby).

Otherwise, **October is going to be an amazing month as we explore the possibilities with CSS Media Queries**. Whether you’re on a desktop, tablet or mobile phone… show us what you can do to bring the Web to devices of all sizes.
