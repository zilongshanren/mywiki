What can I say? Wow.

The [No JavaScript Challenge](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2012/july) was absolutely incredible. It was our most successful [Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby) yet, and by far. You shared more than seventy amazing demos of what you can do without touching a single line of [JavaScript](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/JavaScript). More than seventy amazing demos of how powerful declarative markup can be. That’s nearly twice the number of demos submitted to our second most popular Derby.

![Dev Derby logo Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


As you can imagine, it is extremely difficult for us to name only a handful of winners. But after looking through the entries, our three new expert judges — [David Walsh](http://davidwalsh.name/), [Joe Stagner](http://www.misfitgeek.com/), and (filling in for [John Hammink](http://johnhammink.blogspot.com/) this month) [Chris Heilmann](http://christianheilmann.com/) — have decided on their top five picks.

## Winners

[Honey Pursuit: Yet Another Love Saga](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/honey-pursuit-yet-another-love-saga)by newcomer[Vanco Stojkov](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/van100j)[Funny agenda](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/funny-agenda)by newcomer[Fernando García Gómez](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/StripTM)[Pure CSS slides](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/pure-css-slides)by May winner[Ondřej Žára](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/ondras/)

## Runners-up

[Pure CSS3 Homer](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/pure-css3-homer)by newcomer[bernarddeluna](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/bernarddeluna)[Ship Animation](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/ship)by the very active[halls-of-valhalla](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/halls-of-valhalla)

Congratulations to these winners and to all of our contributors for making this our most exciting contest yet. All of these people have done a fantastic job pushing declarative markup forward and deserve lots of praise for doing so. We might even see some of them again next month… here’s hoping!

*Enjoy the No JavaScript challenge? Remember that we have a new contest every month! We are now accepting entries related to Geolocation (September), CSS Media Queries (October), and the Full Screen API (November). Head over to the Dev Derby to get started.*
