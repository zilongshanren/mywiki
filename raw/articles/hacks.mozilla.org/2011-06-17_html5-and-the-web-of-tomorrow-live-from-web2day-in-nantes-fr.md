I am currently here in Nantes, France at the [Web2Day](http://www.web2day-nantes.org/) conference giving a talk on the topic of HTML5 and how it affects the audience (consisting of business people, startups, managers and a few developers. The [slides of the talk are available Slideshare](http://www.slideshare.net/cheilmann/html5-and-the-web-of-tomorrow):

The talk was [streamed live via UStream](https://hacks.mozilla.org/www.ustream.tv/recorded/15436778) and the recording is available (as a – gasp – Flash embed – we know, so no need for comments like “isn’t it ironic that an HTML5 talk…” unless you want to tell UStream that):
