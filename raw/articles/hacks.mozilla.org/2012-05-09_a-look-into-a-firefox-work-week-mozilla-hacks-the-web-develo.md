*This post was originally published as A Compendium of Awesome, and is a short summary of a Firefox work week. Posted here to give an overview, with focus on some details, about things happening with Firefox developement.*

Two weeks ago, the Firefox team got together for a work week in Toronto. It was amazing. Walking through a room with that many excellent people doing excellent things was inspiringhumblingunbelievable and the hits kept on rolling.![Team Firefox 2012](../../assets/b636c45183614f68.jpg)


The combined mobile and graphics teams **cut the beta blocker list for Fennec Native in half**. The desktop team banged together ** a working prototype of sign in to the browser**. The firefox tech leads worked with product and project management to

**nail down the kilimanjaro bug list**for desktop. Madhava gave a great talk about

[. I would have scored it as a strong success based on those outcomes alone.](http://madhava.com/egotism/archive/005060.html)

**the future of Firefox UX**And then *this* happened:

## Hacking

[Scratchpad backending to github gists](https://github.com/campd/scratchpad-gist/)[Jetpacks in scratchpad](https://github.com/Gozala/scratch-kit/)[Task-specific icons](http://msujaws.wordpress.com/2012/05/01/task-specific-icons-for-windows-7-jumplists/)in Windows 7 jumplists- Finalized
[interactions and designs for the new downloads manager](https://etherpad.mozilla.org/panel-based-download-manager) [Sync meet-up and discussion](https://etherpad.mozilla.org/signIntoBrowserServices)to evaluate how to move forward with a smaller core, by moving data handling to components and allowing the Sync team to concentrate on the real Sync functionality- Session Store meet-up and discussion on how to move forward with “Speedy Session Restore” as well as SS2 (rewriting SS).
- Initial
[explorations of a Fennec reading mode](https://wiki.mozilla.org/Fennec/NativeUI/UserExperience/ReaderMode)(post-1.0) - Implemented
[Windows 8 appbar](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=747784) [Roll your own one-off, bespoke debuggers in the scratchpad](https://twitter.com/#%21/jorendorff/status/199888130965381120).[Responsive Design tool for web developers](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oz9tdV6WH2s&list=UUbd7RPnAunIVDjGtpuod0Qw&index=1&feature=plcp)

## Lightning Talks

- Honza made Firebug’s HTTP Monitor
[a standalone component, and it can now remote-monitor fennec, too](http://www.softwareishard.com/blog/planet-mozilla/http-monitor/) - Gavin gave a
[great talk about code review](http://www.gavinsharp.com/blog/2012/05/03/code-review-lightning-talk/) - Tim Abraldes demoed the
[Web App runtime](https://blog.mozilla.org/tabraldes/2012/05/04/mozilla-appswebapprt-lightning-talk-at-firefox-work-week/) - Margaret walked us through
[Hacking Fennec](http://blog.margaretleibovic.com/post/22055279828/fennec-native-ui) - Brian Bondy updated the status of our
[Windows 8](http://www.brianbondy.com/blog/id/137/)work - Felipe
[found and fixed memory leaks with about:cc](http://felipe.wordpress.com/2012/04/30/using-aboutcc/) - Dietrich walked through the
[state of the browser components team](http://people.mozilla.com/%7Edietrich/fxbc) - David Dahl went meta, and talked about
[giving Firefox talks in our community](https://twitter.com/#%21/deezthugs/status/197350580568596480)

I know I’ve missed things. I know some of it is still being written up. In fact, I’m not even the first to write a round up post. Here’s [Finkle](http://starkravingfinkle.org/blog/2012/04/firefox-for-android-fx-mobile-work-week/) doing the same, and [dcamp](http://campd.wordpress.com/2012/05/03/developer-tools-at-the-firefox-work-week/), and [cwiiis](http://chrislord.net/blog/Software/Mozilla/mobile-platform-at-fxto2012.enlighten).

FX-Team is big enough these days that it’s quite an undertaking to get us all together in one place. But man, it’s phenomenal when we do.
