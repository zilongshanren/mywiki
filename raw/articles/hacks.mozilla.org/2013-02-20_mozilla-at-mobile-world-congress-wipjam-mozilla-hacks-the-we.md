It’s getting close to the end of February, and Mozilla is once again gearing up for [Mobile World Congress (MWC)](http://www.mobileworldcongress.com/). Last year, we made a splash talking about HTML5 and Boot to Gecko, and this year we’re back and bigger than ever, with a booth in the AppPlanet hall where we will be doing a lot of demos of [Firefox OS](http://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefoxos/) and [HTML5 apps](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Apps).

## Being at WIPJam

We will also be back at (and helping to sponsor) [WIPJam](http://www.wipconnector.com/events/entry/mobile_world_congress1) , the biggest developer event at MWC. Last year Christian Heilmann spoke about HTML5; this year we will be giving talks and workshops on HTML5 apps, Firefox OS, and showing off developer tools for [Firefox Marketplace](https://marketplace.firefox.com/) and app creation. WIPJam has gotten pretty big, with 3500 people registered to attend the one day event.

## Our WIPJam schedule

Here’s a quick overview of what we will be doing & presenting at WIPJam on Thursday, 28 February:

- An expert table where we will be answering questions and giving demos of Firefox OS, Marketplace developer tools and more.
- Sponsoring a Hackathon category for apps using WebAPis or WebRTC.
- Christian Heilmann will be back at WIPJam, giving an UnPanel on “HTML5 beyond the hype” and leading several HTML Discussion Group sessions (he’s going to be busy!).
- Mozilla CTO Brendan Eich & Robert Nyman will be giving a 50-minute workshop: Why Open Web Apps & Firefox OS are good for mobile developers.
- There also will be 4 DemoCamp presentations by developers we met at the FirefoxOS App Days in January. We’re excited that Untappd, Jaxogram, Reditr, & WordPress Photos will be at WIPJam showing off what HTML5 really can do.

We’ll be posting [updates on Twitter](https://twitter.com/mozhacks/) during the show, and a recap of the event here once we all get some sleep afterwards. Also stay tuned for some exciting announcements, starting with a press conference on Sunday and continuing throughout the event.
