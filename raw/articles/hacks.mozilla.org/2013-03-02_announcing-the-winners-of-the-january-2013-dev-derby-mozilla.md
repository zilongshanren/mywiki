This past January, creative web developers from around the world showed us what they could do with drag and drop interaction in the [January Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/january) contest. After looking through the entries, our [three new expert judges](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby#tab-judging)–Craig Cook, Franck Lecollinet, and Guillaume Lecollinet–decided on four winners and two runners-up.

![Dev Derby](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


Not a contestant? There are other reasons to be excited. Most importantly, all of these demos are completely open-source, making them wonderful lessons in the exciting things you can do with drag and drop today.

## The Results

### Winners

- First place:
[Pete’s Adventure](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/petes-adventure)by sophomore[Seba](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/meinstuhlknarrt) - Second place: Tie between
[okifu](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/okifu)by newcomer[Jesús Pérez](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/tx2z)and[Block Puzzle](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/block-puzzle)by game lover[ZofiaKorcz](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/ZofiaKorcz) - Third place:
[Beyer Music Player](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/beyer-music-player)by the always creative[Alvaro Mouriño](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/tuxie)

### Runners-up

[Dragmark](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/dragmark)by the inspirational[JoanC](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/JoanC)[Classical Music Jukebox](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/detail/classical-music-jukebox)by newcomer[jvitela](https://developer.mozilla.org/profiles/jvitela)

These contestants were not satisfied just bringing this age-old interaction to the Web. They also made it fun, helpful, musical, and even a little amusing. Congratulations to them and to [all of the amazing contestants](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby/2013/january) who pushed the limit of drag and drop interaction.

*Want to get a head start on an upcoming Derby? We are now accepting demos related to mobile web development in general (March), Web Workers (April), and by popular demand, getUserMedia (May). Head over to the Dev Derby to get started.*
