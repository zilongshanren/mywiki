Firefox 4 is [here](http://blog.mozilla.com/blog/2011/03/22/mozilla-launches-firefox-4-and-delivers-a-fast-sleek-and-customizable-browsing-experience-to-more-than-400-million-users-worldwide-2/)! Yeah!

And to celebrate the launch, we have released another round of demos on ![webgl logo webgl logo](../../assets/3c12dd4bfd008f66.gif)


[Web O’ Wonder](http://webofwonder.org/), with 3 awesome WebGL demos! (

*This new round also introduces mobile-specific demos, see this*).

[dedicated blog post](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/03/webowonder-mobile/)**WebGL: It’s 3D and Web Content together.**

*Demo by Cédric Pinson and Guillaume Lecollinet.*

[GlobeTweeter](http://webofwonder.org/#globetweeter) is a perfect example of how you can mix 3D and Web Content. In this page, you can see real-time geo-located twitter activity represented on the planet earth.

**WebGL animations**

*Demo by The Audio Team.*

[No-Comply](http://webofwonder.org/#nocomply) is a WebGL animation. With [JägerMonkey](https://hacks.mozilla.org/category/jagermonkey/) (Firefox’s new JavaScript engine) and the experimental animation scheduler ([mozRequestAnimationFrame](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2010/08/more-efficient-javascript-animations-with-mozrequestanimationframe/)), we can now create complex WebGL animations.

This demo has been developed by the [audio team](http://audioscene.org) who has also created the [Flight Of The Navigator](http://webofwonder.org/#fotn) demo, where you can find Videos and live Flickr and Twitter content in a 3D city, all build with WebGL:
