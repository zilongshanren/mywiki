Thursday again, and it’s time for our reading tips!


## Weekly links October 13th 2011

If there is anything you think we should read or know about, don’t hesitate to post a comment, contact us on Twitter or through any other mean.

The picks this week are:

### Christian Heilmann

![A picture of Christian Heilmann](../../assets/73c300a3a9b62fcb.jpg)

[jqwerty](http://keithcirkel.co.uk/jwerty/) is a very small library to make it easy to add keyboard controls to your JS solutions. Compatible with jQuery.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Christian, he’s available on Twitter as [@codepo8](http://twitter.com/codepo8).

### Eric “Sheppy” Shepherd

![A picture of Eric](../../assets/fe6c559a2b55cf87.jpg)

[WHATWG](http://twitter.com/whatwg) on Twitter; every change gets noted on their Twitter stream.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Eric, he’s available on Twitter as [@sheppy](http://twitter.com/sheppy).

### Havi Hoffman

![A picture of Havi Hoffman](../../assets/b2d68053fc6219be.jpg)

[The Rands Test](http://www.randsinrepose.com/archives/2011/10/11/the_rands_test.html) – not just for managers, and not just for engineering teams experiencing explosive growth, this is an opinionated set of hacks for team collaboration and communication.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Havi, she’s available on Twitter as [@freshelectrons](http://twitter.com/freshelectrons)

### Jay Patel

![A picture of Jay Patel](../../assets/402b2a9c4e683601.jpg)

[Adobe’s acquisition of PhoneGap](http://www.pcworld.com/article/241557/designers_respond_to_adobes_acquisition_of_nitobi_typekit.html) and TypeKit. Should be interesting to watch what this means for developers and designers looking to adopt HTML5 for their projects.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Jay, he’s available on Twitter as [@jaybhai](http://twitter.com/jaybhai)

### Jeff Griffiths

![A picture of Jeff Griffiths](../../assets/6eccd82dcc467937.png)

[an interesting method](http://lduros.net/posts/blocking-images-and-other-content-mozila-add-sdk-and-nsicontentpolicy/) for implementing content blocking functionality using Jetpack.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Jeff, he’s available on Twitter as [@canuckistani](http://twitter.com/canuckistani).

### Rob Hawkes

![A picture of Rob Hawkes](../../assets/bf1d2a9fda8c5762.jpg)

[rendered a Team Fortress 2 level using WebGL](http://blog.tojicode.com/2011/10/source-engine-levels-in-webgl-video.html). The performance is absolutely amazing and an indication of the type of games that could be supported by this technology.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Rob, he’s available on Twitter as [@robhawkes](http://twitter.com/robhawkes).

### Robert Nyman

![A picture of Robert Nyman](../../assets/520e1af8dc948590.jpg)

[Using CORS with All (Modern) Browsers](http://www.kendoui.com/blogs/teamblog/posts/11-10-04/using_cors_with_all_modern_browsers.aspx) – Great overview how to do Cross-Origin Resource Sharing across web browsers.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Robert, he’s available on Twitter as [@robertnyman](http://twitter.com/robertnyman).

### Tristan Nitot

![A picture of Tristan Nitot](../../assets/cd40e276180b5107.jpg)

[visit the State of Mozilla report](http://www.mozilla.org/en-US/foundation/annualreport/2010/).

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Tristan, he’s available on Twitter as [@nitot](http://twitter.com/nitot).
