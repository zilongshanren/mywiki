With more people going mobile and taking the Web with them, we thought [Geolocation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Using_geolocation) was a great topic for the [September Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2011/september/).

![Mozilla Demo Studio presents Dev Derby on MDN](../../assets/8edf1f06addeed39.png)


Web developers explored a number of ways to bring your physical location into the Web experience and we had [16 demos submitted](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2011/september/) for the Dev Derby.

Voting was tough this month, but once the votes were counted, we had our winners circle:

![Winners of the September Dev Derby - Geolocation Winners of the September Dev Derby - Geolocation](https://hacks.mozilla.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/septderbywinners-500x194.png)


**1st Place:** [Urban arteries](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/urban-arteries) by [Jaume Sánchez aka spite](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/spite/)

**2nd Place:** [I Need A…](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/ineeda) by [David aka d-b-f](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/d-b-f/)

**3rd Place:** [Find Street Art](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/findstreetart) by [Aaron Has](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/aaronhans/)

Thanks to everyone that participated in the September Dev Derby and congratulations to the winners! A special shout out to Jaume for taking 1st place after submitting amazing demos for the past three Dev Derbies. Check out [spite’s MDN profile](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/profiles/spite/) to see what he’s done in the past.

** NOTE: We recently updated our ** That means if you’ve submitted awesome demos and come up short in the past, you still have a chance to win that top spot in future Dev Derbies… so keep those demos coming!

[Dev Derby rules](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/rules)to allow developers to participate in multiple contests until they win 1st place.

If you’re working on CSS Media Queries, **you have a few more days** to [join the October Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby).

Or get ready for the joy of painting in the November Dev Derby with Canvas. We’re looking forward to seeing some creative demos next month. So hack on!
