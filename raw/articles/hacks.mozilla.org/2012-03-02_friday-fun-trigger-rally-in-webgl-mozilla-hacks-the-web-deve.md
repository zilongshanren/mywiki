As reported by [Creative JS](http://creativejs.com/2012/03/trigger-rally-online-edition/), [Jasmine Kent](http://twitter.com/jareiko) ported her open source Linux racing game [Trigger Rally](http://trigger-rally.sourceforge.net/) to WebGL to run in HTML5 browsers. In the [following video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M31heMqQ0Ug) you can see it running pretty smoothly on my MacBook air that is already running 12% of CPU without it starting (as ScreenFlow is recording and VLC was playing music whilst TweetDeck used quite a chunk, too):

If you are the artistic type, you can even [design your own levels for the game](http://qubodup.wordpress.com/2012/01/20/making-trigger-rally-maps-racing-tracks/).

You can play play Trigger Rally online here: [http://triggerrally.com/](http://triggerrally.com/) or follow it on Twitter [@triggerrally](http://twitter.com/triggerrally).
