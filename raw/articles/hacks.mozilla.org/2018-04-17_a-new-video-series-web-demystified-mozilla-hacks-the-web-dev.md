We don’t have to tell you that video is a key channel for sharing information and instructional skills especially for students and developers who’ve grown up with YouTube. At Mozilla, we’ve always been a leader in supporting the open technologies that bring unencumbered video [into the browser and onto the web](https://aomedia.org/av1-features/get-started/).

But on top of the technology, there’s content. In 2018, Mozilla’s Developer Outreach team has launched some projects to share more knowledge in video. Earlier this year, Jen Simmons set a high bar with the launch of [Layout Land](https://www.youtube.com/layoutland), a series about “what’s now possible in graphic design on the web — layout, CSS Grid, and more.”

This post introduces [Web Demystified](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLo3w8EB99pqLEopnunz-dOOBJ8t-Wgt2g), a new series targeting web makers. By web makers, I have in mind everyone who builds things for the web: designers, developers, project and team managers, students, hobbyists, and experts. Today we’ve released the opening two episodes on the [Mozilla Hacks YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/mozhacks), introducing web basics.

Our goal is to provide basic information for beginner web makers, at the start of their web journey. The subject matter will also serve as a refresher on web fundamentals.

The next four episodes cover some basic technologies at the heart of the web (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG). We will release a new show every couple weeks for your viewing pleasure. And then we will continue our journey into details, covering stuff like: how the browser works, image formats for the web, domain names, WebAssembly, and more…

## An invitation to participate

In true Mozilla fashion, we’d welcome your help sharing this new content and helping us promote it.

- If you enjoy those videos, please like them on YouTube, and share them with your friends, colleagues, family, and networks.
- If you have constructive feedback on the series, please share it here in comments. (Reminder: these shows are aimed at beginners and we aim to keep them brief.)
- In general, if there are topics you wish to see covered, tell us and if you have questions about the content itself: Ask!
- Last but not least, if you’re not a native English speaker, please feel free to translate the video captions into your own language. Many people will thank you for that.

Enjoy [Web Demystified](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLo3w8EB99pqLEopnunz-dOOBJ8t-Wgt2g)! And see you in a fortnight.
