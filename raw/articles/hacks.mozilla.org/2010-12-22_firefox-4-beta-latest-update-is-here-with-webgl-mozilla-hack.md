**The new Firefox 4 Beta is here, and comes with WebGL activated by default.** You can download this new beta here: [http://firefox.com/beta](http://firefox.com/beta).

*Flight of the Navigator* is a WebGL + Audio API demo developed by a team of Mozilla volunteers.

You can see the [demo online here](http://videos.mozilla.org/serv/mozhacks/flight-of-the-navigator/) (you need a WebGL compatible browser).

More information about this demo on [David Humphrey’s blog](http://vocamus.net/dave/?p=1233) (one of the developer of this demo) and more [about WebGL in Firefox Beta8 on Vlad’s blog](http://blog.vlad1.com/2010/12/21/webgl-in-firefox-4-beta-8/).
