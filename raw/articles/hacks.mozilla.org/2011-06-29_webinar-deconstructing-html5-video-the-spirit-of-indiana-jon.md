Update 2011-07-20:The video recording of this webinar is now available:



On Thursday, July 14th, at 16:00 UTC, Chris Heilmann will give second in the (so far sporadic) [Mozilla Developer Engagement webinar series](http://hacks.mozilla.org/category/webinars/), discussing syncing HTML5 video with Google maps, using his “Spirit of Indiana Jones” demo as an example. Chris has already [blogged about this demo](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2010/12/spirit-of-indiana-jones-syncing-html5-video-with-maps/), so this webinar is a chance for you to take part in a conversation, rather than just receive a broadcast.

Take a look at the [demo code](https://github.com/codepo8/HTML5---map-and-video-syncing) and the [blog post](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2010/12/spirit-of-indiana-jones-syncing-html5-video-with-maps/), and then bring your questions to the webinar session. If you have a Google account, you can post questions (or respond to others’) ahead of time in this [Google Moderator series](https://www.google.com/moderator/#16/e=9ae94); if not, ask your questions in IRC during the session.

The webinar will be broadcast on [Air Mozilla](http://air.mozilla.org/), with text chat on #airmozilla on irc.mozilla.org. (There’s an IRC widget on the Air Mozilla page if you need it.)

Add this webinar to your calendar:

![](http://www.google.com/calendar/images/ext/gc_button1_en.gif)


Oh, by the way, HTML5 video is the theme of the [Mozilla Dev Derby for July](https://developer.mozilla.org/demos/devderby). Whether you’re new to working with HTML5 video or an “old pro” (if there is such a thing), [submit a demo during July](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/submit?tags=challenge%3A2011%3Ajuly) to show off your stuff and win cool prizes.
