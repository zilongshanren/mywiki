Wait a minute, there are *how* many days in February?

Looks like leap day got the best of us. Earlier today, we ended the February [Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby) prematurely, making it impossible for some of you to submit those last-minute demos for a couple of hours.

To make up for this oversight, we will accept February Dev Derby submissions for one extra day. Please [submit](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/submit?tags=challenge%3A2012%3Afebruary) your entry before March 1 at 11:59:59 p.m. PT for them to be counted.

Want to get a head start on an upcoming Derby? We are now accepting entries related to [CSS 3D Transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/Using_CSS_transforms) (March) and [HTML5 audio](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Using_HTML5_audio_and_video) (April). [Submit today](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby)!
