HTML5 `canvas`

offers a lot of interesting ways to create effects and experiences on the web. One of the methods to do that, which seem lesser known, is `canvas clip`

.


I wrote about it in [Using the HTML5 canvas clip method to hide certain content](http://robertnyman.com/2011/07/26/using-the-html5-canvas-clip-method-to-hide-certain-content/), showing how to stage content and create a looking glass effect. You could also see it in action in the [canvas clip demo](http://robertnyman.com/playground/canvas/clip/).

You could also read up more on it in the [Clipping paths in the Compositing article on MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Canvas_tutorial/Compositing#Clipping_paths) and learn how to create some interesting combinations.
