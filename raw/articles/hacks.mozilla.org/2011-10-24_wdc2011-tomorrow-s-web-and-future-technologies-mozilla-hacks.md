Last Friday I had the pleasure of attending and speaking at the [Web Developer Conference in Bristol](http://webdevconf.com/). This was the fifth conference in the event’s history and was attended by well over 200 Web designers and developers from across the UK.

[In my talk](http://www.slideshare.net/robhawkes/tomorrows-web-and-future-technologies-wdc2011) I covered some Web technologies that are on the horizon and coming your way in the near future. Some topics of particular interest are the [WebAPIs](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/08/introducing-webapi/) that we’re working on, with the [WebVibrator API](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=679966) easily being the most popular amongst the attendees (no idea why).

But in all seriousness, it was a great event and I’m glad to see some of the attendees exploring these new technologies as a result of the talk.
