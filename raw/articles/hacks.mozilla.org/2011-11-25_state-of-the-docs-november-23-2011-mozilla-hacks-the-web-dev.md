This is the second in a series of posts about new or recently improved content or infrastructure on MDN. This post was slightly delayed by the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday (apologies).

## Web standards docs

### New pages!

As the result of a discussion on the [mozilla.dev.platform](http://groups.google.com/group/mozilla.dev.platform/topics) forum, **Boris Zbarsky** wrote an article on [Writing forward-compatible websites](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Web_development/Writing_forward-compatible_websites), with contributions by **Henri Sivonen** and others. Discussion of and improvements to this article are ongoing.

**Jeremie Patonnier** created pages for several SVG DOM interfaces:

**Masayuki** created pages for DOM [CompositionEvent](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/CompositionEvent) and various specific composition events.

**David Bruant** created a page for [event.stopImmediatePropagation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/event.stopImmediatePropagation) and wrote an article on [Historical artifacts to avoid](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Web_development/Historical_artifacts_to_avoid).

### Help wanted!

It would be helpful to have an article about the Web standardization process, what the different spec draft levels are, and how web developers can decide when a new technology is stable enough to use and deploy. It could go under [Web standards](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Web_standards), which currently has some dated articles from the early days of Firefox.

### Other doc updates

**David Bruant** expanded and added examples to DOM [NodeList](https://developer.mozilla.org/En/DOM/NodeList) and the [Javascript var statement](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/JavaScript/Reference/Statements/var).

Our friends at Google were busy, holding their own mini-docsprint:

**Eric Bidelman**created an article on the[Mouse Lock API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/API/Mouse_Lock_API), and improved[window.requestAnimationFrame](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/window.requestAnimationFrame),[window.navigator](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/window.navigator), and[HTMLCanvasElement](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/HTMLCanvasElement).**Ernest Delgado**added to[Using full-screen mode](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/HTMLCanvasElement)and created[document.mozFullScreen](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/document.mozFullScreen).**Sam Dutton**created a live example for[CSS height](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/height).**Meggin Kearney**edited[CSS pointer-events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/pointer-events)and restructured[CSS transitions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/CSS/CSS_transitions).**Kevin Lim**wrote a second draft of the Mouse Lock API, improved the[HTML <track> element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/HTML/Element/track), and started a section on[Optimizing canvas](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Canvas_tutorial/Optimizing_canvas)in the canvas tutorial.**Kathy Walrath**updated[DOM Blob](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Blob).

## Mozilla project docs

The [Mozilla coding style guidelines](https://developer.mozilla.org/En/Mozilla_Coding_Style_Guide) were updated by **Jeff Walden**, **Benjamin Smedberg**, and others.

## MND infrastructure

Version 1.6 of MDN was launched. **Luke Crouch** blogged about the [changes in 1.6](http://groovecoder.com/2011/11/22/mdn-1-6/).

**Eric Shepherd** created new templates to guide readers who visit non-existant pages by following auto-generated links. This should alleviate the problem of non-logged-in readers being bounced to the front page in such cases. If you’re curious about the details, [see his blog](http://www.bitstampede.com/2011/11/22/helping-people-when-content-isnt-there/).
