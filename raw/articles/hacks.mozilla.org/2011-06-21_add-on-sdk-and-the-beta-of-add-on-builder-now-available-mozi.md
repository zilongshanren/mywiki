![Add-on Builder Beta and Add-on SDK are here!](../../assets/84456193cd953133.png)


Firefox offers users complete control over the look and functionality of their Web browser with a gallery of hundreds of thousands of add-ons. With the launch of [Add-on SDK and Add-on Builder Beta](https://addons.mozilla.org/developers/builder), web developers need only knowledge of HTML, JavaScript and CSS to create great add-ons for Firefox that are restartless by default.

The [Add-on SDK](https://addons.mozilla.org/developers/builder) enables local development of add-ons through a command line interface, while the [Firefox Add-on Builder Beta](https://builder.addons.mozilla.org) provides a hosted online build environment.

To find out more, head over to the [Add-ons blog](http://blog.mozilla.com/addons/2011/06/21/add-on_sdk-builder-_beta/) or the new Add-on SDK & Add-on Builder [page](https://addons.mozilla.org/developers/builder).
