If you’re a regular reader of Hacks, you probably know about [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/en-US/), the ground-breaking, community-driven systems programming language sponsored by Mozilla. I covered Rust on Hacks back in July, to note [a major milestone](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2016/07/shipping-rust-in-firefox/) for Mozilla, when we shipped Rust in Firefox for the first time.

Meanwhile in Rust-land, the [trains](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2016/07/07/Rust-1.10.html) [keep](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2016/08/18/Rust-1.11.html) [on](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2016/09/29/Rust-1.12.html) [rolling](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2016/11/10/Rust-1.13.html), more users are [putting Rust in production](https://www.rust-lang.org/friends.html), and the [ecosystem](https://crates.io/) and [worldwide community](https://www.meetup.com/topics/rust/) continue growing. And our investment at Mozilla is growing too! There’s been a lot of news and excitement lately about [Project Quantum](https://medium.com/mozilla-tech/a-quantum-leap-for-the-web-a3b7174b3c12), which leverages the innovations of the [Servo](https://servo.org/) web engine, another vibrant open source community sponsored by Mozilla.

So we’ve created a video series to tell the world about Rust, to celebrate the incredible community, and to demonstrate our investment in Rust and its long-term success. Today, we are publishing *Rust and the Future of Systems Programming*, the first in our six-part series. Visit our [Mozilla Tech Medium](https://medium.com/mozilla-tech) publication over the next week to view each video as it is released or head over to [Mozhacks YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/user/mozhacks) to subscribe to the complete series:
