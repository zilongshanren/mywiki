We kicked off our [series of monthly developer challenges](http://hacks.mozilla.org/2011/06/dev-derby-a-monthly-competition-of-demos-using-open-technologies/) in June to see what Web developers could do with CSS3 Animations. Our [first ever Dev Derby](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby/2011/june/) was a huge success with almost 30 entries and a variety of demos that brought action to the Web without JavaScript. The results have been awesome!

Early fan favorites included [Rofox CSS3](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/rofox-css3-animation-by-anthony-calzadilla) and [CSS Nyan Cat](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/css-nyan-cat), which remain among the most viewed on [Demo Studio](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/).

While all the demos were amazing, we had a tough task in narrowing down the submissions to 5 finalists based on our judging criteria. After careful review and feedback, the following demos surfaced as candidates for our final round of voting:

[Animated menu icons](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/animated-menu-icons)

[Auto-run photo album](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/auto-run-photo-album)

[BATTLEFIELD CSS3](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/battlefield-css3)

[CSS Tricks](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/css-tricks)

[Plan B – Retro Demo](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/plan-b-retro-demo)

With those 5 demos left in the running, we brought in our expert judges and a few members of the Mozilla team to vote on the finalists. Judges rated each demo on a scale of 1 – 5 across the following dimensions:

**Technology**– Does the demo showcase the power of open Web technologies?**Originality**– How innovative and unique is the demo?**Aesthetics**– How good is the visual design and interaction experience?**Practicality**– How useful is this demo in enhancing the Web today?

It was a close race and we’re excited to announce that the winners of the June Dev Derby are:

**1st Place:** [CSS Tricks](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/css-tricks) by Mircea Piturca.

**2nd Place:** [Animated menu icons](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/animated-menu-icons) by Koen Hendrix.

**3rd Place:** [Plan B – Retro Demo](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/plan-b-retro-demo) by matt64.

**Runners-up:**

[Auto-run photo album](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/auto-run-photo-album)

[BATTLEFIELD CSS3](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/detail/battlefield-css3)

Thanks to everyone that submitted their awesome demos for the June Dev Derby. Up next we have [HTML5 video](https://developer.mozilla.org/En/Using_audio_and_video_in_Firefox) for July, [History API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/DOM/Manipulating_the_browser_history) for August and [Geolocation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en/Using_geolocation) for September. We look forward to seeing even more great submissions for [July and beyond](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/demos/devderby)! Hack on.
