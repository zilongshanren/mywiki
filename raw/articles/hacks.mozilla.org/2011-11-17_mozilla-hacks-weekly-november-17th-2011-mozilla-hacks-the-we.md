The week is closing to an end, and since you are perhaps winding down a little, why not check some reading tips from the Developer Engagement Team at Mozilla?


## Weekly links November 17th 2011

If there is anything you think we should read or know about, don’t hesitate to post a comment, contact us on Twitter or through any other mean.

The picks this week are:

### Christian Heilmann

![A picture of Christian Heilmann](../../assets/73c300a3a9b62fcb.jpg)

[Lynx would not be impressed](http://christianheilmann.com/2011/11/16/lynx-would-not-be-impressed-on-semantics-and-html/)I collected a few of the arguments for and against semantics in HTML5 and thought it is time to concentrate on bigger things as there is no “one size fits all: solution any longer.

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Christian, he’s available on Twitter as [@codepo8](http://twitter.com/codepo8).

### Eric “Sheppy” Shepherd

![A picture of Eric 'Sheppy' Shepherd](../../assets/fe6c559a2b55cf87.jpg)

[InstaCSS](http://instacss.com/)web site is a great, easy to use interface to our excellent CSS documentation. Quick, immediate feedback based on regular expression searches makes finding just the right CSS properties and rules super-easy!

If you want to read more tips or discuss the web with Eric, he’s available on Twitter as [@sheppy](http://twitter.com/sheppy).

### Havi Hoffman

![A picture of Havi Hoffman](../../assets/b2d68053fc6219be.jpg)

[Fight SOPA](https://donate.mozilla.org/page/s/SOPA?source=tw_share). Proposed legislation in the U.S. Congress poses a dangerous threat to social media and freedom of expression online, and has the potential to undermine core Internet infrastructure. Learn more about this disturbing development and how you can help.

Havi can be found on Twitter as [@freshelectrons](http://twitter.com/freshelectrons)
