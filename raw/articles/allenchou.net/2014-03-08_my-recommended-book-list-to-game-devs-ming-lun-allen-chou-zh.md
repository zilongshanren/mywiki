Here’s a list of books I have read that I recommend to to game devs.

I’ve divided the books into several categories: computer science, math & graphics, physics, and procedural animation.

I intentionally did not include any “gem” or “pearl” books, since they are collections of multiple stand-alone articles, and not all of them are worth reading.


### Computer Science

[Code](http://www.amazon.com/Code-Language-Computer-Hardware-Software/dp/0735611319/) by Charles Petzold

Write Great Code by Randall Hyde ([Volume 1](http://www.amazon.com/Write-Great-Code-Understanding-Machine-ebook/dp/B0096FEJGQ/), [Volume 2](http://www.amazon.com/Write-Great-Code-Low-Level-High-Level-ebook/dp/B008Z6ASGC/))

[Head First Design Patterns](http://www.amazon.com/First-Design-Patterns-Elisabeth-Freeman/dp/0596007124/) by Elisabeth Freeman, Eric Freeman, Bert Bates, Kathy Sierra, and Elisabeth Robson

[ActionScript 3.0 Design Patterns](http://www.amazon.com/ActionScript-3-0-Design-Patterns-Programming/dp/0596528469/) by William Sanders & Chandima Cumaranatunge

[Design Patterns](http://www.amazon.com/Design-Patterns-Elements-Reusable-Object-Oriented/dp/0201633612/) by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides

[C++ Coding Standards](http://www.amazon.com/Coding-Standards-Rules-Guidelines-Practices/dp/0321113586/) by Herb Sutter & Andrei Alexandrescu

[Effective C++](http://www.amazon.com/Effective-Specific-Improve-Programs-Designs/dp/0321334876/) by Scott Meyers

[More Effective C++](http://www.amazon.com/More-Effective-Improve-Programs-Designs/dp/020163371X/) by Scott Meyers

[Effective STL](http://www.amazon.com/Effective-STL-Specific-Standard-Template/dp/0201749629/) by Scott Meyers

### Math & Graphics

[3D Math Primer for Graphics And Game Development](http://www.amazon.com/Primer-Graphics-Development-Wordware-Library/dp/1556229119/) by Fletcher Dunn & Ian Parberry

[Essential Math for Games and Interactive Applications](http://www.amazon.com/Essential-Mathematics-Games-Interactive-Applications/dp/0123742978/) by James Van Verth & Lars Bishop

[Real-Time Rendering](http://www.amazon.com/Real-Time-Rendering-Third-Tomas-Akenine-Moller/dp/1568814240/) by Tomas Akenine-Moller, Eric Haines, and Naty Hoffman

### Physics

[Real-Time Collision Detection](http://www.amazon.com/Real-Time-Collision-Detection-Interactive-Technology/dp/1558607323/) by Christer Ericson

[Collision Detection in Interactive 3D Environment](https://www.amazon.com/Collision-Detection-Interactive-Environments-Technology/dp/155860801X) by Gino van den Bergen

[Game Physics Engine Development](http://www.amazon.com/Game-Physics-Engine-Development-Commercial-Grade/dp/0123819768/) by Ian Millington

### Procedural Animation

[ActionScript 3.0: Making Things Move!](http://www.amazon.com/Foundation-Actionscript-3-0-Animation-Making/dp/1590597915/) by Keith Peters

[AdvancED ActionScript Animation](http://www.amazon.com/AdvancED-ActionScript-Animation-Keith-Peters/dp/1430216085/) by Keith Peters
