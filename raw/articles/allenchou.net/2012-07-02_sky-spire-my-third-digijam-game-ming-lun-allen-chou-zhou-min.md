Controls: LEFT and RIGHT keys to move. SPACE key to jump.

Sky Spire is a game my classmates and I made during a 48-hour game jam session held by [DigiPen](https://www.digipen.edu/) students. The theme was “psychedelic stick of magic”, generated by [The Video Game Name Generator](http://videogamena.me/).

This time I didn’t work on the game contents; instead, I provided technical support for [Rusher](http://code.google.com/p/rusher/) (my ActionScript 3.0 game engine), fixing bugs and tweaking functionality to suit our needs.
