Instruction: Press space to jump. Collect food and avoid spikes.

Cooncoaster is a game I made with my classmates during a 48-hour game jam session held by [DigiPen](https://www.digipen.edu/) students. The theme was “change of plan”.

We used [Rusher](http://code.google.com/p/rusher/) (my ActionScript 3.0 game engine), and [Stardust](http://code.google.com/p/stardust-particle-engine/) (my ActionScript 3.0 particle engine).
