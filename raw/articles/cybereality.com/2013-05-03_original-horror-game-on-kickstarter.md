Seems like Kickstarter is just churning out hits lately, and this project could be the next success. It’s an original horror game where you play as a two year old boy. The early prototype videos look compelling, and I like that they are attempting a different theme with the title. Should be awesome, but they still have a ways before meeting their goal. So, if possible, [please pledge your support for this game on Kickstarter](http://www.kickstarter.com/projects/krillbite/among-the-sleep/). Also, rumor has it that the developers are interested in supporting the Oculus Rift. That would just be awesome if that happened. So, yeah, please back this project. Thanks.
