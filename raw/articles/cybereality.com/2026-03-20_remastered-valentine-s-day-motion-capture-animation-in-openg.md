Ended up doing a remaster of the 30 second motion capture animation I published on Valentine’s Day. Perhaps a few frames that could be smoother, but this feels like basically production quality to me. Got about 200 videos clips from the studio recording, and this one loop took about a whole day to process, so it may take some time. Hopefully it will get quicker as I get more comfortable with the pipeline.

![](../../assets/649416a6fe9da5cf.jpg)

Also features the recent fixes on the hair rendering. Was “work in progress” for a long time, but feels like it got to a good place. Some minor aliasing-issues, but not a showstopper.
