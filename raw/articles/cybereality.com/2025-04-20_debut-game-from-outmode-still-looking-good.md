Made a new concept visual for my untitled debut game from [Outmode](https://outmode.com/) on my OpenGL/WebGL engine, Degine. Generated using [Midjourney 7](https://www.midjourney.com/home), this is fairly close to my vision, and also matches the art for the game that was already completed. Planning on using this as a test to see what is necessary to complete a minimum viable product for Degine, and also launch on various platforms as a playtest before releasing the engine publicly.

The game itself is designed as a simple one screen shooter, like [the old SEGA game Space Harrier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MEWGnj12GO4). The character model is final (was contracted last year) but doesn’t have animation yet. For the background I’ll likely just do a procedural sky and looping texture on the ground. The [hex tiling](https://github.com/mmikk/hextile-demo) I recently added to the engine should keep the ground high quality, and I’m doing research now into sky rendering. I read [a paper on multiple scattering atmospheric rendering](https://sebh.github.io/publications/egsr2020.pdf), just need time to understand the math. Hoping this could be a short 3 month kinda project, I’ll have to keep the scope manageable.
