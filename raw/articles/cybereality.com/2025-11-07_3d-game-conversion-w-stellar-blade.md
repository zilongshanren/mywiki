People in the community had been asking about [Stellar Blade](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3489700/Stellar_Blade/), so decided to do a 3D video of the game with my ReShade 2D-to-3D effect [Rendepth](https://github.com/outmode/rendepth-reshade). While Stellar Blade actually does have an [official stereoscopic patch](https://steamcommunity.com/games/3489700/announcements/detail/535485157226840244) from the developer, I was not able to record for YouTube when also playing in 3D on the [Samsung Odyssey 3D](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/1892516-REG/samsung_ls27fg900xnxza_27_odyssey_3d_g90xf.html) monitor. So I ended up recording with Rendepth doing the conversion so it would work in the capture. Obviously this will not match a native stereo render, particularly one in an official patch, but I think it still works to demonstrate how good this game is for 3D.

![](../../assets/32b555670e3e5f5d.gif)

For some reason, though, YouTube seems to have butchered [SBS 3D video uploads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PX1_tGKR9cA), likely not on purpose, but just cause the compression algorithms weren’t designed for 3D. The previous [Metal Eden video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_my_bJKaF8A) looks absolutely full 4K quality (as it appears when playing) but I guess Stellar Blade is detailed, with lots of volumetric fog and other post-processing effects, which ruin the quality (it barely looks 1080p). I’ve uploaded an [uncompressed PNG capture](https://cybereality.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/SD_RDR_sbs.png) from that session so you can get a better idea of the quality when playing, and understand that it looks much nicer in real life than it appears on the stream.
