Heya, I’m Linden Reid, a **game developer **at **Uncommon Games**, previously an **engineer** at Blizzard and EA.

[Disclaimer](https://lindenreid.wordpress.com/disclaimer/): my work on this blog is NOT representative of nor sponsored by Uncommon, Blizzard, or any other studio I’ve worked for.

On this blog, I write **tutorials** on creating **shaders** and **procedural geometry** in Unity. I also stream gaming and game development on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/linden_reid).

I love teaching and helping others, including other game industry minorities. If there’s something I can do to help you, feel free to reach out to me. ❤
