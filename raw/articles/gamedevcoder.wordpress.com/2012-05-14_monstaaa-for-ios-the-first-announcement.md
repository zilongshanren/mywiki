Just a quick (and big) announcement today!

The game I’ve been working on for the last 6 months is finally going to be released on iOS. It is called [Monstaaa!](http://www.pixelelephant.com/monstaaa) and should be available on the 29th of May 2012 as a universal app on the Apple Store.

*Monstaaa!* is a truly unique physics based puzzle game with tilt controls and some great painterly graphics done by a friend of mine, [Drew Morrow](http://members.iinet.net.au/~drew1/) (ex artist at Blue Tongue / THQ) and audio done by guys at [Kpow Audio](http://www.kpow.com.au/) (ex audio guys at Team Bondi / Rockstar Games). If you enjoy puzzle and/or physics based games there’s high chance you simply fall in love with it! But more seriously, we put a lot of heart into making *Monstaaa!* and we hope people will notice. Let’s see!

Ps. On the more technical side of things, the game was developed using [Marmalade SDK](http://www.madewithmarmalade.com) and [Box 2D](http://box2d.org/) physics engine but I’ll talk about that in more detail in my next blog posts.

Looking forward to this one!
