Hi there, I’m Maciej Sawitus, an indie game developer at [Pixel Elephant.](http://www.pixelelephant.com)

Since I was born I knew I was going to work on computer games… and here you go. I do, I love it and I can’t imagine doing anything else for living. Working on games, among few other things, makes my life a lot more happy.

My very favourite games are [Braid](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braid_(video_game)), [Portal](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal_(video_game)), [World of Goo](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Goo), [And Yet It Moves](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And_Yet_It_Moves) and… [Quake](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quake_(video_game)). Innovation, fun gameplay and mood are what counts for me in the first place.

You can find some of my projects [here](http://pixelelephant.com/projects/pages/3d_game_dev.html).
