Founder of Ludicon. Graphics engineer with 25+ years in real-time graphics experience.

I’m a graphics programmer with a background in GPU architecture and real-time rendering.

I’ve been developing games since the days of flipcode and the gd-algorithms mailing list,
and I I've worked across a wide range of companies, including
[Crytek](https://www.crytek.com/), [Relic](https://www.relic.com/),
[Oddworld](http://www.oddworld.com/),
[NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/), [Thekla](http://thekla.com/), and
[Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/).

What brings me most satisfaction is developing tools and techniques that have industry-wide impact.

I'm most proud of the work I did on
* The Witness*, not just because of its reception, but because of the amazing team we assembled and the
freedom I had to develop new techniques for

At NVIDIA, I pioneered the use of GPUs for texture compression and developed the
GPU-accelerated
[NVIDIA Texture Tools](https://github.com/castano/nvidia-texture-tools) which
gained [wide adoption](https://steamdb.info/tech/SDK/NVIDIA_Texture_Tools/) in
the game industry. I also had an influential role in the design of the hardware
tessellation pipeline and invented
[many of the techniques](http://www.ludicon.com/publications/Gamefest-2008-DisplacedSubdivisionSurfaceTessellation-Slides.PDF)
that run on it.

As a side effect of this work, I've written papers, organized courses, edited books, and given talks, but I'm most comfortable in a focused environment solving problems that advance the state of the art.

[Ludicon](https://ludicon.com/) is my attempt to create the environment where I
can do my best work.
