**Privacy Policy for all Spark Demos and Examples**

Last Updated: 2023/09/03

**1. Information We Do Not Collect**

We want to make it clear that we do not collect, store, or share any personal data from our users. We respect your privacy and believe in maintaining strict confidentiality. Therefore, you can use our app with the assurance that your data remains safe and private.

**2. Information Automatically Collected**

We do not automatically collect any information about your device, location, or usage patterns. We do not employ cookies, trackers, or any other technology to gather data without your consent.

**3. Third-Party Services**

Our apps do not integrate with any third-party services, and we do not share your data with any external parties. We are solely committed to providing a private and secure user experience.

**4. Contacting Us**

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices or this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [spark@ludicon.com](mailto:spark@ludicon.com). We will respond to your inquiries as promptly as possible.

**5. Changes to this Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will post any changes on this page, and the date of the last update will be revised accordingly. Please check this Privacy Policy periodically for updates.

We appreciate your trust in us and your commitment to maintaining your privacy while using our app.
