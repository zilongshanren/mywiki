I had been working in New York City for just over a year when I sat down at one
of my [favorite cafes](https://sweatshop.coffee/) in my neighborhood to write a
personal journal entry. I gave it the title *“On the crossroads between
goal-oriented and process-oriented”* and I wrote down stream-of-consciousness
reflections on my life, career, and how I wanted to do things differently.

It was October 2015, and I had finished grad school and moved to NYC to work
full-time as a Software Developer in a fin-tech company. I was having what I
have come to see as the seminal quarter-life crisis many folks go through when
they finish their formal years of education. I had been chasing goals all my
life up until then, and now I had the luxury and privilege of deciding whether I
should set another goal or do things radically different than what I had done so
far. *Goal-oriented* versus *Process-oriented*, I called it.
