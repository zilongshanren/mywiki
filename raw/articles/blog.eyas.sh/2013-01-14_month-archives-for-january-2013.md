*On Twitter, @AnonymousIRC claims to have been responsible for bringing the
*.mit.edu network down for a few hours last night in protest of MIT’s legal
“back-and-forth” with Aaron Swartz. Later on they went on to ‘hack’ two
sub-domains in the MIT network (the “Cogeneration Project” and an RLE site,
seemingly they just picked two random ones easy to break) posting a message
about Aaron. Here are some of my thoughts about attacking the MIT network
following this tragedy:*

MIT has more than 10,000 students, researchers, and professors working on areas ranging from cancer research, renewable energy, and urban planning to free software (FSF), open internet (W3C), and the future of computing in general (CSAIL). In many ways, what MIT, as a community, stands for is more representative of the Swartz and Anon cause than the Anonymous themselves.
