Back at the end of April I participated in the [Ludum Dare 53 Jam](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/53/). I figured I’d just do a quick game over the weekend. And I did just that and the game turned out *okay*. Unfortunately on the last day I started feeling like I’m about to get the flu. So I did the adult thing and went to bed instead of pushing through. I did finish the game, but I never got around to making the web version and putting a real release together. It’s here now. Better late than never I guess.

### Conveyor Worm- Release 1

### Download

Windows: [ConveyorWorm.zip (0.16 MB)](https://www.kloonigames.com/download.php?file=conveyorworm.zip) (Release 1)

Play in browser: [At itch.io!](https://kloonigames.itch.io/conveyor-worm)

**Instructions**

Push and pull the blocks, complete the levels!

- Arrow keys: move
- Z: Undo
- Space: Restart level
