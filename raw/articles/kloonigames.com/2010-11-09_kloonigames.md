A while back I experimented with the idea of rendering pixel art using [post it notes](https://www.kloonigames.com/blog/games/sticky_notes). That game was pretty bad, but the idea of rendering pixel art in interesting ways was pretty neat. Inspired by past experiences and this [painting by tubbypaws](http://tubbypaws.blogspot.com/2010/09/warp-pipes-and-waste-disposal-units-are.html) I decided to test out if I could render pixels to look like they were painted. The results were surprisingly good, so I crafted a smallish game around that. A big chunk of the game was made at [Karlshamn game jam](http://digitalamedier.bth.se/1/1.0.1.0/49/1/?item=art_art-s1/148&group=/) with Martin and Niklas.

**Instructions**

A game about killing space monsters.

You’re trapped on a space station.

[Arrow keys] Move the guy around.

[CTRL] or [Z] Fires your gun.

[ALT] or [X] Strafes.

[I] for inventory.
