I made a simple – maybe the simplest – board game! Its called Hide No Seek and it is available at the a print and play service [The Game Crafter](https://www.thegamecrafter.com/games/hide-no-seek). Although if you want to play it you can also just [print it out](https://www.kloonigames.com/download.php?file=boardgame_hide_no_seek.pdf), or write the rules on a card with a sharpie.

The Game Crafter had a [single card game design competition](https://www.thegamecrafter.com/contests/single-card-challenge). You had to design a whole game with rules and everything on a single poker size playing card. I liked the challenge and ended up making a design I thought was rather obvious.
