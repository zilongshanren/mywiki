**Crayon Physics Deluxe is out now for iPad!** And it’s free for the first 24 hours. [Get it from iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/crayon-physics-deluxe-for/id551347494?ls=1&mt=8).

We finally made a proper iPad port of Crayon Physics Deluxe and to celebrate its launch we’re giving it away for free.

There’s a bunch of new stuff in this version of Crayon Physics Deluxe. Biggest of them being new music from [C418](http://c418.org/) (of Minecraft fame).

We’re also getting ready to launch the Android version of Crayon Physics Deluxe on Google Play. The port is being done by [Apportable](http://www.apportable.com/) and they’ve been doing a really great job. We’re still fixing few of the last issues before launching it.

**Edit: ** The PC, Mac and Linux versions of Crayon Physics Deluxe are also free for the next 24 hours. Get them here: [http://crayonphysics.com/free_crayon.php](http://crayonphysics.com/free_crayon.php)
